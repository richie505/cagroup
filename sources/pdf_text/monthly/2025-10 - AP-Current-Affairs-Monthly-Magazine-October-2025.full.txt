o Designation of district headquarters

Introduction
 Significant administrative changes are
expected in Krishna district and
adjoining regions of Andhra Pradesh, as
the Group of Ministers (GoM) formed
by the State Government has reportedly
decided that every Assembly
constituency and its mandals should be
placed entirely within a single revenue
division.
 This move is part of an ongoing review
of district restructuring, revenue
division boundaries, and mandal
jurisdiction that began after the creation
of new districts in 2022.

 The realignment aims to:

forwarded to the State Cabinet for
approval.
