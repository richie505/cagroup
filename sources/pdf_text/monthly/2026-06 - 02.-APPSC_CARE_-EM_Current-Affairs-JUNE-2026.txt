Why in News?

Energy Efficiency Services Limited (EESL), under the Union Ministry of Power, has finalised an action plan to transform Andhra Pradesh’s MSME sector through advanced technologies in energy efficiency and water efficiency.

• The initiative will be implemented under the Centre’s RAMP Programme and the Andhra Pradesh MSME & Entrepreneur Development Policy 4.0. It is being

described as one of India’s largest MSME efficiency programmes.

Background

MSMEs are an important part of Andhra Pradesh’s economy. They provide employment, support local entrepreneurship and contribute to industrial growth.

• However, many MSMEs face problems such as high electricity costs, inefficient machinery, water wastage, low productivity and limited access to modern technologies. These issues reduce their competitiveness.

• To address these problems, Andhra Pradesh is working with EESL to introduce energy and water-saving technologies in MSME clusters.

What is EESL?

Energy Efficiency Services Limited is a public sector company under the Union Ministry of Power.

• It works to promote energy-efficient technologies and reduce energy consumption in different sectors.

• EESL is known for implementing large-scale efficiency programmes in areas such as LED lighting, electric mobility, smart meters and energy-efficient appliances.

About RAMP Programme

RAMP stands for Raising and Accelerating MSME Performance.

• It is a World Bank-supported Central Sector Scheme launched by the Government of India in 2022. It is implemented by the Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises during 2022-23 to 2026-27.

• The programme aims to strengthen the MSME ecosystem by improving access to technology, finance, markets, digitisation, innovation and greening initiatives.

Key Highlights of the AP MSME Sustainability Plan

Under Phase-I of the project, EESL will implement a structured roadmap for MSME efficiency improvement in Andhra Pradesh. The main components include:

• Cluster mapping and diagnostic studies in 15 industrial clusters

• Investment-grade energy audits for 452 MSMEs

• Water audits for 300 MSMEs

• Capacity-building workshops for MSME units

• Deployment of energy-efficient and water- efficient technologies

• Technical support for clean technology adoption

• Promotion of sustainable industrial practices The programme is expected to improve productivity, reduce operational costs and support environmental sustainability.

Objectives of the Initiative

Why in News?

• Google and the Andhra Pradesh government recently broke ground for the Google Cloud India AI Hub in Visakhapatnam.

• The project is expected to involve a massive investment and includes a 1 GW hyperscale AI data centre, high-capacity digital connectivity and international subsea cable landing infrastructure. The project is being developed with partners such as AdaniConneX and Airtel Nxtra.

• The project is seen as a major step in India’s digital infrastructure growth. However, it has also raised concerns about power demand, water use, environmental safeguards, data sovereignty and dependence on foreign technology providers.

What is the Google Cloud India AI Hub?

The Google Cloud India AI Hub is a large digital infrastructure project proposed in Visakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh. It includes:

• A 1 GW hyperscale AI data centre

• High-performance computing infrastructure

• International subsea cable connectivity

• Cloud and AI services

• Digital ecosystem development around Visakhapatnam

APPSC News Summary

A hyperscale data centre is a very large facility that stores, processes and manages huge amounts of data. It is used for cloud computing, artificial intelligence, machine learning, digital services and large-scale internet applications.

What is America-India Connect?

America-India Connect is Google’s international digital connectivity initiative.

• It aims to develop new subsea cable routes linking India with other major regions. Google has stated that the initiative will develop a direct fibre-optic path between Vizag and Chennai on India’s east coast and connect it with routes towards South Africa and the United States.

• This can make Visakhapatnam an important digital gateway on India’s eastern coast.

3\. High Water Requirement

• AI data centres generate large amounts of heat.

• In humid coastal conditions such as Visakhapatnam, efficient cooling can become difficult.

• If evaporative cooling is used, it may require large quantities of water.

• The article estimates that similar facilities may consume more than 2 million litres per day per 100 MW. For a 1 GW facility, this may mean nearly 20 million litres per day.

• This raises concerns because Visakhapatnam district periodically faces water stress, especially in summer.

4\. Environmental Impact Large data centres require land, power infrastructure, cooling systems, water supply and backup generators. Possible environmental impacts include:

• Groundwater stress

• Increased water extraction

• Local air pollution from diesel backup

• Heat generation

• Pressure on land resources

• Carbon emissions if renewable supply is not truly additional Therefore, environmental safeguards are essential.

5\. Data Sovereignty Concerns

• The project can help Indian firms store and process data within India.

• However, dependence on a single foreign cloud provider may create risks.

• India may become a location for “sovereign AI” only in physical terms, while the technology stack, algorithms, cloud architecture and decision-making remain controlled by a foreign company.

• If Indian firms and public institutions depend heavily on one proprietary cloud system, they may face vendor lock-in.

Who was Duggirala Gopalakrishnayya?

Duggirala Gopalakrishnayya was a prominent freedom fighter, social reformer and nationalist leader from Andhra Pradesh. He is popularly known as:

• Andhra Gandhi

• Andhra Ratna He earned these titles because of his commitment to the nationalist cause, his leadership in mass movements and his ability to mobilise people through peaceful and disciplined methods.

Early Life and Education

• Duggirala Gopalakrishnayya studied at the Bapatla Board School.

• He began his career as a clerk in the Bapatla Taluk Office.

• His life shows the journey of an ordinary government employee becoming an extraordinary nationalist leader.

Role in the Freedom Movement

Duggirala Gopalakrishnayya played an important role in spreading nationalist consciousness in Andhra. He inspired people to participate in the freedom struggle and helped create political awareness among the masses. His leadership was marked by:

• Duggirala Gopalakrishnayya emerged as the hero of this movement. It was linked with resistance against colonial administrative policies and became a powerful example of mass mobilisation.

• The movement attracted national attention because it showed how people could resist

An algal bloom is the rapid growth of algae or cyanobacteria in water. Its likely sequence is:

▪ Nutrient-rich water promotes rapid algal growth.

▪ The bloom forms a dense layer near the coast.

▪ Algae die and decompose.

▪ Microorganisms consume dissolved oxygen during decomposition.
