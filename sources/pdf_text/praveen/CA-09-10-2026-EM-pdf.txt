09th OCTOBER 2026 World Post Day–2026: October 9

• World Post Day (WPD) is observed globally every year on 9 October to commemorate the establishment of the Universal Postal Union (UPU) in Bern, Switzerland, in 1874.

• 2026 Theme: “World Post Day: One Postal Network, Infinite Possibilities”.

• In 1969, the UPU Congress held in Tokyo, Japan, decided to observe 9 October as ‘UPU Day’.

• In 1984, at the UPU Congress held in Hamburg, Germany, the name ‘UPU Day’ was officially changed to ‘World Post Day’. The day aims to highlight the importance of postal services in global communication, trade, economic development and social connectivity. Additional Information

• The Universal Postal Union (UPU) was established on 9 October 1874 through the Treaty of Bern.

• Headquarters : Bern, Switzerland.

• The UPU became a specialised agency of the United Nations in 1948.

• Member Countries : 192; India became a member on 1 July 1876.

• The UPU is the main international organisation that coordinates postal policies and services among member countries.

• National Postal Week is observed in India from 9–15 October.

• India Post functions under the Department of Posts, Ministry of Communications.

• India’s first adhesive postage stamp, ‘Scinde Dawk’, was issued in the Sindh region in 1852.
