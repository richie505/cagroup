Innovative Cooling Design for Electronic Devices by IIT Madras

• Researchers at IIT Madras developed and successfully tested a "Flat Plate Pulsating Heat Pipe" (FPPHP), a cutting-edge cooling design to prevent electronic devices from overheating.

• The research was led by Professor Arvind Pattamatta and Dr. Pallab Sinha Mahapatra from the Department of Mechanical Engineering.

• The technology is designed for efficient heat control in devices such as smartphones, laptops, data centers, defense electronics, and electric vehicles (EVs).

NATIONAL New Geographical Indication (GI) Tags for Himachal Pradesh

• Several products from Himachal Pradesh have recently been granted Geographical Indication (GI) status to protect traditional knowledge and cultural heritage.

• The newly recognized products include Seabuckthorn (Charma) from the Spiti region, Salooni Safed Makka (white maize) from Chamba district, and Chamba Metal Art.

• Other products receiving the tag are Sirmauri Loiya, Kinnauri Topi (cap), Kinnauri Apple, Kinnauri Jewelry, and Sepuvadi from Mandi district.

• A GI tag is an intellectual property right given to products that have a specific geographical origin and possess qualities or a reputation due to that origin.

• Geographical Indications of Goods (Registration and Protection) Act: Enacted in 1999 and brought into force in 2003; it is administered by the Ministry of Commerce and Industry.

Status of GI Registrations in India (2025–26)

• A total of 125 new GI tags were registered in India during the 2025–26 financial year, bringing the national total to 822.

• The states receiving the highest number of new tags in this period were Madhya Pradesh (26), West Bengal (24), Himachal Pradesh (13), and Jharkhand (11).

• Nationally, GI registrations are comprised of approximately 53% handicrafts and 31% agricultural products, with the remainder being food, manufactured, or natural products.

Establishment of the Bureau of Port Security (BoPS)

• The Indian government has decided to establish the Bureau of Port Security (BoPS) to protect coastal areas, ports, and ships from physical and cyber threats.

• BoPS is being modeled after the Bureau of Civil Aviation Security (BCAS) and will operate under the Ministry of Ports, Shipping and Waterways.

• The Central Industrial Security Force (CISF) has been designated as the Recognized Security Organization (RSO) to conduct port security audits and train private security personnel.

• Ministry of Ports, Shipping and Waterways: Led by Union Minister Sarbananda Sonowal.
