Portugal and Austria Elected as Non-Permanent Members of the UN Security Council
• At the 80th Session of the UN General Assembly held in New York on 3 June 2026, Portugal and Austria
were elected as non-permanent members of the UN Security Council (UNSC) for the 2027–2028 term.
Their tenure will run from 1 January 2027 to 31 December 2028.
• Representing the Western European and Others Group (WEOG), Portugal secured 134 votes and Austria
131 votes. Both countries will serve on the Security Council for the fourth time.
• Kyrgyzstan, Trinidad and Tobago, and Zimbabwe were also elected for the 2027–2028 term, replacing
Denmark, Greece, Pakistan, Panama and Somalia, whose terms end on 31 December 2026.
• The UNSC is one of the six principal organs of the United Nations and comprises 15 members—5
permanent (China, France, Russia, the United Kingdom and the United States) and 10 non-permanent
members elected for two-year terms.
• United Nations: Established – 1945 | Headquarters – New York, USA | Secretary-General – António
Guterres.
United Nations Establishes International Scientific Panel on AI
• The United Nations established the Independent International Scientific Panel on AI, its first global
scientific body dedicated to Artificial Intelligence.
• The panel consists of 40 global experts appointed for a three-year term to provide independent scientific
assessments and technical advice on AI.
• Prof. B. Ravindran of IIT Madras has been appointed as the sole representative from India.
• United Nations: Established – 1945 | Headquarters – New York, USA.
UN General Assembly Adopts Political Declaration on HIV/AIDS
• The UN General Assembly adopted a new Political Declaration on HIV/AIDS in June 2026 with 149
countries voting in favour, 8 against and 14 abstaining.
• The declaration reaffirmed the global commitment to end AIDS as a public health threat by 2030, while
acknowledging that the world remains off track to achieve this goal.
• The 2026 UN High-Level Meeting on HIV/AIDS was held in New York on 22–23 June under the theme
“United to End AIDS.”
• The declaration aligns with SDG-3, which targets ending the AIDS epidemic by 2030.
• UNAIDS: Established – 1994 | Headquarters – Geneva, Switzerland.
Global Call to Action on Methane
• UN Secretary-General António Guterres launched the Global Call to Action on Methane during London
Climate Action Week 2026.
• He described methane reduction as one of the fastest and most cost-effective ways to curb global warming,
noting that methane contributes about one-third of current global warming.
• The initiative calls for reducing methane leaks in the fossil fuel sector, eliminating routine flaring and
adopting science-based standards.
• The Global Methane Pledge was launched at COP26 (Glasgow) in 2021, with 159 countries and the
European Commission committing to reduce methane emissions by 30% from 2020 levels by 2030.
• The UN also called for AI data centres to operate on renewable energy by 2030.
• Methane (CH₄) is a potent greenhouse gas.

35 Countries Sign AI Opportunity Declaration at Pax Silicon Summit
• At the 2nd Pax Silicon Summit held in Washington, D.C., USA, on 25–26 June 2026, India and 34 other
countries signed a Joint Declaration on Artificial Intelligence Opportunities.
• Pax Silicon is a US-led initiative focused on building trusted and resilient supply chains for AI,
semiconductors, critical minerals, energy, advanced manufacturing and infrastructure.

Union also joined the initiative.

Operation Amistad: India Sends Humanitarian Aid to Venezuela

dispatching over 35 tonnes of relief supplies through Indian Air Force C-17 aircraft.

Cubes (Bharat Health Initiative for Sahyog Hita and Maitri) and other emergency relief materials.
• Amistad means “friendship” in Spanish and reflects India's humanitarian assistance and disaster relief

United States Becomes India’s Largest LPG Supplier
• In 2026, the United States emerged as India's largest supplier of Liquefied Petroleum Gas (LPG),
accounting for over 55% of India's LPG imports in May 2026 by supplying about 6.66 lakh tonnes.
• India accelerated diversification of its energy imports due to West Asian tensions and security concerns in
the Strait of Hormuz affecting supplies from Gulf countries.
• LPG is mainly a mixture of propane and butane and is widely used for domestic cooking, commercial and
industrial purposes.
• The Strait of Hormuz lies between Oman and Iran and is one of the world's most important energy shipping
routes, connecting the Persian Gulf with the Gulf of Oman and the Arabian Sea.
US Renames Indo-Pacific Command as Pacific Command
• In June 2026, the United States renamed the U.S. Indo-Pacific Command (USINDOPACOM) back to the
U.S. Pacific Command (USPACOM).
• Established on 1 January 1947, the command operated as U.S. Pacific Command until 2018, when it was
renamed to reflect the strategic importance of the Indo-Pacific region.
• USPACOM is one of the oldest and largest Unified Combatant Commands of the United States,
headquartered at Camp H.M. Smith, Hawaii.
• The Indo-Pacific is a strategically significant region connecting the Indian and Pacific Oceans, where
India, the United States, Japan and Australia cooperate through the Quad.
15 Countries Adopt Mombasa Declaration Against Illegal Fishing
• Fifteen countries from Africa, Asia, Europe, the Caribbean and the Pacific adopted the Mombasa
Declaration to combat Illegal, Unreported and Unregulated (IUU) Fishing during the 11th Our Ocean
Conference (OOC) held in Kenya.
• The declaration aims to strengthen transparency in fisheries governance, improve access to information
on fishing vessels, ownership and licences, enhance international cooperation and protect marine
biodiversity.
• IUU Fishing is considered a major threat to marine biodiversity, fish stocks, food security and the
livelihoods of coastal communities.
• Our Ocean Conference: Launched – 2014 | Objective – Strengthening international cooperation for ocean
conservation and sustainable marine resource management.
