06th OCTOBER 2026 Question: Which organisation(s) developed the indigenous Autonomous Underwater Vehicle (AUV) ‘Jaya’?

• Answer: CSIR–National Institute of Oceanography (CSIR-NIO), Goa, developed the indigenous ‘Jaya’ AUV in collaboration with the Indian Navy’s Design Directorate.

• CSIR-NIO, Goa, in collaboration with the Indian Navy’s Design Directorate, developed the indigenous Autonomous Underwater Vehicle (AUV) ‘Jaya’ for ocean research, coastal security and underwater surveillance.

• Hindustan Shipyard Limited (HSL), Visakhapatnam, signed a Transfer of Technology (ToT) agreement for large-scale production of ‘Jaya’, marking HSL’s entry into the manufacturing of high-tech marine platforms.

• Weighing around 54 kg and measuring 1.7 metres in length, ‘Jaya’ can operate autonomously at depths of up to 200 metres; nearly 80% of its components are sourced from Indian industries. Its flexible, modular design allows sensors to be changed according to mission requirements, making it a multi-mission platform.

• It is equipped with a CTD sensor for seawater parameters, an optical sensor for oxygen, hyperspectral radiometers for light measurements, and chlorophyll and turbidity sensors for environmental and pollution monitoring.

• A side-scan sonar can be integrated for seabed mapping and surveillance, enabling the detection of underwater objects and potential threats.

• It can be used for port and harbour security, coastal surveillance, harbour mapping, monitoring changes in the marine environment, and detecting underwater mines or other submerged objects.

• Deploying large numbers of small AUVs can provide extensive underwater surveillance at lower cost while reducing the need to send crewed submarines on hazardous surveillance missions.

• HSL plans to manufacture dozens of AUVs annually on an industrial scale; its assembly system can also be scaled up in the future to manufacture larger AUVs without major additional investment. Autonomous Underwater Vehicle (AUV)
