World Press Freedom Index 2026 – Key Highlights

• The World Press Freedom Index 2026 is published by Reporters Without Borders (RSF) and covers 180 countries.

• It is an annual ranking that assesses the level of press freedom across countries. The index is based on five indicators

• Political context

• Legal framework

• Economic context

• Socio-cultural context

• Safety of journalists Global Highlights

• Press freedom has fallen to its lowest level in decades.

• More than 50% of countries fall under the “Difficult” or “Very Serious” categories.

• Around 100 countries recorded a decline in their rankings.

• Only a few countries are placed in the “Good” category.

• Top 5 Countries : Norway (1st), Netherlands, Estonia, Denmark, Sweden.

• Bottom 5 Countries : Eritrea, North Korea, China, Iran, Saudi Arabia.

• India's Rank: 157th out of 180, down from 151st in 2025, and placed in the “Difficult” category.

• Reasons for India's Lower Ranking: Violence against journalists, arrests and legal pressure, concentration of media ownership, political influence, and online harassment.

• Major Global Challenges: Criminalisation of journalism, rise of authoritarian governments, economic pressure on media, impact of wars, and spread of misinformation.

• Exam Relevance: Linked to Article 19(1)(a) of the Constitution of India. Additional Information Reporters Without Borders (RSF)

• Established : 1985

• Headquarters : Paris, France

• Role : Promotion and protection of press freedom.
