Padma Awards 2026 Padma Awards Announced by the Government of India

• Institution Year: 1954.

• Announcement: Made every year on the occasion of Republic Day through an official notification issued by the Ministry of Home Affairs.

• Discontinuation: The awards were suspended twice (1978–1979 and 1993–1997).

• Maximum Limit: Not more than 120 awards in a year (excluding posthumous awards and awards to foreigners). Posthumous Awards: Not awarded normally; however, posthumous awards may be given if the death occurred within one year.

• Cash Prize: No monetary award is attached.

• Highest Padma Award: Padma Vibhushan (Second: Padma Bhushan; Third: Padma Shri). Fields Covered (Different Fields)
