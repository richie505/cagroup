• The vaccine was developed and manufactured by the Serum Institute of India (SII), headquartered in Pune, Maharashtra. It aims to prevent cervical cancer and other HPV-related diseases.

• WHO prequalification recognises that the vaccine meets international standards of quality, safety and efficacy, facilitating procurement by United Nations agencies and international vaccination programmes.

• CERVAVAC is a gender-neutral vaccine designed to protect both females and males against HPV-related infections and diseases.

• The quadrivalent vaccine provides protection against four HPV types — 6, 11, 16 and 18.

• HPV-16 and HPV-18 cause approximately 70% of cervical cancer cases worldwide, while HPV-6 and HPV-11 are responsible for most genital warts.

• The recognition strengthens India’s indigenous vaccine manufacturing capabilities and supports the expansion of affordable HPV vaccination worldwide. Additional Information

13th OCTOBER 2026 Draft National Steel Policy–2047 Released

• The Union Government released the Draft National Steel Policy–2047, aiming to increase India's annual crude steel production capacity to 604 million tonnes (60.4 crore tonnes) by 2047.

• The policy focuses on enhancing the global competitiveness of India's steel industry, strengthening domestic production and addressing challenges arising from steel imports.

• Domestic finished steel demand is projected to reach 505 million tonnes (50.5 crore tonnes) by 2047, while steel exports are expected to increase to 45 million tonnes (4.5 crore tonnes).

• India's per capita steel consumption is projected to rise from 116 kg in 2025–26 to 302 kg by 2047, an increase of approximately 2.6 times.

• Government support will be provided to the secondary steel sector, which comprises around 2,550 units and accounts for 47% of the country's total crude steel production capacity.

• Key areas of support include raw material availability, technological modernisation, skill development and industrial safety. Additional Information

• The Union Ministry of Steel is primarily responsible for formulating policies related to India's steel industry.
