ICC Men’s T20 World Cup 2026

• This was the 10th edition of the ICC Men’s T20 World Cup.

• Winner: India (defeated New Zealand in the final by 96 runs).

• Runner-up: New Zealand.

• The tournament was organized by the International Cricket Council (ICC).

• Host Countries: India and Sri Lanka.

• Tournament Schedule: 7 February 2026 – 8 March 2026.

• Total Teams Participated: 20 teams.

• Final Match Date: 8 March 2026.

• Final Venue: Narendra Modi Stadium, Ahmedabad.

• Finalists: India and New Zealand.

• Player of the Series: Sanju Samson.

• Player of the Match (Final): Jasprit Bumrah.

• New Team Debut: Italy qualified for the tournament for the first time.

• Major Venues in India: Ahmedabad, Mumbai, Chennai, Delhi, and Kolkata.

• Venues in Sri Lanka: Colombo and Kandy.

• The 20-team format, which was introduced in the ICC Men’s T20 World Cup 2024, continued in this tournament as well.
