• For the first time in India, 3D Concrete Printing (3DCP) technology is being introduced in government housing in Andhra Pradesh; 100 houses will be constructed on a pilot basis at Burripalem in Tenali constituency under Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana–Gramin (PMAY-G) 2.0.

• The Central Building Research Institute (CBRI) will construct the houses, and an agreement between the State Housing Department, Central Government and CBRI will be signed on October 4 in the presence of Chief Minister N. Chandrababu Naidu.

• As per Central Government norms, each house will be constructed over 325 sq. ft.; CBRI has modified the technology and developed a special structural design to improve quality and suit Indian conditions.

• Additional assistance will be provided for 3DCP houses over and above the existing financial assistance under PMAY-G 2.0; the Central and State Governments will bear the additional cost to promote the new technology.

• 3DCP is a modern robotic construction technology: first, the house is designed using 3D CAD software; a special concrete mixture is then pumped into the printer; a computer-controlled robotic arm/gantry printer deposits concrete through a nozzle layer by layer to complete the structure.
