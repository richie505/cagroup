Fiscal Health Index 2023–24: Telangana Ranked 7th, Andhra Pradesh Ranked 17th ➢ Key Details 1. Released by: NITI Aayog 2. Release Date: 11 March 2026 3. Edition: Second Edition 4. Data Coverage: Assesses the fiscal performance of states for the financial year 2023–24. Main Objectives

• To evaluate the fiscal health of Indian states.

• To compare states based on revenue generation, expenditure quality, debt levels, and fiscal deficit management. States Covered

• The index evaluated 18 major states. Key Indicators Used in the Fiscal Health Index

• Revenue Mobilisation – Ability of states to generate revenue.

• Quality of Expenditure – Efficiency and effectiveness of government spending.

• Fiscal Prudence – Financial discipline in managing deficits.

• Debt Index – Level of public debt.

• Debt Sustainability – Ability to manage and repay debt. Top 5 States ➢ The top five positions were secured by

• Odisha

• Goa

• Jharkhand

• Gujarat

• Maharashtra ❖ Telangana

• Rank: 7th

• The state increased development expenditure and allocations for social services.

• Greater emphasis was placed on infrastructure and welfare programmes.

• In 2023–24, 80% of total revenue came from the state’s own tax revenue.

• Non-tax revenue grew at an average of about 33% annually since 2019–20.

• Tax revenue increased by 13.4%.

• The share of grants-in-aid declined to about 6%.

• The state moved from a revenue deficit of ₹6,254 crore in 2019–20 to a surplus of ₹5,943 crore in 2022–23.

• Fiscal deficit increased slightly from 3.29% to 3.33%.

• By 2023–24, the state’s total debt reached ₹5.17 lakh crore, which is 34.47% of the Gross State Domestic Product (GSDP). ❖ Andhra Pradesh
