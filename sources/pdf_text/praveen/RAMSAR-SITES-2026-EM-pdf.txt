100th Ramsar Site of India: Jayaprakash Narayan Bird Sanctuary (Surha Tal)

• Jayaprakash Narayan Bird Sanctuary (Surha Tal), located in Ballia district of Uttar Pradesh, has been designated as India's 100th Ramsar Site.

• The recognition highlights India's commitment to wetland conservation and biodiversity protection.

• Surha Tal is an important freshwater wetland ecosystem.

• It serves as a habitat for numerous migratory and resident bird species. The wetland plays a crucial role in

• Ecological balance

• Groundwater recharge

• Fisheries

• Local livelihoods

• Ramsar designation provides international recognition and strengthens conservation efforts.

• With this addition, the total number of Ramsar Sites in India has reached 100. Recent Ramsar Site Additions
