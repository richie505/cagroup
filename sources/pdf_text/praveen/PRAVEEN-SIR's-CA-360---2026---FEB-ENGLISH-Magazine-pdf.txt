India–Israel Bilateral Relations – Special Strategic Partnership

International Climate Initiative (IKI) – India-Germany Climate Partnership

• Germany and India have launched a €20 million (approximately ₹180 crore) large grant project under the International Climate Initiative (IKI), aimed at strengthening India's climate resilience.

• IKI is a key climate financing mechanism of the German Federal Government, funding international projects related to climate change mitigation, adaptation, REDD+, and biodiversity conservation, primarily supporting developing countries.

• Established in 2008, IKI currently operates in over 150 countries. India, Brazil, China, South Africa, Mexico, and Indonesia are among the 14 priority countries.

• IKI supports the implementation and expansion of Nationally Determined Contributions (NDCs) submitted under the Paris Agreement, as well as the achievement of goals set under the Convention on Biological Diversity (CBD).

• International Climate Initiative (IKI) – Headquarters: Berlin, Germany; Established: 2008; Funding Source: German Federal Government; Key Areas: Climate Mitigation, Adaptation, REDD+, Biodiversity Conservation.

• Germany Capital: Berlin; Currency: Euro (€); President: Frank-Walter Steinmeier; Chancellor: Friedrich Merz; one of the main global donors to climate funds.

• Key Exam Information: IKI is Germany’s primary bilateral climate finance mechanism; India is one of the 14 priority countries; NDCs are a key part of the Paris Agreement; Climate Resilience refers to the capacity to withstand climate impacts.

India Signs Five-Year Agreement with World Food Programme

• India has signed a five-year agreement with the United Nations World Food Programme (WFP) to support global hunger alleviation efforts, particularly in Africa, West Asia, and conflict-affected regions.

• The agreement includes a supply of up to 200,000 metric tons of rice, with up to 25% being broken grains, to support emergency food relief efforts.

• The contract is designed to ensure timely delivery of food grains for urgent humanitarian assistance, with the option to extend the agreement beyond the five-year period.

• This collaboration strengthens India's role in global food security and humanitarian cooperation.

Privileges Committee of Parliament

• The Privileges Committee and Ethics Committee of the Lok Sabha were not constituted for nearly two years.

• The Privileges Committee functions as a semi-judicial body, safeguarding the special privileges of the House and its members, rooted in British parliamentary traditions.

• Parliamentary privileges in India are based on constitutional provisions and traditions, as defined under Articles 105 and 194.

Denmark Eliminates Mother-to-Child HIV and Syphilis Transmission

• On 27 February 2026, the World Health Organization (WHO) certified Denmark as the first country in the European Union to eliminate mother-to-child transmission of HIV and syphilis.

India’s First National Biobank for Lysosomal Storage Disorders (LSDs)

• In February 2026, the Government of India inaugurated the country’s first government-supported national biobank for Lysosomal Storage Disorders (LSDs) in Ahmedabad, Gujarat.

• The biobank is established at the Institute of Human Genetics within the Foundation for Research in Genetics and Endocrinology (FRIGE).

• Its primary objective is to create a centralized national repository for research on rare genetic disorders.

• The biobank collects and stores biological samples such as DNA, blood, plasma, urine, and clinical data. It houses samples and clinical-genomic data from 530 patients collected over 17 years (2008–2025).

• LSDs are rare inherited metabolic disorders where enzyme deficiencies cause harmful substances to accumulate in cells, affecting organs such as the brain, liver, spleen, and bones.

• The biobank facilitates advanced research, development of new diagnostic methods, drug targets, and treatment strategies.

• Collaborative programs with Tata Institute for Genetics and Society (TIGS) in Bengaluru aim to develop human stem cell-based disease models.

• FRIGE Location: Ahmedabad, Gujarat; Nature: Genetics and endocrine disease research institute.

• TIGS Established: 2020; Headquarters: Bengaluru; Nature: Genetics and biotechnology research institute.

• Gujarat Capital: Gandhinagar; Chief Minister: Bhupendrabhai Patel; Governor: Acharya Devvrat.

World’s First Doctor-Led AI Home Healthcare System ‘iLive Connect’

• In February 2026, India launched ‘iLive Connect,’ the world’s first doctor-led AI home healthcare system, providing 24×7 ICU-level monitoring from home.

• Developed by Dr. Rahul Chandroda and Dr. Vivek Kumar, the system offers personalized continuous medical care.

• It uses FDA- and CE-certified wearable devices (chest biosensor patches, wristbands) to continuously track critical parameters including two-lead ECG, heart response, respiration rate, oxygen levels, body temperature, blood pressure trends, physical activity, and heart rate variability.

• Collected data is securely sent to the cloud and monitored 24/7 by experts at a central medical command center.
