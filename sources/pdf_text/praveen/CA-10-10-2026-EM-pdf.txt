• CA|TS is an international conservation accreditation framework that assesses the effectiveness of tiger reserve management, habitat protection and conservation outcomes.

• CA|TS was launched at the first Asian Parks Congress held in Japan in 2013.

• It sets international standards for tiger protection, habitat management, local community participation, wildlife monitoring and sustainable conservation. CA|TS consists of seven major pillars and 17 elements used to assess the management of conservation areas.

• The seven pillars are: Importance–Status, Management, Community Participation, Tourism (optional), Protection, Habitat Management and Tiger Population.

• The first five pillars relate to Conservation Assured (CA), while the last two relate to Tiger Standards (TS).

• In India, the Global Tiger Forum (GTF) and WWF-India work as implementation partners with the National Tiger Conservation Authority (NTCA) for CA|TS assessments.
