Women and Men in India 2025 Report – Key Highlights

• The Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (MoSPI) released the 27th edition of the Women and Men in India 2025 report at Bhubaneswar.

• Nature: An annual statistical report, first published in 1995, providing information on the socio-economic status of women and men in India.

• Coverage: Based on data from various ministries, covering population, health, education, economic participation, and violence against women.

• Sex Ratio at Birth: Improved from 904 (2017–19) to 917 (2021–23).

• Infant Mortality: Infant mortality rates for both male and female children declined during 2008–2023.

• Gender Parity in Education: Achieved from the primary to higher secondary level.

• Higher Education: The Gross Enrolment Ratio (GER) for women increased from 28.5 (2021–22) to 30.2 (2022–23).

• Labour Force Participation Rate (LFPR): Increased among persons aged 15 years and above, with the highest growth recorded among rural women.

• Rural Women: LFPR increased from 37.5% (2022) to 45.9% (2025).

• Managerial Positions: The growth in women holding managerial positions was 102.54% (2017–2025), compared to 73.80% for men.

• Significance: The report highlights progress in gender equality in education and employment and provides guidance for policy formulation. Additional Information Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (MoSPI)

• Established : 1999

• Headquarters : New Delhi

• Minister : Rao Inderjit Singh

• Role : Collection and dissemination of official statistics.
