Assembly Elections in 5 States/Union Territory – Results & Chief Ministers

• The Election Commission of India (ECI) conducted Assembly elections in five States/Union Territory.

• The elections were held in Assam, Kerala, Puducherry, Tamil Nadu, and West Bengal. Polling Schedule

• Assam – 9 April

• Kerala – 9 April

• Puducherry – 9 April

• Tamil Nadu – 23 April

• West Bengal – 23 April (Phase I) and 29 April (Phase II)

• Counting of Votes – 4 May

• Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar announced several new measures for the conduct of the elections.

• Electronic Voting Machines (EVMs) displayed colour photographs of all candidates to help voters identify them easily.

• A maximum of 1,200 voters were allotted to each polling station to reduce long queues.

• The Voter Information Slip clearly displayed the polling station details and voter information. Additional Information Election Commission of India (ECI)

• Headquarters : New Delhi

• Established : 25 January 1950

• Chief Election Commissioner: Gyanesh Kumar

• Role: Constitutional body responsible for conducting elections to Parliament, State Legislatures, and the offices of the President and Vice President. Assam
