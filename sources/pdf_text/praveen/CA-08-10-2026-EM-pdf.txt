• Each member has one vote; in the event of a tie, the RBI Governor has a casting vote.

• The MPC determines the policy repo rate to achieve the inflation target while keeping economic growth in mind. Inflation Target

08th OCTOBER 2026 Union Cabinet Approves Establishment of Integrated Transport and Logistics Authority (ITLA)

• The Union Cabinet approved the establishment of the Integrated Transport and Logistics Authority (ITLA) on 6 October 2026 to strengthen integrated planning across India's transport and logistics sectors.

• ITLA will function as a Special Purpose Vehicle (SPV) for research, planning, project appraisal, monitoring and impact assessment.

• It aims to coordinate fragmented planning across transport-related ministries and agencies and promote multimodal infrastructure development. ITLA will prepare a National Transport Master Plan with a planning horizon of 10 years or more.

• The Master Plan will integrate roads, railways, ports, civil aviation, inland waterways, coastal shipping, urban transport and logistics.

• ITLA will examine whether sector-specific plans covering approximately five years and annual plans are aligned with the National Transport Master Plan.

• ITLA will conduct technical appraisals of Government of India infrastructure projects valued at ₹500 crore or more, while financial appraisals will continue through existing mechanisms.

• It will strengthen coordination among transport infrastructure ministries and agencies and promote multimodal connectivity across different transport subsectors.

• Special Purpose Vehicle (SPV): A separate legal entity established for a specific project, activity or objective.

• SPVs are widely used in infrastructure projects for implementation, financing, management and risk allocation. Depending on the project, Central/State Governments, Public Sector Undertakings (PSUs) and private entities may participate in SPVs.
