Sahitya Akademi Awards 2025 Sahitya Akademi Awards 2025

• The Sahitya Akademi announced the Sahitya Akademi Awards 2025 in 24 Indian languages.

• These awards are given to honour outstanding literary works published in Indian languages.

• A total of 24 books were selected across different literary categories. Category-wise Distribution

• Poetry: 8 books

• Novels: 4 books

• Short Story Collections: 6 books

• Essays: 2 books

• Literary Criticism: 1 book

• Autobiography: 1 book

• Memoirs: 2 books

• The selection process began with an open call on 30 January 2025.

• Awards were finalized based on the recommendations of jury members for each language.

• The award ceremony will be held on 31 March 2026 in New Delhi.

• Each awardee will receive a casket with an engraved copper plaque, a shawl, and a cash prize of ₹1,00,000. Award-winning Books (24 Languages)
