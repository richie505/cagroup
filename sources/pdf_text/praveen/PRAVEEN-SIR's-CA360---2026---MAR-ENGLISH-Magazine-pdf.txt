Tamil Nadu Leads in India’s Textile Exports (2024–25)

• In the 2024–25 fiscal year, Tamil Nadu topped India’s textile exports with USD 7,997.17 million, accounting for 21.84% of the country’s total exports.

• It surpassed Gujarat (USD 5,646.01 million) and Maharashtra (USD 3,831.29 million).

• Exports increased from USD 6,193 million in 2020–21 to approximately USD 8 billion in 2024–25, recording over 29% growth in four years.

• Growth factors include strong industrial infrastructure, improved supply chains, and policy support, with key textile hubs like Tiruppur and Coimbatore playing a central role.

• The Ministry of Textiles is responsible for textile sector development; Union Minister: Giriraj Singh.

• The Indian textile sector contributes 2–3% of GDP and provides employment to over 45 million people, making India one of the largest cotton producers globally.

Global Methane Emission Hotspots – UCLA “Stop Methane” Project (2025)

• The “Stop Methane” project by the University of California, Los Angeles (UCLA), using data from the Carbon Mapper satellite, identified over 4,400 methane clouds across 2,489 oil and gas facilities worldwide in 2025.

• Turkmenistan accounted for 15 of the top 25 methane emission sites. Other major emission hotspots include Iran, Venezuela, Texas (USA), and Sindh (Pakistan). Emission rates ranged from 3.7 to 10.5 tons per hour.

• In India, the Gazi Pur landfill in Delhi was identified as a major methane emission site, with peak leakage of 400 tons per hour recorded in 2022. Methane is produced due to anaerobic decomposition of organic waste in the landfill.

• Methane (CH₄) has 28–34 times the global warming potential of carbon dioxide over 100 years and remains in the atmosphere for about 12 years.

Decline in India’s Carbon Emissions Growth Rate in 2025 – CREA Report

• According to the Center for Research on Energy and Clean Air (CREA), India’s carbon emissions growth rate in 2025 was 0.7%, the lowest in the past two decades except for the 2020 COVID year; in the second half of 2025, it was 0.5%.
