Andhra Pradesh Budget 2026–27 (₹3,32,205 Crore)

• The Andhra Pradesh Budget 2026–27 was presented by Finance Minister Payyavula Keshav.

• The total outlay for the financial year 2026–27 is ₹3,32,205 crore.

• He stated that, in their view, a budget is not merely an announcement of allocations, but a commitment to drive state development and public welfare forward with momentum.

• Special emphasis has been placed on Asset Creation and increasing Capital Expenditure. Efforts have also been made to reduce deficits and move towards fiscal consolidation. Budget Profile

• Total Budget Outlay: ₹3,32,205 crore.

• Revenue Expenditure: ₹2,56,143 crore.

• Capital Expenditure: ₹53,915 crore.

• Revenue Deficit: ₹22,002 crore.

• Fiscal Deficit: ₹75,868 crore.

Where Does the Rupee Come From? (Sources of Revenue)

• State Taxes: 38%.

• Share in Central Taxes: 19%.

• Grants-in-aid: 10%.

• Open Market Borrowings: 30%.

• Non-Tax Revenue: 3%.

• Observation: A major portion of the State’s revenue depends on tax collections and market borrowings. Where Does the Rupee Go ? (Expenditure Pattern)

• Welfare: 17%.

• Education: 11%.

• Economy (Economic Services): 8%.

• Panchayati Raj & Rural Development: 7%.

• Health & Family Welfare: 6%.

• Water Resources: 6%.

• Municipal Administration & Urban Development (MA\&UD): 4%.

• Power Sector: 4%.

• Agriculture & Allied Sectors: 4%.

• Other Departments: 15%.

• Interest Payments + Debt Repayment: 18%.

Key Fiscal Concepts

• Revenue Expenditure: Day-to-day operational expenses such as salaries, pensions, interest payments, and subsidies.

• Capital Expenditure: Expenditure that leads to asset creation, infrastructure construction, irrigation projects, and long-term investments.

• Revenue Deficit = Revenue Expenditure – Revenue Receipts.

• Fiscal Deficit = Total Expenditure – (Total Receipts excluding borrowings). Indicates annual borrowing requirement.
