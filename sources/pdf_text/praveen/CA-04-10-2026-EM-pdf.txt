• QNu Labs, Bhaskaracharya National Institute for Space Applications and Geo-Informatics (BISAG-N) and IIT Gandhinagar jointly demonstrated India’s first 5.56-km Free-Space Quantum Key Distribution (QKD) link.

• During a recent field trial, a secure quantum communication channel was established between BISAG-N and IIT Gandhinagar. Using QNu Labs’ Pointing, Acquisition and Tracking (PAT) system, the Quantum Bit Error Rate (QBER) was consistently maintained below 5%, while secure keys were generated at 230–260 bits per second (bps).

• These keys were integrated with BISAG-N’s post-quantum cryptography-based ‘Vedic Kavach’ platform, successfully enabling end-to-end encryption and decryption of test messages.

• QNu Labs’ hardware-based ‘Armos’ QKD operated over a free-space optical channel, while BISAG-N’s software-based ‘Vedic Kavach’ combined post-quantum cryptography with quantum random number generation to provide two layers of quantum security.

• A key feature of this hybrid system is that cryptographic security can continue even when the physical quantum channel is temporarily unavailable. Quantum Key Distribution (QKD)

• The Department of Commerce extended the Remission of Duties and Taxes on Exported Products (RoDTEP) Scheme till 31 December 2026.

• It also covers eligible indirect taxes accumulated at earlier stages on inputs and transportation.

• RoDTEP came into effect on 1 January 2021, replacing the Merchandise Exports from India Scheme (MEIS), which had faced objections over compliance with WTO rules.

• The Department of Commerce under the Ministry of Commerce and Industry notifies the scheme through the Directorate General of Foreign Trade (DGFT).

• The Department of Revenue under the Ministry of Finance implements the scheme through the Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs (CBIC).

• Its key objectives are to prevent domestic taxes from being embedded in exports, reduce cost disadvantages for producers and improve the global competitiveness of Indian merchandise exports.
