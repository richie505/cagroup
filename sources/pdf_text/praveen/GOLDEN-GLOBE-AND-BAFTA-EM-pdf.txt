Golden Globe Awards 2026

• The 83rd Golden Globe Awards ceremony was held on January 11, 2026, honoring films and television programs released in 2025.

• The event took place at the The Beverly Hilton in California, United States.

• The Golden Globe Awards are organized annually by the Hollywood Foreign Press Association (HFPA).

• The 2026 ceremony marked the 83rd edition of the Golden Globe Awards.

• Comedian Nikki Glaser hosted the event for the second consecutive year.

• In 2026, a new category titled “Best Podcast” was introduced for the first time.

• The film One Battle After Another won the highest number of awards, securing four Golden Globe Awards.

• The television series Adolescence also won four awards, making it the most-awarded TV series of the night.

• No Indian film received a nomination. However, actress Priyanka Chopra Jonas represented India by presenting an award at the ceremony.

• The Golden Globe Awards were first presented in 1944 and are popularly known as the “Hollywood Party of the Year.” Since 2023, the awards have been conducted under the Golden Globe Foundation with a restructured international voting system.

• Amy Poehler won the first-ever Best Podcast Golden Globe Award for her podcast Good Hang with Amy Poehler.

• Wagner Moura made history by becoming the first Brazilian actor to win a Golden Globe for Best Actor in the Drama category for the film The Secret Agent.
