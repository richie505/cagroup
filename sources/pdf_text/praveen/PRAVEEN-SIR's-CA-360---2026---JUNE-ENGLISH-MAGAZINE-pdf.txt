• The UN General Assembly adopted a new Political Declaration on HIV/AIDS in June 2026 with 149 countries voting in favour, 8 against and 14 abstaining.

• The declaration reaffirmed the global commitment to end AIDS as a public health threat by 2030, while acknowledging that the world remains off track to achieve this goal.

• The 2026 UN High-Level Meeting on HIV/AIDS was held in New York on 22–23 June under the theme “United to End AIDS.”

• The declaration aligns with SDG-3, which targets ending the AIDS epidemic by 2030.

Global Call to Action on Methane

• UN Secretary-General António Guterres launched the Global Call to Action on Methane during London Climate Action Week 2026.

• He described methane reduction as one of the fastest and most cost-effective ways to curb global warming, noting that methane contributes about one-third of current global warming.

• The initiative calls for reducing methane leaks in the fossil fuel sector, eliminating routine flaring and adopting science-based standards.

• The Global Methane Pledge was launched at COP26 (Glasgow) in 2021, with 159 countries and the European Commission committing to reduce methane emissions by 30% from 2020 levels by 2030.

• The UN also called for AI data centres to operate on renewable energy by 2030.

• Methane (CH₄) is a potent greenhouse gas.

• The Centre stated that no new policy was introduced on 24 June 2026 and explained that, under the Passports Act, 1967, passports or travel documents may be issued to certain non-citizens in specific circumstances.

• While a passport may be considered during verification, it is not an independent legal proof of citizenship.

• Passports Act, 1967 regulates the issuance of passports and travel documents, while the Citizenship Act, 1955 governs the acquisition and termination of Indian citizenship.

Delhi High Court Recognises ‘Right to be Forgotten’

• The Delhi High Court recognised the Right to be Forgotten as part of the Right to Privacy under Article 21 of the Constitution.

• The right enables individuals to seek removal or restricted access to information that no longer serves a public interest and adversely affects their privacy or dignity.

• The K.S. Puttaswamy Judgment (2017) recognised privacy as a fundamental right, while the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 provides a legal basis for correction and erasure of personal data.

• Justice Sachin Datta observed that a balance must be maintained between an individual's dignity and the public's right to information. In appropriate cases, search engines may be directed to delist judgments linked to a person's name.

Onkalo – World’s First Permanent Nuclear Waste Repository

• Finland has established Onkalo, the world’s first permanent deep geological repository for high-level nuclear waste, at Eurajoki.

• The repository is designed for the permanent disposal of high-level radioactive waste from nuclear power plants.

• Nuclear waste will be stored in copper canisters at a depth of 433 metres within 1.9-billion-year-old bedrock, surrounded by bentonite clay for long-term protection.

• The facility has a storage capacity of 6,500 tonnes of nuclear waste and is expected to be permanently sealed after about 100 years of operation.

• Bentonite clay swells on contact with moisture, forming a natural protective barrier.

• Finland: Capital – Helsinki | President – Alexander Stubb.

India Conducts First Satellite-Based Jet Landing Using GAGAN

• India successfully carried out its first satellite-based jet landing using GAGAN at Udaipur Airport.

• The demonstration was conducted by the Directorate General of Civil Aviation (DGCA) using an IndiGo Airbus A320 aircraft.

• GAGAN (GPS Aided GEO Augmented Navigation) is India's satellite-based augmentation system, jointly developed by ISRO and the Airports Authority of India (AAI).

• It enhances GPS accuracy and supports Localiser Performance with Vertical Guidance (LPV), enabling safe landings even at airports without a conventional Instrument Landing System (ILS).

• Since 1 July 2021, GAGAN-compatible equipment has been mandatory for all newly registered aircraft in India. IndiGo began GAGAN-enabled operations for its ATR aircraft at Kishangarh Airport in 2022.

• India is among the few countries/regions with a Satellite-Based Augmentation System (SBAS), alongside the United States, Europe and Japan.

• DGCA is India’s civil aviation safety regulator, while AAI is responsible for the development and management of airports.

• Scientists found that rocky mineral clouds form in the planet's morning atmosphere and disappear by evening.

• The findings improve understanding of the atmospheres of planets beyond the Solar System.

• James Webb Space Telescope: Launched – December 2021; Joint mission of NASA, ESA and CSA; Operates in the infrared spectrum from the L2 Lagrange Point, about 1.5 million km from Earth.

• Exoplanet: A planet that orbits a star other than the Sun.

• Hubble Space Telescope: Launched – 1990; Joint NASA–ESA mission.

SOLAR-1 – America’s First Dedicated Space Weather Monitoring Satellite

• The United States activated the SOLAR-1 satellite for space weather monitoring to provide early warnings of solar storms.

• Operated by NOAA, the satellite was earlier known as SWFO-L1.

• Launched on 24 September 2025 aboard a SpaceX Falcon-9 rocket, it operates from the Earth–Sun L1 Lagrange Point.

• It measures solar wind in real time and uses a coronagraph to monitor solar storms, providing early warnings for GPS, communications, satellites and power grids.

• India's Aditya-L1 also operates at the L1 point, using the VELC instrument to study the solar corona.

• L1 Lagrange Point: Gravitational balance point between the Earth and the Sun, ideal for continuous solar observation.

• NOAA: Established – 1970; Headquarters – Washington, D.C., USA.

• Aditya-L1: Launched – 2 September 2023; India's first solar mission by ISRO.

• VELC: Visible Emission Line Coronagraph; Developed by the Indian Institute of Astrophysics, Bengaluru.
