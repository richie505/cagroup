## India–Oman CEPA | from: 2025-12-23
# 1. News
- **Signed:** India–Oman **Comprehensive Economic Partnership Agreement (CEPA)** — in presence of **Narendra Modi** and **Sultan Haitham bin Tarik**
| Side | Tariff lines | Trade covered |
|---|---|---|
| **Oman (duty-free for India)** | **98.08%** | **99.38%** of India's exports |
| **India (liberalised)** | **77.79%** | **94.81%** of imports from Oman |
- **Excluded by India:** dairy, tea, coffee, rubber, tobacco, gold/silver bullion and jewellery, footwear, sports goods, base metal scrap
- **Mode 4:** Intra-Corporate Transferees quota **20% → 50%**; Contractual Service Suppliers stay **90 days → two years** (+2 years extension)
- **FDI:** **100%** by Indian firms in major services sectors in Oman
- ⚠ Oman's **first** bilateral trade agreement since **2006**; India's **second CEPA with a GCC country** after **UAE (2022)**
# 2. Trade 2024–25
- **Exports to Oman:** **$4.06 billion** (**0.93%** of India's exports) — petroleum products **35.1%**
- **Imports from Oman:** **$6.5 billion** — crude/petroleum gases **38%**, fertilisers **16.3%**
# 3. Background — Oman
- **Capital:** **Muscat**; **GCC** member; oldest independent state in the Arab world
- **Borders:** **Yemen**, **Saudi Arabia**, **UAE**; **Arabian Sea**, **Gulf of Oman**
- **Features:** **Rub al-Khali** (Empty Quarter), **Hajar Mountains** (north), **Dhofar Range** (south)
- **Exercise Naseem Al Bahr:** India–Oman bilateral **naval** exercise since **1993**; ⚠ Oman — first GCC country to hold bilateral naval exercise with India

## India–New Zealand Free Trade Agreement | from: 2025-12-30
# 1. News
- **Concluded:** **December 2025** — in a record **nine months**; negotiated by a **women-led team**
- ⚠ India's **first FTA with New Zealand**
| Side | Commitment |
|---|---|
| **New Zealand** | Zero duty on **100%** of Indian exports |
| **India** | Tariff relief on **95%** of NZ imports; **57%** duty-free from day one |
| **FDI** | NZ commits **$20 billion** by **2030** (also reported as over 15 years), with clawback |
- **Services:** NZ's **first-ever** commitment on **Ayurveda**, **yoga**, traditional medicine (Health and Traditional Medicine Services Annex); mobility for IT professionals, engineers, yoga instructors, chefs, music teachers; **5,000** skilled professionals pathway
- **Students:** work up to **20 hours/week**; extended post-study work visas
- **Excluded by India:** dairy (milk, cheese, butter, yogurt), onions, sugar, edible oils, spices, rubber
- **Agri cooperation:** **kiwifruit**, **apples**, **honey**
# 2. Context
- **Bilateral trade:** **$1.3 billion**, to double in five years; NZ per capita income about **$49,380**
- **Indian diaspora:** about **5%** of NZ population (about **3 lakh**)
- **NZ criticism:** coalition partner called it "neither free nor fair"; Foreign Minister to vote against it in **2026**
- **India–US trade:** **$132 billion**; **50%** US tariffs; exports to US fell **12% (September)**, **8.5% (October)**
# 3. Background — trade agreement types
| Type | Feature |
|---|---|
| **Partial Scope Agreement** | Limited goods |
| **FTA** | Tariffs cut among members; own tariffs for others |
| **Customs Union** | + common external tariff |
| **Common Market** | + free movement of capital and labour |
| **Economic Union** | + macroeconomic coordination |
- **ASEAN FTA partners** include **Australia**, **China**, **India**, **Japan**
