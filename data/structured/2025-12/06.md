## Rare-earth elements — why everyone wants them | from: 2025-12-29
# 1. Basics
- **REEs:** **17** elements — **15 lanthanides** (**lanthanum** to **lutetium**) + **scandium** and **yttrium** (Group 3)
- ⚠ Not geologically rare — **cerium** as abundant as **copper**; rarity is economic/technological (hard separation)
- ⚠ **Lithium**, **cobalt**, **gallium**, **germanium** are critical minerals, **not** REEs
- **Properties:** from localised **4f electrons** — strong magnetism, sharp phosphor emission
# 2. Uses
| Use | Elements |
|---|---|
| **Permanent magnets (NdFeB)** | **Neodymium** — EV motors, wind turbines |
| **Phosphors (LEDs, displays)** | **Europium**, **terbium**, **yttrium** |
| **Lasers, fibre optics** | **Neodymium**, **erbium** |
| **Refining catalysts** | **Lanthanum**, **cerium** |
- **Ores:** **bastnäsite**, **monazite**, ion-adsorption clays; may contain **thorium/uranium**
- **Midstream bottleneck:** beneficiation, cracking, leaching, **solvent extraction**, calcination, reduction
# 3. Reserves and processing (USGS)

## Nuclear power in space — US lunar fission reactor | from: 2025-12-05
# 1. News
- **US plan:** **Lunar Fission Surface Power Project** — small fission reactor on the **Moon** by **early 2030s**, for NASA's **Artemis Base Camp**
- ⚠ Would be the **first permanent nuclear power source beyond Earth orbit**
# 2. Space nuclear technologies
| Technology | Key point |
|---|---|
| **RTG** (Radioisotope Thermoelectric Generator) | Decay heat of **plutonium-238**; few hundred watts; **Voyager**, **New Horizons**, **Curiosity**, **Perseverance** |
| **Compact fission reactor** | Shipping-container size; **10–100 kW**; NASA **KRUSTY** demo up to **10 kW** |
| **Nuclear Thermal Propulsion (NTP)** | Reactor heats propellant (liquid hydrogen); high thrust; US **DRACO** test by **2026** |
| **Nuclear Electric Propulsion (NEP)** | Reactor electricity powers **ion/plasma thrusters**; low continuous thrust |
- **Why needed:** lunar night about **14 Earth days**, below **–170°C**; Mars dust storms
# 3. Legal framework
- **Outer Space Treaty 1967, Article IV:** bans nuclear weapons/WMDs in orbit and on celestial bodies — ⚠ does **not** ban peaceful nuclear power
- **UN Principles on Nuclear Power Sources in Outer Space, 1992** (**UNGA Res. 47/68**): non-binding; ⚠ ignore **NTP/NEP** propulsion
- **1972 Liability Convention:** **absolute** liability for damage on Earth/aircraft; **fault-based** in outer space
- **COPUOS:** UN Committee on the Peaceful Uses of Outer Space
- **NPT Nuclear Weapon States:** **USA**, **Russia**, **UK**, **France**, **China** — India, Pakistan, Israel not recognised
- ⚠ **NSG** membership does **not** mean automatic NPT membership
