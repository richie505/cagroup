## National Energy Conservation Awards 2025 | from: 2025-12-16
# 1. News
- **Date:** **14 December 2025** — **National Energy Conservation Day**
- **Presented by:** President **Droupadi Murmu**, **New Delhi** — **NECA 2025** and **National Painting Competition** on Energy Conservation
- **Energy savings 2023–24:** **53.60 Mtoe**
# 2. Background
- **NECA instituted:** **1991**, by **Bureau of Energy Efficiency (BEE)**
- **BEE:** statutory body, established **1 March 2002** under **Energy Conservation Act, 2001**
- **BEE initiatives:** **Standards & Labeling**, **DSM**, **PAT** → replaced by **Carbon Credit Trading Scheme (CCTS), 2023**
- **BEE star label:** on ceiling fans, electric geysers, tubular fluorescent lamps
# 3. Schemes cited
| Scheme | Key facts |
|---|---|
| **PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana** | **February 2024**; **1 crore** households; up to **300 units** free/month; subsidy **₹30,000 (1 kW)**, **₹60,000 (2 kW)**, **₹78,000 (3 kW+)** |
| **National Green Hydrogen Mission** | **January 2023**; **₹19,744 crore**; **5 MMT/year by 2030**; **₹8 lakh crore** investment; avert **50 MMT** GHG; **SIGHT** incentives |
| **Renewable Consumption Obligation** | Formerly **RPO**; under **Energy Conservation (Amendment) Act, 2022**; **29.91% (FY25)** → **43.33% (FY30)** |
| **PLI** | **14** sectors; incentives for **4–6 years** |
