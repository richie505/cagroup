## 150 years of Vande Mataram — Rajya Sabha debate | from: 2025-12-12
# 1. News
- **Occasion:** **Rajya Sabha** discussion commemorating **150 years of Vande Mataram**
- **J.P. Nadda** (Leader of House): Vande Mataram should get the **same constitutional status as the national anthem**; Opposition (**Mallikarjun Kharge**) accused him of distorting history
- **Union Cabinet:** nationwide celebration of 150 years
# 2. Origin
- **Author:** **Bankim Chandra Chattopadhyay** — composed in **Sanskrit** (⚠ not Bengali)
- **Year:** **1875** (also given as "after 1872"); in novel **Anandamath** (**1882**; also given as **1881**)
- **Popularised:** **Swadeshi agitation (1905)**
- **Translations:** Marathi, Kannada (**1897**), Gujarati (**1901**), Hindi (**1906**), **Telugu (1907)**, Tamil (**1908**), Malayalam (**1909**)
- **1937 CWC:** only **first two stanzas** to be sung at national gatherings
- **National song:** designated by **Dr Rajendra Prasad** in the Constituent Assembly on **24 January 1950** — equal status with **Jana Gana Mana**
- **Views:** **Tagore** backed first two stanzas; **Gandhi** (Harijan, **July 1939**) — drop if anyone objects in mixed gathering
# 3. Legal position
- ⚠ **Article 51A(a)** mentions **Flag** and **Anthem**, not the national song
- **Prevention of Insults to National Honour Act, 1971:** covers anthem and flag, **not** the national song
| Court | Year | Point |
|---|---|---|

| **Allahabad HC** | **2018** | Dismissed PIL on municipal house |
| **Delhi HC** | **2022** | PIL by **Ashwini Upadhyay** for parity |
| **Bombay HC** | **2024** | Quashed FIR over WhatsApp message |
| **Supreme Court** | — | No concept of "National Song" in law |
- **National Flag design adopted:** **22 July 1947**; **Saka calendar** 1 Chaitra = **22 March** (21 March in leap year)

## World AIDS Day and NACP | from: 2025-12-01
# 1. News
- **Date:** **December 1** — **World AIDS Day**
- **Theme 2025:** **"Overcoming disruption, transforming the AIDS response"**
- **Context:** why HIV remains **incurable** after **42 years** of research
# 2. HIV facts
- **HIV identified:** **1983** by **Françoise Barré-Sinoussi** and **Luc Montagnier** (**Institut Pasteur**)
- **Attacks:** **CD4** white blood cells (CD4+ T-cells); **AIDS** = advanced stage of untreated HIV
- **India prevalence:** **0.33% (2010)** → **0.20% (2024)**
- **India supplies:** **70%** of global **ART** medicines
- **Cured cases:** only a handful, all through **bone-marrow transplants**
- **Advanced HIV Disease (WHO):** **CD4 < 200 cells/mm³** or **WHO Stage 3/4**; all children **< 5 years** with HIV are AHD
- **Window period:** antibodies develop in about **28 days**; infants need **virological testing** at birth or **6 weeks**
- **PEP:** within **72 hours** of exposure
- **WHO PrEP options:** **oral TDF-based PrEP**, **Dapivirine vaginal ring**, long-acting injectable **cabotegravir**, long-acting injectable **lenacapavir**
- **Not transmitted by:** kissing, hugging, handshakes, food, water, utensils
- **WHO strategy:** **Global Health Sector Strategies (GHSS) 2022–2030**; end AIDS by **2030** (**SDG 3.3**)
- **Why hard to treat:** retrovirus integrates into host DNA (reverse transcription), **latent reservoirs**, high mutation rate, destroys immune cells
# 3. National AIDS Control Programme phases
| Phase | Years | Key point |
|---|---|---|
| **NACP I** | **1992–1999** | India's first comprehensive HIV/AIDS programme |
| **NACP II** | **1999–2006** | Reduce spread; build national capacity |
| **NACP III** | **2007–2012** | Halt and reverse by 2012; created **DAPCUs** (District AIDS Prevention and Control Units) |
| **NACP IV** | **2012–2017** (extended **2017–2021**) | **50%** cut in new infections vs **2007**; **HIV/AIDS Act 2017**, **Mission Sampark**, **Test and Treat** |
| **NACP V** | **2021–2026** | **Central Sector Scheme**, outlay **₹15,471.94 crore** |
- **Mission Sampark:** bring back PLHIV **lost to follow-up** on ART
- **NACP-V:** dual **HIV-syphilis** test kits for antenatal screening
- **Targeted Interventions:** **1,587** TI projects (Oct 2025)
- **Ombudsmen:** appointed in **34 States/UTs** under the **HIV and AIDS (Prevention and Control) Act, 2017**

## INSV Kaundinya — stitched ship's maiden overseas voyage | from: 2025-12-31
# 1. News
- **Voyage:** Indian Navy's stitched sailing vessel **INSV Kaundinya** — maiden overseas voyage **Porbandar (Gujarat) → Muscat (Oman)**
- **Inspired by:** ship in **Ajanta Cave** paintings (**5th century CE**)
- **Built by:** **Ministry of Culture**, **Indian Navy**, **Hodi Innovations**; Kerala artisans led by master shipwright **Babu Sankaran**
- **Method:** **Tankai method** — hull stitched first, ribs later; **coir rope**, natural resin; no metal or engine
# 2. Details
| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Length** | about **19.6 m** |
| **Width** | about **6.5 m** |
| **Draft** | about **3.33 m** |
| **Crew** | about **15** |
- **Symbols:** sails — **Gandabherunda** and **Sun**; bow — **Simha Yali**; deck — **Harappan-style stone anchor**
- **Named after:** **Kaundinya**, ancient mariner linked to voyages to **Southeast Asia**

## Batadrava Than — birthplace of Srimanta Sankardeva redeveloped | from: 2025-12-31
# 1. News
- **Inaugurated by:** **Amit Shah** — **₹227-crore** redeveloped **Batadrava Than**, **Nagaon district, Assam**
- **Official name:** **Mahapurush Srimanta Sankardeva Abhirbhav Kshetra**; first proposed in **Assam Budget 2021–22**
- **Present:** CM **Himanta Biswa Sarma**, **Sarbananda Sonowal**, **Pabitra Margherita**, **Bimal Bora**; **Gayan-Bayan** welcome; sanctum has the **Guru Asana**
# 2. Srimanta Sankardeva
- **Life:** **1449–1568**; family **Siromani Bhuyans**; pilgrimage **1481**; first **Borgeet** at **Badarikashrama (Badrinath)**
- **Faith:** **Eka-Sarana-Nama-Dharma** (Neo-Vaishnavism) — Krishna alone; rejected idol worship and caste; stressed **Śravaṇa** and **Kīrtana**
- **Institutions:** **Namghars** (prayer halls), **Satras** (monasteries)
- **Literature:** translated **8 of 12** books of **Bhagavata Purana** into **Brajavali**
| Work | Note |
|---|---|
| **Kirtana-Ghosha** | Magnum opus |
| **Gunamala** | Condensed Bhagavata, composed in one night |
| **Bhakti Pradipa** | Against worship of other deities |
| **Rukmini Harana Kavya**, **HariScandra-Upakhyana** | Early works |
- **Arts:** **Ankiya Naat** (one-act plays), **Bhaona**, **Sattriya** dance (classical), about **240 Borgeets**
- ⚠ Sattriya is **not** based on songs of Tulsidas, Kabir, Mirabai (UPSC 2014)
- **Associated figure:** **Madhavdeva**
