## Global Environment Outlook 2025 (GEO-7) | from: 2025-12-11
# 1. News
- **Publisher:** **UNEP** — **7th Global Environment Outlook**, released at UNEP's **7th session** in **Nairobi**
- **Called:** world's most authoritative environmental "health check"
# 2. Key findings
| Indicator | Figure |
|---|---|
| **GHG emissions rise** | **1.5% per year** since **1990** |
| **Global temperature 2024** | **1.55°C** — highest ever |
| **Species facing extinction** | **1 million** of **8 million** |
| **Degraded land** | **20–40%**, affecting **3 billion+** people |
| **Climate disaster cost** | **USD 143 billion** a year (20-year average) |
| **Air pollution health loss (2019)** | **USD 8.1 trillion** |
| **Pollution deaths** | **9 million** premature deaths a year |
| **Plastic waste** | **8,000 million tonnes** |
| **Chemical exposure damage** | **USD 1.5 trillion** a year |
- **Projections:** 1.5°C breached by **early 2030s**, **2.0°C** by **2040s**; global GDP **-4% by 2050**, **-20% by 2100**; per-capita food **-3.4% by 2050**
- **Land loss:** fertile land equal to **Colombia** or **Ethiopia** lost every year
- **Investment needed:** **USD 8 trillion** a year till 2050; benefits up to **USD 20 trillion/year by 2070**
- **Food transformation benefit:** **200 million** out of undernutrition, **100 million** out of extreme poverty
# 3. Background — UNEP
- **Founded:** **5 June 1972**; HQ **Nairobi, Kenya**
- **Reports:** **Emission Gap Report**, **Frontiers**, **GEO**; campaigns **Beat Pollution**, **World Environment Day**
- **Common Carbon Metric** (UNEP): carbon footprint of **building operations**
