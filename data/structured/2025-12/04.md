## 8th Economic Census in 2027 and Census facts | from: 2025-12-04
# 1. News
- **Announced by:** Statistics Secretary **Saurabh Garg**
- **Sequence:** two-phase **Population Census 2026–27**, followed by **8th Economic Census in 2027**
- **Use:** to build a **Statistical Business Register (SBR)** — unified enterprise database
# 2. Economic Census
- **First:** **1977**
- **Nodal:** **MoSPI**; conducted by **NSO** with State **Directorates of Economics & Statistics (DES)**
- **Covers:** all establishments producing/distributing goods and services (other than for sole consumption)
# 3. Population Census facts
- **Authority:** **Registrar General & Census Commissioner (RGI)**, under **MHA**; ad-hoc organisation till **1951**
- **Law:** **Census Act, 1948** (piloted by **Sardar Patel**); individual data confidential
- **Subject:** **Union List**, **Entry 69**, **Article 246**
- **2026–27:** **16th** decennial Census; **first fully digital** with **self-enumeration**; separate head for **transgender-headed** households
| Year | Milestone |
|---|---|
| **1830** | First complete census of a city — **Dacca**, by **Henry Walter** |
| **1872** | First all-India (non-synchronous) census — **Lord Mayo** |
| **1881** | First **synchronous** census — **W.C. Plowden** |
| **1921** | **"Great Divide"** — only decadal decline (**-0.31%**), 1918 flu |
| **1971** | Question on fertility of currently married women |
| **1991** | Literacy counted for **7+** age group |
| **2001** | **ICR** technology used |
| **2011** | Fertility decline in **EAG** States |
- **Ancient references:** **Rigveda**, **Arthashastra**, **Ain-i-Akbari** (Abul Fazl)
# 4. SECC and caste surveys
- **SECC 2011:** first caste enumeration since **1931**; data **not confidential** — used for scheme targeting
- **Article 340:** commission on backward classes
- **State powers for surveys:** Concurrent List **Entry 20** (economic and social planning), **Entry 23** (social security), **Entry 30** (vital statistics)
- **Patna High Court (2023):** upheld **Bihar caste survey**; SC issued notice
- **Criminal Tribes Act, 1871:** colonial law labelling tribes as "born criminals"

## Banking Laws (Amendment) Act, 2025 | from: 2025-12-08
# 1. News
- **Notified:** in two stages — **1 August** and **1 November 2025**
- **Amends five Acts:** **RBI Act 1934**, **Banking Regulation Act 1949**, **SBI Act 1955**, **Banking Companies (Acquisition and Transfer of Undertakings) Acts 1970** and **1980**
# 2. Key reforms
| Reform | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Nominees** | Up to **four** per account; **simultaneous** (percentage, total 100%) or **successive** (lockers) |
| **'Substantial interest'** | **₹5 lakh (1968)** → **₹2 crore** |
| **Cooperative bank directors** | Max tenure **8 → 10 years** (excl. chairperson, WTDs) — aligns with **97th Amendment** |
| **PSB audit** | PSBs fix **auditor remuneration**; unclaimed shares/interest to **IEPF** |
| **Reporting** | "last Friday" replaced by "last day of the month/fortnight" |
- ⚠ Director tenure is **10 years**, not 15
# 3. Background
- **SBI Act 1955:** Imperial Bank → SBI
- **Nationalisation:** **14 banks** in **1969**; more in **1980**
- **Banking Regulation (Amendment) Act, 2020:** RBI control over cooperative banks
- **Banks Board Bureau:** recommends PSB heads — ⚠ RBI Governor is **not** its chairman
- **Urban Cooperative Banks:** brought under BR Act by **1966** amendment

## Biostimulants for sustainable agriculture | from: 2025-12-10
# 1. News
- **Fertiliser use:** **139.81 kg/ha** nationally; **247.61 kg/ha** in **Punjab**; fertilisers give **19%** of agriculture GHG emissions
- **Biostimulants:** stimulate plant processes **independent of nutrient content** — ⚠ not fertilisers, not insecticides (excluded from **Insecticides Act, 1968**)
# 2. Regulation
- **Schedule VI** of **Fertiliser Control Order (FCO) 1985** — introduced **February 2021**
- **Categories:** **nine** (botanical extracts, humic acids, protein hydrolysates, vitamins, antioxidants, live microbes etc.)
- **Mandatory:** bio-efficacy trials, toxicity analysis, heavy-metal testing
- **Provisional approvals:** about **30,000** products → after expiry on **16 June 2025** only **132** approved
| Crop group | Share of approved products |
|---|---|
| **Vegetables** | **50%** |
| **Cereals** | **18%** |
| **Pulses & oilseeds** | **15%** |
| **Fruits** | **9%** |
| **Cash crops** | **7%** |
# 3. Seaweed
- **Seaweed extracts:** **41%** of global biostimulant market
- **ICAR–CMFRI:** one hectare of **Kappaphycus** can generate **₹13.28 lakh** a year
- **India:** production **74,083 tonnes**; potential **9.7 million tonnes**
- **Hydrocolloids:** **agar**, **alginate**, **carrageenan**

## Bitcoin's slide after October record and crypto regulation | from: 2025-12-19
# 1. News
- **Bitcoin:** record **$126,000** (October 2025) → below **$85,000**; about **$1 trillion** crypto value wiped out; fears of **"crypto winter"**
# 2. Reasons
- **US Fed:** cut rates by **25 bps** on **December 10** but signalled no further cuts
- **10/10 crash:** **October 10** — Trump threatened **100% tariffs** on Chinese goods; **$19 billion** leveraged trades liquidated; **$500 billion** wiped out in hours
- **Long-term holders:** **800,000** Bitcoins sold within a month
- ⚠ Bitcoin behaves like a risk asset, not "digital gold"
# 3. Why winter fears may be exaggerated
- **Halving:** four-year cycle; last **April 2024**
- **Bitcoin ETFs:** approved in US **January 2024**; hold **1.4 million** BTC (**6.8%** of supply)
- **US policy:** **US Bitcoin Reserve** executive order (**March 2025**); **GENIUS Act** (**June 2025**) on stablecoins
# 4. Crypto basics and India's status
- **Bitcoin:** **2009**, **Satoshi Nakamoto**; capped at **21 million**; **Bitcoin Cash** — hard fork **2017**
| Year | India |
|---|---|
| **2018** | RBI banking ban |
