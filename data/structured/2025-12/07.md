## Aravalli range — SC uniform 100-m definition and mining pause | from: 2025-12-18
# 1. News
- **Supreme Court (November 2025):** adopted a **uniform definition** — only hills above **100 m** elevation are Aravallis; **paused all fresh mining leases** in **Delhi**, **Haryana**, **Rajasthan**, **Gujarat**
- **Direction:** prepare a **Management Plan for Sustainable Mining (MPSM)** for the entire range
- ⚠ Concern: **100-m rule** excludes **90%+** of the landscape
- ⚠ No total ban — total bans fuel illegal mining
# 2. Aravalli facts
- **Age:** nearly **two billion years** — India's **oldest** range (⚠ an old UPSC 2001 key gives **370 million years**)
- **Length:** **650 km**, **Delhi to Gujarat**
- **Highest peak:** **Guru Shikhar (1,722 m)**, **Mount Abu, Rajasthan** — ⚠ not Gujarat
- **Rivers:** **Banas**, **Sahibi**, **Luni** (to Rann of Kutch); also linked to **Chambal**, **Sabarmati**
- **Groundwater:** fell to **1,000–2,000 feet** in **Mahendergarh**
# 3. Protection timeline
| Year | Action |
|---|---|
| **2009** | SC ban on mining in **Gurugram**, **Faridabad**, **Mewat** |
| **2024 (May)** | Halt on new mining leases |
| **2024** | **Central Empowered Committee (CEC)** recommendations |
| **2025** | **Aravalli Green Wall Project** — **5-km** green buffer in **29 districts** of **four** States |
| **2025 (Nov)** | Uniform **100-m** definition |
- **Land restoration goal:** **26 million hectares** by **2030** (**UNCCD**)

## Methane emissions from landfills — satellite evidence | from: 2025-12-17
# 1. News
- **Satellite data:** methane from Indian landfills **far higher** than official estimates; globally landfill emissions up to **1.8 times** model estimates
- **India:** **third-largest** methane emitter (after **China**, **USA**); **31 million tonnes/year** (about **9%** of global)
- **Waste sector:** about **15%** of India's methane
- **3rd Biennial Update Report (2016):** methane **409 million tonnes CO₂e** (excl. LULUCF)
# 2. Methane facts
- **CH₄:** simplest alkane; **80–95%** of natural gas; lighter than air
- **GWP:** **84×** CO₂ over **20 years**; **28–34×** over **100 years**
- **Platforms:** **ISRO (2023 study)**, **CarbonMapper – Tanager**, **SRON**, **ClimateTRACE**, **WasteMap**
# 3. Landfill hotspots
| City | Landfill |
|---|---|
| **Ahmedabad** | **Pirana** |
| **Mumbai** | **Deonar**, **Kanjurmarg** (**10×** model estimates) |
| **Delhi** | **Ghazipur**, **Bhalswa** |
| **Hyderabad** | **Jawaharnagar** |
| **Kolkata** | **Dhapa** |
- **Example of capture:** **Indore**'s **Bio-CNG** plant under **GOBARdhan**
- **Methane hydrate:** found in **Arctic tundra** and under sea floor; methane oxidises to CO₂ in a decade or two

## Indoor air pollution and air-purifying plants | from: 2025-12-01
# 1. News
- **Finding:** indoor air can be **2.5 times** more polluted than outdoor, up to **100 times** in extreme cases
- **Burden:** indoor pollution linked to **4.1%** of global deaths annually
- ⚠ India has **no uniform Indoor Air Quality (IAQ) protocol**
# 2. WHO data — household air pollution

## Bioremediation — why India needs it | from: 2025-12-03
# 1. Concept
- **Bioremediation:** use of **bacteria**, **fungi**, **algae**, plants to break down pollutants into non-toxic by-products (**water**, **CO₂**, organic acids)
| Type | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **In situ** | Clean on the spot | Oil-eating bacteria sprayed on ocean spill |
| **Ex situ** | Remove, treat in facility, return | Soil washing for pesticide farmland |
- ⚠ **Heavy metals** (cadmium, lead) cannot be completely degraded — only transformed/immobilised (UPSC 2017)
- **GM microbes** can be engineered for bioremediation
# 2. India's efforts
- **DBT Clean Technology Programme**; **CSIR–NEERI** projects
- **IIT:** cotton-based **nanocomposite** mats to mop up oil spills
- **Firms:** **BCIL**, **Econirmal Biotech**
- **Gaps:** no unified national bioremediation standards; no biosafety rules for GM microbes
- **Abroad:** **China** uses GM bacteria under its soil pollution law; **Japan** integrates into city waste systems
