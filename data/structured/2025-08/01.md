## Swachh Ratham — waste-for-groceries barter | from: 2025-08-21
# 1. News
- **What:** pilot under **Swachh Andhra** campaign — villagers exchange **dry waste** for groceries/essentials (rice, soaps, shampoos) of **equal value**
- **Where:** **seven panchayats** of **Guntur Rural Mandal**; expansion to **Vijayawada rural**
- **Vehicles:** drivers of **Material Delivery Units (MDUs)** (jobless after door-delivery scheme scrapped) repurpose vehicles as Swachh Rathams
- **Waste:** sold to **recyclers** — ⚠ **not** used for biogas
# 2. Pilot numbers (15 days)
| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Total dry waste** | **2,599 kg** — plastic **1,271 kg**, old books 490 kg, iron 448 kg, cardboard 208 kg, steel 92 kg, paper 75 kg |
| **Value** | waste **₹47,373**; commodities given **₹47,604** |
| **Top villages** | **Vengalayapalem** (801 kg), **Challavaripalem** (788 kg), **Lalpuram** (331 kg) |
# 3. Circular Economy Policy
- **Swachh Andhra Corporation Chairman:** **Pattabhi Ram Kommareddy** — AP to adopt **Circular Economy Policy**, ⚠ claimed **first of its kind in India**
- **Plan:** **2–3 recycling parks**; link Rathams with **50 waste processing centres**; wet-waste manure marketed via **MarkFed** for **ZBNF**
- **Target:** CM aims to cut chemical fertiliser use by **4 lakh tonnes** next year
