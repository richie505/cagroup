## NFSA ration card verification — 1.17 crore flagged ineligible | from: 2025-08-20
# 1. News
- **Flagged:** **~1.17 crore** ration card holders potentially ineligible under **NFSA, 2013** — Centre's **first** such list
- **By:** **Department of Food and Public Distribution** — inter-ministerial data matching
- **Deadline:** States/UTs to verify and remove by **30 September 2025**
- **Tool:** **Rightful Targeting Dashboard** (API-based) for states
- **Earlier:** **1.34 crore** bogus/ineligible cards deleted in **2021–2023**
- ⚠ Deletion is the responsibility of **States/UTs**; Centre only gives data
| Database | Category | Flagged |
|---|---|---|
| **Income Tax Dept** | Taxpayers | **94.71 lakh** |
| **MoRTH** | Four-wheeler owners | **17.51 lakh** |
| **MCA** | Company directors | **5.31 lakh** |
| CBDT, CBIC, PM-Kisan | Overlaps | being verified |
# 2. Background — NFSA 2013
- **Enacted:** **5 July 2013**
- **Coverage:** **67%** of population — **75% rural**, **50% urban**
- **Prices:** rice **₹3/kg**, wheat **₹2/kg**, coarse grains **₹1/kg** — via **TPDS**; made **free** under **PM Garib Kalyan Anna Yojana**
- **Ineligible:** taxpayers, government employees, households with income **≥ ₹1 lakh**, four-wheeler owners, company directors

## Cancer as a notifiable disease — Rajya Sabha Committee on Petitions | from: 2025-08-22
# 1. News
- **Committee:** **Committee on Petitions, Rajya Sabha** — headed by **Narain Dass Gupta**
- **Report:** **163rd report** (**20 August 2025**) — declare **cancer a notifiable disease** in all States/UTs
- **Current data:** **National Cancer Registry Programme (NCRP)** covers only **18%** of population
- **Present norm (WHO):** only **communicable** diseases notifiable
- **Oral cancer:** ~**60,000** new cases a year; over **5 deaths every hour**; Indians affected younger (**≤40 years**)
- **Other recommendations:** high-risk **cess on tobacco** earmarked for cancer care; compulsory service for govt-funded medical graduates; more oncology **R&D**; inter-ministerial plan on environmental carcinogens
- **Linked programme:** **NPCDCS**; **SDG 3**

## Census 2027 and disability inclusion | from: 2025-08-26
# 1. News
- **Census 2027:** India's **first digital Census**; first since **2011** (17 years)
# 2. Disability data
| Source | Prevalence |
|---|---|
| **Census 2011** | **26.8 million** — **2.21%** |
| **NFHS-5** | **4.52%** |
| **WHO estimate** | ~**15%** |
- **UDID cards:** only **1 crore** issued since **2015**
- **Economic loss:** ~**₹4.5 lakh crore** a year (**4% of GDP**)
- **Employment:** **23.8%** disabled (15–59) employed vs **50%** others (NSS 2018); private sector **0.4%** (ILO 2021)
- **Education:** **46%** illiterate (2011); **22.5 lakh** disabled children enrolled (UDISE+ 2021-22)
- **Census 2011 flaw:** medical binary questions in **8 categories**
- **Suggested:** **Washington Group – Short Set (WG-SS)**; align with **21 categories** of **RPwD Act, 2016**
- ⚠ India has **not** adopted WG-SS in all surveys

## India's drug safety deficit | from: 2025-08-27
# 1. Facts
- **GMP** introduced only in **2005**; post-marketing surveillance and annual inspections mandatory only in **2015**
- **CDSCO:** ⚠ **not independent** — reports to **DGHS** → Health Ministry; States also license
- **WHO:** **1 in 10** drugs in developing countries fake/substandard
- **Branded generics:** **87%** of Indian market
- **Pharma sector:** **$50 billion** → **$130 billion** by **2030**; supplies **40%** of US generics, **50–60%** of world's vaccines
- **Cough syrup deaths:** **Gambia**, **Uzbekistan**
- **Jaipur study:** **2 of 8** online pharmacies sold without prescription
- **Retail chains:** quality **+5%**, prices **−2%**

## PM SVANidhi restructured and extended till 2030 | from: 2025-08-28

## Demographic dividend — risk of a "time bomb" | from: 2025-08-29
# 1. Facts
- **Working-age (15–64):** ~**two-thirds** of population; **800 million+** below 35; window till the **2040s**
- **Job-ready graduates:** only **42–43%** (⚠ not 80%)
- **Career awareness:** **93%** of high school students know only **6–7** careers vs **20,000+** paths
- **AI by 2030 (global):** **170 million** jobs created, **92 million** displaced
- **Engineering graduates:** **40–50%** unemployed/underemployed
- **Skill India target:** **400 million by 2022** — missed
- **Schemes:** NEP 2020, **NCrF**, **NAPS 2.0**, **NATS**, NSDM, PMKVY, PM-DAKSH, **PM Vishwakarma**, **JSS**, Atal Tinkering Labs, Start-up India, Stand-up India
