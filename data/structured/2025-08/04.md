## GST two-rate structure proposal and state compensation | from: 2025-08-22, 2025-08-29
# 1. News
- **GoM on Rate Rationalisation:** chaired by **Bihar Deputy CM Samrat Choudhary** — accepted Centre's proposal
- **Keep:** **5%** and **18%**; **remove 12% and 28%**
  - **99%** of 12% items → **5%**; **90%** of 28% items → **18%**
- **Sin goods** (tobacco, cigarettes, online real-money gaming): new **40%** slab
- **Compensation cess:** to be **abolished**
- **Final say:** **GST Council** (chaired by Union Finance Minister); meeting expected **September 2025**
- **PM Modi:** hinted at a **"Deepavali gift"**
- **Concern:** Kerala FM **K.N. Balagopal** — demanded compensation for states
# 2. Background
- **GST introduced:** **2017**; current slabs **5, 12, 18, 28%** + cess
# 3. Revenue impact and compensation debate
- **Average effective rate:** ~**11.5% → ~10%**
- **Revenue loss:** **₹60,000–1,00,000 crore** a year (**0.2–0.3% of GDP**); FY2025-26 ~**₹45,000 crore**
- **18% slab:** ~**70%** of GST revenue — kept unchanged
- **More affected:** manufacturing/service states (**Maharashtra, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu**); 2018 rate cut caused **3–4%** monthly dip
- **Less affected:** agrarian states (Bihar, UP, North-East)
- **Old compensation:** **5 years** from **July 2017**, via compensation cess — ended **June 2022**
- **Options:** equalisation fund (like **Kerala's flood cess**), CFI grants, performance-linked transfers
- ⚠ GST Council works by **consensus** — voting rare

- **Module capacity:** ~**100 GW** (2025); target **190 GW** cumulative solar by **2027**
- **Solar capacity growth:** over **4,000%** in a decade
- **FY25 installations:** **24 GW**, up **60%** y-o-y (needing ~**50 GWdc** modules)

## Unified Pension Scheme — one-time switch to NPS | from: 2025-08-26
# 1. News
- **Finance Ministry:** **one-time, one-way switch** from **UPS → NPS** for Central govt employees
- **Deadline for switch:** not later than **1 year before superannuation** or **3 months** before deemed retirement (VRS); **irreversible**
- ⚠ Switch is back to **NPS**, **not** to OPS
# 2. UPS features
- **Effective:** **1 April 2025** — option under NPS with **assured payout**
- **Gratuity:** retirement and death gratuity extended
- **Rules:** **CCS (Pension) Rules, 2021**; **CCS (Extraordinary Pension) Rules, 2023**
- **Tax:** same **Income Tax Act, 1961** benefits as NPS
- **Uptake:** **31,555** employees as of **20 July 2025**; last date to enrol **30 September 2025**
| Feature | OPS | NPS | UPS |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Nature** | Defined benefit | Defined contribution | **Hybrid** |
| **Pension** | **50%** of last pay | Market-linked | **Assured** |
| **Employee contribution** | None | Yes | Yes |
- **NPS for govt employees:** from **2004**
