## Vithalbhai Patel — 100 years as first Indian President of Central Legislative Assembly | from: 2025-08-26
# 1. News
- **Event:** **All India Speakers' Conference** (Delhi Legislative Assembly, **August 2025**) — centenary of his election
- **India Post:** commemorative **stamp** in **2025**; Lok Sabha centenary souvenir **1973**
# 2. Life
| Year | Event |
|---|---|
| **27 Sept 1873** | born **Nadiad, Gujarat** — elder brother of **Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel** |
| **1912** | Bombay Legislative Council |
| **1918** | Imperial Legislative Council |
| **1923** | co-founded **Swaraj Party** with **Motilal Nehru**, **C.R. Das** (opposed suspension of NCM after Chauri Chaura, 1922) |
| **22 August 1925** | **first Indian President** of **Central Legislative Assembly** (Speaker-equivalent) |
| **1928** | Motilal Nehru motion (backed by **Lala Lajpat Rai**) for separate Assembly Department |
| **1929** | independent **Legislative Assembly Secretariat** created |
| **22 Oct 1933** | died in **Geneva** |
# 3. Contributions
- Refused to stand to receive the **Viceroy** — dignity of the chair
- After **Bhagat Singh–Batukeshwar Dutt bomb (1929)**, kept "**ward and watch**" security under the President — continued till **2024** when **CISF** took over
- Later allied with **Subhas Chandra Bose**; will left estate for Bose's work — family won court case
