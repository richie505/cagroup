## Swadeshi in India — historical overview | from: 2025-08-28
# 1. Evolution
| Period | Person / event | Idea |
|---|---|---|
| **1822** | Bengali weekly **Samachar Chandrika** | use Indian goods for religious/social reasons |
| 19th c. | **Gopal Hari Deshmukh** (1823–1892), **Rajnarayan Basu** (1826–1899) | locally produced goods |
| Mid-19th c. | **M.G. Ranade** (1842–1901) | prefer domestic goods even if costlier |
| Late 19th c. | **Dadabhai Naoroji** — **Drain of Wealth**; **R.C. Dutt** — **Economic History of India** | economic nationalism, deindustrialisation |
| Gandhi | **khadi**, **charkha** | economic + moral + cultural swadeshi, village self-sufficiency |
| Post-1947 | mixed economy, **import substitution** | small-scale industry support |
- ⚠ Gandhi's swadeshi was **not purely economic**
- **National Handloom Day:** **7 August** — Swadeshi Movement launched on **7 August 1905**
