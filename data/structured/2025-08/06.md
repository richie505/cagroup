## IISc study — Asgard archaea with two FtsZ genes | from: 2025-08-25
# 1. News
- **By:** **IISc Bengaluru** — in **The EMBO Journal**
- **Organism:** **Odinarchaeota** (an **Asgard archaea**) — has **two FtsZ genes** (**OdinFtsZ1**, **OdinFtsZ2**) plus a **tubulin-like** gene
- **OdinFtsZ1:** straight protofilaments, anchors directly to membranes (bacteria-like)
- **OdinFtsZ2:** spiral ring-like structures, needs adaptor proteins
- **Methods:** phylogenetics, **cryo-electron microscopy**
# 2. Background
- **Asgard archaea:** named after **Norse gods** (Odin-, Loki-, Thorarchaeota); found in **deep-sea sediments**; **closest living relatives of eukaryotes**
- **FtsZ:** bacterial division protein forming the **Z-ring**; **tubulin** is the **eukaryotic** hallmark
- ⚠ Tubulin is **not** unique to bacteria — it is eukaryotic

## Brain-eating amoeba (PAM) cases in Kerala | from: 2025-08-20
# 1. News
- **Disease:** **Primary Amoebic Meningoencephalitis (PAM)** — caused by **Naegleria fowleri**
- **2025:** **three cases** in **Kozhikode district** — death of a **nine-year-old**, a **three-month-old** infected
- **Kerala fatality:** **~25%** vs global **~97%** (early diagnosis)
| Year | Milestone |
|---|---|
| **1971** | **First Indian case** |
| **2016** | Kerala's first case — **Alappuzha** |
| **2016–2023** | only **8** cases |
| **2023** | **36 cases**, **9 deaths** |
| **2024** | **14-year-old** boy in **Kozhikode** — **first Indian to survive PAM**; only **11** survivors globally |
# 2. Background
- **Habitat:** warm freshwater and soil; thrives up to **46°C**
- **Route:** water through the **nose** → **olfactory nerve** → brain
- ⚠ **Not** communicable; **not** spread by drinking contaminated water
- **Death:** within **5–18 days** of symptoms
- **Drugs:** Amphotericin B, Azithromycin, Fluconazole, Rifampin, **Miltefosine**, Dexamethasone

## Rare quadruple star system UPM J1040−3551 AabBab | from: 2025-08-27
# 1. News
- **System:** **two cold T-type brown dwarfs** orbiting a pair of **young red dwarfs** — ⚠ first such arrangement seen
- **Journal:** **Monthly Notices of the Royal Astronomical Society** (Sept 2025)
- **Led by:** Prof. **Zenghua Zhang**, **Nanjing University**
# 2. Background
- **Brown dwarfs:** "**failed stars**" — cannot sustain hydrogen fusion; up to **70 times Jupiter**'s mass; companion chance **<5%**
- **Red dwarfs:** most common stars — ~**70%** of Milky Way stars; long-lived
- ⚠ **Blue supergiants** are among the **hottest**, most luminous stars — not cold

## Entangled atomic clocks to probe quantum–gravity interface | from: 2025-08-20
# 1. News
- **Study:** **Jacob Covey**, **Igor Pikovski**, **Johannes Borregaard** — in **PRX Quantum** (**July 2025**)
- **Proposal:** tabletop experiment with **three entangled ytterbium atomic clocks** at different elevations (km-scale)
- **Entanglement:** **W state** (robust to particle loss, unlike **GHZ state**)
- **Expected:** frequency shift **~0.02 Hz** for **1 km** separation; coherence time **~50 seconds**
- **Tests:** unitarity, linearity, **Born rule** in curved spacetime
# 2. Background
- **Quantum gravity theories:** **String Theory** (extra dimensions, **gravitons**), **Loop Quantum Gravity** (discrete spacetime at **Planck scale**)
- Quantum mechanics vs **general relativity** — not yet unified
