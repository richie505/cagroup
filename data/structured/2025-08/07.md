## Asian giant tortoise artificially incubated in Manipur | from: 2025-08-26
# 1. News
- **Where:** **Manipur Zoological Garden, Imphal** with **India Turtle Conservation Program (ITCP)**
- **Result:** **28 hatchlings** (**August 2025**) — ⚠ **first** artificial hatching of the species in **mainland Asia**
- **Training:** **25** zookeepers and forest staff (**21 August 2025**)
- **Persons:** Director **Laishram Biramangol Singh**; ITCP coordinator **Sushmita Kar**
- **Manipur Zoo also known for:** **Sangai deer** breeding
# 2. Background — Manouria emys phayrei
- **Family:** **Testudinidae**; **IUCN: Critically Endangered**
- **Largest tortoise** in **mainland Asia**
- **India range:** **Assam, Manipur, Meghalaya, Mizoram, Nagaland** — ⚠ **not** Andaman & Nicobar
- **Global:** NE India, Bangladesh, Myanmar, Thailand, Malaysia
- **India:** **29** species of freshwater turtles and tortoises

## National Designated Authority for carbon markets | from: 2025-08-27
# 1. News
- **Notified:** **22 August 2025** by **MoEFCC**
- **Why:** mandatory under **Article 6** of the **Paris Agreement (2015)**
- **Composition:** **21 members**, headed by **Secretary, MoEFCC**; MEA, MNRE, NITI Aayog, Steel Ministry etc.
- **Functions:** list activities eligible for carbon credits; approve projects; allow credits to count toward **NDCs**
# 2. Background
- **COP29 (Baku, Nov 2024):** finalised Article 6 rules
- **ITMOs:** internationally transferred mitigation outcomes
| India's 2030 NDC | Target |
|---|---|
| **Emissions intensity of GDP** | cut **45%** from **2005** levels |
| **Non-fossil installed capacity** | **50%** |
| **Additional carbon sink** | **2.5–3 billion tonnes** CO2e |

## Vikalpa report on coal operations and NGT | from: 2025-08-28
# 1. News
- **Report:** **"Regulating Coal Operations: Environmental and Social Impacts through the Lens of the National Green Tribunal"** — by non-profit **Vikalpa**, **26 August 2025**
- **Authors:** **Shripad Dharmadhikari**, **Kush Tanwani** — analysed **eight NGT cases**
- **Coal share:** coal and lignite **73%** of electricity in **2022–23**; ~**50%** by **2031–32**
- **Air:** coal dust PM10 up to **five times** limit (**460 μg/m³**)
- **Cases:** **Ennore** (TN) — fly ash clogging, data manipulation, mangrove restoration order; **Sonbhadra**, **Mejia** — restoration committees
- **Health:** silica (**silicosis**), **cadmium**, **lead**
- ⚠ NGT compensation often **delayed** or partial

## Monsoon 2025 disasters and need for preventive resilience | from: 2025-08-29
# 1. News
- **August 2025:** floods in **Punjab** and **Delhi**; landslides in **Himachal Pradesh**; deaths in **J&K**
- **Pattern:** rainfall in concentrated bursts; "compound disasters" (IPCC, IMD)
- **Issues:** weak last-mile warnings; reactive relief; ⚠ compensatory afforestation **does not** restore same biodiversity
- **Plans:** **NAPCC**, **SAPCCs**; NDMA/SDMA district plans
- **Global models:** floodplain zoning (**Netherlands**), slope stabilisation (**Japan**), community preparedness (**Philippines**)

## 33 years after the Rio Earth Summit | from: 2025-08-29
# 1. Background
- **Summit:** **UNCED, 1992, Rio** — **125** countries, **100** heads of state, **12,000** delegates
- **Outcomes:** **UNFCCC** (in force **1994**); **CBDR** principle in Rio Declaration; **Biodiversity Convention**; **Desertification Treaty**
- ⚠ Binding **Forest Convention** was **not** adopted — blocked by Global South (G77, India); only non-binding forest principles
- **Paris Agreement (2015):** shift to voluntary **NDCs**
- **Climate finance promise:** **$100 billion** a year — unmet
- **Commentators:** **Sunita Narain**, **Anumita Roychowdhury** (**CSE**)
