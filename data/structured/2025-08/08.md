=== TOPIC 2025-08:08 | Reports, Indices & Rankings

## NARI 2025 — women's safety index | from: 2025-08-29
# 1. News
- **Index:** **National Annual Report and Index on Women's Safety (NARI) 2025** — perception survey of **12,770 women** in **31 cities**
- **By:** conceived by **Pvalue Analytics**, **The NorthCap University**, **Jindal Global Law School**; published by **Group of Intellectuals and Academicians (GIA)**; supported by **NCW**
- ⚠ **Not** an NCRB publication
- **National score:** **65%**
| Safest cities | Least safe cities |
|---|---|
| **Kohima**, **Visakhapatnam**, **Bhubaneswar**, Aizawl, Gangtok, Itanagar, **Mumbai** | **Patna**, **Jaipur**, Faridabad, **Delhi**, **Kolkata**, Srinagar, Ranchi |
# 2. Findings
- **6 in 10** women felt safe; **40%** not so safe/unsafe
- **Educational institutions:** **86%** safe by day
- **Workplace:** **91%** feel safe; half unsure of **POSH** policies
- **Harassment 2024:** **7%** in public spaces; **14%** for women under 24; hotspots — neighbourhoods **38%**, public transport **29%**
- **Reporting:** only **1 in 3** report; **25%** trust authorities
