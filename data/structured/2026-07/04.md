## Nine years of GST | from: 2026-07-01, CA360-2026-07, OL-2026-07
# 1. What happened
- **GST completed nine years** on **July 1, 2026** (in force since **July 1, 2017**)
- **Registered taxpayers:** **66.5 lakh (2017)** → **1.65 crore (May 2026)**
- **Gross GST collections:** **₹7.4 lakh crore (2017–18)** → **₹22.27 lakh crore (2025–26)**
# 2. Evolution
| Year | Step |
|---|---|
| **2003** | **Kelkar Task Force** on Indirect Taxes recommended GST |
| **2006** | Union Budget proposed national GST |
| **2014** | **122nd Constitutional Amendment Bill** introduced |
| **2016** | **Constitution (101st Amendment) Act, 2016** |
| **2017** | GST in force **July 1, 2017** — subsumed **17 taxes and 13 cesses** |
# 3. Constitutional framework
| Article | Provision |
|---|---|
| **246A** | **Concurrent** GST law-making power to Parliament and States; Parliament **exclusive** for inter-State |
| **269A** | GST on **inter-State** supplies — levied by Union, apportioned |
| **279A** | **GST Council** constituted by the **President** |
- **CGST / SGST:** intra-State; **UTGST:** intra-UT; **IGST:** inter-State and **imports**
- ⚠ GST is a **destination-based consumption tax** (not origin-based)
- **Outside GST:** **alcoholic liquor** for human consumption; **petroleum crude, petrol, HSD, natural gas, ATF** (until Council recommends); Basic Customs Duty, stamp duty, electricity duty, motor vehicle tax
# 4. Next-Generation GST reforms
- **Approved:** **56th GST Council meeting**; in effect **September 2025**
- **Slabs:** mainly **5%** and **18%**; **40%** for luxury and sin goods
- **Registration threshold (goods):** **₹20 lakh → ₹40 lakh**
- **Composition Scheme limit:** **₹1.5 crore**
- **QRMP:** turnover up to **₹5 crore** — quarterly returns, monthly payment
- **NIL returns** via **SMS**
- **GSTN:** IT backbone — registration, returns, payments, e-invoicing, ITC matching
# 5. June 2026 collections (CA360)
- **Gross GST June 2026:** **₹1,94,812 crore** — up **13.9%** from **₹1,71,105 crore** (June 2025)
- **Imports GST:** **₹60,038 crore** (+**34.6%**); **domestic:** **₹1,34,774 crore** (+**6.5%**)
- **GST Council** (2016, Art. 279A) chaired by **Nirmala Sitharaman**; implemented by **CBIC**

## Rising inflation — WPI near 10% | from: 2026-07-21
# 1. What happened
- **WPI inflation:** close to **10% in June** (near zero/negative until December) — driven by **fuel and power**, **manufactured products**, food supply shocks

## White Gold — India's cotton story | from: 2026-07-22, CA360-2026-07, OL-2026-07
# 1. Key facts
- ⚠ India — **only country** growing all **four** cotton species; **1st in area**, **2nd in production and consumption**
- **Area:** **114.84 lakh ha** — about **38%** of world area; **62% rain-fed**
- **Production:** **297.24 lakh bales (2024–25)**; **290.91 lakh bales (2025–26 P)**; consumption **328 lakh bales**; **1 bale = 170 kg**
- **Exports 2024–25:** about **18 lakh bales** (0.31 MMT, **3.37%** of world); cotton exports **US$ 11.49 bn** (FY25)
- **Fabric/made-ups destinations:** **USA 26.35%**, Bangladesh 19.81%, Sri Lanka 5.11%
- **Farmers:** about **6 million**; **40–50 million** in processing and trade
- **Ahmedabad:** "**Manchester of India**"
# 2. Species and staple
| Species | Name |
|---|---|
| **G. Arboreum** | Asian cotton |
| **G. Herbaceum** | Asian cotton |
| **G. Barbadense** | Egyptian cotton |
| **G. Hirsutum** | American upland — about **90%** of hybrid output; all **Bt** hybrids |

- **Extra-Long Staple:** **32.5 mm and above**; Long staple 27.5–32 mm; Short 20 mm and below
- **Zones:** North — Punjab, Haryana, Rajasthan; Central — Gujarat, Maharashtra, MP; South — **Telangana, Andhra Pradesh**, Karnataka

## Compressed Biogas and energy security | from: 2026-07-14
# 1. Context
- India imports about **85%** of crude oil; about **90%** of LPG imports transit the **Strait of Hormuz**
- **Biogas:** mainly **methane** (**50–80%**) + CO2 (20–50%); produced by **anaerobic digestion**; upgraded **CBG** used like **CNG**
# 2. Schemes
- **SATAT** (Sustainable Alternative Towards Affordable Transportation): launched **2018**; target **5,000 CBG plants by 2023**; only **132** completed by **June 3, 2026**
- **GOBARdhan** (Galvanising Organic Bio-Agro Resources Dhan): up to **₹50 lakh per district** for community plants; **₹564 crore** for biomass machinery; **₹994 crore** for pipelines to gas grid
- **CBG Blending Obligation:** approved **2023** by **National Biofuels Coordination Committee** — **1% in FY26** rising to **5% by FY29**, for **CNG** (transport) and **PNG** (domestic)
- ⚠ Blending starts at **1%**, not 5%
- **Ethanol blending:** **1.5% (2014)** → **20% by December 2025** — five years ahead of **2030** target
# 3. Global lessons
- **Europe, China, USA:** about **90%** of global biogas
- **Germany:** **Renewable Energy Sources Act, 2000** — maize feedstock boom, later capped
- **Denmark:** target **only biomethane** in gas grid by **2030**; manure/residues, no food crops
- **Economic Survey 2026:** maize yield **2.56 t/ha (FY16)** → **3.78 t/ha (FY25)**; maize-ethanol price CAGR **11.7%** (FY22–FY25)

## Heat stress hits milk production — Haryana study | from: 2026-07-06
# 1. What happened
- **Study:** published in **Scientific Reports** — climate change cutting milk yield in **Haryana** (**trans-Gangetic plains**)
- **Data:** **2004–2019**, **1,148 villages**; **4.66 million** cross-bred cattle, **2.86 million** indigenous cattle, **35.56 million** buffaloes
- **Threshold:** temperature above **38°C** with humidity above **70%** — worst in **July–August**; winter impact negligible
- **Buffaloes:** unit rise in **Potential Evapotranspiration** cuts yield by about **1.4 litres/buffalo/day** (dark skin, fewer sweat glands)
- **Resilient breeds:** **Sahiwal**, **Hariana**
- **Heat stress** can cut yield by **20–30%**
- **Current loss:** about **3.2 million tonnes** of milk, worth **₹2,661 crore**; could reach **15 million tonnes by 2050s**
- **80 million** smallholder dairy farmers
