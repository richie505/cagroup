## PM Modi's State Visit to Indonesia | from: 2026-07-09, CA360-2026-07, OL-2026-07
# 1. What happened
- **PM Modi** received **Bintang Adipurna** — Indonesia's **highest civilian honour**
- ⚠ **Second** Indian PM to get it after **Jawaharlal Nehru**
- **Indonesian President:** **Prabowo Subianto**
# 2. Outcomes
- **Defence:** supply of **BrahMos** supersonic cruise missiles; export order for **Astra** air-to-air missile; ToT and industrial cooperation
- **Maritime:** Indonesia to station a **Liaison Officer** at **IFC-IOR** (Information Fusion Centre – Indian Ocean Region); training slots at **NDA** and **DSSC**
- **Critical minerals** extraction; telecom (**wireless, quantum**); medicines supply
- **Education:** **IIM Bangalore campus in Indonesia**
- **Culture:** restoration of **Prambanan Temple Complex**, **Yogyakarta** — Indonesia's **largest Hindu temple complex**
# 3. Background
- **Comprehensive Strategic Partnership:** **2018**; **Shared Vision of Maritime Cooperation in the Indo-Pacific** — **2018**
- **Indonesia:** about **21%** of world **nickel** reserves; top producer of nickel, copper, bauxite, tin; near **Strait of Malacca**
- **Independence:** declared **17 August 1945** by **Soekarno, Mohammad Hatta, Sutan Sjahrir**; former **Dutch East Indies**; Japan occupied **1942**; **POETARA** set up **9 March 1943**
- **Biju Patnaik** flew **Sutan Sjahrir** to New Delhi in **1947**
- **23rd Indian Division** reached **Batavia** on **29 September 1945**; **Battle of Soerabaya**
- **Bandung Conference:** **1955**
- ⚠ G20: **Indonesia** is a member; **Singapore, Malaysia, New Zealand, Iran, Vietnam** are not

## Strait of Hormuz — US withdraws 20% transit fee | from: 2026-07-16
# 1. What happened
- **US President Donald Trump** proposed a **20% reimbursement fee** on cargo through **Strait of Hormuz** ("Guardian of the Hormuz Strait") — **withdrawn one day later**, replaced by trade/investment deals with Gulf countries
- **IMO** opposed — no legal basis for tolls on international strait transit
- ⚠ **Neither US nor Iran** has ratified **UNCLOS** — but transit passage is customary law; tolls apply to artificial canals (**Suez, Panama**)
- **Iran's FM** called 20% **excessive**
# 2. US–Iran context
- **Conflict began:** **February 2026**; Iran asserted sovereignty, temporarily closed Strait
- **Interim US–Iran pact:** **June 17, 2026** — transits crossed **90** on **June 24**; avg **40–50/day** vs pre-war up to **140**
- **Hormuz:** between **Iran** and **Oman**; links **Persian Gulf** with **Gulf of Oman**; nearly **one-fifth** of global oil and LNG
# 3. India's exposure
| Item via Hormuz | Share |
|---|---|
| **Crude oil imports** | about **40%** |
| **LNG imports** | about **60%** |
| **LPG imports** | about **90%** |
- **Import dependence:** crude **88%+**; LPG **60%**; natural gas about **50%**
- India imports **1.8–2 billion barrels/yr**; every **USD 1/barrel** adds up to **USD 2 billion**
- Fee could have added about **USD 9 billion/yr**
- **Oil imports March–May 2026:** up **47%** to **USD 48.88 billion**
