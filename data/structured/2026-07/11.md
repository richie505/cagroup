## Hul Diwas — Santal Hul of 1855 | from: 2026-07-03, CA360-2026-07, OL-2026-07
# 1. What
- **Hul Diwas:** **June 30** — commemorates Santal rebellion that began **June 30, 1855**
- **Leaders:** **Sido and Kanhu Murmu**; proclaimed at **Bhognadih**
- **Region:** **Damin-i-Koh** — tract along the **Rajmahal Hills** (eastern **Jharkhand**), demarcated by British in **1832** for Santal settlement
- **Hul:** means **rebellion / movement** in Santali
# 2. Causes and aftermath
- **Causes:** rising **rents**, **moneylender** (mahajan) exploitation, **land alienation**, administrative injustice, humiliation
- ⚠ Directed against **moneylenders, zamindars, revenue officials** too — not only British
- **After suppression:** migration to **Purnea, Malda, Burdwan, Jalpaiguri, Assam tea gardens**; by **1901** about **31,000** Santal Pargana residents in **Assam**, **10,000** in Jalpaiguri (**Bengal District Gazetteer 1910**)
- **Proverb** recorded by **W.G. Archer:** "a million Santals have a single word"
# 3. Background
- **Duration:** **June 1855 – January 1856**; a precursor of the **1857** revolt
- **Jharkhand:** formed **November 15, 2000**; capital **Ranchi**; CM **Hemant Soren**
- **Ol Chiki script:** by **Pandit Raghunath Murmu**, **1925**
- **Santali** in **Eighth Schedule** in **2003**
- **Origin narratives:** **Hihiri-Pipiri**, **Sasan Beda**
- **Jharkhand Migration Survey 2023:** about **45 lakh** residents work elsewhere

## Sarnath — India's 45th UNESCO World Heritage Site | from: 2026-07-27, CA360-2026-07, OL-2026-07
# 1. What happened
- **Ancient Buddhist Site of Sarnath** (near **Varanasi, UP**) inscribed at **48th World Heritage Committee**, **Busan, South Korea**
- India now has **45** World Heritage Sites — **6th globally**, **2nd in Asia-Pacific**
- On **tentative list since 1998** (about **28 years**)
- **Previous inscription:** **Maratha Military Landscapes of India** — **47th session, Paris**
# 2. Significance
- **Buddha's first sermon** (after enlightenment at **Bodh Gaya**) — **Dharmachakra Pravartan** (turning of the wheel of Dharma), **6th century BCE**
- **Other names:** **Rishipatana**, **Isipatana**, **Mrigadava**, **Deer Park**
- **Monuments:** **Chaukhandi Stupa**, **Dhamek Stupa**, **Dharmarajika Stupa**
- **Finds:** **Mauryan Lion Capital** (National Emblem); **Dharmachakrapravartana Buddha** (Gupta art)
- **Sarnath Museum:** **1904** — ⚠ India's **earliest site museum**
- **Lion Capital:** shaft (**stambha-yashti**), circular carved abacus (elephant, bull, horse, lion with Dharmachakras), inverted lotus petals
# 3. Background
- **World Heritage Convention:** **1972**; **196** States; India's tentative list **69** sites; one nomination per year; nodal **ASI**
- **India's sites:** **36 cultural** (incl. Sarnath), **7 natural**, **1 mixed** (**Khangchendzonga NP**, Sikkim)
