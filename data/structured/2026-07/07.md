- **Global Tiger Forum:** HQ **New Delhi** — only intergovernmental body for tigers
- **CA|TS:** Conservation Assured Tiger Standards
- ⚠ Largest Critical Tiger Habitat (PYQ): **Nagarjunasagar-Srisailam** (AP/Telangana)

## El Niño and weak monsoon — economic risk | from: 2026-07-07
# 1. What happened
- **First month of monsoon:** rainfall deficit of about **40%**
- **IMD forecast:** **below-normal** rainfall in July (below **94%** of normal)
- **El Niño + drought** could cut GDP growth by **20–65 basis points**
- **Vulnerable districts:** **315**; **111 districts in 12 States** of primary concern (weak irrigation)
- **Reservoirs (166, CWC):** **47.725 BCM** on July 2 vs **78.077 BCM** last year
- **Foodgrain 2024-25:** **357.73 MMT** (+**25.43 MMT**)
- **NBS subsidy (kharif):** **₹41,533 crore** for P&K fertilisers, **28 grades**
- **Agri exports:** CAGR **8.2%** (2020–2025), about **12%** of core exports
- **Agriculture:** about one-fifth of GVA; **46%** of workforce; **55%** of population linked
- **El Niño drought years:** **1972, 1982, 2009, 2015**
# 2. Background
- **El Niño:** warming of **central-eastern equatorial Pacific**; Spanish for "**Little Boy / Christ Child**"; weakens trade winds, weakens **Humboldt (Peruvian) Current** and upwelling
- **ENSO phases:** El Niño, La Niña, Neutral; ⚠ **La Niña** generally **strengthens** Indian monsoon
- **IMD:** est. **1875**; **Ministry of Earth Sciences**; HQ **New Delhi**; **6 Regional Meteorological Centres** — Mumbai, Chennai, New Delhi, Kolkata, Nagpur, Guwahati; one of six **WMO RSMCs** — names cyclones in **North Indian Ocean**

## Glacial lake expansion and GLOF risk — Mago Chu Basin | from: 2026-07-24
# 1. What happened
- **Study:** **Mago Chu Basin**, **Tawang district, Arunachal Pradesh** — **4 of 5** glacial lakes expanded between **2016 and 2026**, including **high** and **very high risk** lakes
# 2. Types of glacial lakes (ICIMOD)
| Type | Note / Example |
|---|---|
| **Glacial erosion** | Cirque and valley lakes |
| **Moraine-dammed** | ⚠ **Most vulnerable** to GLOF (loose debris) — **Samudra Tapu**, **Gepang Gath** (HP) |
| **Ice-blocked** | Upper **Indus** and **Yarkand** basins (Karakoram) |
| **Supraglacial** | On glacier surface — **Barashigri** (HP), **Rongbuk** (Everest) |
| **Subglacial** | Beneath ice — need **Ground Penetrating Radar** |
- **Glacial Lake Atlas 2023:** by **NRSC (ISRO)** — **Brahmaputra basin** has highest concentration
- **Elevation-dependent warming:** high altitudes warm faster
# 3. Events and mitigation
- **Kedarnath 2013** — **Chorabari** lake; **South Lhonak Lake, Sikkim, 2023**
- **NDMA GLOF Guidelines:** **2020**; **CWC** risk indexing of lakes
- **National GLOF Risk Mitigation Project (NGRMP):** launched **2025** — **Arunachal Pradesh, Himachal Pradesh, Sikkim, Uttarakhand**
- **Siachen Glacier:** north of **Nubra Valley**

## Flash floods and cloudbursts — Assam floods | from: 2026-07-31
# 1. What happened
- **Assam floods** first blamed on cloudburst in **Nagaland**; IMD said it was **heavy rainfall**, not cloudburst threshold
- **IMD cloudburst definition:** more than **100 mm in one hour** over **20–30 sq km**
- **Flash flood:** develops in less than **six hours**
- ⚠ Not every flash flood is a cloudburst (dam release, GLOF, saturated soil, poor drainage)
- **1°C** warming → about **7%** more atmospheric moisture
- **Flash floods** most frequent in **Brahmaputra** basin, then Ganga and Krishna
# 2. NDMA background
- **Disaster Management Act, 2005**; NDMA constituted **27 September 2006**; chaired by **PM**; **PM + 9 members**
- **High Powered Committee 1999**; **Gujarat earthquake 2001**
- **SDMA** — CM; **DDMA** — District Collector; supervises **NIDM**

| Aspect | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Scientific name** | **Ardea insignis** |
| **IUCN** | **Critically Endangered** |
| **WPA 1972** | **Schedule I** |
| **Habitat** | Fast-flowing Himalayan rivers, mature **riparian** forests |
| **Diet** | Fish |

# 3. IUCN Red List background
- **Established 1964**; "**Barometer of Life**"; criteria **A–E**
- **Criterion D:** CR < **50**, EN < **250**, VU < **1,000** mature individuals
- **Criterion E:** CR if extinction probability ≥ **50%** in **10 years / 3 generations**
- **ISSG** (Global Invasive Species Database) belongs to **IUCN** (SSC), est. **1994**
