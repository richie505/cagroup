## VB-G RAM G Act replaces MGNREGA | from: CA360-2026-07, OL-2026-07
# 1. What happened
- **Viksit Bharat – Guarantee for Rozgar and Ajeevika Mission (Gramin) Act, 2025** in force **July 1, 2026** — ⚠ **replaces MGNREGA, 2005**
- **Guaranteed work:** **100 → 125 days** per household per year
- **National average wage:** **₹298.80 → ₹327.40** (~**10%**); temporary minimum wage floor **₹300/day**
| State | Daily wage |
|---|---|
| **Andhra Pradesh** | **₹312** |
| **Telangana** | **₹308** |
| **Haryana** | **₹409** |
| **Sikkim (high-altitude GPs)** | **₹450** — highest |
- **Arunachal and Nagaland:** highest increase **24.5%** (to ₹300)
- **Interim fund:** **₹95,692.31 crore**
- AI and GPS monitoring; assets — water security, rural infrastructure
- ⚠ **MGNREGA** launched **2006** from **Bandlapalli, Anantapur**; **VB-G RAM G** initiated from **Tirupati district**, AP
- **Ministry of Rural Development** — **Shivraj Singh Chouhan**

## Employees' Provident Funds Scheme, 2026 | from: 2026-07-03
# 1. What happened
- **Notified by:** Union government — **replaces EPF Scheme, 1952**
- **In force:** **June 29, 2026** — under the **Code on Social Security, 2020**
- **Administered by:** **EPFO**, **Ministry of Labour and Employment**
# 2. Key changes
- **Partial withdrawals:** earlier **13 categories** → **3 groups**: **essential needs** (illness, education, marriage), **housing needs**, **special circumstances**
- **Minimum balance:** at least **25%** of aggregate fund balance must remain → effectively max **75%** withdrawable
- **Eligibility:** most partial withdrawals after **12 months** of membership
| Purpose | Limit |
|---|---|
| **Education** | max **10** withdrawals in membership |
| **Marriage** | max **5** withdrawals |
| **Housing** | max **5** withdrawals; up to **75%** of balance |
| **Unemployment** | **entire balance** after **one year** unemployed |
- **Principal employer** made explicitly **ultimately liable** for contract workers' PF (deposit within **15 days** of month-end)
- **Voluntary contributions** above **statutory wage ceiling** or above **12%** allowed
# 3. Unchanged
- **Contribution:** **12%** employee + **12%** employer; **10%** for certain notified establishments
- **International workers:** mandatory coverage continues; withdrawal at **58 years** or as per **Social Security Agreements**
- ⚠ Contractor is **not solely** responsible — principal employer remains liable
# 4. Background
- **EPF & MP Act, 1952:** general threshold **20 employees**; **Section 16A** — employer may maintain own PF if **100 or more** employees

## Anaemia Mukt Bharat Abhiyaan — revised guidelines | from: 2026-07-01
# 1. What happened
- **Released by:** **Union Health Ministry** at the **16th meeting** of the **Central Council of Health and Family Welfare**
- **Shift:** from **prophylactic supplementation** to **testing, therapeutic treatment, Eating Right, digital tracking** and **Jan Chetna / Jan Bhagidari**
# 2. 6×6×6 → 7×7×7
| Element | Added (7th) |
|---|---|
| **Beneficiary group** | **Low Birth Weight babies aged 0–6 months** |
| **Intervention** | **Eating Right** approach — iron-rich, diversified diets |
| **Institutional mechanism** | Strengthened **Monitoring and Evaluation** framework |
- **T3 → T4:** **Test, Treat, Talk** + **Track**
- **IV iron therapy** for severe anaemia / non-responders among pregnant and lactating women: **Ferric Carboxymaltose**, **Iron Sucrose**
- **Unified AMB Abhiyaan Portal** integrates: **JANANI** (pregnant women), **RBSK** (child screening), **U-WIN** (immunisation records)
# 3. Background
- **Anaemia Mukt Bharat launched:** **2018** under **National Health Mission** — lifecycle approach
- **WHO cut-offs:** women of reproductive age **Hb < 12 g/dL**; children under 5 **Hb < 11 g/dL**
- **Original interventions:** IFA supplementation, **biannual deworming (Albendazole)**, testing and treatment, food fortification, BCC, non-nutritional causes (**malaria, fluorosis, haemoglobinopathies**)
- ⚠ AMB gives prophylactic **Iron-Folic Acid**, **not calcium**; promotes **delayed cord clamping**
| NFHS-5 group | Anaemic |
|---|---|
| **Children under 5** | **67.1%** |
| **Adolescent girls 15–19** | **59.1%** |
| **Women 15–49** | **57%** |
| **Pregnant women** | **52.2%** |
- **Global:** **40%** of children 6–59 months, **37%** of pregnant women, **30%** of non-pregnant women 15–49
- **WIFS:** weekly supervised IFA for adolescent girls and boys + biannual deworming
- **PMSMA:** special antenatal check-ups on the **9th of every month**
- **Health** is a **State subject**

## PM Vidyalaxmi Scheme | from: 2026-07-30
# 1. Features
- **Approved:** Union Cabinet **6 November 2024**; Ministry of **Education**
- **Collateral-free, guarantor-free** education loans for merit admissions to **1,425 Quality Higher Educational Institutions (QHEIs)**
- **Credit guarantee:** **75%** for loans up to **₹7.5 lakh**
- **Interest subvention:** **3%** on loans up to **₹10 lakh** for family income up to **₹8 lakh** — **1 lakh** fresh slots/year
- **Repayment:** up to **15 years** after moratorium (course + 1 year)
- **Eligible institutions:** top **100** in NIRF; top **200** State/UT govt HEIs; all Central govt HEIs
- ⚠ **Management / NRI quota** excluded; foreign campuses excluded
- **Delivery:** unified portal; **Digital Rupee / CBDC wallet**
- **GER (higher education):** **23.7% (2014-15)** → **30.0% (2023-24)**
# 2. Comparison
| Item | PM-USP CSIS | PM Vidyalaxmi |
|---|---|---|
| **Income limit** | **₹4.5 lakh** | **₹8 lakh** |
| **Subvention** | **Full** during moratorium | **3%** |
| **Courses** | Professional/technical | All degree/diploma |
| **Slots** | No limit | **1 lakh/year** |

## Hepatitis control — NVHCP and World Hepatitis Day | from: 2026-07-28, OL-2026-07
# 1. Key facts
- **World Hepatitis Day:** **28 July**
- **National Viral Hepatitis Control Program (NVHCP):** launched **2018** — covers **A, B, C, D, E**; free diagnostics and drugs; **NVHCP-MIS** portal
- **Progress:** **21.06 crore** screened; **6.12 lakh** Hep B and C patients treated; **1,153** treatment sites
- **Seroprevalence (HIV Sentinel Surveillance 2021):** Hep B **0.85%** (1 in 118); Hep C **0.29%** (1 in 345)
# 2. Virus types
| Virus | Spread | Prevention |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | Faecal-oral (food/water) | Sanitation |
| **B** | Mother-to-child, blood | ⚠ **Vaccine** under **UIP**; birth dose within **24 hours** |
| **C** | Unsafe injections, blood | ⚠ **No vaccine**; cured by **Direct-Acting Antivirals** |
| **D** | Only in **Hep B carriers** | Preventing B prevents D |
| **E** | Faecal-oral | Sanitation; serious in pregnancy |
- **A and E** monitored via **IDSP**
# 3. Global
- Hep B + C: about **1.3 million deaths/yr**; **304 million** living with infection
- **WHO GHSS 2022–2030:** by **2030** — **90%** cut in incidence, **65%** cut in mortality vs **2015**
