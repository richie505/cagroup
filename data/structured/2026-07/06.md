## Quantum computing and National Quantum Mission | from: 2026-07-10
# 1. Why in news
- **Google Willow** processor (**2024**) — scaling **3×3 → 5×5 → 7×7** qubit lattices cut encoded error rate by **factor of two** each step
- **IBM** aims at **fault-tolerant** quantum computer by **2029**
- **Current era:** **NISQ** (Noisy Intermediate-Scale Quantum)
# 2. Concepts
- **Qubit:** 0, 1 or both (**superposition**); **entanglement**; **interference**
- **Decoherence:** loss of quantum state due to environment; cooling near **-273°C**
- **Error rates:** quantum **1%–0.1%**; classical one in a **quintillion**
- **Quantum Error Correction:** many physical qubits → one **logical qubit**
- **QKD:** photons; based on **observer effect** and **no-cloning** rule
| Qubit type | Note |
|---|---|
| **Superconducting** | **Josephson junctions** — Google Willow |
| **Quantum dot** | Semiconductor (silicon, GaAs, germanium) |
| **Trapped ion** | Charged atoms in EM fields |
| **Photonic** | Photons |
| **NMR** | Used in **first quantum computer demonstration, 1998** |
- **Nobel Physics 2025:** **John Clarke, Michel H. Devoret, John M. Martinis** — macroscopic quantum tunnelling in electric circuit
# 3. National Quantum Mission
- **Sanctioned:** **April 2023**; outlay **₹6,000 crore+**; target **2031**
- **Qubits:** **20–50** in 3 years; **50–100** in 5 years; **50–1000** in 8 years
- **Satellite quantum communication:** **2,000 km**; inter-city QKD **2,000 km**
- **T-Hubs:** **IISc Bengaluru** (computing); **IIT Madras + C-DOT** (communication); **IIT Bombay** (sensing); **IIT Delhi** (materials)
- **Model:** **Hub-Spoke-Spike**; **14 Technical Groups**, **17 States + 2 UTs**
- ⚠ NQM covers communication, sensing, materials too — not only computing

## China's helium export ban | from: 2026-07-13
# 1. What happened
- **10 July 2026:** **China** imposed immediate temporary **ban on helium exports**
- ⚠ China is **not** a major producer — produces about **1.6%** of world helium, imports **80%+**
| Producer | Share |
|---|---|
| **United States** | about **43%** |
| **Qatar** | about **33%** (key for Asia, via **Strait of Hormuz**) |
| **Others** | Russia, Canada, Algeria |
- **Russia:** exports need high-level approval through **2027**
- **US Federal Helium Reserve** privatised in **2024**
# 2. Background
- **Helium (He):** atomic number **2**; **Group 18** noble gas; second-lightest; **non-renewable**
- **Formed by:** radioactive decay of **uranium and thorium** (alpha particles); extracted with **natural gas** (viable at **0.3%** by volume); commercial purity **99.997%**
- **Boiling point:** about **-269°C** (−268.9°C) — lowest of all; cannot solidify at normal pressure (needs **~25 atm** at ~1 K)
- **Uses:** **MRI** magnets, **semiconductor** wafers, **quantum computers**, leak detection, **optical fibres**, rocket fuel-tank pressurisation (ISRO, NASA)
- **Transport:** **vacuum-jacketed stainless steel** vessels; **boil-off** risk
- **Scuba tanks:** air diluted with **helium** (avoids nitrogen narcosis)
