## Global Terrorism Index 2026 | from: 2026-07-22
# 1. Key findings
- **Edition:** **13th**; publisher **Institute for Economics & Peace (IEP)**
- **2025:** deaths down **28%** (about **5,582**); incidents down **22%** (about **2,944**); **81 countries** improved
- **Concentration:** about **70%** of deaths in **Pakistan, Burkina Faso, Nigeria, Niger, DR Congo**; **Sahel** — over **half** of fatalities
- **99%** of deaths in conflict-affected / unstable countries
- **60%+** of attacks within **100 km** of borders
- **West:** **93%** of fatal attacks (last 5 years) by **lone actors**
- **Major groups:** Islamic State, **JNIM**, **TTP**, Lashkar-e-Taiba, **Al-Shabaab**
# 2. India's counter-terror architecture
- **Terrorist act defined:** **Section 15, UAPA 1967**; also **BNS 2023**
- **PRAHAAR Framework:** intelligence-led, pre-emptive deterrence; decouples terrorism from religion
- **Laws:** **UAPA Amendment 2019** (individuals as terrorists); **NIA Amendment 2019** (offences abroad); **Arms Amendment 2019**
- **53 Special NIA Courts**; **MAC** connects **28 agencies**; Cyber MAC; **NATGRID**; **CCTNS 2.0**
- **Police modernisation:** **₹6,300 crore+** (2021–2026)
- **Operations:** Surgical Strikes **2016**; Balakot **2019**; **Operation Sindoor 2025**
- **FATF member** since **2010**; **No Money for Terror** conference **2022**; **CCIT** proposed at UN **1996**; JWGs with **27 countries** and **5** forums; **BHARATPOL 2025**; **Delhi Declaration 2022**
- **J&K terror incidents:** **228 (2018)** → **12 (2025)**
