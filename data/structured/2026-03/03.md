## World TB Day 2026 — towards person-centred TB care | from: 2026-03-24
# 1. Day
- **World Tuberculosis Day:** **March 24** — marks discovery of the TB bacterium by **Robert Koch** in **1882**
- **Theme 2026:** **"Yes! We Can End TB"**
# 2. TB facts
- **Mycobacterium tuberculosis**; airborne; mainly lungs but ⚠ also other organs
- **10 million+** ill, **1 million+** deaths a year; leading single infectious killer; ~**one-fourth** of world infected
- Untreated mortality ~**50%**; **4–6 months** treatment cures ~**90%**
- **Global target 2030** (SDGs, WHO End TB); ⚠ India's target **2025**; UN High-Level Meetings **2018**, **2023**; next **2028**; WHO Global TB Report since **1997**
# 3. Integration
- **NTEP** bidirectional TB–diabetes screening; **Chennai** cohort — **34%** of TB patients diabetic, **41%** of them poorly controlled
- Pilot: of **26,000** screened, ~**3,000** had **COPD/asthma**
# 4. Indian initiatives
| Initiative | Feature |
|---|---|
| **NTEP** | Free diagnosis and treatment |
| **Nikshay Portal** | Digital patient tracking |
| **Nikshay Poshan Yojana** | **₹1,000/month** nutrition support |
| **PM TB Mukt Bharat Abhiyaan** | Community participation, patient adoption |
| **UDST** | Universal Drug Susceptibility Testing |
