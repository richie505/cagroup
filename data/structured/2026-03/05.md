## Strait of Hormuz crisis — Iran's selective blockade and toll system | from: 2026-03-31, CA360-2026-03, OL-2026-03
# 1. News
- Amid the **US–Israel–Iran war (2026)**, **Iran** imposed a **selective blockade** — closed for **US, Israel and allies**; open to "friendly nations" **India, China, Russia, Iraq, Pakistan**
- **Traffic:** ~**130 ships/day** before → **3–4/day**; **20+** vessels attacked; ~**2,000** stranded
- **Toll-booth system:** geopolitical vetting, clearance code, escort; up to **$2 million per ship**, some in **yuan** (Iran denies)
- **India:** ships via Hormuz incl. **Pine Gas**, **Jag Vasant**, **Shivalik**, **Nanda Devi**, **Jag Laadki**; ⚠ **no formal agreement**, **no payments**
# 2. Legal position — UNCLOS
| Article | Provision |
|---|---|
| **Article 38** | Right of **transit passage** |
| **Article 39** | Duties of ships during passage |
| **Article 44** | Coastal states cannot suspend/hamper passage |
- ⚠ Natural straits — **no tolls**; artificial canals (**Suez**, **Panama**) charge fees; **GCC** called it a clear violation
# 3. Geography
- Between **Iran (north)** and **Oman & UAE (south)**; links **Persian Gulf → Arabian Sea**
- Width ~**50 km** at entry, ~**33 km** narrowest; ~**20 mb/d** (~**20%** of oil trade); ~**$600 billion** a year
- **Users:** Iran, Iraq, Kuwait, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, UAE; Gulf produces **22%** of global oil
# 4. Permission and UN task force

- Hormuz links the **Persian Gulf** and **Gulf of Oman**; ~**20%** of global oil and LNG trade; India imports **85%+** of crude
