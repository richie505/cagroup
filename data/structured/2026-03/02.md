- **Case:** from **Andhra Pradesh High Court** — complaint under SC/ST (PoA) Act by **Pastor Chintada Anand Paul**; HC quashed charges

## Right to die with dignity — Harish Rana case | from: 2026-03-17, CA360-2026-03, OL-2026-03
# 1. News
- **Supreme Court** allowed **withdrawal of life support** for **Harish Rana** — right to die with dignity part of **Article 21**
- Rana injured in a fall in **2013** (aged **20**); in **Persistent Vegetative State (PVS)** for **13+ years**, on **CANH**
- **Held:** **CANH (Clinically Assisted Nutrition and Hydration)** is **medical treatment**; withdrawal in patient's **best interest** (family + medical boards)
# 2. Evolution of law
| Case | Holding |
|---|---|
| **Gian Kaur v. State of Punjab (1996)** | Right to live with dignity; ⚠ **rejected** right to die |
| **Aruna Shanbaug v. Union of India (2011)** | **Passive euthanasia** allowed in exceptional cases |
| **Common Cause v. Union of India (2018)** | Right to refuse treatment; **living wills**; guidelines simplified in **2023** |
- **Passive euthanasia:** withholding/withdrawing life-sustaining treatment — legal under strict conditions
- **Article 21 cases:** **Hussainara Khatoon** (speedy trial); **Suchita Srivastava**, **Puttaswamy** (reproductive choice)
# 3. More facts
- ⚠ **First time** the SC actually implemented passive euthanasia in a specific case; **active euthanasia** remains **illegal**
- **P. Rathinam (1994)** had recognised a right to die under Article 21 — overruled by **Gian Kaur (1996)**; **2023** simplified **living wills**

## Nine-judge bench on the meaning of "industry" | from: 2026-03-18, CA360-2026-03, OL-2026-03
# 1. News
- **Nine-judge Constitution Bench** led by **CJI Surya Kant** hearing what constitutes an **"industry"** under labour law
- Re-examines **Bangalore Water Supply and Sewerage Board vs A. Rajappa (1978)** — **seven-judge** bench, majority by **Justice V.R. Krishna Iyer**
# 2. 1978 tests
- **Triple Test:** (1) **systematic activity**, (2) **employer–employee cooperation**, (3) production/distribution of goods or services for human wants — ⚠ profit motive and capital **irrelevant**
- **Dominant Nature Test:** primary function decides status
- Brought **hospitals**, **universities**, charities and some welfare departments under labour law; two judges dissented
# 3. Later developments
- **Industrial Disputes (Amendment) Act, 1982** sought to exclude hospitals, education, sovereign functions — ⚠ **never notified**
- **1996:** Maharashtra Forest Dept held an industry; **2001:** opposite for Gujarat Forest Dept
- **State of UP vs Jai Bir Singh (2005)** — five-judge reference; **2017** seven-judge bench referred to nine judges
- **Issues framed:** validity of triple test (in light of 1982 amendment and **Industrial Relations Code, 2020**); welfare activities; meaning of **sovereign functions**
- **MSME (from July 1, 2020):** medium — investment ≤ **₹50 crore**, turnover ≤ **₹250 crore**; all MSME loans are **priority sector**
# 4. Bench
- **Hearings began March 17**; members: **CJI Surya Kant**, **B.V. Nagarathna**, **P.S. Narasimha**, **Dipankar Datta**, **Ujjal Bhuyan**, **Satish Chandra Sharma**, **Joymalya Bagchi**, **Alok Aradhe**, **Vipul M. Pancholi**
- **1978** judgment paras **140 and 144** under review
- **Constitution Bench:** **Article 145(3)** — at least **five** judges

## Transgender Persons (Protection of Rights) Amendment Bill, 2026 | from: 2026-03-20, CA360-2026-03, OL-2026-03
# 1. News
- **Introduced by Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment**; amends the **2019 Act**; may override **NALSA (2014)**
# 2. Key changes
- **Abolishes self-identification** — removes **Section 4(2)**
- **Narrower definition:** only socio-cultural identities (hijra, kinner etc.) and specific biological variations — ⚠ excludes gender-fluid, non-binary
- **Medical Board (CMO-led)** recommendation before **District Magistrate** issues certificate
- **National Council** state reps must be **Director-level** officers
- **New offence — forced identity:** kidnapping to force identity **10 years to life**; for children **life + ₹5 lakh fine**; forced begging — adults **5–10 years**, children **10–14 years RI**
# 3. Background
- **NALSA v. Union of India (2014):** "**third gender**"; self-determination of gender identity
- **2019 Act:** self-identification; DM certification; **National Council for Transgender Persons**; penalties **6 months–2 years**; **32,424** ID cards issued
- **Census 2011:** ~**4.88 lakh** transgender persons — highest in **UP**, **Andhra Pradesh**, **Maharashtra**
- **World Bank:** inclusion could add **1.7%** to GDP; **Koovagam Festival**
- **Legal Services Authorities** give free legal aid to transgender persons with income below **₹2,00,000**
# 4. More facts
- **Introduced by** Union Minister **Virendra Kumar** (MoSJE, est. **1985**); identities listed — **Kinner**, **Hijra**, **Aravani**, **Jogappa**, intersex variations
- After recognition, gender can be changed in documents incl. **birth certificates**
