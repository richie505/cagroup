## India's updated NDC for 2035 | from: 2026-03-31, CA360-2026-03, OL-2026-03
# 1. News
- **Union Cabinet** approved India's updated **NDC**, to be communicated to the **UNFCCC**
- ⚠ **India and Argentina** were the only **G20** countries without a **2035 NDC** by end-2025
| Parameter | 2022 NDC | New NDC (2035) |
|---|---|---|
| **Non-fossil installed capacity** | **50% by 2030** | **60% by 2035** |
| **Emissions intensity cut (vs 2005)** | **45%** | **47%** |
| **Carbon sink** | **2.5–3 bn t CO₂e** | **3.5–4 bn t CO₂e** |
- Non-fossil share already ~**52.6%** (early 2026); ⚠ India uses **emissions intensity**, not absolute emissions
# 2. Emissions data (CREA / Carbon Brief)
- **CO₂ emissions rose only 0.7% in 2025** — slowest since **2001** (excluding 2020)
- **Power sector emissions** fell **3.8%** — coal power fell for first time outside Covid since **1973**
- **2025 additions:** **47 GW solar**, **6.3 GW wind**, **4 GW hydro**, **0.6 GW nuclear**
- **Steel** emissions +**8%**, **cement** +**10%**; **37+ GW** RE stranded
- **Plans:** **100 GW** new coal in 7 years; **$1 trillion** petrochemicals by **2040**; CEA — **786 GW** non-fossil by **2035-36** (~**70%**)
- **Forest cover** ~**24%** vs **33%** goal
# 3. Background
- **UNFCCC:** adopted **1992** (**Rio**), in force **1994**; **CBDR**; Annex I / Non-Annex I
- **Paris Agreement:** **COP21, 2015**; in force **2016**; well below **2°C**, preferably **1.5°C**; NDCs updated every **5 years**
- **INDCs** — plans before Paris; became NDCs on ratification
- **UNEP Emissions Gap Report 2025** titled **"Off Target"**; **WRI** — NDCs close <**14%** of gap; **COP28 UAE Consensus** — triple RE, double efficiency by 2030
# 4. CREA report
- Emissions growth **0.5%** in second half of 2025; coal use in thermal plants down **20%**; **CREA** est. **2019**, HQ **Helsinki**
# 5. CEA National Generation Adequacy Plan (2026–27 to 2035–36)
- Part of **20th Electric Power Survey**; capacity **520 GW (Jan 2026) → 1,121 GW by 2035–36**; peak demand **459 GW**; energy need **3,365 BU**
| Source (2035–36) | Capacity |
|---|---|
| **Solar** | **509 GW (45%)** |
| **Coal** | **315 GW (28%)** |
| **Wind** | **155 GW (14%)** |
| **Hydro** | **77 GW (7%)** |
| **Nuclear / Gas / Biomass / Small hydro** | **22 / 20 / 16 / 6 GW** |
- Non-fossil **786 GW** — ~**49%** of generation; **CEA** est. **1951**
# 6. Progress figures
- NDC covers **2031–2035**; emission intensity already cut **36%** (2005–2020); non-fossil share **52.57%** (Feb 2026); carbon sink **2.29 bn t** created by 2021
- **UNFCCC** secretariat HQ **Bonn**

## Amrabad Tiger Reserve turns Lantana into biocharcoal | from: CARE-2026-03
# 1. News
- **Pilot project** in **Amrabad Tiger Reserve (ATR)** converts invasive **Lantana camara** into **biocharcoal** — "**black gold**"
- **Lantana** occupies about **one-fourth** of the ATR forest
- **Method:** uprooted lantana covered with soil and burned in **low-oxygen** conditions; initial output ~**3 kg** biochar
- **Price:** ~**₹7,000 per tonne**; livelihoods through **Joint Forest Management Committees (JFMCs)**
# 2. About Lantana camara
- **Native** to **Central and South America**; spread globally in the **17th century** as an ornamental
- ⚠ **IUCN** — among the **world's worst invasive species**
- **Spreads by:** **allelopathy** (chemicals suppress nearby plants), bird/monkey seed dispersal, deep roots, **resin-coated leaves** (drought resistance); toxic to livestock
