## NavIC satellite IRNSS-1F loses atomic clock | from: 2026-03-16, CA360-2026-03, OL-2026-03
# 1. News
- **ISRO:** atomic clock on **IRNSS-1F** stopped on **13 March 2026** — no positioning data; continues **broadcast messaging**
- **IRNSS-1F** launched **March 2016**; completed **10-year** design life
- After **NVS-01 (2023)**, five satellites gave positioning: **IRNSS-1B, 1C, 1F, 1I, NVS-01**
# 2. NavIC basics
- **Official name:** **IRNSS**; planned **7-satellite** constellation; coverage India + **1,500 km**; accuracy ~**10 m**
- ⚠ Regional systems use **geosynchronous** orbits (~**35,000 km**); global systems **MEO** (~**20,000 km**)
- **New-generation (NVS):** **indigenous atomic clock**; **12-year** life; **L1**, **L5**, **S** bands — **L1** enables smartphones/wearables
# 3. Setbacks
- **NVS-02:** launched **January 2025** by **GSLV-F15** — ⚠ ISRO's **100th launch**; failed to reach final orbit — signal did not reach **pyro valve of oxidiser line** (connector disengagement)
- **IRNSS-1H (2017)** failed — heat shield did not open; **IRNSS-1A (2013)** almost defunct
- **CAG 2018:** user segment funding **₹200 crore (2006)**, work began only **2017**

|---|---|

- **Hit-to-kill**; **360° firing** via **Vertical Launch System** + rotating multi-function radar

## Gut microbiome and food systems | from: 2026-03-12
# 1. Gut microbiome facts
- **Trillions** of microbes (~equal to human cells); weight **1–2 kg** (like the brain)
- **2–20 million** microbial genes vs ~**20,000** human genes; ~**2,000** bacterial species, mostly in the **colon**
- ~**90%** beneficial — **Bacteroidetes**, **Firmicutes**; unique like a **fingerprint**
- **Dysbiosis** → "**leaky gut**", inflammation; **SCFAs** (short-chain fatty acids) reduce inflammation; synthesises **vitamin K** and some B vitamins; **gut–brain axis**
# 2. Disruptors
- **Ultra-processed foods (HFSS)**, low fibre; **emulsifiers**, **artificial sweeteners**, preservatives
- **Sugar** favours **E. coli**, **Candida**; reduces **Bifidobacteria**, **Lactobacilli**
- **Estrobolome** — gut bacteria regulating **estrogen** metabolism; disrupted by pesticides
- **Antibiotics in livestock** → **AMR**; ⚠ bacteria, viruses **and** fungi can all develop AMR
- **Fat** digested by **lipases**; proteins by trypsin/chymotrypsin; starch by amylase
