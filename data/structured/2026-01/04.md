## Sustainability in rice production | from: 2026-01-07, CA360-2026-01, OL-2026-01
# 1. News
- ⚠ **2025:** India overtook **China** as **world's largest rice producer**
- India supplies ~**40%** of global rice exports — over **20 million metric tonnes**
# 2. Facts
- Staple for ~**65%** of Indians; ~**25%** of total cropped area
- **Top states:** **Uttar Pradesh**, **Punjab**, **West Bengal**
- **Kharif crop:** temperature **>25°C**, rainfall **>100 cm**
- Transplanted paddy: **25–27 irrigations**/season; **1 kg rice = 3,000–4,000 litres** water
- Punjab–Haryana water table fell **~30 ft → 80–200 ft**; extraction exceeds recharge by **35–57%**
- Rice = **10–20%** of India's agricultural GHG emissions (methane); Punjab spends **₹39,000/ha** on power and fertiliser subsidies
- Climate change may cut yields **6–10%**
# 3. Solutions
- **DSR** (Direct Seeded Rice), **AWD** (Alternate Wetting and Drying — cuts methane), **SRI**, micro-irrigation under **PMKSY**
- **ICAR NICRA** programme; fortified rice under **NFSA** and **PM-POSHAN**
- ⚠ Flooded transplanted paddy **increases** methane (anaerobic)
# 4. Official figures
- Announced by **Shivraj Singh Chouhan**: India **150.18 million tonnes** vs China **145.28 million tonnes**
- **184** improved varieties of **25** crops by **ICAR** released — **122** cereals, **6** pulses, **13** oilseeds, **11** fodder, **6** sugarcane, **24** cotton–jute, **1** tobacco

## Seafood exports amid US tariffs | from: 2026-01-07, CA360-2026-01, OL-2026-01
# 1. News
- **Apr–Oct 2025:** marine exports up **16%** in value, **12%** in volume — **$4.87 billion** (from $4.19 bn); volume **9.62 → 10.73 lakh MT**
- **Apr–Nov 2025:** up **16%+** to **$5.75 billion**
- **US duties on Indian shrimp:** nearly **60%** (anti-dumping + countervailing + additional) — exports to US down **4%** value, **11%** quantity
- ⚠ **US** still India's **single largest** seafood market
- **Diversification:** **China** **$845.67 million**; **Vietnam** 100%+ growth; **Belgium** ~90%
- **Main item:** frozen **Vannamei shrimp**
# 2. Background
- India — **3rd largest fish producer**, **2nd largest aquaculture producer**; coastline **7,500 km+**
- **MPEDA** — export promotion; **PMMSY** — infrastructure
- **ReALCRaft:** web-based platform for online **registration and licensing of fishing vessels**
- Richest fishing grounds — where **warm and cold currents meet** (Grand Banks; Kuroshio–Oyashio)
# 3. Fisheries decade (Ministry data)
- Fish production **95.79 lakh t (2013–14) → 197.75 lakh t (2024–25)** — up **106%**
- India **2nd** largest fish producer (~**8%** of world); **1st** in **shrimp** production and exports; **2nd** in aquaculture and capture
- Aquaculture productivity **4.77 t/ha**; seafood exports 2024–25 **16.98 lakh t**, record **₹62,408 crore** (+3.11%)
- Value-added exports **₹4,863.40 → ₹7,589.93 crore** (+56%, 5 yrs) — ~**11%** of fisheries exports

- **Karna-18 (Co-18022):** sugarcane by **Sugarcane Breeding Institute Regional Centre, Karnal** — salinity and drought tolerant; resists **red rot**, **top borer**; for NW India
- **Root wilt of coconut:** caused by **phytoplasma** (phloem); Kerala, TN, Karnataka; first identified ~**150 years** ago at **Erattupetta (Kerala)**; reduces yield, does not kill tree
