## India–EU Free Trade Agreement concluded | from: 2026-01-29, CA360-2026-01, OL-2026-01
# 1. News
- India and **EU** concluded negotiations on a comprehensive **FTA** — ⚠ EU is India's **22nd FTA partner**
- **Next:** legal scrubbing, translation, ratification by **27 member states** and **European Parliament**
# 2. Commitments
| Item | EU | India |
|---|---|---|
| **Tariff lines liberalised** | **97%** | **92.1%** |
| **Share of partner's exports covered** | **99.5%** of Indian exports | **97.5%** of EU exports |
| **Services subsectors** | **144** | **102** |
- **Labour-intensive gains:** textiles, apparel, leather, footwear, marine, gems & jewellery, toys, sports goods — current tariffs **4–26%**; ~**USD 33 billion** exports become duty-free
- **India protected:** **dairy**, **cereals**, **poultry**, **soymeal**; gradual for **automobiles**, **wines and spirits**
- **Rules of origin:** self-certification via **Statements of Origin**; flexibility for shrimps, downstream aluminium
- **IPR:** **TRIPS** compliance; safeguards for generics, **TKDL**
- **Combined:** ~**25% of global GDP**, **one-third** of global trade (India **4th**, EU **2nd** largest economy)
# 3. Concerns
- **CBAM** (steel, aluminium, cement, chemicals) — Indian steel may face **20–35%** cost rise from **2026**
- **EUDR** (deforestation after **2020**); **CSDDD** (from **2027**); EU objections to India's **QCOs**
- India avg tariff **10–12%** vs EU **3–4%**
# 4. Background
- **EU:** **ECSC 1951** → **Treaties of Rome 1957** → **Maastricht 1992** → **Brexit 2020** (27 members)
- **Eurozone:** **20** members; **Bulgaria** to join in **2026**; **Schengen** includes **4** non-EU states
- **India–EU:** relations **1962**; Strategic Partnership **2004**; summits since **2000 (Lisbon)**
- **Trade:** EU largest goods partner — **USD 135 billion (FY24)**; services **USD 53 billion**; EU FDI **USD 117 billion+**
- **Other:** **Operation Atalanta** (EUNAVFOR); EU joined **IPOI (2023)**; **CECP (2016)**; **IMEC**; ISRO launched ESA's **Proba-3 (2024)**; India associate member of **CERN**
- **EU Stability and Growth Pact** — limits budget deficits
# 5. More details
- Concluded after summit in New Delhi — **Ursula von der Leyen**: "**mother of all deals**"; **António Costa** present; **5-year** strategic action plan
- **Car tariffs:** **70–110% → 40% → 10%** in phases; wine **20–30%**, spirits **40%**, beer **50%**; **olive oil** duty eliminated
- EU to cut tariffs on **99.5%** of Indian goods over **seven years**
- Talks began **2007**; revived after **2022**; bilateral trade ~**USD 190 billion (2024–25)**; EU ~**17%** of India's exports; EU FDI **USD 117.4 billion (2020–24)**
