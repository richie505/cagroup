## UGC (Promotion of Equity in HEIs) Regulations, 2026 | from: 2026-01-23
# 1. News
- **UGC** notified new regulations against **caste-based discrimination** in HEIs — replacing **2012** regulations
- **Covers:** **SCs, STs and OBCs** (OBCs were excluded in draft) — aligns with **Articles 15(4), 15(5)**
# 2. Provisions
- **Equal Opportunity Centre (EOC):** mandatory in every HEI; **bi-annual** reports
- **Equity Committee:** under each EOC, chaired by **Head of Institution**; SC, ST, OBC, PwD, women members; meets at least **twice a year**
- **Annual equity compliance report** to UGC; Head personally responsible
- **National monitoring committee** by UGC
- **Penalties:** debar from UGC schemes, bar degree/online/distance programmes, remove from UGC list
- **Dropped from draft:** fine on students for "**false complaints**", OBC exclusion
- **Gaps:** no explicit admission-stage discrimination; ban on "separate educational systems" (2012) removed
- **Evidence cited:** **IIT Delhi (2019)** — **75%** of marginalised students faced discrimination; **Thorat Committee (2007)**
# 3. Background — UGC
| Year | Event |
|---|---|
| **1944** | **Sargeant Report** — recommended University Grants Committee |
| **1945** | Committee for **Aligarh, Banaras, Delhi** universities |
| **1948** | **University Education Commission** — **Dr. S. Radhakrishnan** |
| **1952** | UGC established |
| **1953** | inaugurated by **Maulana Abul Kalam Azad** |
| **1956** | statutory body — **UGC Act, 1956** |

## Malaria — Asia-Pacific and World Malaria Report 2025 | from: 2026-01-23
# 1. News
- **World Malaria Report 2025** (WHO, December) — Asia-Pacific a bright spot
- **Asia-Pacific:** **10 of 17** endemic countries declined; cases **9.6 million (2023) → 8.9 million (2024)**; big drop in **Pakistan**
- Historic lows 2nd year in **Cambodia**, **Lao PDR**, **Vietnam**; **Greater Mekong Subregion** tackled drug resistance
- **Financing:** only **42%** of global needs met in **2024**
- **India target:** **zero indigenous cases by 2027** (global **2030**); currently **off track**
- **Five States + Northeast** = ~**80%** of India's burden
- ⚠ **Artemisinin resistance not yet established in India**; oral artemisinin **monotherapy banned**
# 2. Background
- **Cause:** protozoan **Plasmodium**; vector — **female Anopheles**
- **Species:** **P. falciparum** (deadliest), **P. vivax**, **P. malariae**, **P. ovale**; India mainly falciparum and vivax
- **Incubation:** **10–14 days**; liver (pre-erythrocytic) then RBC stage
- **Treatment:** **ACT**; **vaccines:** **RTS,S** and **R21** (prioritised for Africa)
- ⚠ TB is caused by **Mycobacterium**, malaria by **Plasmodium**

## Kyasanur Forest Disease (monkey fever) | from: APCA-2026-01
# 1. News
- **29-year-old** man in **Karnataka** died of **KFD**
# 2. Facts
- Tick-borne viral haemorrhagic disease; first identified **1957**, **Kyasanur Forest, Karnataka**
- **Virus:** family **Flaviviridae**, genus **Flavivirus**; vector — hard tick **Haemaphysalis spinigera**
- ⚠ **No human-to-human transmission**; season **Oct–Nov** start, peak **Jan–April**
- Fatality **5–10%**; no antiviral; **vaccine available**
