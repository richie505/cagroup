## Delimitation after Census 2027 | from: 2026-01-28
# 1. News
- Freeze on inter-State Lok Sabha seat distribution ends after **Census 2027** — first redistribution since **1976**
# 2. Background
- **1976 (42nd Amendment):** seats frozen at **1971** population
- **84th Amendment (2001):** freeze extended till first Census after **2026**; allowed internal boundary readjustment (2001 Census)
- **2002–08 Delimitation Commission:** only redrew internal boundaries
- Representation still reflects **548 million** people vs today's **1.47 billion**
- ⚠ **Four** Delimitation Commissions constituted (till Dec 2023)
# 3. Projections (Lok Sabha ~888 seats)
| State | Now → projected | Share |
|---|---|---|
| **Uttar Pradesh** | **80 → ~151** | — |
| **Bihar** | **40 → ~82** | UP+Bihar over **26%** |
| **Tamil Nadu** | **39 → ~53** | **7.2% → 6.0%** |
| **Kerala** | **20 → ~23** | **3.7% → 2.6%** |
# 4. Options
- Extend freeze (Article **14** risk); expand Lok Sabha to **750–900**; weighted formula (population **70–80%** + development **20–30%**); strengthen **Rajya Sabha** (15/10/5 seats model); bifurcate UP; phased — half in **2034**, rest **2039**

## Governor's address to State Legislature — controversy | from: 2026-01-30, CA360-2026-01, OL-2026-01
# 1. News
- Governors in Opposition-ruled States walked out / omitted parts of cabinet-approved addresses
- **Tamil Nadu:** portions skipped **2022, 2023**; since **2024** no Article 176 address
- **Kerala:** paragraphs omitted; **Karnataka:** brief statement then exited joint session
# 2. Provisions
| Article | Content |
|---|---|
| **176** | **mandatory** address — first session after general election and first session of every year; **Motion of Thanks** |
| **175** | **discretionary** address/messages to House(s) |
| **163** | aid and advice of Council of Ministers |
| **159** | Governor's oath |
- **Origin:** **Section 63, GoI Act 1935**; convention from **April 1937** (provincial autonomy)
# 3. Judgments and commissions
| Case/Body | Point |
|---|---|
| **Shamsher Singh (1974)** | **7-judge** bench — Governor is constitutional head, acts on aid and advice |
| **Nabam Rebia (2016)** | **5-judge** bench — Arts 175/176 exercised on ministerial advice |
| **State of TN v. Governor of TN (2024)** | discretion cannot stall elected government |
| **Sarkaria Commission (1988)** | Governor — "lynchpin of cooperative federalism" |
| **Punchhi Commission (2007)** | limit extra roles like Chancellor |
# 4. Background
- **Governor:** minimum age **35**; cannot be MP/MLA; office of profit disqualifies; dual role — head of State and Union representative
- **Discretionary:** report for President's Rule (**Art 356**), reserving Bills (**Art 200**)
- **Article 174:** summoning, proroguing, dissolving — only on cabinet advice; **Nabam Rebia (2016)** — no independent discretion under Art 174
- **Rajasthan HC (1966)** and **Syed Habibullah** cases — procedural defects in delivery do not invalidate proceedings

## Passive euthanasia — Harish Rana case | from: 2026-01-20
# 1. News
- **Supreme Court** reserved judgment (**January 2026**) in **Harish Rana vs Union of India (2025)** — plea to withdraw life support
- Petitioner in **permanent vegetative state** for **13 years** (since **2013** fall), **100% quadriplegic**; medical boards recommended withdrawal
# 2. Law
- **Active euthanasia:** illegal; **passive:** legal under guidelines
- **BNS 2023:** Sec **100** culpable homicide, **101** murder, **108** abetment of suicide
- **Article 21** includes **right to die with dignity**
| Case | Contribution |
|---|---|
| **Maruti Dubal (1987)** | right to die recognised (HC) |
| **Gian Kaur (1996)** | right to die rejected |
| **Aruna Shanbaug (2011)** | passive euthanasia permitted |
| **Common Cause (2018)** | **living wills** recognised |
| **SC simplification (2023)** | faster process |
- **2023 reforms:** two boards (primary — hospital; secondary — district); experience **20 → 5 years**; **48-hour** deadline; board of **3 members**
- **Abroad:** Netherlands, Belgium — active & assisted; Switzerland — assisted suicide
