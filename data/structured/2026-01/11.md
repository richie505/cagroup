## Railways drops bandhgala uniform | from: 2026-01-19
# 1. News
- Railways Minister **Ashwini Vaishnaw:** railway staff to stop wearing **bandhgala** — called it a "**colonial relic**"
# 2. Background
- **Origin:** princely state of **Jodhpur (Marwar)** under **Rathore dynasty** — aka **Jodhpuri jacket**, **Prince suit**
- **Roots:** Mughal **Jama**, **Angrakha**; Rajput **Bago**, **Dagali**, **Gudadi**; immediate precursor **Achkan**
- **Polo:** jacket with breeches became "**Jodhpurs**"; refined by **Savile Row** tailors
- **Revival (1990s):** **Raghavendra Singh Rathore**
- ⚠ Historically **indigenous**, not colonial
