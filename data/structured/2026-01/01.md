## Kakinada green ammonia project — AM Green | from: 2026-01-19, APCA-2026-01, CA360-2026-01, OL-2026-01
# 1. News
- **Foundation stone:** CM **N. Chandrababu Naidu** and Deputy CM **Pawan Kalyan** at **Kakinada**
- ⚠ **India's first** and **world's largest** green ammonia project
- **Developer:** **AM Green (Greenko Group)**
# 2. Numbers
| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Investment** | **₹18,000 crore** (~**$2 billion**) initially; ecosystem **$10 billion** |
| **Area** | **495 acres** |
| **Capacity** | **1.5 MTPA** green ammonia |
| **Phasing** | **0.5 MTPA (2027)**, **1.0 MTPA (2028)**, **1.5 MTPA (2030)** |
| **Electrolyser** | **1,950 MW** |
| **Renewables** | **7.5 GW** solar + wind; **2 GW** round-the-clock power with **pumped hydro storage** |
| **Jobs** | **8,000** during construction |
- **Exports to:** **Germany**, **Japan**, **Singapore**; key offtaker **Uniper (Germany)** — ⚠ first green energy export from India to **Europe**
- **Backers:** **Petronas (Malaysia)**, **GIC (Singapore)**, **ADIA (UAE)**
- **Brownfield:** conversion of **Nagarjuna Fertilisers** plant
- **Policy:** **Integrated Clean Energy (ICE) Policy, 2024**
# 3. More details
- **Foundation date:** **17 January 2026**; final investment decision done; offtake with **Uniper**
- **Target:** **5 million tonnes** green ammonia by **2032** — ~**20%** of **National Green Hydrogen Mission** targets
- Part of **WEF Transitioning Industrial Clusters** initiative
- **AM Green–Uniper agreement:** documents exchanged in **Ahmedabad** (Jan 2026) before PM and **German Chancellor** — **5 lakh tonnes/yr** from **2028**, from the **1 MTPA** Kakinada plant; meets EU **RFNBO** standard
- ⚠ CA360 calls it **AP's largest** green-hydrogen-based ammonia project and the Uniper supply plant **1 MTPA**; CARE daily calls it **India's first and world's largest** at **1.5 MTPA** (phased)

## Section 22-A — five land categories removed from prohibited list | from: 2026-01-02, APCA-2026-01
# 1. News
- **Decision:** AP **Revenue Department** removed **five categories** of land from the prohibited list under **Section 22-A of the Registration Act**
- **Directive:** CM **N. Chandrababu Naidu**; implementation led by Revenue Minister **Anagani Satyaprasad**
# 2. Categories removed
- Lands allotted to **former and serving defence personnel**
- Lands granted to **freedom fighters**
- Lands allotted to **political victims**
- Assignments made prior to **18 June 1954**
- **Private patta lands**
- In partially disputed survey numbers — only the **disputed portion** stays prohibited
# 3. Pending (Group of Ministers to decide)
- **Conditional patta lands**, **service inam lands**, certain **dotted lands**, **freehold lands** (within two months)
- ⚠ **Conditional patta lands** were **not** removed — still under consideration
# 4. Other reforms
- **Single-document sufficiency:** old revenue registers, assignment records, Record of Holdings, registration documents, **DKT pattas**
- **Tech:** resurvey, digitisation, **auto-mutation** of pattadar passbooks, **AI and Blockchain**
- **Collectors** empowered to cancel fake/double registrations
- **21.8 lakh** new **pattadar passbooks** distributed **1–9 January 2026**
- ⚠ **2026** declared "**Year of Land Reforms**" in AP
# 5. Background
- **Section 22-A:** empowers State to prohibit registration of certain lands to prevent illegal transfers

- Disaster losses ~**₹2.25 lakh crore** in past **30 years**
