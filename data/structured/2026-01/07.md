## Paris Agreement — 10 years | from: 2026-01-07
# 1. News
- **Completed 10 years** in **November 2025**; re-evaluation after **US exit (early 2025)** and **COP30 (Belém, Brazil)**
# 2. Background
- **Adopted:** **2015**, **COP21 Paris**, under **UNFCCC**; legally binding framework, **voluntary** national targets
- **Goal:** well below **2°C**, preferably **1.5°C**
- **NDCs** every **5 years**; **Global Stocktake** every 5 years — first at **COP28 (2023)**
- **Paris Rulebook:** **COP24 (Katowice)** and **COP26 (Glasgow)**
- **Parties:** **194 countries + EU**
- **Finance:** **USD 100 billion/yr** till 2025; **NCQG (COP29, 2024)** — at least **USD 300 billion/yr by 2035**; Global South demands **USD 1.3 trillion** (≥ **USD 600 billion** grants)
- **Current NDCs** → **2.5–2.9°C** warming
- ⚠ **Kyoto Protocol** applied only to developed nations (top-down); Paris is **bottom-up**
# 3. India
- **Targets:** **45%** cut in emissions intensity (from **2005**) by **2030**; ~**50%** non-fossil capacity by 2030; carbon sink **2.5–3 billion tonnes CO₂**; **Net Zero 2070**
- ⚠ **50% non-fossil electricity capacity achieved in 2025** — **5 years early**
- **China:** peak before **2030**, net zero **2060**

## Bomb cyclone hits northern US | from: 2026-01-01
# 1. News
- **Date:** **29 December 2025** — northern **United States** (Midwest, East Coast)
- **Impact:** **27,000+** power outages (over one-third in **Michigan**); **9,000+** flight delays; **889** cancellations
# 2. Concept
- **Bomb cyclone:** intense **mid-latitude (extra-tropical)** storm
- **Definition:** central pressure drops by at least **24 millibars in 24 hours** — process called **bombogenesis**
- **Formation:** cold **Arctic/Canadian** air meets warm moist air (often over oceans)
- **Season:** any season; most frequent in **fall and winter**
- **Where:** mostly **western North Atlantic** (energy from **Gulf Stream**); also **NW Pacific**, occasionally **Mediterranean**
- **Study:** **Northern Illinois University (2021)** — ~**7%** of non-tropical lows near North America (**1979–2019**) were bomb cyclones; avg **18 per year**
- **Hazards:** winds **80 kmph+**, blizzards, lakeshore flooding, **thundersnow** (snow with lightning)
| Aspect | Bomb cyclone | Hurricane |
|---|---|---|
| **Type** | Mid-latitude (extra-tropical) | Tropical cyclone |
| **Energy** | Temperature contrast | Warm ocean waters |
| **Season** | Fall and winter | Summer, early autumn |
| **Core** | **Cold-core** | **Warm-core** |
- ⚠ Bomb cyclones are **not** powered by warm ocean water (that is hurricanes)
- ⚠ Eye of a tropical cyclone is **warmer**, not colder, than surroundings
