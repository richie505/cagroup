## India–US trade deal — US tariff cut to 18% | from: 2026-02-05, 2026-02-11
# 1. Deal
- **US tariff on Indian goods:** reciprocal tariff **25% → 18%**; additional **25% punitive duty** (August **2025**, over **Russian oil** purchases) **removed**
- **Effective burden:** **~50% → 18%** — lower than **Vietnam/Bangladesh (~20%)** and **China (30–35%)**
- **India's expected commitments:** cut Russian crude, buy from **US** (and possibly **Venezuela**); lower tariffs/NTBs; purchases up to **USD 500 billion** of US energy, agriculture, coal, technology
- ⚠ India has **not** regained **GSP** (withdrawn in **2019**)
# 2. Background
- **Pre-deal steps by India:** duty cuts on heavy motorcycles, bourbon whisky; **SHANTI Act, 2025** (Sustainable Harnessing and Advancement of Nuclear Energy for Transforming India) — opened nuclear sector to private players
- **Bilateral trade FY25:** **USD 132 billion** (FY24 **119.71 bn**); India's surplus **USD 40.82 billion**
- **US FDI in India:** **3rd largest** investor — **USD 70.65 billion** (2000–2025)
- **US–India COMPACT (2025):** Catalyzing Opportunities for Military Partnership, Accelerated Commerce & Technology
- **Mission 500:** bilateral trade **USD 500 billion by 2030**; **BTA** under negotiation
- India's energy import dependence **88.2%** (FY25); tech cooperation under **iCET**
# 3. Bilateral Trade Agreement — details
- **Access:** preferential access to a **USD 30-trillion** US market
- **What India gets:** **18%** rate on **USD 900 bn** of US imports; **zero duty** on **USD 150 bn**; no additional duty on **USD 720 bn**; continued exemption on **USD 350 bn**; preferential treatment on **Section 232** tariffs
- **Reciprocal relief:** exports of **USD 40.96 bn** faced 50% — **USD 30.94 bn** cut to **18%**, **USD 10.03 bn** cut to **zero**
- **Section 232 (national security tariffs):** zero reciprocal duty for **USD 28.30 bn** of exports
- **Zero-duty industrial exports:** **USD 38 billion** — aircraft parts, generic drugs, elementary auto parts, diamonds, platinum
| Sector | Tariff | US market |
|---|---|---|
| **Textiles & apparel** | **50% → 18%**; ⚠ **silk 0%** | **USD 113 bn** |
| **Leather & footwear** | **50% → 18%** | **USD 42 bn** |
| **Gems & jewellery** | 18%; **0%** diamonds, platinum, coins | **USD 29 bn** (0% segment) |
| **Home décor** | 18%; 0% seats, chandeliers | **USD 52 bn** |
| **Toys** | **18%** | **USD 18 bn** |
| **Machinery** | **18%** | **USD 477 bn** (India exports **USD 2.35 bn**) |
- **Competitors' tariffs:** **China 35%**, **Vietnam 20%**, **Bangladesh 20%**, **Malaysia 19%**
# 4. Agriculture
- India's agri surplus with US **USD 1.3 bn** (exports **3.4 bn**, imports **2.1 bn**)
- **USD 1.36 bn** agri exports face zero additional duty (spices, tea, coffee, cashew, mango, fruit pulp); **USD 1.035 bn** assured zero reciprocal tariff
- ⚠ **Fully protected:** **dairy, poultry, meat, GM foods, millets, maize, cereals, pulses, oilseeds**, honey, ethanol, tobacco
- Tools: phased elimination up to **10 years**; **Tariff Rate Quotas** (⚠ quantity-limited, not unlimited)
- **Digital trade:** global digitally delivered services **USD 4.78 trillion (2024)**; India's digital exports **USD 0.28 trillion** (+10.3%); India **5th** in exports, **11th** in imports
