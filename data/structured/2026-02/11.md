## Lesser-known Hoysala monuments of Karnataka | from: 2026-02-24
# 1. Hoysala dynasty
- **Period:** **11th–14th century CE**; feudatories of **Western Chalukyas of Kalyana**; founder **Nripa Kama II**
- **Capitals:** **Belur (Velapuri)** → **Dwarasamudra (Halebidu)**; territory included parts of **Tamil Nadu**
- **Rulers:** **Vishnuvardhana (Bittideva)** — Jain, converted to Vaishnavism under **Ramanuja**; **Veera Ballala II** (peak); Veera Ballala III; **Vira Someshwara**
- **Style:** **Vesara** (Nagara + Dravida); material **chloritic schist (soapstone)**; **stellate (star-shaped) jagati**
- **Plans:** Ekakuta, Dvikuta, **Trikuta**, Chatushkuta/**Panchakuta**
# 2. Monuments
| Monument | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Nageshwara & Govindeshwara, Koravangala** | **c. 1160 CE** |
| **Bucheshwara, Koravangala** | **1173 CE** — honour of Veera Ballala II |
| **Lakshminarasimha, Javagal** | **c. 1250 CE**, trikuta — Vira Someshwara |
| **Lakshmidevi, Doddagaddavalli** | **1114 CE** — merchant **Sahaja Devi**; four shrines (Lakshmi, Shiva, Vishnu, Kali) |
