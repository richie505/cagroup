=== TOPIC 2026-02:04 | Economy, Banking & Budget

## Union Budget 2026–27 — key announcements | from: 2026-02-02, 2026-02-20, CA360-2026-02, OL-2026-02
# 1. Basics
- **Presented by:** FM **Nirmala Sitharaman** — a **"Yuva Shakti-driven"** Budget
- ⚠ **First** Union Budget prepared in **Kartavya Bhawan**
- **Three Kartavya (duties):** (1) accelerate and sustain **economic growth**; (2) fulfil **aspirations** and build capacity; (3) **Sabka Sath, Sabka Vikas** — access for every family, community, region, sector
- **Reforms since Independence Day 2025:** **350+** reforms — GST simplification, **Labour Codes** notified, QCO rationalisation
- **Six intervention areas (Kartavya 1):** manufacturing in **7** strategic/frontier sectors; legacy industries; **Champion MSMEs**; infrastructure; energy security; **City Economic Regions**
# 2. Fiscal numbers
| Item | RE 2025–26 | BE 2026–27 |
|---|---|---|
| **Fiscal deficit (% GDP)** | **4.4%** | **4.3%** |
| **Debt-to-GDP** | **56.1%** | **55.6%** |
| **Public capex** | **₹11.2 lakh cr** (BE 2025–26) | **₹12.2 lakh cr** |
- **Capex 2014–15:** **₹2 lakh crore**
# 3. Manufacturing and industry
- **Biopharma Shakti:** outlay **₹10,000 crore** over **5 years** — biologics and biosimilars; **3 new NIPERs**, **7 NIPERs upgraded**; **1,000+** accredited clinical trial sites; strengthen **CDSCO**
- **Integrated Textile Programme — 5 parts:** **National Fibre Scheme** (silk, wool, jute, man-made, new-age fibres); **Textile Expansion and Employment Scheme**; **National Handloom and Handicraft Programme**; **Textile Ecology Initiative**; **Samarth 2.0** (skilling)
- **SME Growth Fund:** **₹10,000 crore** — to create future champion enterprises
# 4. Infrastructure
- **Dedicated Freight Corridor:** **Dankuni** (east) to **Surat** (west)
- **20 new National Waterways** over 5 years — starting with **NW-5 in Odisha** (Talcher, Angul, Kalinga Nagar to **Paradeep** and **Dhamra** ports)
- **City Economic Regions:** **₹5,000 crore per CER** over **5 years** — challenge mode, reform-cum-results financing
- **7 high-speed rail corridors:** Mumbai–Pune, Pune–Hyderabad, **Hyderabad–Bengaluru**, **Hyderabad–Chennai**, Chennai–Bengaluru, Delhi–Varanasi, Varanasi–Siliguri
# 5. Social and skilling (Kartavya 2)
- **~25 crore** people exited multidimensional poverty
- **5 Regional Medical Hubs** with private sector (incl. AYUSH, Medical Value Tourism Facilitation Centres)
- **Veterinary:** loan-linked capital subsidy — **20,000+** more veterinary professionals
- **Creative economy:** support to **Indian Institute of Creative Technologies, Mumbai**; **AVGC Content Creator Labs** in **15,000 secondary schools** and **500 colleges**
- **Girls' hostel** in **every district** (STEM higher education) — VGF and capital assistance
- **National Institute of Hospitality** — by upgrading **NCHMCT**; pilot to upskill **10,000 tourist guides** at **20 sites**, **12-week** course with an **IIM**
- **Khelo India Mission** — transform sports over next decade
# 6. Kartavya 3 — Sabka Sath, Sabka Vikas
- **Bharat Vistaar:** multilingual **digital tool** for farmers — agri portals + **ICAR** practices
- **Self-Help Entrepreneur (SHE) Marts:** community-owned retail outlets at cluster-level federations
- **Mental health:** **second NIMHANS**; institutes at **Ranchi** and **Tezpur** upgraded as regional apex institutions
- **Purvodaya / NE:** **East Coast Industrial Corridor** with node at **Durgapur**; **5 tourism destinations** in Purvodaya states; **4,000 e-buses**; **Buddhist circuits** in Arunachal, Sikkim, Assam, Manipur, Mizoram, Tripura
# 7. Taxation
- **New Income Tax Act, 2025** effective **April 2026**
- **TCS cut to 2%:** overseas tour packages; education and medical remittances under **LRS**
- **One-time 6-month foreign asset disclosure scheme**; decriminalisation of minor tax offences
- **Cooperatives:** deduction extended to **cattle feed** and **cotton seed**; **3-year exemption** for notified national cooperative federations
- **IT services:** single category; common **safe harbour margin 15.5%**; threshold **₹2,000 crore**
- **Tax holiday till 2047** for **foreign cloud service providers** using Indian data centres
- **5-year income-tax exemption** for global experts under notified schemes; MAT exemption for non-residents on presumptive taxation
- **Customs:** duty exemption on lithium-ion battery capital goods, critical-mineral processing equipment, **17 medicines**; tariff on **personal imports 20% → 10%**; **value cap on courier exports removed**
# 8. Background
- ⚠ **Macro-Economic Framework Statement** is mandated by the **FRBM Act, 2003** (not a constitutional article)
- **Vote-on-account** deals only with expenditure; **interim budget** has both expenditure and receipts
# 9. Spending-share analysis (CMIE data)
- Union Budget size grew **10.4%** a year on average since **2009**
- **Roads + Railways** share: **<4% (2009) → ~11%** — largest post-pandemic increase
- **MSME** share only **0.24%** — though MSMEs give **~30% of GDP**, **45%+** of exports
- **Interest payments:** about **1 in every 4 rupees** spent
- **Skill Development Ministry** share **0.06% → 0.05%**; health share declined after peak in **2017–18**; tourism share nearly vanished
- **J.K. Galbraith:** governments overinvest in things, underinvest in people

# 11. More industry and infrastructure measures
- **India Semiconductor Mission 2.0**; electronics components scheme raised to **₹40,000 crore**
- **Rare earth corridors** in **Odisha, Kerala, Andhra Pradesh, Tamil Nadu**; **3** chemical parks; **2** high-tech tool rooms via CPSEs
- **Container manufacturing scheme** — **₹10,000 crore+** over 5 years; revival of **200 legacy industrial clusters**
- **Atmanirbhar Bharat Fund** + **₹2,000 crore**; **"Corporate Mitras"** in Tier-2/3 towns
- **Infrastructure Risk Guarantee Fund**; **REITs** for CPSE real estate; river-vessel repair at **Varanasi** and **Patna**
- **Coastal cargo share** target **12% by 2047**; **CCUS** — **₹20,000 crore** over 5 years
- High-level committee on banking; restructuring of **PFC** and **REC**; **₹100 crore** incentive for **municipal bonds**
- Khadi/handloom via **Mahatma Gandhi Gram Swaraj** programme; mega textile parks
# 12. More tax and customs measures
- **MACT interest** exempt from income tax (no TDS); TCS on overseas tours **2–20% → 2%**; LRS education/medical **5% → 2%**
- Revised return deadline **31 December → 31 March**; updated returns allowed after reassessment with **10%** extra tax
- Foreign non-immovable assets below **₹20 lakh** — immunity from prosecution retrospectively from **1 October 2024**
- **IT safe harbour** threshold **₹300 crore → ₹2,000 crore**; data-centre related-party safe harbour **15%**; bonded warehousing margin **2%** (effective tax ~0.7%)
- **Buybacks** taxed as capital gains — effective **22%** corporate promoters, **30%** non-corporate
- **STT:** futures **0.02% → 0.05%**; options **0.15%**
- **MAT** becomes final tax from **1 April 2026** — rate **15% → 14%**; set-off capped at one-fourth
- TCS on tendu leaves **5% → 2%**; liquor, scrap, minerals **2%**
- **ICDS** to merge into **Ind AS**; separate ICDS removed from **2027–28**
- **Customs:** seafood duty-free inputs **1% → 3%** of FOB; nuclear project import exemption extended to **2035**; BCD exemption on **sodium antimonate** (solar glass); **7** more rare-disease drugs for personal import
- **AEO Tier-2/3** duty deferment **15 → 30 days**; Advance Rulings validity **3 → 5 years**; **₹10 lakh courier export cap removed**; fish caught in EEZ/high seas duty-free

## Civil aviation sector stress and UDAN | from: 2026-02-11
# 1. Sector
- India — **third-largest domestic aviation market**; **840+** aircraft, **350+ million** passengers/year; demand **715 million** by **2030**
- **Events:** **June 2025 Ahmedabad crash**; **December 2025 IndiGo** mass disruptions (revised **FDTL** norms)
- **IndiGo:** **360+** aircraft, **~5,038** pilots — **~14 pilots/aircraft** vs global **18–20**
- **Pilot need:** **7,000** (2024–26); **25,000–30,000** next decade; only **~5,700 CPLs** issued 2020–24
- **Market share:** **IndiGo ~63–65%**, **Air India group ~27–28%** — together **~90%**; IndiGo sole carrier on **~60%** of routes
- **DGCA:** **19** safety violation notices by late 2025; nearly half technical posts vacant
