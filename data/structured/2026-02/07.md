# 3. Schemes and measures
- **MISHTI:** restore **540 sq km** mangroves; **~4.5 million tonnes** sequestration potential
- **Amrit Dharohar (2023):** livelihood + eco-tourism; first-phase sites **Sultanpur, Sirpur, Yashwant Sagar, Bhitarkanika, Chilika**; training **ALP** and **Paryatan Navik Certificate**
- **Wetland City Accreditation:** ⚠ **Indore** and **Udaipur** — India's first WCA cities (**January 2025**)
- **Mission Sahbhagita / Wetland Mitras:** **2 million** sensitised; **80,000** wetlands surveyed; **6,200** health cards; **18,000** Wetland Mitras

- **SC ground-truthing:** wetlands below **2.25 ha**; **1,70,000** ground-truthed, **1,00,000** demarcated; **2017** SC penalty **₹50,000** for no inventories
- **Revised NPCA guidelines:** catchment-scale management
- **Blue carbon:** mangroves/peatlands sequester **10–50 times** faster than forests
- **Losses:** India lost nearly **one-third** of wetlands in three decades; India on **Central Asian Flyway**

## NGT upholds Great Nicobar Island Project clearance | from: 2026-02-18, CA360-2026-02, OL-2026-02
# 1. Verdict
- **NGT** upheld environmental clearance for **₹80,000–81,000 crore** **Great Nicobar Island Project** — "adequate safeguards"
# 2. Project
- **Conceived by:** **NITI Aayog**; launched **2021**; aligned with **Maritime Vision 2030**, **Amrit Kaal Vision 2047**
- **Sites:** **Galathea Bay**, Pemmaya Bay, Nanjappa Bay — near-equidistant from **Colombo**, **Port Klang**, **Singapore**
- **Components:** **International Container Transshipment Terminal (ICTT)**; greenfield **international airport** (dual-use); township; **gas and solar** power plant
- **Tribal safeguards:** **AAJVS**, Ministry of Tribal Affairs; **Jarawa Policy (2004)**, **Shompen Policy (2015)**; **NCST** under **Article 338A(9)**
- **8 wildlife corridors**; compensatory afforestation in **Haryana** (and MP); island forest cover **75%+**
# 3. Concerns
- **~130 sq km** rainforest (**~15%** of island); **10 million+** trees; **leatherback turtle** nesting at Galathea Bay (sanctuary denotified)
- **~700 ha** allegedly in prohibited zones under **ICRZ Notification 2019**
- **Shekhar Singh Commission (2002)** — ban on felling in tribal reserves/NPs
- Strategic: near **Malacca, Sunda, Lombok** straits; **Sabang** port (Indonesia); **Kra Canal** (Thailand); China's presence at **Coco Islands** (Myanmar)
# 4. Background
- ⚠ **Ten Degree Channel** — separates **Andaman** and **Nicobar**; **Nine Degree** — Minicoy from rest of Lakshadweep; **Eight Degree** — Minicoy and **Maldives**
- **Barren Island** — India's only active volcano, **~135 km NE of Port Blair**; erupted after 1991 too (2005, 2017, 2018)
# 5. CA360 details
- **NGT order dated 16 February 2026** — **6-member bench** led by Chairperson **Justice Prakash Shrivastava**; dismissed fresh petitions
- Noted review of **2022 Environmental Clearance** by a **High-Powered Committee**
- Development area **166 sq km**; forest diversion **~130 sq km**; tribes **Shompen** and **Nicobarese**

## Solid Waste Management Rules, 2026 | from: 2026-02-05, CA360-2026-02
# 1. Basics
- **Notified by:** **MoEFCC**; in force **1 April 2026**; ⚠ **replaces SWM Rules, 2016**
- **Waste data:** **620 lakh tonnes**/year; **~1.85 lakh t/day** generated; **1.14 lakh t/day** processed; **~40,000 t/day** landfilled
- **Hierarchy:** Prevention → Reduction → Reuse → Recycling → Recovery → Disposal (last resort)
# 2. Four-stream segregation (mandatory)
| Stream | Examples |
|---|---|
| **Wet** | biodegradable |
| **Dry** | plastic, paper, metal, glass — to **Material Recovery Facilities** |
| **Sanitary** | napkins, tampons, condoms |
| **Special-care** | medicines, paint cans, bulbs, tube lights |
- ⚠ SWM Rules **2016** had **three** categories (wet, dry, domestic hazardous)
# 3. Bulk Waste Generators
- **Definition:** built-up area **≥ 20,000 sq m**, or water use **≥ 40,000 litres/day**, or waste **≥ 100 kg/day**
- **Extended Bulk Waste Generator Responsibility (EBWGR):** on-site wet waste processing, portal registration, certification-based compliance
- Bulk generators produce **~30%** of solid waste
# 4. Enforcement and energy
- **Polluter Pays:** environmental compensation; higher landfill fees for unsegregated waste; **CPCB** frames guidelines, **SPCBs** enforce
- **Centralised online portal** tracking full lifecycle
- **Legacy dumpsites** mapped by **31 October 2026**
- Waste with calorific value **≥ 1,500 kcal/kg** → energy recovery (**RDF**, co-processing)
- **RDF in industries:** **6%** initially → **15% within six years**
- District Collectors oversee landfills; special provisions for **hilly areas and islands**
# 5. Four-bin colours (CA360)
| Bin | Waste |
|---|---|
| **Green** | **wet** — composting/biomethanation |
| **Blue** | **dry** — to MRFs |
| **Red** | **sanitary** — wrapped separately |
| **Black** | **special care** — to authorised agencies only |

## Elephant crisis in central India and SAIEE 2021–25 | from: 2026-02-26
# 1. Conflict
- "**Elephant emergency**" after attacks in **Jharkhand, West Bengal, Odisha**
- **<8%** of elephants cause **~50%** of conflict casualties
- **Causes:** droughts **1982–83**, **1986–87** (El Niño); mining in **Singhbhum** (Jharkhand), **Keonjhar** and **Sundargarh** (Odisha); **Rengali Dam (1985)**; linear infrastructure
- **Spread:** West Bengal from **Dalma** (1986) — **~200** in **Jangalmahal**; Chhattisgarh — **Surguja, Surajpur, Hasdeo Arand**; MP — **Bandhavgarh, Sanjay-Dubri**; Maharashtra — **Gadchiroli, Tadoba-Andhari**

- **Population:** **22,446** vs **29,964 (2017)** — decline ~**25%**
- **Method:** **DNA mark–recapture**; **SECR** model; **21,056** dung samples; **4,065** individuals profiled; **6.66 lakh km** walked
| State | Elephants |

| **Assam** | **4,159** |

- Declines in **Western Ghats** and **NE Hills & Brahmaputra Plains**; **Shivalik–Gangetic** nearly unchanged
