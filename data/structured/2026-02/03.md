## Free nationwide HPV vaccination for adolescent girls | from: 2026-02-26, OL-2026-02
# 1. Programme
- **Union Health Ministry** — HPV vaccination for **girls aged 14**; **voluntary and free**
- **Vaccine:** **Gardasil (quadrivalent)** — **HPV 16 & 18** (cervical cancer) + **HPV 6 & 11** (warts); **single-dose** (optional second dose after 3–5 years)
- Procured via **Gavi, the Vaccine Alliance**; ⚠ indigenous **Cervavac** not yet WHO-approved for programme
- Based on **NTAGI** recommendation; only at **government facilities** (Ayushman Arogya Mandirs, CHCs, hospitals, medical colleges); tracked on **U-WIN**
- **Safety/efficacy:** **500 million+** doses since **2006**; **93–100%** effective; non-live vaccine
# 2. Burden
- Cervical cancer — **2nd most common cancer** among Indian women; **~80,000** cases, **42,000+** deaths a year; HPV 16/18 cause **>80%**
- ⚠ Vaccine does **not** replace screening — Pap smear from **21 years**; HPV DNA test after **30**
- **WHO 90–70–90 targets by 2030 (WHA 73.2):** **90%** girls vaccinated by 15; **70%** women screened (35 and 45); **90%** treated
- **90+** countries use single-dose HPV schedule
- **Mission Indradhanush** — immunisation of children and pregnant women

## PM-RAHAT — cashless treatment for road accident victims | from: CA360-2026-02, OL-2026-02
# 1. Scheme
- **PM-RAHAT** = **Road Accident Victim Hospitalization and Assured Treatment**; **MoRTH**; 2026
- **Cover:** up to **₹1.5 lakh** per victim for **7 days** from accident
- **ERSS-112** integration — call **112**; stabilisation **24 hours** (non-critical), **48 hours** (critical)
- Reimbursement from **Motor Vehicle Accident Fund** — **Section 164B**, MV Act 1988; **Golden Hour** — **Section 162**
- Grievance officer appointed by **District Road Safety Committee**

## Health programmes — Janaushadhi, LF elimination, NPS Swasthya | from: CA360-2026-02, OL-2026-02
# 1. PMBJP
- Generic medicines **50–80%** cheaper; **~2,110** medicines and **315** surgical items
- Target **20,000 Janaushadhi Kendras by 31 March 2026**; launched **2008**, revamped **2015**; run by **PMBI** (est. 2010, Chemicals and Fertilizers ministry)
# 2. Lymphatic Filariasis MDA
- Launched by **J.P. Nadda** (Feb 2026); target elimination by **2027**
- ⚠ From **2026** — **one MDA round a year** (instead of two); target microfilaria rate **<1%**
- **124 districts** in **12 states**: **55 DA** (DEC + Albendazole) and **69 IDA** (Ivermectin + DEC + Albendazole); **719 blocks**
- Caused by **Wuchereria bancrofti**; vector **Culex quinquefasciatus**
# 3. NPS Swasthya Pension Scheme
- **PFRDA** pilot under **Regulatory Sandbox** — NPS savings usable for outpatient and hospital costs
- PFRDA est. **2003** (Act **2013**); Chair **Sivasubramanian Ramann**; NPS **2004** (all citizens **2009**)
