## Sodium-ion batteries — case for India | from: 2026-02-06
# 1. Lithium-ion status
- **Li-ion price:** **~$1,100/kWh** (early 2010s) → **~$108/kWh** (2025)
- **PLI for Advanced Chemistry Cells (2021):** **~40 GWh** allocated, only **~1 GWh** commissioned
- **Cathode:** lithium metal oxides — LiCoO₂, LiMn₂O₄, **LiFePO₄**; ⚠ **anode = graphite** (graphite is **not** a cathode material)
# 2. Sodium-ion advantages
- Energy density approaching **LFP**; layered-oxide chemistries outperform polyanionic and **Prussian blue** analogues
- **Safety:** lower thermal-runaway temperature; ⚠ **not "Dangerous Goods"** for transport; can be stored/shipped at **zero volts** (Li-ion at **≤30%** charge)
- **Aluminium** current collectors on both electrodes (Li-ion uses **copper**); sodium from **soda ash**
- **Global capacity:** **~70 GWh (2025)** → **~400 GWh by 2030**; expected to undercut Li-ion costs by **2035**

## PERT — single genome-editing strategy for many disorders | from: 2026-02-17
# 1. Study
- **Journal:** **Nature**; by **Broad Institute**, **Harvard**, **University of Minnesota**
- **PERT:** **Prime-Editing-mediated Readthrough of Premature Termination codons** — edits a **tRNA** gene into a **suppressor tRNA**
- **Target:** **nonsense mutations** (premature stop codon, e.g. **TAG**) — **~25%** of disease-causing genetic changes
# 2. Key numbers
- Human cells have **418 tRNA genes**; 4 promising — **leucine, arginine, tyrosine, serine**
- **17,000+** configurations tested; enzyme **PE6c** + **PE3** strategy
- **Efficiency:** **60–80%** vs **HDR 10–20%**
- **Mouse models:** **Batten**, **Tay-Sachs**, **Niemann-Pick C1**; **Hurler syndrome** — activity restored to **1.7%** of normal
- **Delivery:** **AAV9** viral vector; guide molecule **pegRNA**

## Lenacapavir — Zimbabwe rollout and HIV capsid science | from: 2026-02-25, CA360-2026-02
# 1. Drug
- ⚠ **First twice-yearly injectable PrEP** for HIV — developed by **Gilead Sciences**
- **US FDA approved** on **18 June 2025** — world's **first capsid-based HIV inhibitor**; **100%** effective in trials
- ⚠ Not a vaccine; only for **HIV-negative** persons; does not replace **ART**
# 2. Zimbabwe
- Rollout **19 February 2026** at **Epworth** (~**20 km south of Harare**); **46,000 doses**, **24 sites**
- Funded by **US government** and **Global Fund**; Zimbabwe among **9 countries** chosen by **WHO** for early access
- Zimbabwe PrEP toolkit: oral PrEP (**2016**), **dapivirine vaginal ring (2021)**, injectable **cabotegravir (2024)**, **lenacapavir (2026)**
# 3. Science
- Resistant HIV replicates at only **20–30%** of normal — capsid is a high-constraint target; capsid shape paper **1999** (Science)
- **AZT (zidovudine), 1987** — first drug, targeted **reverse transcriptase**
- **Global:** ~**41 million** living with HIV; ~**27 million (67%)** in Sub-Saharan Africa; **44 million+** deaths since 1980s
- ⚠ Tattooing can transmit **Hepatitis B** and **HIV** — not **Chikungunya**
# 4. CA360 details
- Brand name **"Yeztugo"**; ⚠ CA360 cites **~99.9%** effectiveness (daily: 100% in trials); Gilead est. **1987**, Foster City
