=== TOPIC 2025-11:11 | Important Days & Events

## National Constitution Day (Samvidhan Divas) 2025 | from: 2025-11-26
# 1. News
- **Observed:** **26 November 2025** — **76 years** since adoption of the Constitution
- **Declared:** **2015** (**125th birth anniversary** year of **Dr. B.R. Ambedkar**); earlier observed as **Law Day**
- ⚠ **Not** a public holiday
# 2. Timeline
| Event | Date |
|---|---|
| **First meeting of Constituent Assembly** | **9 Dec 1946** |
| **Rajendra Prasad elected President** | **11 Dec 1946** |
| **Drafting Committee** (Chairman **Ambedkar**) | ⚠ **29 Aug 1947** (not 26 Nov 1949) |
| **Draft introduced** | **4 Nov 1948** |
| **Adopted** | **26 Nov 1949** |
| **In force** | **26 Jan 1950** |
- **Time taken:** **2 years, 11 months, 18 days**
