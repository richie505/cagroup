# 1. News
- **IMD:** heavy rain in **Coastal AP** and **Rayalaseema** **29 Nov – 2 Dec 2025** from two low-pressure systems over **Bay of Bengal**

## Hayli Gubbi volcano erupts after ~12,000 years | from: 2025-11-25
# 1. News
- **Volcano:** **Hayli Gubbi**, north-eastern **Ethiopia** (near **Red Sea**, **Afar Rift**)
- **Erupted:** **23 November 2025** — ⚠ first in nearly **12,000 years**
- **Type:** explosive — ash and gases, **no lava flows**
- **Ash plume:** via **Arabian Peninsula**, **Arabian Sea** → **Gujarat, Rajasthan** → **Delhi, UP** → towards **China**; at **10–14 km** altitude — flights disrupted
- **Alerts:** **Volcanic Ash Advisory Centres (VAACs)**
# 2. Background
| Type | Example |
|---|---|
| **Active** | **Kilauea** (Hawaii), **Santa Maria** (Guatemala) |
| **Dormant** | **Mount Kilimanjaro** (Tanzania) |
| **Extinct** | **Dhinodhar Hill** (Gujarat) |
| **Shield** (basaltic, gentle) | **Mauna Loa** |
| **Composite / Stratovolcano** (explosive) | **Mayon**, **Mount Fuji**, **Mount Rainier** |
- **Mid-ocean ridge** system: over **65,000 km**

## Dugongs under threat in India | from: 2025-11-19
# 1. News
- **IUCN assessment** at **IUCN World Conservation Congress, Abu Dhabi (2025)** flagged threats to Indian dugongs (**Gulf of Kutch**, **Andaman & Nicobar**)
- **Study (Marine Pollution Bulletin):** **arsenic, cadmium, chromium, mercury, lead** in **46** stranded dugongs
# 2. Species facts
- **Dugong dugon** — "**sea cow**"; ⚠ **only herbivorous marine mammal** in India; eats **seagrass** (**30–40 kg/day**)
- **Size:** ~**3 m**, ~**420 kg**; lives up to **70 years**; matures at **9–10 years**; calves every **3–5 years**
- **Seagrass:** **Cymodocea, Halophila, Thalassia, Halodule**; depth **<10 m**
| Status | Listing |
|---|---|
| **IUCN** | **Vulnerable** |
| **CITES** | **Appendix I** |
| **WPA 1972** | **Schedule I** |
- **World Dugong Day:** **28 May**
# 3. Distribution in India
| Area | Estimate |
|---|---|
| **Gulf of Mannar – Palk Bay (TN)** | ~**150–200** — ⚠ largest population |
| **Andaman & Nicobar** | **<50** |
| **Gulf of Kutch (Gujarat)** | **<20** |
- ⚠ **Not** found along the entire coast
# 4. Conservation
- **Dugong Conservation Reserve:** **Palk Bay**, **2022**, **448 sq km**
- **MoEFCC Task Force:** **2010**; **National Dugong Recovery Programme** (TN, Gujarat, A&N)
# 5. Background — IUCN Congress
- **Quadrennial**; first **1948**; previous **Marseille, France (2021)**; India a State Member since **1969**
