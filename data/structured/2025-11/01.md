## Chief Minister Employment Generation Programme (CMEGP) | from: APCA-2025-11
# 1. News
- **Scheme:** AP plans **CMEGP** — self-employment through micro and small enterprises, **rural and urban** areas
- **Implemented by:** **Department of MSME**, AP; via **District Industries Centres (DICs)**
- **Modelled on:** Centre's **PMEGP**; encourages **first-generation entrepreneurs**
- **Target:** **5,000–6,000** new units per year
| Unit | CMEGP ceiling | PMEGP ceiling |
|---|---|---|
| **Manufacturing** | ⚠ up to **₹75 lakh** | **₹50 lakh** |
| **Service** | ⚠ up to **₹25 lakh** | **₹20 lakh** |
- **Complementary:** dedicated policy for **Common Facility Centres (CFCs)** for MSME clusters
# 2. Background — PMEGP
- **Central Sector Scheme**, launched **2008**
- **Eligibility:** above **18 years**; **no income ceiling**; **Class VIII pass** for projects above **₹10 lakh** (manufacturing) / **₹5 lakh** (service)
- **Subsidy:** **15%–35%** of project cost
- **Implementing agency:** **KVIC** — statutory body under **KVIC Act, 1956**

## Building Penalisation Scheme (BPS) 2025 | from: APCA-2025-11
# 1. News
- **Notified via:** **G.O. Ms. 225** (**MA&UD** Department)
- **Regularises:** unauthorised constructions from ⚠ **1 January 1985** to **31 August 2025** — final one-time chance
- **Replaces:** earlier **2019 BPS** (low participation)
- **UCIMS Portal** (Urban Construction Information Management System): **59,041** unauthorised buildings identified (as of **31 Aug 2025**)
- **Demand from:** **CREDAI**, **NAREDCO**, **CLESA**
# 2. Provisions
- **Coverage:** Municipal Corporations, Municipalities, Nagar Panchayats, Gram Panchayats under UDAs; **APCRDA, VMRDA, VK–PCPIR UDA, IALAs**
- ⚠ **Capital City Area of APCRDA** is **excluded**
- **Application:** online; window **120 days** from notification
| Charge | Amount |
|---|---|
| **Initial fee** | **₹10,000** per application |
| **Penalisation** | **₹40–₹400 per sq ft** |
| **Concession** | **25%** for pre-**1997** buildings; **50%** for residential buildings in notified **slums** |
- **Excluded:** public land, environmentally restricted zones, disputed ownership, new constructions after 31 Aug 2025

## School mergers in Andhra Pradesh | from: 2025-11-18, APCA-2025-11
# 1. News
- **Issue:** school restructuring/mergers disrupted rural schooling — some children missed up to **six months** of classes
- **Trigger:** village protests over shifting **Classes 3–5** to "**Model Primary Schools**"
- **UDISE data (2014–2024):** government schools in AP **fell 4%**; private schools **rose 7%**
- **Example:** **Bangaramma Kandriga** village — students asked to walk **2.5 km**
# 2. Two rounds of restructuring
| Government | Model |
|---|---|
| **YSRCP (2019–24)** | **School Rationalisation Policy (2023)** — primary schools kept only **Classes 1–2**; **Classes 3–5** merged with nearby **high schools** |
| **NDA–TDP (current)** | **Model Primary Schools** (**one per panchayat**) for **Classes 3–5**; other schools downgraded to **Classes 1–2** |
- **Framework:** both followed **NEP 2020**-aligned restructuring
- **Model school gaps:** only **1** usable classroom, **3 teachers instead of 5**, **1 washroom** shared
# 3. Welfare link
- **Amma Vodi** (YSRCP) / **Thalliki Vandanam** (TDP): **₹13,000/year per child** to mothers — private schools collect it, driving migration
# 4. Background
- ⚠ **RTE Act, 2009** neighbourhood norm: school within **1 km** for **Classes 1–5** — mergers forced **2–3 km** walks
- **UDISE+:** **Unified District Information System for Education**
