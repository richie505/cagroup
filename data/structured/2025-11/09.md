## Batukeshwar Dutt — birth anniversary | from: 2025-11-18
# 1. Profile
- **Born:** **18 November 1910**, **Burdwan, Bengal**; died **20 July 1965**
- **Central Assembly Bombing:** **8 April 1929** with **Bhagat Singh** — slogans "**Inquilab Zindabad**", "Down with Imperialism"; ⚠ bombs **harmless** / symbolic, not to kill
- **Convicted:** **Delhi Assembly Bomb Case**, **12 June 1929**
- **Jails:** **Multan, Jhelum, Trichinopoly, Salem, Andamans** — **9 years**
- In **Salem Jail** when Bhagat Singh, Rajguru, Sukhdev were hanged (**23 March 1931**)
- **Released 1938**; re-arrested in **Quit India (1942)** — **4** more years
- **Organisations:** **HSRA**, **Naujawan Bharat Sabha**; met Bhagat Singh in **Kanpur**
- **Later life:** settled in **Patna**; six-month nomination to **Bihar Legislative Council**; **bone cancer** — **AIIMS**
- **Cremated:** **Hussainiwala**, beside Bhagat Singh, Rajguru, Sukhdev
