## Four Labour Codes come into force | from: 2025-11-26
# 1. News
- **Effective:** **21 November 2025** — **4 Labour Codes** replace **29 Central labour laws**
- **Labour:** in **Concurrent List** (Entries **22–24, 35**, List III)
| Code | Laws merged |
|---|---|
| **Code on Wages, 2019** | **4** |
| **Code on Social Security, 2020** | **9** (EPF, ESI, gratuity, maternity benefit) |
| **Industrial Relations Code, 2020** | **3** (Trade Unions, Industrial Disputes, Industrial Employment) |
| **OSH Code, 2020** | **13** (factories, mines, contract labour, construction) |
# 2. Code on Wages
- Applies to **all employees**; **National Floor Wage** — States cannot fix minimum wage below it
- **Wage** = **Basic + DA + Retaining Allowance**; deductions max **50%**
- **Hours:** **8–12/day**, max **48/week**; overtime at least **2x**
- **Payment:** monthly within **7 days**; on resignation/termination within **2 days**; mandatory **wage slips**
# 3. Code on Social Security
- ⚠ **First time** legal definition of **gig** and **platform workers**; **Social Security Fund**
- **Aggregators:** contribute **1–2% of annual turnover**, capped at **5%** of amount payable to gig workers
- **Fixed-term employees:** ⚠ **gratuity after 1 year** (earlier **5 years** for permanent)
- **EPFO:** establishments with **20+** employees; **ESIC:** pan-India, mandatory even with **1** worker in hazardous work (earlier **10**)
- **Inspector-cum-facilitators** replace inspectors
# 4. Industrial Relations Code
- **Worker** includes supervisory staff earning **< ₹18,000/month**
- ⚠ **Layoff/retrenchment/closure permission threshold:** **100 → 300** workers
- **Strike:** notice within **60 days** before; no strike within **14 days** of notice; mass casual leave by **>50%** workers = strike
- **Negotiating union:** **51%** membership; council — unions with **≥20%**
# 5. OSH Code
- **Factory licence:** with power **10 → 20** workers; without power **20 → 40**
- **Contract labour:** applies when contractor has **50+** (earlier **20**)
- **Women:** may work before **6 am** / after **7 pm** with consent and safety
- Mandatory **appointment letters**; **annual free health check**
- **Safety Committees:** **≥500** factory, **≥250** construction, **≥100** mine workers

## Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Rules, 2025 | from: 2025-11-17
# 1. News
- **Notified:** **14 November 2025** — operationalise the **DPDP Act, 2023** (passed **August 2023**)
- **Draft Rules:** released **January 2025**
- **Immediate effect:** formation of **Data Protection Board of India (DPBI)** + the **RTI amendment**
- **Phased:** most provisions apply after **12–18 months** (consent system, erasure, DPOs, audits, breach reporting — **2026–27**)
# 2. Key features
- **Citizen rights:** **erasure**, **correction**, **grievance redressal**, appoint **digital nominee** (after death/incapacity)
- **Consent:** informed consent; withdrawal as easy as giving; **Consent Managers**
- **Children:** targeted ads restricted; limited exemption for parents to **track child's location**
- **Significant Data Fiduciaries (SDFs):** graded obligations
- **DPBI:** "**digital by design**" — works as a **digital office**; online complaints; can drop proceedings on a **voluntary undertaking**
- ⚠ **DPBI** is a subordinate office of **MeitY** (criticised for lack of independence)
# 3. RTI amendment
- ⚠ **Section 8(1)(j)** of **RTI Act, 2005** — the **public interest** override for personal information **removed**
# 4. Background
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Data Principal** | Individual whose data is processed |
| **Data Fiduciary** | Entity deciding purpose and means of processing |
- **Puttaswamy (2017):** privacy a fundamental right under **Article 21**
- **Srikrishna Committee** draft — **2018**

## Chandigarh under Article 240 — Constitution (131st Amendment) Bill, 2025 | from: 2025-11-25
# 1. News
- **Proposal:** **Constitution (131st Amendment) Bill, 2025** to add **Chandigarh** to **Article 240**
- **Backlash:** in **Punjab** (AAP CM **Bhagwant Mann**, Congress, SAD, Punjab BJP chief **Sunil Jakhar**)
- **MHA clarified:** no final decision; ⚠ **no Bill in Winter Session 2025**
- **Effect if passed:** independent **Administrator/L-G**; **President** could make regulations for Chandigarh
# 2. Article 240
- **President** makes regulations for "**peace, progress and good government**" of UTs: **A&N Islands, Lakshadweep, DNH & DD, Puducherry** (certain conditions)
- Regulation has force of an **Act of Parliament**; can amend/repeal Parliament's laws for that UT
# 3. Chandigarh status
| Fact | Detail |
|---|---|
| **UT + joint capital** | Of **Punjab** and **Haryana** — **Punjab Reorganisation Act, 1966** (passed **18 Sep 1966**) |
| **Haryana** | **17th state** |
| **Administrator** | ⚠ **Governor of Punjab** (since **1984**) — not Haryana Governor |
| **Property split** | ⚠ **60:40** (Punjab:Haryana) |
| **Capital law** | **Capital of Punjab (Development and Regulation) Act, 1952** |
| **Periphery** | **Punjab New Capital (Periphery) Control Act, 1952** — **16 km** radius |
| **Became Punjab capital** | **1953**, inaugurated by President **Rajendra Prasad** |
| **Land** | **22 villages** of **Kharar** |
| **1970 (29 January)** | Centre said Chandigarh should go to Punjab as a whole |
| **2016** | Attempt to appoint **K. J. Alphons** as Administrator shelved |

## Cannabis cultivation vs bhang — Kerala HC on NDPS Act | from: 2025-11-18
# 1. News
- **Kerala High Court (4 Nov 2025):** dismissed plea of a man caught with **five cannabis plants** on terrace
- **Ruling:** cultivation of **any cannabis plant** is illegal even with **no flowering/fruiting tops**; includes keeping in **pots**
# 2. NDPS Act, 1985 — definitions
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Charas** | **Resin** of cannabis (incl. **hashish oil**) |
| **Ganja** | **Flowering or fruiting tops** (contain **THC**) |
| **Excluded** | **Leaves and seeds** without flowering tops |
| **Cannabis plant** — **Section 2(iv)** | Any plant of genus cannabis, irrespective of age/flowering |
- ⚠ **Bhang** is made from **leaves** — **not** a narcotic under NDPS; **States** regulate it (**UP, Rajasthan** licensed shops; **Assam** bans it)
- **Section 8(b):** prohibits cultivation of any cannabis plant without approval
- **Section 14:** Govt may allow cultivation for **industrial (hemp)**, **horticultural**, **medical/scientific** use
# 3. Punishments
| Item | Small quantity | Commercial quantity |
|---|---|---|
| **Ganja** | up to **1 kg** — jail up to **1 year**, fine up to **₹10,000** | **20 kg+** — **10–20 years**, fine **₹1–2 lakh** |
| **Charas** | up to **100 g** | **1 kg+** |
- **Cultivation:** up to **10 years RI** and **₹1 lakh** fine
# 4. Background
- **Industrial hemp:** ⚠ **Uttarakhand** first to issue licences (**2018**); also **UP, HP, MP**
- **India's first medical cannabis clinic:** **Bengaluru (2020)**
- **UN Commission on Narcotic Drugs:** founded **1946**, HQ **Vienna**
- **Recreational use legal:** **Canada**, **Uruguay**, **15 US states**; **50+** countries allow medicinal cannabis
- **Delhi HC** hearing challenge by **Great Legalisation Movement India Trust**
- **NDPS Act** enacted under international pressure, mainly **USA**
