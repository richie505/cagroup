=== TOPIC 2025-11:06 | Science, Tech, Space & Defence

## From GM crops to genome-edited crops | from: 2025-11-28
# 1. News
- **GE rice varieties:** improved **Samba Mahsuri** and **MTU-1010**; **canola-quality low-pungency mustard** (2nd year trials, possible release **2026**)
- ⚠ Only commercial GM crop in India: **Bt cotton**
| Variety | Edit / benefit |
|---|---|
| **Samba Mahsuri (GE)** | **Gn1a** (cytokinin oxidase) edited → more spikelets, ~**19%** yield gain |
| **MTU-1010 (GE)** | Drought/salt tolerance — saline/alkaline soils |
| **Mustard (GE)** | Low pungency, disease/pest resistance |
# 2. Regulation and funding
- **March 2022 MoEFCC OM:** transgene-free GE plants exempt from GM biosafety rules — need **IBSC** certification, ⚠ not **GEAC**
- **Funding:** **National Agricultural Science Fund** (~**2018**); **₹500 crore** in **Union Budget 2023–24**
- **ICAR** mapped ~**178 genes** in **24 field crops** and **43 genes** in horticultural crops
# 3. Tools
- **Cas9**, **Cas12a**; newer **GeoCas9**, **CasLambda** supplied by **Innovative Genomics Institute** (founded by **Jennifer Doudna**) — training at **IARI** (**Feb 2025**)
- **Indigenous TnpB-based mini editor:** team led by **Kutubuddin Ali Molla**, **Central Rice Research Institute (CRRI), Cuttack**
- **Training:** **9** scientists trained abroad (US, Europe, Australia, **CIMMYT Mexico**); **12** more scheduled
- **GM vs GE:** GM inserts foreign genes (transgenic); GE edits own genes, no foreign DNA
