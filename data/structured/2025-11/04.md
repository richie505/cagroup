## Rupee hits record low, rebounds on RBI intervention | from: 2025-11-28
# 1. News
- ⚠ **All-time low:** **₹89.41 per USD** on **21 Nov 2025**; rebounded to **₹89.22–89.23** (24–25 Nov)
- **Expected band:** **₹88.9–₹90.2**
# 2. Causes
- **India–US trade deal** uncertainty
- **Record goods trade deficit:** ~**USD 42 bn** (**October**); exports fell ~**12%**; imports record ~**USD 76 bn** (gold)
- **FPI** selling; **Japanese yen** carry-trade unwind
- RBI extended **export proceeds realisation** from **9 to 15 months**
# 3. RBI support
- **Forex reserves:** ~**USD 690 bn**
- **Net spot sales (Jan–Sep 2025):** ~**USD 20.1 bn**; forward sales **USD 53.4 bn (Aug)** → **59.4 bn (Sep)**
- ⚠ RBI does **not target a level** — only curbs volatility (**managed float**)
# 4. Background
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **NEER** | Rupee vs basket, no inflation adjustment — rise = appreciation |
| **REER** | Inflation-adjusted — ⚠ rise = **loss** of competitiveness |
| **Trade deficit** | Goods imports – exports only |
| **CAD** | Goods + services + income + transfers |
- **Forex reserve components:** **FCA** (largest), **Gold**, **SDRs**, **Reserve Tranche Position**; held by RBI, owned by **GoI**

## Draft Seeds Bill, 2025 | from: 2025-11-17
# 1. News
- **Ministry:** **Agriculture & Farmers Welfare**
- **Replaces:** **Seeds Act, 1966** and **Seeds (Control) Order, 1983**
# 2. Key provisions
- **Registration:** no seed sold without **VCU (Value for Cultivation and Use)**-based registration; varieties notified under 1966 Act **deemed registered**; existing varieties get **3-year provisional** registration
- **Farmers' rights:** may save, use, resow, exchange, sell **farm-saved seeds** — ⚠ but **not under a brand name**
- **Quality:** Centre notifies germination, purity, seed-health standards; mandatory **QR code** labelling
- **Traceability:** mandatory onboarding on **SATHI Portal**
- **Central Accreditation System:** multi-state firms "**deemed registered**"
- **Imports:** quarantine + **Indian Minimum Seed Certification Standards**; unregistered varieties only for **research/trials**
- **Price control:** during scarcity, hoarding, monopoly
| Offence | Penalty |
|---|---|
| **Trivial** | Warning + small fine |
| **Minor** | up to **₹2 lakh** |
| **Major** (e.g. spurious/unregistered seeds) | up to **₹30 lakh**, cancellation or imprisonment |
# 3. Concerns
- Community seed banks, **FPOs**, women collectives treated as commercial units; foreign entities can do **VCU testing** (GM seed risk)
