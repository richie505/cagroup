## Krishnadevaraya-era 1518 inscription sinking at Vankeswara temple, Palnadu | from: APCA-2025-04
# 1. News
- **Where:** **Vankeswara temple**, **Durgi** mandal HQ, **Palnadu** district
- **Date:** **22 November 1518 CE**, reign of **Sri Krishnadevaraya** (Vijayanagara)
- **Commissioned by:** **Timmapa Nayanim Garu**, a doorkeeper in the royal court
- **Records:** grant of **Gundala** village for temple festivities
- **Text:** **55 lines** classical Telugu — only top **19** visible
- **Inspected by:** **E. Sivanagireddy**, CEO, **Pleach India Foundation** (tip from sculptor **Ch. Srinivasachary**)

## Srisailam copper plates (1420 CE) — first use of 'Swarajya' in a Telugu inscription | from: APCA-2025-04
# 1. News
- **Where:** **Srisailam Devasthanam** (one of **12 Jyotirlingas**)
- **Date:** **Saka 1341**, Sarvari, Magha Sudha Trayodasi = **28 January 1420 CE**
- ⚠ Headline says **14th century** but 1420 CE is **15th century**
- **Format:** **three copper leaves**, bilingual — **Sanskrit** and **Telugu**
- **Term:** **'Swarajya Punarudhharana'** (restoration of independent state) — ⚠ **first-time** use in a Telugu inscription (per Dr **K. Munirathnam Reddy**, Director (Epigraphy), **ASI Mysore**)
- **Ruler:** **Recharla Lingama Nayaka** (**Recharla** dynasty) — reclaimed areas from the **Turakas**; restored **eight agraharas**
- **Services restored:** Nityaapahara, Akhanda-Dipala-Chamuru, Nityasatram, **Sivaratri Mahotsavam**, Bhuri Satram
# 2. Link to freedom movement
- **Bal Gangadhar Tilak:** "Swarajya is my birthright"
- **Tanguturi Prakasam** started the newspaper **'Swarajya'** in **three languages**
