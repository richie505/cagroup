## AP Cabinet approves Ordinance on SC sub-categorisation | from: APCA-2025-04
# 1. News
- **Approved by:** AP Cabinet chaired by CM **N. Chandrababu Naidu** — draft **Ordinance**
- **59 SC communities** in **three groups**:
| Group | Castes | Reservation |
|---|---|---|
| **Group-I** | **12** | **1%** |
| **Group-II** | **18** | **6.5%** |
| **Group-III** | **29** | **7.5%** |
- ⚠ Total = **15%** SC quota
- **Roster:** **200-point** roster in two cycles of **100**
- **Unit:** whole State as one unit, based on **2011 census**; districts become units after next census; all **26** districts
- **First use:** **DSC** teacher recruitment
