## US 26% tariff hits AP seafood exports | from: APCA-2025-04
# 1. News
- **What:** US President **Donald Trump** announced **26%** tariff on aqua products — AP shipments to US halted within **24 hours**
- **Total duties:** up to **34%** (tariff + anti-dumping + countervailing)
- **Shrimp price:** 100 count fell from **₹235/kg** to **₹195/kg**
- **Competitor:** **Ecuador** faces only **10%** tariff

# 3. Recovery after 90-day pause
- **What:** US **paused** the **26%** levy for **90 days** — AP exports to US **back to normal** (per **SEAI**, AP unit)
- **Loss during halt:** unconfirmed over **₹1,000 crore**
- **Revived districts:** **Eluru, Krishna, East and West Godavari, Nellore, Prakasam**
- **Farm-gate shrimp prices after recovery:** 90-count **₹235/kg**, 80-count **₹255**, 70-count **₹275**, 60-count **₹295**, 50-count **₹315**
- **Demand:** call for an **FTA with the US**
