## AP Minor Mineral Policy 2025 approved | from: APCA-2025-04
# 1. Why
- **Mining share in GSDP:** **3.53%** (2018–19) → **2.71%** (2023–24); **2022 auction-only** rule stalled applications
# 2. Key reforms
| Reform | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Pending applications** | over **6,000** revived — first-come first-served; **LOI** within **1 year** |
| **Dual grant** | **application-based** for minor minerals (building material, granite); **auction-based** for industrial minerals |
| **Lease premium** | cut from **10x** to **5x** dead rent; **limestone slabs 3x** |
| **Lease period** | granite and industrial minerals **20 → 30** years; road metal (captive) **15 → 30** years |
| **'Consideration Amount'** | **abolished** (introduced during COVID) |
| **OTS scheme** | rationalised penalties; closure of abandoned leases |
- **Tech:** digital lease lifecycle; **drone** and **CCTV** surveillance

## Telangana–AP stalemate over ₹8,929 crore EAP debt | from: APCA-2025-04
# 1. Issue
- **What:** **15** Externally Aided Projects (EAPs) with **23** loans worth **₹8,929 crore** at bifurcation (2014)
- **PAG classification:** **2** loans AP-only, **9** Telangana-only, **12** shared
- **Disputed four projects (₹4,265 crore):** **Hussain Sagar** lake, transmission modernisation, **Hyderabad Ring Road**, **Krishnapatnam** supercritical power station
- **AP view:** by location — AP bears **₹1,335 crore** (Krishnapatnam), Telangana **₹2,929 crore**; utilisation formula for **₹3,755 crore** (AP share **₹1,892 crore**)
- **DEA (Feb 2025, MHA meeting):** single-state projects borne by that state; shared by **variable ratios**
- **Telangana view:** **population-based** split under **Section 54(1)** of AP Reorganisation Act; claims excess liability ~**₹1,449.75 crore**
# 2. Background — AP Reorganisation Act, 2014
- **Presidential assent:** **1 March 2014**; in force **2 June 2014** (Telangana Formation Day)
- **Separate High Courts:** from **1 January 2019**
- **Polavaram** declared a **national project**
