## AP reports first human H5N1 death — Palnadu child | from: APCA-2025-04
# 1. News
- **Who:** **two-year-old girl** from **Palnadu** district
- **Died:** **16 March** at **AIIMS-Mangalagiri** (multi-organ failure; also had **leptospirosis**)
- **Confirmed:** **31 March** by **NIV, Pune**
- ⚠ **Second** human H5N1 death in **India**; **first** in **AP**
- **Likely source:** habit of eating **raw chicken**; Palnadu had no prior bird flu outbreak
- **Response:** RRTs from **Guntur** GGH; contacts tested negative
# 2. Background
- **H5N1** = highly pathogenic avian influenza (**HPAI**); human-to-human transmission unconfirmed

## Displaced Gutti Koya tribals — call for permanent settlement | from: APCA-2025-04
# 1. Issue
- **Who:** **Gutti Koya (Gond)** tribals displaced from **Chhattisgarh** by anti-Maoist operations (**Strategic Hamlet Programme**, since **2005**) — living on forest lands in AP and Telangana ~**2 decades**
- **Numbers:** **Telangana 24,000**, **AP 8,000** — treated as **migrants**, not STs
- ⚠ ST in Chhattisgarh but **not** ST in AP/Telangana
- **Strategic hamleting:** ~**50,000** Gonds moved to roadside camps; strategy borrowed from **US war in Vietnam**; earlier used in **Mizoram** (1960s)
# 2. Law
- **FRA 2006, Section 3.1(m):** tribals displaced before cut-off **13 December 2005** entitled to alternative forest land
- **No national IDP policy**; contrast **Bru Rehabilitation Plan, 2019** (Bru settled in **Tripura**)
- **NCST** ordered surveys; suggested alignment with **UN Guiding Principles on Internal Displacement**

## 25% RTE quota in AP private schools from 2025–26 | from: APCA-2025-04
# 1. News
- **What:** **Section 12(1)(c)** of **RTE Act, 2009** — **25%** of **Class 1** seats in private unaided schools for disadvantaged/EWS children, from **2025–26**
- **Applications:** **28 April – 15 May**, via School Education Dept website; also at Village Secretariat, MRC
- **Age:** **5 years** completed by **31 March** (IB/CBSE/ICSE) or by **1 June** (State syllabus)
# 2. Background — RTE Act
- **Free and compulsory education** for ages **6–14**; implements **Article 21-A**
- **No detention** till Class **8**; no capitation fees; School Management Committees
