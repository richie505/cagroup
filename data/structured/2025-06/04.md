## India–US trade deal — dairy and agriculture hurdles | from: 2025-06-25
# 1. News
- **Deadline:** **9 July 2025** — US to reimpose reciprocal tariffs (back to **2 April** levels) if no deal
- **Sticking points:** **agriculture** and **dairy** (small producers; hormones in US milk)
- **Plan:** a **"mini trade deal"** then first tranche of **Bilateral Trade Agreement (BTA)** by **Fall 2025**
- **Earlier:** US withdrew India's **GSP** status in **2019**
- India "the only country" in such elaborate talks with the US
- **Alternatives to Hormuz route:** **Chabahar**, **INSTC**

## SEBI governance reforms for Market Infrastructure Institutions | from: 2025-06-28
# 1. News
- **Consultation paper:** **24 June 2025**; comments till **15 July 2025**
- **MIIs:** stock exchanges, clearing corporations, depositories
- **Proposal:** at least **two Executive Directors (EDs)** per MII, reporting to the MD
| Vertical | Head |
|---|---|
| **Critical Operations** | ED (mandatory) |
| **Regulatory, Compliance, Risk Management, Investor Grievances** | ED (mandatory) |
| **Commercial Interests & Business Development** | ED at MII's discretion |
- **Board:** EDs of first two verticals on the **Governing Board**, status like MD; appointment needs **SEBI approval**
- **Reports:** **quarterly** to Board and SEBI
- **Directorships:** MDs may hold non-executive posts in **unlisted govt companies** or **not-for-profits**; ⚠ EDs **none** except MII subsidiaries
