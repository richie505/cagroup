## AP High Court — trans woman is a woman under Section 498A IPC | from: 2025-06-25
# 1. News
- **Judge:** Justice **Venkata Jyothirmai Pratapa**, **Andhra Pradesh High Court**
- **Ruling:** trans women are legally women and can invoke **IPC Section 498A** (cruelty by husband/in-laws); womanhood **cannot** be defined by reproductive capacity
- **Case:** petition by **Viswanathan Krishnamurthy** and parents against a case by **Shabana**, a trans woman from **Ongole**
- **Outcome:** right to file upheld, but charges **quashed for lack of evidence** (not on gender grounds)
# 2. Basis
- **NALSA v. Union of India (2014)** — right to self-identify gender
- **Articles 14, 15, 21**
- ⚠ **Article 15** bars discrimination on grounds of **sex** too
