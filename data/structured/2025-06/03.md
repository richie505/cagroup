## Zero-dose children — Lancet study | from: 2025-06-28
# 1. News
- **Study:** **Global Burden of Disease Vaccine Coverage Collaborators**, **The Lancet**, **25 June 2025**
- **India:** **1.44 million** zero-dose children in **2023** — ⚠ **second-highest** after **Nigeria**
- **Global:** **15.7 million** zero-dose in 2023; over half in **eight countries**; South Asia **13%**
- **Measles coverage** fell in **100 of 204** countries (2010–2019); **21 of 36** high-income countries saw dips
- **Zero-dose:** no dose of **DTP** vaccine by age **one**

## CBSE Class 10 — two board exams from 2026–27 | from: 2025-06-28
# 1. News
- **From:** academic year **2026–27**; aligned with **NEP 2020**
- **First exam:** **mid-February** — **mandatory**; results by **April**
- **Second exam:** **May** — **optional**, improvement in up to **three** subjects (Science, Maths, Social Science, two languages); results by **June**
- **Better** of the two scores counts
- **Essential Repeat:** failed or absent in **3+** subjects — ⚠ **cannot** take second exam
- **Compartment:** 1–2 subjects — can use second exam
- **Marksheet:** via **DigiLocker**
- **Flexibility:** sports persons, snow-bound areas (e.g. **Ladakh**)

## Organ transplantation programme — Health Ministry report | from: 2025-06-24
# 1. News
- **Report:** Union **Health Ministry**, **19 June 2025**
- **Kidney transplants 2024-25:** only **13,476** vs demand over **1 lakh**
- **Gaps:** no dedicated transplant OTs/**TICUs**; no in-house **HLA cross-matching** labs in many **AIIMS**; delays in **Brain-Stem Death (BSD) Committees**; manpower shortage
- **Finance:** **AB PM-JAY** does **not** cover **liver and heart** transplants or long-term **immunosuppressants**
- **Recommendations:** new govt transplant centres, HLA labs, include liver/heart in PM-JAY, **case-based incentives** for teams, mandatory data sharing with **NOTTO**
