## CAR T-cell therapy for cancer | from: 2025-06-28
# 1. Basics
- **CAR:** **Chimeric Antigen Receptor** — patient's own **T-cells** (⚠ not B-cells) engineered to attack cancer
- **Steps:** **leukapheresis** → genetic modification → expansion → infusion
- **Approved for blood cancers:** **ALL**, **Non-Hodgkin Lymphoma**, **Multiple Myeloma** — ⚠ not yet solid tumours
- **Side effects:** **Cytokine Release Syndrome (CRS)**, **ICANS** (neurotoxicity)
- **Cost abroad:** over **₹3–4 crore** per patient
# 2. India
- **ICMR**-funded trials at **CMC Vellore** — hospital-based manufacture
- **IIT Bombay + Tata Memorial Centre** — India's first cost-effective platform **'InCART'**
- **DBT** supports Make-in-India CAR-T
