## WMO State of the Climate in Asia 2024 | from: 2025-06-25
# 1. Key findings
- **Publisher:** **World Meteorological Organization (WMO)** — SG **Celeste Saulo**
- **Asia warming:** at **twice** the global average; **2024 warmest year** on record in Asia
- **Global 2024:** highest since **1850** (beat **1.45 °C** of 2023) — all **six** datasets
- **Warming 1991–2024:** almost **double** 1961–1990
- **Sea level rise:** above global average on Pacific and Indian Ocean sides
- **Glaciers:** **23 of 24** monitored glaciers (central Himalaya, **Tian Shan**) lost mass
