## US strikes on Iranian nuclear sites and Iran–Israel ceasefire | from: 2025-06-24, 2025-06-25, 2025-06-28
# 1. News
- **Targets:** **Natanz**, **Fordow**, **Isfahan** — US joined the **Israel–Iran conflict** (reignited **June 2025**)
- **Trump** had given Iran "**two weeks**" to negotiate; Iran recently censured by **IAEA**
| Site | Detail | Weapon |
|---|---|---|
| **Natanz** | ~220 km SE of Tehran; **main enrichment** site; up to **60%** enrichment; partly underground | **Tomahawk** + bunker busters |
| **Fordow** | under a mountain near **Qom**, ~100 km SW of Tehran; exposed **2009** | **12 GBU-57 MOPs** from **six B-2** bombers |
| **Isfahan** | ~350 km SE of Tehran; **three Chinese-built** research reactors | **Tomahawk** |
- **GBU-57 MOP:** **30,000-pound** conventional bunker buster
- ⚠ Fordow is **underground**; Natanz (not Fordow) is the main enrichment centre
# 2. Reactions
- **Iran FM Abbas Araghchi:** "grave violation" of UN Charter and **NPT**
- **Israel PM Netanyahu:** "historic"
- **Iran and NPT:** signed **1968**, ratified **1970**
- **Law:** **UN Charter Article 2** — bars use of force; **Strait of Hormuz** oil risk
# 3. Iran's retaliation and ceasefire
- **Strike:** Iran hit the US **Al-Udeid Air Base, Qatar** (heart of **CENTCOM**) a day after US strikes — with **advance notice**, **no casualties**
- **Ceasefire:** Trump announced a "**Complete and Total Ceasefire**" between Iran and Israel
- **Israel's campaign:** **11 days** — IRGC HQ, air defences, leadership
- **Iran's missile:** solid-fuel **Kheybar Shekan**
- **Restraint:** no blockade of **Strait of Hormuz**; proxies stayed silent
| Proxy | Country |
|---|---|
| **Houthis** | **Yemen** |
| **Hezbollah** | **Lebanon** — leader **Hassan Nasrallah** killed **2024**; now **Naim Qassem** |
| **Hashd al-Shaabi** (Popular Mobilization Forces) | **Iraq** |
- **India stakes:** Strait of Hormuz ~**40%** of India's oil imports; **8 million+** Indians in West Asia
- **Arab nuclear plans:** **UAE** runs a nuclear power plant; **Saudi Arabia** fast-tracking
# 4. Nuclear order concerns
- **Conflict length:** ceasefire after **12 days** (⚠ one source counts Israel's strikes over **11 days**)
- **Iran:** NPT signatory; accepted inspections under **JCPOA** (**2015**, with **P5+1** — US, UK, France, Russia, China + Germany)
- **Iran parliament:** debating a bill to **exit NPT** — ⚠ **no formal withdrawal** yet (June 2025)
- **Israel:** ⚠ **not an NPT signatory**; undeclared arsenal, no IAEA inspections
- **Related treaty:** **TPNW** (Treaty on the Prohibition of Nuclear Weapons)
