## Oil and gas well blowouts — ONGC Sivasagar | from: 2025-06-24
# 1. News
- **Event:** blowout at an **ONGC** well in **Sivasagar district, Assam** on **12 June 2025**
- **Evacuated:** over **1,500** residents
- **Experts:** well-control experts from the **United States**
- **Controlled:** by **21 June**
# 2. Background
- **Blowout:** uncontrolled release of gas/oil when well pressure beats **blowout preventers (BOPs)** and **drilling mud**
- **Causes:** faulty valves, wrong mud weight; fire if ignited
- ⚠ Hard to control — needs experts, days or weeks

## "Thirstwaves" — extreme evaporative demand | from: 2025-06-25
# 1. News
- **Coined by:** **Meetpal S. Kukal** (University of Idaho) and **Mike Hobbins** (University of Colorado/NOAA) — **Earth's Future**, **March 2025**
- **Definition:** **three or more consecutive days** of high **atmospheric evaporative demand (EED)**
- **Measured by:** standardised **short-crop evapotranspiration (ET₀)** — theoretical **12 cm** grass crop
- **Drivers:** temperature, low humidity, wind, solar radiation — ⚠ **not** temperature alone; ⚠ not about rainfall
- **India studies:** **Chattopadhyay & Hulme (1997)** — PET declined 1960s–90s; **IIT Roorkee & NIH (2022)** — rising actual ET in North India, Himalayas
- **Indian collaborator:** **Shailza Sharma** (NIT Jalandhar)
| Aspect | Heatwave | Thirstwave |
|---|---|---|
| **Based on** | high air temperature | high evaporative demand |
| **Impact** | human health | crop water stress |

## Dravidogecko coonoor — new gecko from Nilgiris | from: 2025-06-25
# 1. News
- **Species:** **Dravidogecko coonoor** — endemic to **Coonoor**, **Upper Nilgiris, Tamil Nadu** (Western Ghats)
- **Journal:** **Bionomina**
- **Discovered by:** A. Abinesh, R.S. Naveen, A.N. Srikanthan, S. Babu, **S.R. Ganesh**
- **Earlier thought:** **Hemidactylus anamallensis** (now **Dravidogecko anamallensis**)
- **Count:** **nine** Dravidogecko species in Western Ghats
- **Habitat:** shola forests, plantations, building walls
- ⚠ Found entirely **outside protected areas**
- **Western Ghats:** one of the world's **eight "hottest hotspots"**
