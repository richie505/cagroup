=== TOPIC 2025-06:02 | Polity & Governance

## 50 years of the Emergency — Cabinet resolution | from: 2025-06-28
# 1. News
- **25 June 2025:** Union Cabinet resolution honouring resistance to the Emergency; day called **'Samvidhan Hatya Diwas'**
- **Emergency:** declared **25 June 1975** by PM **Indira Gandhi** on "**internal disturbance**" under **Article 352**; lasted **21 months** till **21 March 1977**
- ⚠ Article **352**, not 356
# 2. Background
| Type | Article |
|---|---|
| **National Emergency** | **352** — war, external aggression, armed rebellion |
| **President's Rule** | **356** (with **365**) |
| **Financial Emergency** | **360** |
- **Emergency provisions:** **Articles 352–360**
- **Features:** Article 19 rights suspended; press censorship; arrests under **MISA**; **ADM Jabalpur v. Shivkant Shukla** (habeas corpus suspended); forced sterilisation
- **42nd Amendment (1976):** "**Mini-Constitution**" — added **Socialist, Secular** to Preamble; DPSPs over FRs; curbed judiciary
- **44th Amendment (1978):** Janata govt — reversed many changes; "internal disturbance" → "**armed rebellion**"

## ECINET used in June 2025 bypolls | from: 2025-06-28
# 1. News
- **ECINET:** **Election Commission Integrated Network** — announced **4 May 2025**; integrates **40+** ECI apps
- **First used:** bypolls of **19 June 2025** in **Kerala, Gujarat, Punjab, West Bengal**; results **23 June**
- **Turnout upload:** directly by **presiding officers** (⚠ not returning officers) before leaving polling station
- **Index Cards:** non-statutory constituency-level statistical reports — auto-filled, published within **72 hours**

## Draft Registration Bill, 2025 | from: 2025-06-24
# 1. News
- **Released by:** **Ministry of Rural Development (MoRD)** — draft for public comments
- **Replaces:** **Registration Act, 1908** (**117 years** old)
- **Why:** land/property disputes are over **two-thirds** of civil cases
# 2. Key provisions
| Section | Provision |
|---|---|
| **Sec 29** | end-to-end **digital** + offline registration; Aadhaar or offline ID; **29(4)** — no denial for lack of Aadhaar |
| **Sec 12** | compulsory registration expanded — **agreement of sale**, **power of attorney**, mortgage by deposit of title deeds, corporate mergers/demergers (Companies Act 2013) |
| **Sec 13** | "optional registration" — undefined |
| **Sec 4(5)** | new **Additional/Assistant Inspector Generals** of Registration |
| **Sec 58, 64** | refusal (e.g. delay over **four months**, wills exempt); **64(3)** — **Inspector General** can cancel fraudulent registration; appeal in **30 days** |
| **Penalty** | jail cut from **7 years** to **3 years** |
- ⚠ Aadhaar is **not** mandatory; penalty is **reduced**, not increased
- **Concern:** delegation to **Common Service Centres (CSCs)**
