## PM SHRI schools | from: 2026-09-10
# 1. Key facts
- **Full form:** **Pradhan Mantri Schools for Rising India**; launched **7 September 2022**
- **Type:** **Centrally Sponsored Scheme** — Department of School Education and Literacy, **Ministry of Education**
- **Target:** **14,500+** schools; **13,092** selected (July 2026, ~**90%**) in **36** States/UTs; **20 lakh+** students
- **Period:** **2022–23 to 2026–27**; **cost:** **₹27,360 crore** (central share **₹18,128 crore**)
- **PAB central share:** **₹2,520 crore** (2023–24) → **₹5,224 crore** (2026–27), ~**107%** rise
- **Coverage:** **725** districts, **8,340** blocks/ULBs; **913 KVS**, **620 NVS**, **80 KGBVs**, **4 NCERT** schools
- **Selection:** 3-stage **Challenge Mode** using **UDISE+**; Expert Committee under **Secretary, School Education**; urban ≥ **70%**, rural ≥ **60%**
- **Six pillars:** curriculum-pedagogy-assessment; access and infrastructure; HR and leadership; management and governance; inclusion and gender equity; beneficiary satisfaction
- **Teachers (UDISE+ 2025–26):** **6,102 Sanskrit**, **1,994 Urdu**, **9,152** regional language
- **DIET support:** up to **₹3 lakh/DIET**; **₹2,500/teacher**
# 2. Background
- India: **14.71 lakh** schools, **24.69 crore** students; NEP **5+3+3+4** structure

## Food fortification and biofortification — hidden hunger | from: 2026-09-09
# 1. Key facts
- **Global Nutrition Report 2026:** ~**2.6 billion** people cannot afford a healthy diet
- **WHO:** India has ~**one-third** of global undernutrition
- **Hidden hunger:** deficiency of micronutrients (iron, iodine, zinc, folic acid, vit A, B12, D)
- **Global:** **100+** countries iodise salt (prevented ~**750 million** goitre cases in **25 years**); cereal fortified in **86** countries; oil in **30+**
- **India biofortified varieties (2014–2024):** **152** — wheat **53**, millets **26**, maize **24**, oilseeds **21**, rice **14**, pulses **9**, amaranth **5**
- **Examples:** iron rice (**Philippines**), iron pearl millet (**India**), iron beans (**Rwanda**)
# 2. Rice fortification timeline
| Year | Event |
|---|---|
| **2018** | **Anemia Mukt Bharat** |
| **2019** | Pilot — **iron**, **folic acid**, **vitamin B12** |
| **March 2024** | All custom-milled rice in PDS, ICDS, PM-POSHAN, PMGKAY fortified |
| **Till Dec 2028** | Extension — **₹17,082 crore** |
| **Feb 2026** | ⚠ Temporarily discontinued under **PMGKAY** (based on **IIT Kharagpur** report); not PDS/ICDS/MDM |
- **PM-POSHAN:** **Double Fortified Salt**, oil with **vitamins A and D**, fortified rice

## Vibrant Villages Programme — VVP-I and VVP-II | from: 2026-09-07
# 1. Key facts
- **Launched:** **2023**; border villages called the nation's **"first villages"**
- **Nodal ministry:** **Ministry of Home Affairs**; **High-Powered Committee** under **Cabinet Secretary** can relax guidelines
| Feature | VVP-I | VVP-II |
|---|---|---|
| **Type** | **Centrally Sponsored Scheme** | **Central Sector Scheme** (100% Centre) |
| **Focus** | **Northern border** villages | Other international land borders |
| **Launched at** | **Kibithoo**, **Anjaw**, Arunachal Pradesh | **Nathanpur**, **Cachar**, Assam (**2026**) |
| **Outlay** | **₹4,800 crore** | **₹6,839 crore** |
| **Period** | **2022–23 to 2026–27** | Up to **2028–29** |
| **Villages** | **662** | **1,954** |
- **Combined outlay:** **₹11,639 crore**; VVP-I announced in **Budget 2022–23**
- **VVP-I states/UT:** **Arunachal Pradesh**, **Himachal Pradesh**, **Sikkim**, **Uttarakhand**, **Ladakh**
- **VVP-II:** **15 States + 2 UTs** (J&K, Ladakh); approved **2025**; ⚠ excludes only **blocks** already in VVP-I, not whole States
- Roads under **PMGSY-IV** (Ministry of Rural Development); VVP-I uses **hub-and-spoke** livelihood model
- **ITBP** buys milk, vegetables, eggs from vibrant villages in Arunachal; reverse migration in **Kurung Kumey**, **Dibang Valley**, **Shi-Yomi**
- **Viksit Vibrant Village Programme:** youth volunteers in Ladakh, HP, Uttarakhand
# 2. Background
- **Border Area Development Programme (BADP):** **1986–87**, began on western border — now in **sunset phase**
- ⚠ Longest land border — **Bangladesh**; Kibithoo linked to **1962** war
