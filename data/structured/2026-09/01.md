## Krishna water sharing — AP seeks 66:34 formula | from: 2026-09-09, CARE-2026-09
# 1. News
- **AP:** continue **66:34** formula (AP:Telangana) until **July 2027**; **Telangana** demands **50:50**
- Only ~**100 TMC-ft** left; ~**212 TMC-ft** inflow this season (**El Niño**, low rain)
- Meeting convened by **Union Ministry of Jal Shakti** — remaining water for **drinking**, livestock; crops ruled out

- **1 TMC-ft** = **1 billion cubic feet** ≈ **28.32 million m³**

## Valmiki Caves near Gooty | from: 2026-09-30, CARE-2026-09
# 1. Key facts
- Near **Gooty Fort**, Gooty–Mantralayam Road; **Erramala hills**, **Rayalaseema**
- Rocks ~**1.6 billion years** old — **dolomite** and **limestone**; traces of ancient **blue-green algae**
- Among the **deepest** cave systems in South India; stalactites, stalagmites; some formations sound like **tabla or bell**
- **Oxygen isotopes** in stalagmites show stronger monsoons after the last Ice Age (~**11,700 years** ago)
- Underground water bodies — **Edu Bavulu** / **Gundalu**
- Linked to **Sage Valmiki** (Ramayana); worship on **Maha Shivaratri**
- Surveyed by **H.D. Gebauer** (1980s); German tourists explored in **1993**; **300+** steps now
