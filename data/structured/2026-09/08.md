## NCRB cybercrime data and Lokniti-CSDS scam study | from: 2026-09-30
# 1. NCRB
- **Cybercrime:** **86,420** (2023) → **1,01,928** (2024), up **17.9%**; ⚠ overall crime **fell 6%**
# 2. Lokniti-CSDS + Common Cause study
- **8,306** citizens, **16** States — part of **Status of Policing in India Report 2026**
- **13%** were direct cyber-crime victims (2–3 yrs): **financial fraud 54%**, hacking **13%**, data theft **11%**, cyberbullying **7%**, sexual harassment **4%**
- Fake delivery calls **33%**; investment scams **31%**; bank impersonation **27%**
- High internet users: **46%+** moderate/high exposure vs **19%** of non-users
- ⚠ Graduates (**59%**) and wealthiest (**57%**) faced more financial fraud than uneducated (**40%**)/poor (**47%**)
- **23%** lost over **₹20,000**
