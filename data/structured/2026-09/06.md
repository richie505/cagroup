## EOS-05 on GSLV-F17 — first imaging satellite from geosynchronous orbit | from: 2026-09-08, CA360-2026-09, OL-2026-09
# 1. Mission
- **Launched:** **4 September 2026**, **GSLV-F17**, **Second Launch Pad**, **SDSC-SHAR Sriharikota**
- ⚠ India's **first imaging satellite from geosynchronous orbit**
- **Mass:** ~**2,367 kg**; placed in **Sub-GTO** (perigee **170 km**, apogee **28,934 km**, inclination **19.28°**, azimuth **104°**) — not directly in GEO
# 2. GSLV-F17
- **19th** GSLV flight; height **51.7 m**; lift-off mass **420.5 t**; **3 stages**; **4 m** ogive fairing
| Stage | Detail |
|---|---|
| **GS1** | **S139** solid core + **4 L40H** liquid strap-ons |
| **GS2** | **GL40HT** liquid stage |
| **GS3** | **CUS15** cryogenic (LH2 + LOX) |
- **GSLV Mk II capacity:** ~**2,250 kg** to GTO, **6,000 kg** to LEO; first flight **18 April 2001**; indigenous cryo stage from **GSLV-D5** (**January 2014**)
# 3. Orbits background
- **LEO** 160–2,000 km (orbit 90–120 min) — ~**15,562** active payloads (Sept 4, 2026); India has **22** operational LEO satellites (April 2026 ISRO report)
- **Geosynchronous** period ~**23 h 56 min** — may be elliptical/inclined; **geostationary** — circular, equatorial, zero inclination, ~**35,786 km**; India has **31** GEO satellites
- **Galileo** (Europe): **30** satellites in MEO
- CA-360: EOS-05 also called **GISAT-1A**; images subcontinent every **30 min** (priority areas ~**5 min**) from ~**36,000 km**
# 4. CA-360 extras
- **Uses:** **agriculture**, land resources, environmental and **weather monitoring**, **disaster management**
- ⚠ Unlike LEO/**Sun-Synchronous** EO satellites, it watches the **same large area** with very high **temporal frequency**
- **Geostationary orbit:** ~**35,786 km** above the equator; period ~**24 hours**; appears stationary — used for communications, weather, continuous regional monitoring
- **SDSC:** **Sriharikota**, AP — ISRO's principal launch centre; former name **Sriharikota Range**; named after Prof. **Satish Dhawan**

## BAM-H24 rocket propellant patent | from: CA360-2026-09, OL-2026-09
# 1. News
- **BAM-H24** — high-energy **rocket-propellant** material — got an **Indian patent**
- Developed by **DRDO** and **University of Hyderabad**'s **Advanced Centre of Research in High Energy Materials (ACRHEM)**
- **Boron-, hydrogen- and nitrogen-rich** compound; high chemical and **thermal stability**; resists **friction** — safer than conventional high-energy materials

# 2. Background

- **Solid propellant:** fuel + oxidiser in a solid mixture — long storage, rapid readiness
- **DRDO:** est. **1958**; under **Department of Defence Research and Development**, Ministry of Defence
