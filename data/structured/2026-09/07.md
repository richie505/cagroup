## Nepal flash flood — rock–ice collapse | from: 2026-09-01
# 1. Event
- **Date:** **August 26, 2026** — rock–ice collapse near **Nepal–China border**
- **Toll:** **579 deaths** in Nepal; ~**2,000 missing**; **93,000+** need assistance; Kailash Mansarovar pilgrims missing
- Initially read as a **4.4-magnitude** quake; **USGS** said signal was from the collapse; energy equal to ~**magnitude 5.2**
- Material plunged ~**1 km**; ⚠ **not a conventional GLOF** — a **rock–ice collapse-triggered debris flood**
- **Nepal:** ~**475 MW** hydropower capacity lost
- **China:** **Gyirong Port** (Tibet) buried — upgraded at **$175.8 million**
- **India:** Rivers **Gandak**, **Kosi**, **Ganga**; **Bihar** and **UP** alert; **Valmikinagar Barrage** watched; **Maharajganj**, **Kushinagar**; India sent **47.5 tonnes** of relief
# 2. India's Himalayan hazard measures
- **National GLOF Risk Mitigation Programme:** **₹150 crore** — **Arunachal Pradesh**, **Himachal Pradesh**, **Sikkim**, **Uttarakhand**
- **CWC + NRSC-ISRO** monitoring from 2025: **2,843** water bodies incl. **2,485** glacial lakes
- **NDMA Guidelines on GLOFs:** **2020**; **Dam Safety Act:** **2021**
- **National Landslide Risk Mitigation Project:** **₹1,000 crore**, **15** States

|---|---|

# 2. Background

- **Adaptations:** **pneumatophores** (breathing roots), **prop roots**, **vivipary**; mangroves called **"Kidneys of the Planet"**
- **Bhitarkanika** (Odisha) — Ramsar, **estuarine crocodiles**; **Sundarbans** — largest, Royal Bengal Tiger

|---|---|

- Combined **1,632 sq km**; **Great Nicobar** also has a **₹72,000-crore** project (transshipment port, airport, power plant, 2 cities)
# 2. Background
- **Polymetallic nodules:** nickel, cobalt, manganese, copper, REEs; ISA zones — **Clarion–Clipperton** (off Mexico), **Central Indian Ocean Basin**, **Peru Basin**
- **Polymetallic sulphides:** copper, zinc, lead, silver, gold
- **ISA contracts:** **2016** (Central Indian Ridge, Southwest Indian Ridge) and **September 2025** — ⚠ India **first** country with **two** ISA sulphide contracts; executed by **NCPOR, Goa**
- **EEZ** — **200 nm** (~370 km) under **UNCLOS**; seabed beyond = **common heritage of mankind**
- **Offshore Areas Mineral (D&R) Act, 2002**; India coastline **11,098 km**; UN Ocean Decade **2021–2030**
- **Deep Ocean Mission:** **2021**, **₹4,077 crore** (MoES)
- **Sagar Manthan** ocean research vessel — launched by **GRSE** on **September 9** for **NCPOR** (89.5 m, 5,900 GT)
- **Sagar Manthan** (CA-360): **₹840 crore**, built at **Rishi Bankim Shipyard**, Kolkata; operational **2028**; surveys to **6 km** depth; for equatorial Indian Ocean, Bay of Bengal, Antarctic waters
- **NCPOR:** est. **1998**, **Goa**; **Samudrayaan** — manned deep-sea mission under Deep Ocean Mission (2021–2026)
