## Legal Metrology (Indian Standard Time) Rules, 2026 | from: 2026-09-02
# 1. Rules
- **Notified:** **August 27, 2026** by the Union government
- Make **IST** the single legal reference for legal, administrative, commercial and official purposes
- **Come into force:** **180 days** after Gazette publication
- Organisations must sync with time **traceable to IST**; ⚠ **no** need for every organisation to own an **atomic clock**
- **Enforcement:** **Legal Metrology Department**; technical guidance by **CSIR-NPL**
- Other time zones may be displayed only **along with IST**
# 2. IST facts
- **Offset:** **UTC+5:30**; maintained by **CSIR–National Physical Laboratory** (India's **National Metrology Institute**)
- **IST®** trademark registered by CSIR-NPL in **2024**; uncertainty **< 3 nanoseconds** vs UTC
- **5 Regional Reference Standard Laboratories (RRSLs)** keep secondary time scales
- **White Rabbit**-based IST dissemination network commissioned **July 2026** at **RRSL Bengaluru**
| Technology | Accuracy |
|---|---|
| **NTP** | **Milliseconds** |
| **PTP** | **Microseconds** |
| **Satellite links** | **Nanoseconds** |
| **Optical links** | **Picoseconds** |
# 3. Background
- **NavIC** (earlier **IRNSS**) — developed after GPS data was denied during the **Kargil War**; carries atomic clocks

## SC: grounds of arrest in writing | from: CA360-2026-09, OL-2026-09
# 1. Ruling
- Grounds of arrest must be given **in writing** and in a language the accused understands — under **Article 22(1)**, for all offences incl. **PMLA** and **UAPA**
- ⚠ Written grounds at least **2 hours** before production for remand, else arrest illegal
- **Section 47, BNSS** — inform grounds and bail rights
- Exceptional cases: oral first, written within reasonable time; failure → arrest and remand **illegal**
- Re-arrest: written grounds + permission of **Judicial Magistrate**; probe to another officer; disciplinary action on erring officer
