## UPI Merchant Discount Rate framework | from: 2026-09-18
# 1. Key facts
- **NPCI** MDR framework effective **October 15, 2026**
- Follows govt exemption of UPI payments up to **₹2,000** and **RuPay debit** cards from bank charges
| Category | MDR |
|---|---|
| **Merchant payment ≤ ₹2,000** | **Nil** |
| **Merchant payment > ₹2,000** | **0.4%** |
| **₹75,000 or more** | Max **₹300** |
| **Essential sectors** (railways, telecom, fuel, electricity, education, agri inputs...) ≥ ₹2,000 | Flat **₹5** |
| **Capital market** | **0.02%**, cap **₹300** |
| **P2P / P2PM / AutoPay** | **Nil** |
- **P2PM** (micro-merchants up to **₹1 lakh/month**) — moved to P2M if over limit for **3 consecutive months**
- **MDR split:** payer bank **40%**, payee bank **30%**, UPI app (TPAP) **20%**, PSP **10%**
- **5%** of MDR collections to a small-merchant adoption fund
- UPI running cost ~**₹20,000 crore/yr**; theoretical max MDR ~**₹28,000 crore/yr**
- Card MDR: debit up to **0.90%**; credit **1.5–2.5%**
# 2. Data
- P2P ~**37%** + merchant ≤ ₹2,000 **60.5%** = **97.5%** volume free; P2M > ₹2,000 = **2.5%** volume but ~two-thirds of merchant value
- **2025–26:** **24,000 crore+** UPI txns worth **₹314 lakh crore**
- **Yes Bank** — payer bank in **50%+** txns (ICICI ~18.3%), payee ~**55%** (Axis ~19%); **PhonePe ~46%**, **Google Pay ~32%**
# 3. Background — NPCI
- In-principle approval by RBI DPSS **24 September 2009**; took NFS from **IDRBT Hyderabad** on **14 December 2009**
- Initiative of **RBI** and **IBA**; not-for-profit — **Section 8, Companies Act 2013** (earlier Section 25, 1956); under **PSS Act 2007**
- Systems: UPI, RuPay, IMPS, NACH, AePS, APBS, NFS, **NETC FASTag**

## ECLGS 5.0 — Emergency Credit Line Guarantee Scheme | from: 2026-09-07
# 1. Key facts
- **Approved:** **2026**; facilitates additional credit up to **₹2.55 lakh crore**
- **Implemented by:** **National Credit Guarantee Trustee Company (NCGTC)** via **Member Lending Institutions**
- **Valid till:** **March 31, 2027** or until **₹2.55 lakh crore** guarantees, whichever earlier
- **Eligibility:** working capital with MLIs as on **March 31, 2026**; overdue ≤ **60 days**
- **Access:** **Jan Samarth Portal**; ⚠ guarantee is **not a loan waiver**
| Feature | MSMEs / non-MSMEs | Scheduled passenger airlines |
|---|---|---|
| **Guarantee cover** | **100%** MSME / **90%** non-MSME | **90%** |
| **Extra credit** | **20%** of peak working capital (Q4 FY 2025–26) | Up to **100%** of eligible base |
| **Cap** | **₹100 crore** per borrower | **₹1,500 crore** (promoter equity beyond **₹1,000 crore**) |
| **Interest** | Benchmark + **0.75 pp**, ceiling **9%**; NBFC max **13%** | Lender's board policy |
| **Tenure** | **5 years** incl. **1-year** moratorium | **7 years** incl. **2-year** moratorium |
| **Guarantee fee** | **Nil** | **Nil** |
- Bank benchmark: **EBLR** for MSMEs, **MCLR** for non-MSMEs
- **Non-MSME excluded sectors:** NBFCs, power, telecom, sugar and ethanol, IT, paper, education, beverages (except tea/coffee), tobacco
- Overlap adjusted with **Credit Guarantee Scheme for Exporters (CGSE)**
# 2. Evolution
| Phase | Coverage |
|---|---|
| **1.0 (2020)** | MSMEs, Mudra — **Aatmanirbhar Bharat** package (COVID) |
| **2.0** | **Kamath Committee** stressed sectors + healthcare |
| **3.0** | Hospitality, travel, tourism, civil aviation |
| **4.0** | Hospitals, oxygen facilities |
| **5.0** | External disruptions — MSMEs, non-MSMEs, airlines |
- Earlier phases ended in **2023**
