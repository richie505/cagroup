# 7. Background
- **Article 18(1):** no titles except **military** and **academic** distinctions
- **Balaji Raghavan v. Union of India (1996):** national awards are **not titles**; cannot be used as prefix/suffix
- **Padma Awards instituted:** **1954**; suspended **1977–80** and **1992–95**
- ⚠ **Bharat Ratna:** max **three** per year
- Govt servants (incl. PSUs) not eligible except **doctors** and **scientists**
