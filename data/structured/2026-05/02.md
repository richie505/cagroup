## CIC: BCCI not a public authority under RTI | from: 2026-05-21
# 1. Ruling
- **Central Information Commission**, **18 May 2026**: **BCCI** is **not** a "public authority" under **Section 2(h)** of **RTI Act, 2005**
- BCCI — private society registered under **Tamil Nadu Societies Registration Act, 1975**; not substantially controlled or financed by govt
- ⚠ **2018:** CIC (Info Commissioner **M. Sridhar Acharyulu**) had held BCCI a public authority — set aside by **Madras HC**
# 2. Cases and reports
| Case / report | Holding |
|---|---|
| **Zee Telefilms v. Union of India (2005)** | BCCI **not "State"** under **Article 12** |
| **Thalappalam Service Coop. Bank (2013)** | "Control" = **substantial** control |
| **BCCI v. Cricket Association of Bihar (2016)** | **Lodha Committee** reforms; BCCI subject to **Article 226** review |
| **Law Commission 275th Report (2018)** | Recommended BCCI under RTI |
- **National Sports Governance Act, 2025:** govt-funded sports bodies under RTI only to extent of those funds
- **RTI Section 24 / Second Schedule:** security bodies exempt (e.g. **NSCS**, **Assam Rifles**) except corruption and human rights
