## Somnath Swabhiman Parv | from: 2026-05-11, OL-2026-05
# 1. Why
- Marks **1000 years** since first recorded attack (**January 1026**) and **75 years** since reopening (**May 1951**); **PM Modi** visited on **11 May**
- Special pujas for next **1,000 days**; theme "**Vikas Bhi, Virasat Bhi**"
- **Jan 2026:** **72-hour Omkar** chanting, **Shaurya Yatra**, procession of **108 horses**
# 2. Temple facts
- **Where:** **Prabhas Patan**, near **Veraval**, **Saurashtra** coast, **Gujarat**
- ⚠ **First** of the **12 Jyotirlingas** (Dwadasha Jyotirlinga Stotram)
- **Present temple consecrated:** **Dr. Rajendra Prasad**, **11 May 1951**; style **Kailash Mahameru Prasad**
- **Restorers:** **Kumarapala** (12th c.), King of **Junagarh** (13th c.), **Ahilyabai Holkar** (18th c.), **Sardar Patel** (resolved 1947)
- **Al-Biruni** described it in **Kitab-ul-Hind**
| Feature | Size |
|---|---|
| **Shikhar** | **150 ft** |
| **Kalash** | **10 tonne** |
| **Dhwajdand** | **27 ft** |
# 3. Trust facts
- **Swachh Iconic Place:** **2018**; Miyawaki forest **72,000 sq ft**; **Somganga jal**
- **Shree Somnath Trust:** **906** employees, **262** women; **Bilva Van** managed by women
- One-liner calls it **Somnath Amrit Mahotsav** — 1,000 years of legacy and post-independence reconstruction
