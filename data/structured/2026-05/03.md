## VB-G RAM G replacing MGNREGA — draft rules | from: 2026-05-28, CA360-2026-05
# 1. Scheme
- **Viksit Bharat–Guarantee for Rozgar and Ajeevika Mission (Gramin) Act, 2025 (VB-G RAM G)** replaces **MGNREGA** (2005)
- **Ministry of Rural Development** released draft rules (wages, unemployment allowance, allocation, Central Gramin Rozgar Guarantee Council)
| Feature | MGNREGA | VB-G RAM G |
|---|---|---|
| **Workdays** | **100** | **125** |
| **Wage funding** | Centre **100%** | States bear **40%** (NE/Himalayan & UTs with legislature **90:10**; UTs without legislature **100%** Centre) |
| **Allocation** | State labour budgets | Centre's **normative allocation** (**16th FC** horizontal devolution formula) |
| **Farm season** | — | **60-day pause** in peak sowing/harvest |
| **Payment** | Bank/post office | **DBT** |
- Old job cards valid after renewal + **e-KYC** until **Gramin Rozgar Guarantee Cards** issued
- Part of allocation **performance-linked** (timely wages, **social audit**)
- ⚠ MGNREGA: **unskilled** manual work; at least **one-third** women
# 2. Notification
- Govt notified implementation — Act to come into force from **31 July 2026**; allocation **₹95,692 crore**
- **Childcare facilities** at worksites made mandatory

## Hantavirus outbreak and zoonotic spillovers | from: 2026-05-15
# 1. News
- **Hantavirus** outbreak renewed concern on **zoonotic spillover** (animal to human)
- **Hantavirus spread:** urine, droppings, saliva of infected **rodents**
- Drivers: deforestation, climate change, industrial livestock farming, global travel
- **Vectors expanding:** **Aedes aegypti**, **Aedes albopictus**; **Anopheline** mosquitoes at higher elevations
- Approach: **One Health** (human + animal + environment)
# 2. Zoonoses
| Disease | Source |
|---|---|
| **Rabies** | Dogs, bats |
| **Nipah** | **Bats**, pigs, contaminated fruit |
| **Ebola** | Bats, wild animals |
| **Plague** | Fleas on rodents |
| **Leptospirosis** | Water/soil with animal urine |
| **Brucellosis** | Cattle, goats, unpasteurised milk |
