## AP beach sand minerals hub | from: 2026-05-01, CARE-2026-05
# 1. Why in news
- **AP** emerging as leading State in exploration of **Beach Sand Minerals (BSM)** along eastern coast
- **Share:** ~**25%** of India's reserves — ⚠ **second** only to **Tamil Nadu**
- **Reserves:** AP ~**211 million tonnes** out of India's ~**838 million tonnes**
- ⚠ AP has **largest** reserves of **rutile** and **monazite**; **second-largest** of **ilmenite**
# 2. Where
- **Northern coast:** **Srikakulam**, **Visakhapatnam** — **Gara**, **Etcherla**, **Ranasthalam**, **Bheemunipatnam**, **Nakkapalli**
- **Also:** **West Godavari**, **Krishna**, **Konaseema** coastal belts
- **Formation:** **Godavari** and **Krishna** carry sediments; waves/tides concentrate heavy minerals → **placer deposits**
# 3. Minerals and uses
| Mineral | Use |
|---|---|
| **Ilmenite** | **Titanium dioxide** — paints, plastics, paper |
| **Rutile** | Higher-grade titanium ore → **titanium metal** (aircraft, defence) |
| **Monazite** | **Rare earth elements** + **thorium** (nuclear) — highly regulated |
| **Zircon** | **Ceramics**, nuclear reactors |
| **Garnet** | **Abrasive** — sandblasting, waterjet cutting |
| **Sillimanite** | **Refractory** — furnace linings, kilns |
# 4. Exam angle
- **Thorium** from monazite → India's **three-stage nuclear programme**
- **Regulation:** **CRZ** norms + **EIA**
