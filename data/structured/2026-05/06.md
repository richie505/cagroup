## Operation Sindoor — lessons and nuclear doctrine | from: 2026-05-07
# 1. Key points
- India's calibrated response after **Pahalgam terror attack** — "**aggression with restraint**"; multi-domain (cyber, **electronic warfare**, intelligence fusion, precision strikes)
- **Grey zone warfare:** hostile acts below threshold of conventional war
# 2. India's Nuclear Doctrine
- **Adopted:** **January 2003** (after **Pokhran-II**)
- **Credible Minimum Deterrence**; **No First Use (NFU)**; **massive retaliation**
- **Authorised by:** civilian **Nuclear Command Authority (NCA)**
- No use against **non-nuclear weapon states**

## IMD block-level monsoon forecast and Bharat Forecast System | from: 2026-05-14, CA360-2026-05
# 1. New system
- **IMD** unveiled **block-level** monsoon forecast — **3,196 blocks** in **15 States + 1 UT** (monsoon core zone)
- **Four-week** probabilistic forecast; blends two models (framework by **IITM**); uses **AI**
- Developed at request of **Ministry of Agriculture**
- **1-km resolution pilot** for **Uttar Pradesh** — valid **10 days** (dense automatic weather stations)
# 2. Bharat Forecast System (BFS)
- Developed by **IITM, Pune**; resolution **6 km** (replaced **12 km** model); ⚠ among world's highest-resolution operational models (US/UK/EU ~**9–14 km**)
- **Supercomputers:** **Arka** (IITM Pune), **Arunika** (**NCMRWF**, Delhi)
- **64%** better accuracy; extreme events prediction up to **30%** better
# 3. Background
- Monsoon normally reaches **Mumbai ~10 June**, **Delhi ~29 June**
- **IOD:** SST difference between **western** and **eastern** tropical **Indian Ocean**; positive IOD can offset El Niño
# 4. More details
- ⚠ Called India's **first AI-based** block-level monsoon forecast; with **IITM** and **NCMRWF** (**Noida**); AI + **NWP**; error margin ~**4 days**
- **IMD:** est. **1875**

## SMILE mission — ESA and China | from: 2026-05-19, CA360-2026-05, OL-2026-05
# 1. Facts
- **SMILE:** **Solar wind Magnetosphere Ionosphere Link Explorer** — joint mission of **ESA** and **Chinese Academy of Sciences (CAS)**
- **Launch:** **19 May 2026** on **Vega-C** from **Europe's Spaceport, French Guiana**
- ⚠ **First** mission to observe Earth's **magnetosphere in X-ray** light
- **Instruments (4):** **Soft X-ray Imager**, **Ultraviolet Aurora Imager**, **Light Ion Analyser**, **Magnetometer**
- **Mission life:** ~**3 years**
- ⚠ ESA and CAS's **first full-scale joint** space mission; studies Earth's response to solar storms and **CMEs**
- **ESA:** est. **1975**, HQ **Paris**; **CAS:** est. **1949**, HQ **Beijing**
# 2. Background
- **Magnetosphere:** region controlled by Earth's magnetic field; deflects **solar wind**; **CMEs**, **solar flares** cause space weather; **auroras** near poles
- **LVM3 (GSLV Mk III):** **three-stage** — 2 solid strap-ons, liquid core, cryogenic upper stage
