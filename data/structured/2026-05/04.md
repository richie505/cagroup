## Rupee depreciation — to defend or not | from: 2026-05-26
# 1. Key points
- RBI approach: curb **excess volatility**, no specific exchange-rate target (**BIS** paper)
- **Forex reserves:** ~**$701.4 bn** on **16 January 2026** — ~**11 months** of goods imports (PIB)
- RBI defends rupee by **selling dollars** (absorbs rupees)
- Weak rupee → **imported inflation**; export gain limited due to import content and **price-taker** status
# 2. Background
- **Forex reserves components:** foreign currency assets, **gold**, **SDRs**, **reserve tranche** in IMF
- Expansionary monetary policy does **not** stop rupee slide; **Masala Bonds**, easier **ECB** help

## IBAM vs EU CBAM | from: 2026-05-04
# 1. News
- **EU CBAM** financially binding from **January 2026**; India got **no exemption** despite **India–EU FTA**
- India considering **India Border Adjustment Mechanism (IBAM)** — charge on exports to CBAM countries so carbon revenue stays in India
# 2. CBAM basics
- **By:** **European Union** — carbon cost on imports equal to **EU ETS** price; aims to stop **carbon leakage**
- **Sectors:** **cement**, **iron and steel**, **aluminium**, **fertilisers**, **electricity**, **hydrogen**
- Importers buy **CBAM certificates**; carbon price already paid at origin can be **deducted**
# 3. India's tools
- **Carbon Credit Trading Scheme (CCTS)** — domestic carbon market
- **Annex 14-A** of India–EU FTA — technical dialogue on carbon measures

## IBC (Amendment) Act, 2026 | from: 2026-05-29
# 1. IBC performance (till March 2026)
- **CIRPs admitted:** **8,987**; resolved via plans: **1,419**
- **Realised:** ~**₹4.32 lakh crore** — **116.85%** of liquidation value, **94.56%** of fair value
# 2. Key changes in 2026 Amendment
- Adjudicating Authority to decide admission within **14 days** (record reasons if delayed)
- **Withdrawal** barred before CoC constituted and after resolution plans invited
- **CoC** role extended into **liquidation** — can replace liquidator
- **Guarantor assets** may be included; moratorium covers guarantee situations
- **Dissenting creditors** get at least lower of liquidation value or **Section 53 waterfall** amount
- Defines **avoidance transaction**, **fraudulent trading**, **wrongful trading**, **service provider**
- One-time **restoration** of resolution before liquidation; new **creditor-led insolvency process**
# 3. Evolution
| Year | Change |
|---|---|
| **2016** | IBC enacted; **IBBI** set up **1 October 2016** |
| **2018** | **Section 29A** eligibility, voting thresholds |
| **2019** | Overall limit **330 days** |
| **2020** | COVID suspension; immunity post-resolution |
| **2021** | **Pre-packaged** insolvency for **MSMEs** |
- **CIRP:** **180 days**, extendable to **330**; supervised by **NCLT**; appeals to **NCLAT**
