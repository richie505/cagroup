## Rupee depreciation — to defend or not | from: 2026-05-26
# 1. Key points

# 2. Background

- Expansionary monetary policy does **not** stop rupee slide; **Masala Bonds**, easier **ECB** help

## IBAM vs EU CBAM | from: 2026-05-04
# 1. News
- **EU CBAM** financially binding from **January 2026**; India got **no exemption** despite **India–EU FTA**
- India considering **India Border Adjustment Mechanism (IBAM)** — charge on exports to CBAM countries so carbon revenue stays in India
# 2. CBAM basics
- **By:** **European Union** — carbon cost on imports equal to **EU ETS** price; aims to stop **carbon leakage**
- **Sectors:** **cement**, **iron and steel**, **aluminium**, **fertilisers**, **electricity**, **hydrogen**
- Importers buy **CBAM certificates**; carbon price already paid at origin can be **deducted**
# 3. India's tools
- **Carbon Credit Trading Scheme (CCTS)** — domestic carbon market
- **Annex 14-A** of India–EU FTA — technical dialogue on carbon measures

## Jaggery production and processing | from: 2026-05-18, CA360-2026-05, OL-2026-05

- ⚠ India produces over **70%** of world's **jaggery** — largest producer

- **Exports:** **$197 mn** (2015–16) → **$406.8 mn** (2024–25) — **+106.5%** value, **+61.2%** volume

## Punjab paddy dwarfing disease | from: 2026-05-22
# 1. Facts
- **Cause:** **Southern Rice Black-Streaked Dwarf Virus (SRBSDV)**
- **Vector:** **white-backed plant hopper** — **persistent propagative** transmission
- ⚠ **First reported in Punjab:** **2022**
- Symptoms ~**1 month** after transplantation; plants stunted to half or one-third; yield fell from **30–32 q/acre** to **1–2 q/acre** in severe cases
- **Control:** **yellow-light traps**, weed removal, **PAU**-recommended insecticides; transplant **20–25 June**

## Salaya port and Gujarat's dhow trade | from: 2026-05-29
# 1. News
- From **21 May**, **Mechanised Sailing Vessels (MSVs)** of **Salaya** and **Okha** allowed crew sign-on/sign-off at **Porbandar Immigration Check Post** — safe return before monsoon amid **West Asia** conflict / **Strait of Hormuz** risk
# 2. Facts
- **Salaya:** **Gulf of Kutch**, Gujarat — tidal port with creeks, mangroves; boat repair hub
- **MSVs (dhows):** **200–3,000 MT** capacity (mostly ~1,000 MT); diesel-run
- India ~**450–500** Indian-flagged MSVs; Gujarat ~**275**; Salaya ~**175**; **Mandvi** ~**60**
- Trade: ~**5 lakh tonnes** exports, ~**50,000 tonnes** imports/year — Gulf, Indian Ocean, East Africa
