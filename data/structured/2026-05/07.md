## Climate terms — Western Disturbance, ENSO, heatwave | from: 2026-05-08
# 1. News
- **IMD** forecast fresh **Western Disturbance** over North India **11–13 May**
- **US Climate Prediction Center (April 2026):** ENSO-neutral now; **El Niño** likely in **May–July 2026** — **61%** chance, persisting to end-2026
# 2. Terms
- **Western Disturbance:** east-moving rain system from beyond **Afghanistan/Iran**; moisture from **Mediterranean**, Black, Caspian, Arabian seas; helps **wheat**
- **ENSO phases:** **El Niño** (warm), **La Niña** (cool), **Neutral**
| IMD heatwave criterion | Value |
|---|---|
| **Minimum max temp** | **40°C** plains, **30°C** hills |
| **Heat wave (departure)** | **4.5–6.4°C** above normal |
| **Severe heat wave (departure)** | over **6.4°C** |
| **Heat wave (absolute)** | **45°C** |
| **Severe heat wave (absolute)** | **47°C** |
- **Wet bulb temperature:** above **35°C** sustained can be fatal
- **Feels-like (apparent) temperature:** temp + humidity + wind

## Solid Waste Management Rules, 2026 | from: 2026-05-12
# 1. Facts
- **In force:** **1 April 2026**, replacing **SWM Rules 2016**
- **Aims:** source segregation, bulk generators, scientific processing, landfill reduction, **legacy dumpsite** remediation, **circular economy**, digital monitoring (reporting to **CPCB**)
- ⚠ Extends duties to **rural local bodies** (Gram Panchayats)
# 2. Background
- **Environment Protection Act, 1986** enacted under **Article 253** (treaty implementation) — after **Stockholm Declaration 1972**
- **Subsidiarity:** functions at lowest capable level
