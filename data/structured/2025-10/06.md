## Chandrayaan-2's CHACE-2 records first CME impact on Moon | from: 2025-10-21
# 1. News
- **Payload:** **CHACE-2** (**Chandra's Atmospheric Composition Explorer-2**) on **Chandrayaan-2 orbiter**
- ⚠ **First-ever** direct observation of a **Coronal Mass Ejection (CME)** impact on the **lunar exosphere**
- Dayside exosphere density rose by **more than one order of magnitude** (sodium, potassium, argon knocked off regolith)
- **Developed by:** ISRO's **Space Physics Laboratory**
# 2. Background
- **Chandrayaan-2:** launched **22 July 2019** on **GSLV Mk-III-M1**; **eight** payloads; **Vikram** lander failed, orbiter operational
- **LUPEX:** India–Japan lunar mission

## Gene-edited rice varieties under scrutiny | from: 2025-10-31
# 1. Varieties
| Variety | Developer | Parent |
|---|---|---|
| **DRR Rice 100 (Kamala)** | **ICAR-IIRR, Hyderabad** | **Samba Mahsuri (BPT-5204)** |
| **Pusa DST Rice 1** | **ICAR-IARI, New Delhi** | **Cotton Dora Sannalu (MTU 1010)** |
- **Technology:** **CRISPR-Cas9**; claimed **17–30%** higher yield, **20 days** early maturity, drought/salinity tolerance; non-transgenic
# 2. Criticism
- **Coalition for a GM-Free India** analysed **AICRIP** (2023–24) data — Pusa DST Rice 1 equal/lower yield in **12 of 20** sites; Kamala underperformed in **8 of 19**; Kamala flowering **101 vs 104** days
- Activist **Kavitha Kuruganti**
# 3. Background
- **DBT guidelines (2022):** gene-edited crops without foreign DNA **not treated as GMOs**; oversight **RCGM**, **GEAC**
- GM examples: **Bt Cotton**, **GM Mustard**
