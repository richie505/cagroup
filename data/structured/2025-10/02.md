## UPSC centenary — 100 years (1 October 2025) | from: 2025-10-01
# 1. News
- **Date:** **1 October 2025** — UPSC entered its **centenary year**
# 2. History
| Year | Milestone |
|---|---|
| **1919** | **Government of India Act, 1919** first envisaged an independent recruitment body |
| **1924** | **Lee Commission** recommended it |
| **October 1926** | **Public Service Commission** set up — first chairman **Sir Ross Barker** |
| **1935** | **GoI Act, 1935** — **Federal Public Service Commission** |
| **1950** | Became **UPSC** under **Articles 315–323** |
# 3. Features
- **CSE:** world's **largest competitive examination** — **10–12 lakh** applicants; **2,500+** centres
- **Mains:** **48** optional subjects; answers in **English** or any of the **22** constitutionally recognised languages
- **Reforms:** online portals, **face-recognition technology**, **PRATIBHA Setu** (helps interview-stage candidates not finally selected to find jobs)
- ⚠ UPSC recruits for civil, technical, medical, forest, engineering and statistical services — not civil services only

## SC: serving judicial officers can be directly recruited as district judges — Rejanish KV vs K Deepa | from: 2025-10-10
# 1. Verdict
- **Date:** **9 October 2025**; **five-judge Constitution Bench** led by **CJI B.R. Gavai**
- **Held:** judicial officers in subordinate judiciary can compete for **district judge** via **direct recruitment** (earlier only advocates with **7 years** practice)
- **Overruled:** **Satya Narain Singh (1984)** and **Dheeraj Mor vs High Court of Delhi (2020)**
- **Concurring:** **Justice Sundresh** — absolute bar violates **Article 14**
# 2. Directions
- **7 years** continuous experience (advocate + judicial officer combined)
- **Minimum age:** **35 years** uniform
- Eligibility assessed at **time of application**
- States/HCs to amend rules within **three months**; **prospective** only
# 3. Background
- **Articles 233–237** (Part VI) — subordinate courts
- **Article 233:** district judges appointed by **Governor** in consultation with **High Court**
- Other judicial officers: Governor after consulting **State PSC** and **High Court**
- **CJM:** offences up to **7 years**; **Judicial Magistrate:** up to **3 years**; death sentence by Sessions Judge needs **HC confirmation**

## 130th Constitution Amendment Bill, 2025 — removal of detained PM/CM/Ministers | from: 2025-10-14
# 1. Provision
- PM, CMs or Ministers to **vacate office** if **arrested and detained for 30 consecutive days** on charges punishable with **at least five years** imprisonment
# 2. Concerns
- Presumption of innocence; separation of powers; collective responsibility; potential misuse
# 3. Background
- **Article 75(1):** President appoints PM, Ministers on PM's advice; **Article 164(1):** Governor appoints CM
- **Articles 75(3), 164(2):** collective responsibility to legislature
| Case | Point |
|---|---|
| **Manoj Narula v Union of India (2014)** | PM's advice binding unless constitutionally disqualified |
| **Rai Sahib Ram Jawaya Kapur v State of Punjab (1955)** | Parliamentary accountability |
| **S.R. Bommai v Union of India (1994)** | Floor test, legislative supremacy |

## Pan-India Special Intensive Revision (SIR) of electoral rolls; SC examines ECI powers | from: 2025-10-27, 2025-10-29
# 1. News
- **ECI** to announce pan-India **SIR** on **27 October 2025** — first phase **10–15 states**, incl. 2026 poll states **Tamil Nadu**, **West Bengal**, **Kerala**, **Assam**, **Puducherry**
- **CEOs conference:** **22–23 October 2025**, New Delhi — map current electors with last SIR
- **Tools:** **Voter Helpline App**, **NVSP**, **ERONet**
# 2. Background
- **Representation of the People Act, 1950**; **Registration of Electors Rules, 1960**; **Article 324**
- **SVEEP:** Systematic Voters' Education and Electoral Participation
# 3. SC examines ECI's powers (Article 324)
- **First phase (Bihar)** under **Section 21(3), RPA 1950** (source dates it **June 2024**); second phase — **12 States/UTs**, ~**51 crore** voters; **Aadhaar** allowed as identity proof
- **SC order, 10 July 2025** framed the question whether ECI can conduct SIR; did **not stay** Bihar SIR — ordered draft roll publication and Aadhaar inclusion
- **Petitioners' counsel:** **Prashant Bhushan**, **Vrinda Grover**, **Neha Rathi** — citing **Article 326**, **14**, **21**
| Case / provision | Point |
|---|---|
| **Lal Babu Hussain (1995)** | Burden of proving citizenship lies on **new registrants**, not existing voters |
| **Mohinder Singh Gill (1978)** | Article 324 powers are **plenary** but within constitutional limits |
| **Article 326** | Universal adult suffrage — only citizens vote |
| **Section 16, RPA 1950** | Only citizens can be registered |
# 4. Background — ECI
- **Article 324(2):** CEC + ECs as President decides; currently **three-member**
- **Selection committee (2023):** **PM** (chair), **Leader of Opposition in Lok Sabha**, a **Union Cabinet Minister** nominated by PM
- **Tenure:** **6 years or 65 years**; CEC removable only like an **SC judge**
- Advises President/Governors on disqualification — **Articles 103 and 192**

## SC Constitution Bench on seniority in Higher Judicial Services | from: 2025-10-30
# 1. News
- **Hearings began:** **28 October 2025** — five-judge bench led by **CJI B.R. Gavai**: seniority between **promotees** and **direct recruits**
- Promotees ~**three-fourths** of cadre but direct recruits dominate senior posts
| High Court data | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Bihar** | 91 Principal District Judges — **86 direct recruits**, 5 promotees |
| **UP** | 70 — 58 direct, 12 promotees |
| **Bombay** | 19 elevated since 2020 — 16 direct |
| **Rajasthan** | 12 of 13 elevated were promotees |
| **Andhra Pradesh HC** | Recommended **50%** of HC appointments from service be those who began as **Civil Judges** |
# 2. Framework
- **All India Judges Association case (2002):** **75%** promotion (**50%** merit-cum-seniority + **25% LDCE**) and **25%** direct recruitment; **40-point roster** (from **R.K. Sabarwal, 1995**)
- **May 2025 SC:** LDCE **25%** restored; qualifying service **5 → 3 years**; **3 years' practice** for Civil Judge entry; quotas on sanctioned strength
- District judiciary handles ~**90%** of litigation
# 3. Background — NALSA
- **Legal Services Authorities Act, 1987**; **Article 39A**; Patron-in-Chief **CJI**; Executive Chairman — **second senior-most SC judge**
