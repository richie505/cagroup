## Cyber frauds and India's digital economy | from: 2025-10-03
# 1. Key points
- **Target:** **$1 trillion digital economy by 2030**
- **Fraud types:** phishing/remote access, **OTP & UPI** frauds, identity theft, job/loan scams, **"digital arrests"** (impersonating police/customs)
- **Mule accounts:** incomplete/fake **KYC**, used to layer stolen funds
- **Example:** retired banker lost **₹23 crore** in **21** transfers
- ⚠ No statutory **24-hour** freeze mandate by RBI yet — the **24-hour window** is the recovery window

## India's solar industry — third-largest producer | from: 2025-10-23
# 1. Data
- **2024-25 generation:** **1,08,494 GWh** — surpassed **Japan (96,459 GWh)**; ⚠ India **third** after **China** and **USA**
- **Module manufacturing capacity:** **2 GW (2014) → ~100 GW (2025)** (MNRE)
- **Installed solar (September 2025):** **117 GW**; effective ~**85 GW**
- **Solar parity with coal:** around **2017**
- **Target:** 500 GW non-fossil by 2030 — **250–280 GW** solar; needs ~**30 GW/year** vs actual **17–23 GW**
- Indian modules **1.5–2×** costlier than Chinese; exported ~**4 GW** to US in 2024 vs China's **236 GW**
# 2. Background — ISA
- **International Solar Alliance:** launched by **India and France** in **2015** at **COP21**; HQ **Gurugram**; **120+** members; aims **$1 trillion** by 2030
