## State of Global Air (SoGA) 2025 — air pollution deaths in India | from: 2025-10-24
# 1. Report
- **By:** **Health Effects Institute (HEI)** and **IHME (USA)**
- **India 2023:** ~**2 million** deaths — **+43%** from **1.4 million (2000)**; **89%** from **NCDs**
- **Death rate:** **186 per 100,000** vs **17** in high-income countries (>10×)
- **States >1 lakh deaths:** **Maharashtra**, **UP**, **Rajasthan**, **Bihar**, **West Bengal**
- Air pollution share: **70% COPD**, **33% lung cancer**, **>25% heart disease**, ~**20% diabetes** deaths
- **75%** of Indians exposed above WHO interim target **35 µg/m³**
- **Dementia:** **626,000** global deaths; India **54,000**
# 2. Global
- **8 million** deaths/year — **4.9 million** outdoor, **2.8 million** household, rest ozone
- **SDG 3.4:** cut premature NCD deaths by one-third by **2030**

## Himachal Pradesh Human Development Report 2025 | from: 2025-10-28
# 1. Findings
- **Released by:** HP CM **Sukhvinder Singh Sukhu** in **Chandigarh**; with **UNDP**
- **HDI:** **0.78** vs national **0.63**
- **Literacy:** **99.3%** — fully literate; reading/writing rank **5th** (from 21st in 2021)

- **Schemes:** **Rajiv Gandhi Self-Employment Start-up Scheme** (**₹680 crore**, **50%** subsidy on e-taxis); **Rajiv Gandhi Self-Employment Solar Energy Scheme** (100 kW–2 MW; interest subsidy **5%** tribal / **4%** non-tribal)

# 2. India in HDR 2025
- **India HDI 0.63**; rank **130 of 193**; **Medium** human development; top — **Switzerland**

## Lancet Countdown 2025 — health and climate change | from: 2025-10-29
# 1. Air pollution
- **Prepared by:** **University College London** with **WHO**
- **India:** **1.72 million** deaths/year from anthropogenic PM2.5 — **+38% since 2010**; global **2.5 million** (source: India ~**70%** of global)
- **Fossil fuels:** **44%** (**7.52 lakh**) — **coal 394,000** (power plants **298,000**); **petrol & diesel 269,000**
- **Wildfire smoke:** **10,200** deaths/year (2020–2024), **+28%**
- **Household pollution:** **113** per 100,000 (2022) — rural **125**, urban **99**
- **Economic cost:** **$339.4 billion (2022)** = **9.5% of GDP**
# 2. Heat and disease
- **50% more heatwave days in 2024**; **366** extra hours per person
- **419 lost labour hours** per person (+124% vs 1990s); income loss **$194 billion**
- Extreme drought area **+138%**; dengue (**Aedes albopictus**) potential doubled; **Vibrio** +46%
- **NCAP:** launched **2019**

## NITI Aayog report — India's services sector and employment | from: 2025-10-30
# 1. Findings
- **Report:** "**India's Services Sector: Insights from Employment Trends and State-Level Dynamics**" (2025); companion report on **GVA trends**
- Services: **>55% of GVA** but < **one-third** of workforce
- Services employment share **26.9% (2011–12) → 29.7% (2023–24)**; ~**40 million** jobs added; global average **50%**
- **Urban** >60% workers in services vs **rural** <20%; rural women only **10.5%**
- **Leading states:** **Karnataka**, **Maharashtra**, **Tamil Nadu**, **Telangana**; **lagging:** Bihar, MP, UP, Odisha
