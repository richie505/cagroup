=== TOPIC 2025-10:03 | Schemes & Social Sector

## NCRB 2023 — rise in illness suicides, sudden deaths | from: 2025-10-01
# 1. Suicides due to illness
- **Share:** **19%** of all suicides — **second** cause after **family problems (32%)**
- **Number:** **31,484 (2022) → 32,503 (2023)** (**+3.2%**)
- **Most affected age:** **30–45 years** (8,964)
- **Gender:** men **22,987**, women **9,501**, transgender **15**
- **Mental illness:** **13,978** suicides — ⚠ **declined 4%**; **cancer**-related suicides **rose 14%** (1,251 → 1,427)
- **States:** **Tamil Nadu** highest (**21%**), Maharashtra (12%), Karnataka (10%)
# 2. Sudden deaths and maternal deaths
| Item | 2022 → 2023 |
|---|---|
| **Sudden deaths** | **56,450 → 63,643** (**+13%**) |
| **Heart attacks** | **32,457 → 35,715** (**+10%**) |
| **Other sudden causes** | 23,993 → 27,928 (**+16.4%**) |
| **Maternal deaths in pregnancy** | **1,073 → 1,131** (+5.4%) |
| **Abortion-related deaths** | **80 → 127** (**+59%**) |
- **Age:** **45–60 years** most affected (**34%** of sudden deaths; **38%** of heart-attack deaths)
# 3. Background
- **NCRB:** **National Crime Records Bureau**; abortions governed by **MTP Act**

## Anshakalin Stri Parichars (ASPs) — Maharashtra | from: 2025-10-01
# 1. News
- **Who:** **Anshakalin Stri Parichars (ASPs)** — women community health workers in rural **Maharashtra**; ⚠ **predate ASHAs**
- **Wage:** stagnant at **₹3,000/month since 2016**; State promised **₹6,000/month by December 2025**
- **2023:** **Nagpur labour court** said ASPs deserve **Minimum Wages Act** protection — left implementation to State (⚠ did not mandate parity with MPWs)
# 2. Background
- **ASHA:** introduced under **National Rural Health Mission (2005)** — classified as **volunteers**, paid by incentives

## ICMR-INDIAB 2025 — high carbs, low protein drive obesity | from: 2025-10-06
# 1. Findings
- **Survey:** **ICMR-INDIAB (2025)** — **1,21,077** adults across **36 States/UTs**
- **Carbohydrates:** **62%** of daily calories — white rice, milled grains, added sugars
- **Protein:** below RDA in many states — "overfed yet under-nourished"
- **Diabetes:** over **100 million** adults (**ICMR, 2023**)
- **Child obesity (Lancet):** **>12 million** children grossly overweight in **2022** vs **0.4 million in 1990**
# 2. Measures
- **International Year of Millets 2023**; **CBSE "sugar boards"** in schools; **FSSAI Front-of-Pack Labelling (FoPL)** proposals

## Coldrif cough syrup deaths — drug safety | from: 2025-10-07
# 1. News
- **Coldrif** cough syrup contaminated with **diethylene glycol (DEG)** — at least **16 children** died in **Rajasthan** and **Madhya Pradesh**
- **Tamil Nadu** Drugs Control lab confirmed DEG; **CDSCO** recommended cancelling licence; a doctor arrested
- **Health Ministry:** strict compliance with revised **Schedule M** (**Good Manufacturing Practices**)
# 2. Background
- **India pharma:** **3rd** by volume, **14th** by value; **20%** of global generics; **60%** of vaccine demand; exports **USD 25–27 billion**; supplies **200+** countries
| Year | Earlier DEG incident |
|---|---|
| **2022** | **Gambia** — over **60** child deaths |
| **2023** | **Uzbekistan** — at least **18** deaths |
- **Law:** **Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 1940**; right to health under **Article 21**

## EPFO premature withdrawals and new withdrawal norms | from: 2025-10-23
# 1. Data
- ~**half** of members have < **₹20,000** at final settlement; **75%** < ₹50,000; **87%** < ₹1 lakh
- **EPFO:** **30 crore+** accounts, **7 crore** active; corpus > **₹26 lakh crore**; wage ceiling **₹15,000/month**
- **95%** of final settlements in 2024–25 were premature; of **52.95 lakh**, **24.21 lakh (46%)** rejoined employment
- **Para 69(2), EPF Scheme 1952:** full withdrawal after **two months** of unemployment
- **Illness claims 2024–25:** **3.25 crore** worth ₹52,633.92 crore (**+55%**)
# 2. Reforms
- Withdrawal categories **13 → 3** (essential needs, housing, special circumstances)
- **25% minimum balance**; education up to **10 times**, marriage up to **5 times**
- Premature final settlement wait raised **2 → 12 months**; **75%** withdrawable immediately after job loss
- ⚠ **EPS:** pension eligibility needs **10 years** pensionable service

## Mainstreaming stroke rehabilitation | from: 2025-10-29
# 1. Facts
- ~**1.8 million** new stroke cases/year; only **1 in 5** survivors get structured rehab (< 20%)

- Campaign **'FAST'** (Face, Arms, Speech, Time); programme **NPCDCS**

## 8th Central Pay Commission — Terms of Reference | from: 2025-10-31
# 1. News
- **Chair:** **Justice Ranjana Prakash Desai**
- **ToR (five points)** include ⚠ "**unfunded cost of non-contributory pension schemes**" — signal against return to **OPS**; impact on **state finances**; parity with CPSUs and private sector
- **7th CPC** chaired by **Justice Ashok Kumar Mathur**
# 2. Pension schemes
| Feature | OPS | NPS | UPS |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Nature** | Non-contributory, defined benefit | Contributory, defined contribution | Contributory, partially guaranteed |
| **Pension** | 50% of last basic + DA | Market annuity | **50%** of average basic of last **12 months** (25 years' service) |
| **Employee share** | None | **10%** | **10%** |
| **Govt share** | 100% | **14%** | **18.5%** |
| **Minimum pension** | ₹9,000 | None | **₹10,000** (10+ years) |
| **Family pension** | 50–60% | Annuity-based | **60%** |
- **OPS** till **31 December 2003**; **NPS** from **1 January 2004** (except armed forces) — regulator **PFRDA** (**PFRDA Act 2013**); NPS: **60%** tax-free withdrawal, 40% annuity
- **UPS (2024):** from **T.V. Somanathan Committee**; indexed to **CPI-IW**; lump sum **1/10th** of monthly pay per 6 months' service; choice irreversible
- States back to OPS: **Rajasthan**, **Chhattisgarh**, **Punjab**, **Himachal Pradesh**
