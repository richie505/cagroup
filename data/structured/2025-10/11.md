## PPP for heritage conservation — role of ASI | from: 2025-10-17
# 1. Key points
- **PPP successes:** **Elephanta Caves** and **Dr Bhau Daji Lad Museum**, Mumbai
- **INTACH** and ASI central to heritage management
# 2. Background — ASI
- **Ministry of Culture**; governed by **AMASR Act, 1958**
- Manages **3,650+** monuments of national importance
- **Founded 1861** by **Alexander Cunningham** — first DG, "**Father of Indian Archaeology**"; empowered by **Lord Curzon**

## World Food Day 2025 — 80 years of India–FAO partnership | from: 2025-10-21
# 1. News
- **World Food Day 2025:** India and **FAO** marked **80 years** of partnership — ⚠ India a **founding member** of FAO (**1945**)
- **Book launched:** **"Sowing Hope, Harvesting Success"**
- **FAO Representative:** **Takayuki Hagiwara**; **UN Resident Coordinator:** **Shombi Sharp**
- India has < **4%** of world's agricultural land and freshwater; **NFSA (2013)** covers **800 million+**

## 150 years of 'Vande Mataram' | from: 2025-10-27
# 1. News
- PM Modi in **127th Mann Ki Baat** (**26 October 2025**) marked 150 years; 150th-year celebration from **7 November 2025** (November 2025–November 2026)

| **Music** | **Jadunath Bhattacharya** |
