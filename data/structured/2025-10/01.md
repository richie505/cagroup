## Cyclone Montha — AP on high alert | from: 2025-10-27
# 1. Forecast
- **Formed:** deep depression over **southeast Bay of Bengal** → cyclonic storm **'Montha'** → expected **severe cyclonic storm**
- **Landfall:** between **Machilipatnam** and **Kakinada**, evening/night of **28 October 2025**; winds **90–100 kmph**, gusts **110 kmph**
- **Position (26 October):** **670 km** west of **Port Blair**, **720 km** ESE of **Chennai**, **780 km** SE of **Kakinada**
- **Name 'Montha'** suggested by **Myanmar**
# 2. Preparedness
- **Red alert districts:** Srikakulam, Vizianagaram, Visakhapatnam, Anakapalle, Kakinada, Konaseema, Krishna, Bapatla
- **APSDMA** sanctioned **₹19 crore**; **6 NDRF** and **13 SDRF** teams; **14 rescue boats**
# 3. Background
| Category (IMD) | Wind speed (kmph) |
|---|---|
| **Depression** | **31–49** |
| **Deep Depression** | **50–61** |
| **Cyclonic Storm** | **62–88** |
| **Severe Cyclonic Storm** | **89–117** |
| **Very Severe Cyclonic Storm** | **118–165** |
| **Extremely Severe Cyclonic Storm** | **166–220** |
| **Super Cyclone** | **>221** |
- **Formation conditions:** SST > **26.5°C**, Coriolis force (beyond **5°** latitude), low wind shear
- **Naming:** **WMO/ESCAP Panel on Tropical Cyclones** — **13** members (India, Bangladesh, Pakistan, Sri Lanka, Maldives, Myanmar, Thailand, Oman, Iran, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, UAE, Yemen); list maintained by **IMD**
