## Dugong conservation — Palk Bay Dugong Conservation Reserve | from: 2025-10-01
# 1. Reserve
- **Established:** **2022** in **Palk Bay** (Tamil Nadu) under **Wildlife (Protection) Act, 1972**
- **Area:** over **12,000 hectares** of **seagrass meadows**
- ⚠ **First** dedicated dugong conservation area in **India**
- **Population:** risen to **over 200** individuals (**WII** surveys)
- **Tech:** drones, acoustic sensors, satellite mapping of seagrass; funding from **CAMPA**
- **Recognition:** hailed by **IUCN** as an exemplar for marine conservation
# 2. Background
- **Dugong (Dugong dugon):** 'sea cow', **herbivorous** marine mammal; found in **Gulf of Mannar**, **Palk Bay**, **Gulf of Kutch**, **Andaman & Nicobar**
- ⚠ **Schedule I** of WPA (not Schedule II); ⚠ not found along the entire Indian coast
- **Gujarat** and **Andaman** populations are smaller and less protected

## E-waste and public health in India | from: 2025-10-03
# 1. Burden
- **2025:** India generated **2.2 million MT** — ⚠ **third-largest** after **China** and **USA**
- **Growth:** **150%** rise from **0.71 million MT (2017–18)**; projected to **double by 2030**
- **65 cities** produce **>60%**; hotspots **Seelampur**, **Mustafabad** (Delhi), **Moradabad** (UP), **Bhiwandi** (Maharashtra)
- **Formal units:** **322** registered recyclers (capacity 2.2 MT); only **43%** processed formally (2023–24)
- **PM2.5** in Seelampur > **300 μg/m³** — **12×** WHO 24-hour limit (**25 μg/m³**)
# 2. Background
- **E-Waste (Management) Rules, 2022:** **Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR)**; mandatory registration of recyclers/dismantlers
- **WHO:** **18 million children** and **13 million women** in informal waste zones globally
- **Released toxins:** beryllium, cadmium, chromium, mercury, lead (UPSC 2013 PYQ)

## Baratang mud volcano — samples ~23 million years old (GSI) | from: 2025-10-21
# 1. News
- **Site:** **Baratang**, **Andaman & Nicobar** — ⚠ **India's only active mud volcano**
- **Fresh eruption:** **2 October 2025**; GSI team visited **8–9 October**
- **Rocks:** sandstone and shale of **Mithakari Group** — **Oligocene** (**33.9–23 million years ago**)
- **Nature:** **cold mud** and gases (not lava) — **29–30°C**, **pH 8.0–8.3**; traces of **methane** and **ethane**
- **Cause:** subduction of **Indian Plate** beneath **Burma Microplate**; shale diapirism
# 2. Background
- **GSI:** founded **1851**; **Ministry of Mines**; HQ **Kolkata**
- Other mud volcano belts: **Azerbaijan**, **Sidoarjo (Indonesia)**, **Makran (Pakistan)**
