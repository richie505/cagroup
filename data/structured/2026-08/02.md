## Supreme Court judges' strength raised to 38 | from: 2026-08-04
# 1. The Bill
- **Bill:** **Supreme Court (Number of Judges) Amendment Bill, 2026** — passed by **Lok Sabha**; replaces an **ordinance** issued earlier in 2026
- **Change:** puisne judges **33 to 37**; total **34 to 38** including the **CJI**
- **Amends:** **Supreme Court (Number of Judges) Act, 1956**
- ⚠ First increase since **2019** (31 to 34); **no constitutional amendment** needed — **Article 124(1)** lets Parliament fix the number by law
- **Puisne judge:** a Supreme Court judge other than the CJI
# 2. Why
- **Pendency:** about **92,101** cases on **1 January 2026**
- **2025:** **75,410** fresh cases vs **65,615** disposed
- **Constitution Bench:** at least **5 judges**
- **All courts backlog:** over **5.5 crore** cases
- **India Justice Report 2025:** about **15.9 judges per million**; Law Commission recommended **50 per million**
# 3. Evolution of strength (including CJI)
| Year | Strength |
|---|---|
| **1950** | **8** |
| **1956** | **11** |
| **1960** | **14** |
| **1978** | **18** |
| **1986** | **26** |
| **2009** | **31** |
| **2019** | **34** |
| **2026** | **38** |
# 4. Background
- **Article 128:** CJI, with **President's** consent, can ask a retired SC judge to sit
- **Article 137:** SC review power; **Article 215:** High Courts as **courts of record**
- **Article 123:** President's ordinance power

## Creamy layer in SC/ST quotas: Centre opposes | from: 2026-08-12
# 1. News
- **Centre** opposed petitions in the **Supreme Court** seeking **creamy layer** in **SC/ST** reservations — disadvantage is historical (untouchability), not just economic; change must come from **Parliament**
- **Creamy layer** now applies to **OBCs** only; ⚠ not to SC/STs
# 2. Cases and articles
| Case / Article | Point |
|---|---|
| **Indra Sawhney (1992)** | Upheld OBC quota; creamy layer for **OBCs** |
| **E.V. Chinnaiah (2005)** | Any SC creamy-layer exclusion must come from **Parliament** |
| **Ashoka Kumar Thakur (2008)** | Creamy layer does **not** apply to SC/STs |
| **Davinder Singh (August 2024)** | **7-judge** bench allowed **sub-categorisation** of SC/STs by States |
| **Articles 341, 342** | President notifies SC/ST lists; changes only by **Act of Parliament** |
| **Article 32** | Centre says petition not maintainable |
- **Sub-categorisation:** divides quota among groups; creamy layer excludes individuals

## Police custody under BNSS Section 187(2): SC ruling | from: 2026-08-13, CARE-2026-08
# 1. Case
- **Case:** **State of Andhra Pradesh vs Suda Suresh Veera Venkata Naga Raju** — custodial death probe in AP
- **Ruling:** police custody can be sought in **parts** during the **initial 40 or 60 days**; ⚠ total still capped at **15 days**
- Magistrate cannot impose a blanket "**no extension**" condition; SC allowed **7 more days** (within 15)
- **Section 38 BNSS:** accused may meet an advocate **during** interrogation, not **throughout**
# 2. Law
| Provision | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Sec 58 BNSS** | No detention beyond **24 hours** without magistrate |
| **Sec 187(2) BNSS** | Police custody **15 days** total, in parts, within first **40/60 days** |
| **Sec 187(3) BNSS** | Detention up to **90 days** (death/life/10+ years) or **60 days** (others); then **default bail** |
| **Sec 167 CrPC (old)** | 15 days police custody, generally in the first **15 days** |

## Vande Mataram gets protection: National Honour (Amendment) Act 2026 | from: 2026-08-24, CA360-2026-08, OL-2026-08
# 1. The amendment
- **Act:** **Prevention of Insults to National Honour (Amendment) Act, 2026** — amends **Section 3** of the **1971 Act**
- **Effect:** **National Song (Vande Mataram)** gets same protection as **National Anthem** — punishes **intentional prevention or disturbance** of singing
- **Punishment:** up to **3 years**, fine or both; **Section 3A** (added **2003**) — repeat offence minimum **1 year**
- ⚠ Does **not** make singing compulsory, prescribe stanzas or punish respectful silence
- **Passage:** introduced **Rajya Sabha 24 July 2026**; passed RS **29 July**, LS **30 July**
# 2. 1971 Act
- **Enacted:** **23 December 1971**; **Section 2** — Flag and Constitution (burning, defacing etc.)
# 3. Vande Mataram history
- **Composer:** **Bankim Chandra Chatterjee**; first published in **Bangadarshan**, **7 November 1875**; in novel **Anandamath (1882)**
- **Tagore** sang it at **1896 INC session, Calcutta**; slogan on **7 August 1905** (Swadeshi)
- **October 1937:** CWC — only **first two stanzas** at official gatherings
- **24 January 1950:** **Rajendra Prasad** — equal honour with Jana Gana Mana; ⚠ Constitution does not mention a "National Song"
- **Bijoe Emmanuel v. State of Kerala (1986):** Jehovah's Witness students — silence protected under **Art 19(1)(a)** and **Art 25**
# 4. Extra
- ⚠ **2026** marks the **150th anniversary** of Vande Mataram
- **President Droupadi Murmu** gave assent
