# 2. Reforms

# 3. Capabilities
- **Launch vehicles:** PSLV, GSLV, GSLV Mk III, SSLV — **12** missions July 2023–June 2026
- **Sriharikota:** 2 pads, third coming; **Kulasekarapattinam (Tamil Nadu):** second launch site (SSLV, private)
- **Chandrayaan-3 (2023):** first near **south pole**; **4th** to soft-land
- **Mars Orbiter:** orbit **24 September 2014**; **XPoSat:** **January 2024**
- **SPADEX:** **January 2025** — India **4th** nation to dock autonomously
- **NavIC:** coverage up to **1,500 km** beyond India

# 5. Gaganyaan thermal shield and systems
- **Re-entry temperature:** ~**1,800°C**; **ablative TPS** ~**30–35 mm** thick; crew module is **double-walled** and **blunt-shaped**
- **Ablative materials:** **carbon phenolic**, **silica phenolic** — single use
- **HLVM3:** ~**53 m**, ~**640 t**, **3 stages**, ~**10 t** to LEO
- **Orbital Module:** pressurised **Crew Module** + unpressurised **Service Module** (separates before re-entry)
- **Parachutes:** **10** of **4** types (apex cover, drogue, pilot, main); sea splashdown
- **Milestones:** **SRE-1 (2007)**; **LVM3-X/CARE (2014)**; **Pad Abort Test (2018)**; **TV-D1**; Integrated Air Drop Tests
- ⚠ Re-entry heat comes mainly from **compression of air**, not friction

## Generative biology: AI-designed bacteriophages | from: 2026-08-25
# 1. Experiment
- **Who:** **Stanford University** and **Arc Institute**; models **Evo 1** and **Evo 2** (genome language models)
- **What:** AI designed complete genomes of **ΦX174-like bacteriophages**; **285** designs tested in **E. coli**, **16** functional — some beat bacterial resistance
- ⚠ AI proposed blueprints; humans synthesised and tested — no human virus made
- **ΦX174:** **first complete DNA genome sequenced (1977)**
| Stage | Period |
|---|---|
| **Reading** genomes | **1970s** |
| **Writing** (synthesis) | **2000s** |
| **Modifying** (editing, gain-of-function) | **2010s** |
| **Designing** (AI) | Present |
- **Uses:** **phage therapy** against **AMR**, AAV gene-delivery vectors, **oncolytic viruses**
- **Risk:** capability amplification; need **functional DNA screening** (what a sequence does, not only what it resembles)
