## Deep-sea fishery report on India's EEZ | from: CA360-2026-08, OL-2026-08

- **Title:** "**National Report on Deep-Sea and Distant-Water Fishery Resources of the Indian EEZ**"
- **Released by:** **Centre for Marine Living Resources and Ecology (CMLRE)**, **Kochi** — **Ministry of Earth Sciences**
- **Data:** ~**four decades** of oceanographic research, ~**three decades** of fishery surveys; **400+** cruises incl. **FORV Sagar Sampada**
- **EEZ assessed:** ~**2.37 million sq km**

- **EEZ:** under **UNCLOS**; up to **200 nautical miles** from baseline — sovereign rights over natural resources

## Malaria cases fall ~80% (2015–2025) | from: CA360-2026-08
# 1. Progress
- **Decline:** nearly **80%** in malaria cases, **2015–2025** (Ministry of Health)
- **High-burden districts:** **155 → 33**; **160** districts reported **no indigenous** cases (2022–2025)
- ⚠ India **exited** WHO's "**High Burden to High Impact**" group
- **Strategy:** active surveillance, "**Test, Treat and Track**", insecticide-treated nets, indoor residual spraying
- **Targets:** zero indigenous cases by **2027**; elimination by **2030**
# 2. Background
- **Cause:** *Plasmodium* parasite; **vector** — infected **female Anopheles** mosquito; species **P. falciparum** (most severe), P. vivax, P. malariae, P. ovale, P. knowlesi
- **National Framework for Malaria Elimination:** **2016**, target **2030**
- **World Malaria Day:** **25 April** (WHO)
