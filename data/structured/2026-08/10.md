## 23rd Commonwealth Games 2026 — Glasgow | from: CA360-2026-08, OL-2026-08
# 1. Facts
- **Dates:** **23 July – 2 August 2026**, **Glasgow, Scotland**; ceremonies at **Scotstoun Stadium**
- **Motto:** "**Sport is just the Beginning**"; mascot **Finnie**; ~**3,000** athletes, **74** CGAs
- **215** events in **10 sports**; record **47** para events; ⚠ first CWG since **1994** with only **10** sports
- **Victoria (Australia)** withdrew in **2023**; Scotland hosted **1970, 1986, 2014, 2026**
- ⚠ First CWG under **King Charles III**; ⚠ **Braille medals** used for the first time (triangular)
- **India:** flag bearer **Mirabai Chanu**; baton bearer **Lovlina Borgohain**
- **Previous:** Birmingham **2022**; **next:** **Ahmedabad 2030** (centenary); India hosted **New Delhi 2010**
- **Commonwealth Sport (CGF):** est. **1930**, HQ **London**, President **Donald Rukare**; para-sports integrated since **2002**
# 2. India's performance (one-liners)
- **India 4th** overall — **39 medals** (**13 gold, 17 silver, 9 bronze**); **Mirabai Chanu** won India's first gold — her **3rd straight** CWG gold
| Rank | Country | Medals (G-S-B) |
|---|---|---|

| **2** | **England** | **110** (29-45-36) |
| **3** | **Canada** | **62** (19-20-23) |
| **4** | **India** | **39** (13-17-9) |
| **5** | **Scotland** | **39** (13-9-17) |
- **Firsts:** **Tejaswin Shankar** — ⚠ first Indian CWG medal in **decathlon** (bronze); **Asmita Dey** and **Harsh Singh** — first Indian **judo** golds
- **Gulveer Singh:** only Indian with **two** medals; **boxing** gave India most medals
- **Para:** **28**-member team won **7** (3-2-2) — equal to all previous editions combined
- **Women's Mile** introduced; **Men's Mile** back after **1966**
