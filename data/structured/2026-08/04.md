## India's solar growth and PM Surya Sarovar Yojana | from: 2026-08-03, 2026-08-14, CA360-2026-08, OL-2026-08
# 1. PM Surya Sarovar Yojana
- **Approved:** **31 July 2026**
- **Target:** **5,000 MW** of **floating solar PV** projects with **co-located Energy Storage Systems**
- **Minimum storage:** **2 hours / 10,000 MWh**
- **Outlay:** **₹5,070 crore**; period **FY 2026–27 to FY 2030–31**
- **Sites:** reservoirs and industrial ponds; **all States and UTs** eligible
- **Floating solar potential:** about **102.18 GWp** — assessment by **National Institute of Solar Energy (NISE)**
- **Expected benefit:** cuts about **10 million tonnes CO₂** a year; **16,000–17,000** full-time-equivalent jobs
# 2. Solar capacity

## Critical minerals and India's mineral security | from: 2026-08-06, 2026-08-27
# 1. Basics
- **India has identified 30 critical minerals** — lithium, cobalt, nickel, graphite, copper, rare earths
- **Uses:** Li, Co, Ni — Li-ion batteries; **graphite** — battery anodes; **REEs** — permanent magnets; silicon, tellurium, indium, gallium — solar cells
- **Top-3 refiners' share** (Cu, Li, Ni, Co, graphite, REE): **82% (2020) to 86% (2024)**
- **China:** leading refiner in **19 of 20** strategic minerals (~**70%**); rare earths **>90%**, graphite **>90%**, cobalt ~**75%**, lithium chemicals ~**70%**; rare earth export controls in **2025**
- **Copper:** possible **30%** shortfall by **2035**
- **EU Critical Raw Materials Act (2030):** **10%** extraction, **40%** processing, **25%** recycling, max **65%** dependence on one country
- **India net-zero demand:** ~**169 million tonnes** by **2070** (**51%** above current-policy path)
# 2. India's resources
| Mineral | Potential |
|---|---|
| **Cobalt** | ~**44.9 MT** |
| **Copper** | ~**163.9 MT** |
| **Graphite** | ~**211.6 MT** |
| **Nickel** | ~**189 MT** |
| **Lithium** | **Karnataka**, **Jammu & Kashmir** |
- **Rare earth States:** Andhra Pradesh, Karnataka, Odisha, Kerala (monazite sands)
# 3. Policy
- **National Critical Mineral Mission:** **1,200** exploration projects by **2030–31**; at least **15** minerals produced; **50** overseas assets
- **MMDR Act 1957**, amended **2023** — Centre can auction critical minerals
- **KABIL (Khanij Bidesh India Ltd):** **15,703 ha** in **Catamarca, Argentina** for lithium
- **Budget 2026–27 rare earth corridors:** **Odisha, Kerala, Andhra Pradesh, Tamil Nadu**
- **India–US critical minerals and rare earths framework:** signed **May 2026**
- **SDG links:** **7**, **9**, **13**
# 4. MMDR Amendment Act 2026 — uniform mineral taxation
- **New Section 9D:** States cannot levy tax/cess on **mineral rights** or **mineral-bearing land** except under Centre-prescribed conditions; **Section 13** amended for rules
- ⚠ Unpaid past levies **invalid**; amounts already collected **not refunded**; **minor minerals** (~**50**) stay with States; **DMF/PMKKKY** unchanged
- **Why:** ~**14** levies on mining; some State levies up to **20%**; mineral imports ~**₹10.12 lakh crore** (2025–26); iron ore exports ~**₹15,136 crore**
- **State revenue:** ~**90%** of mining revenue; up **354%** since 2014 (**₹7 lakh crore+**); ~**₹1.14 lakh crore** in 2025–26
- **Auctions (since 2015):** **723** blocks in **17** States — **Rajasthan 140**, **MP 127**, **Odisha 76**; record **212** in 2025–26 (**36** operational); coal **141** auctioned
- **Royalty** ~**₹2.32 lakh crore** (1,200 mines); **auction premium** ~**₹96,000 crore**; **Odisha** ~**₹87,000 crore** premium
- **2025–26 output:** iron ore **313 MT**; limestone **484 MT**; major mineral value up **26.8%**
- **India's rank:** **2nd** limestone, **3rd** zinc, **4th** iron ore, **5th** bauxite
- **NCMM:** approved **January 2025**, **₹16,300 crore** to 2030–31 (**₹2,600 crore** budgetary); NMEDT **777** projects (**255** critical)
- **Recycling scheme:** **₹1,500 crore**, **October 2025**; **Critical Mineral Processing Parks** in **AP, Gujarat, Odisha, Maharashtra**
- **MAHA mission:** **9** CoEs, **₹210 crore**; NMEDT contribution raised to **3%**; **51** private exploration agencies; GSI **457** projects (FS 2025–26)
- **DMFs:** **656** (**106** in aspirational districts); **Unified Mining Portal**
