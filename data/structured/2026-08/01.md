- **Four States:** AP, Karnataka, Kerala, Tamil Nadu — total **₹999.87 crore** (incl. **₹500 crore** Central)
| State | Share |
|---|---|
| **Karnataka** | **38%** (largest) |

| **Kerala** | **20%** |
| **Andhra Pradesh** | **17%** |
# 2. Elephants in AP
- ⚠ AP is a **relatively new elephant range** — elephants returned from **Hosur–Dharmapuri** forests in the **1980s**
- **Population:** **65 (2017)** to **142 (2023)**
- **Concentrated in:** **Chittoor, Tirupati, Annamayya**; small groups in **Srikakulam**, **Parvathipuram Manyam**
- **Critical habitat:** **Koundinya Wildlife Sanctuary**

- **Measures:** Elephant Task Force; **Gaja Praja** app (report movement, compensation claims); **735 km** elephant-proof trenches; solar fencing

- **Asian elephant:** *Elephas maximus*; **Schedule I**; **CITES Appendix I**; **National Heritage Animal**
- **Project Elephant:** **1991–92**, Centrally Sponsored
- ⚠ CA-360 dates **Project Elephant** to **1992**; World Elephant Day **12 August** (since **2012**); **Gaj Gaurav Awards 2026** given at the same Visakhapatnam event

# 2. About pangolins
- **Nocturnal mammals** with hard scales; eat **ants and termites**; curl into a ball when threatened
- ⚠ Pangolins are **mammals**, not reptiles
- **8 species:** African — Black-bellied, White-bellied, Giant Ground, Temminck's Ground; Asian — **Indian**, Chinese, Sunda, Philippine
- **Indian Pangolin range:** India, Nepal, Sri Lanka, Bangladesh
