- **Signed:** **August 2026** by **Saudi Arabia**, **Türkiye** and **Pakistan**

- **Builds on:** **Saudi–Pakistan Strategic Mutual Defence Agreement (2025)**
- ⚠ **Not NATO** — full text and operational obligations not public; no confirmed Pakistani nuclear guarantee

| **Peninsula Shield Force** | **1984**, **GCC**; deployed in **Bahrain 2011** |
