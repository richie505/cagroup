## Government school closures and RTE | from: 2026-08-03
# 1. Key data
- **NITI Aayog report:** about **94,000** government schools closed in a decade — nearly **25 a day**; enrolment fell by **2.26 crore**
- **Government share of enrolment:** **71% (2005)** to **49.24% (2024-25)**
| Indicator | Earlier | Later |
|---|---|---|
| **Government schools** | **11.07 lakh (2014-15)** | **10.17 lakh (2023-24)** — about **89,000** fewer |
| **Private schools** | **2.88 lakh** | **3.31 lakh** |
# 2. Worst-affected
- **Uttar Pradesh:** **1.63 lakh** to **1.38 lakh** in one year — drop of **25,000+**
- **Madhya Pradesh:** **1.22 lakh (2018-19)** to **99,411** — about **22,600** fewer; **11.38 lakh** children in "**Drop Box**" category; **Jhabua** and **Shivpuri** about **29,000**
- **Jammu and Kashmir:** **4,300+** closed or merged; **23,167 (2020-21)** to **18,785 (2022-23)**; about **13,000** vacant teaching posts
# 3. Background
- **RTE Act 2009:** in force **2010**; free and compulsory education for **6–14 years**
- ⚠ Education is in the **Concurrent List** — not State List
| Article | Provision |
|---|---|
| **21A** | Right to education (FR) |
| **28** | Religious instruction in educational institutions |
| **30** | Minorities' right to run educational institutions |
| **41** | Right to work, education, public assistance (DPSP) |
| **45** | Early childhood care and education below **6 years** (DPSP) |
| **51A(k)** | Parent's duty to educate child of **6–14** (Fundamental Duty) |
| **350A** | Instruction in **mother tongue** at primary stage |

## GOBARdhan — National Circular Bioenergy Scheme | from: 2026-08-13, 2026-08-14
# 1. Approval
- **Approved by:** **Union Cabinet**; outlay **₹23,731 crore** over **10 years** (**2026-27 to 2035-36**)
- **Full form:** **Galvanising Organic Bio-Agro Resources Dhan**
- **Ministry:** **Petroleum and Natural Gas**
- **Aim:** raise **Compressed Biogas (CBG)** output about **10 times**
# 2. Viability measures
- **CBG blending obligation** (CNG/PNG, City Gas Distribution): **3% (2026-27)**, **4% (2027-28)**, **5% from 2028-29**
- **Administered price:** **₹2,110 per MMBTU**
- **Capital aid:** up to **₹2 crore per tonne per day** capacity
- **Credit guarantee** for MSME CBG projects; pipeline support
- **Existing:** **SATAT** (Sustainable Alternative Towards Affordable Transportation), Market Development Assistance, Biomass Aggregation Machinery, National Bioenergy Programme — **200+** CBG plants commissioned
# 3. Science
- **Biogas:** **55–65% methane**, **35–44% CO₂**; CBG upgraded to ~**98% methane**, similar to **CNG**
- **Anaerobic digestion stages:** **Hydrolysis → Acidogenesis → Acetogenesis → Methanogenesis**
# 4. Update
- **Cabinet approval date:** **6 August 2026**
- **CBG/Bio-CNG plants (Aug 2026):** **1,929** registered, **217** commissioned, **357** under construction

## Bharat Taxi — first cooperative ride-hailing platform | from: 2026-08-18
# 1. Basics
- ⚠ **India's first cooperative-based ride-hailing platform**; set up **6 June 2025** under **Multi-State Cooperative Societies Act 2002**
- **Motto:** "**Saarthi Hi Maalik**"; vision "**Sahkar se Samriddhi**"
- **Founders (8):** **NCDC**, **IFFCO**, **NABARD**, **KRIBHCO**, **Amul (GCMMF)**, **NAFED**, **NDDB**, **National Cooperative Export Ltd**
- **Scale (25 July 2026):** ~**8 lakh** Sarathis, ~**41 lakh** customers; nationwide by **2029**
- **Cities:** Delhi-NCR, Gujarat, Lucknow, Chandigarh, Mumbai, Jaipur, Kanpur, Pune
# 2. Model
- **Zero-commission**, **surge-free**; share **₹100** each, ownership with **₹500**
- **Earnings:** **20%** credited as Sarathis' capital, **80%** shared by kilometres; **7%** charge only at airport prepaid booths
- **Sarathi Didi:** women passengers choose women drivers; **Bike Didi:** **150+** women (Feb 2026)
- **MoU with NeGD:** DigiLocker, UMANG, API Setu
- **Welfare:** **e-Shram** registration → **AB-PMJAY** up to **₹5 lakh**; **₹5 lakh** accident cover (**IFFCO Tokio**)

## National Animal Disease Control Programme (NADCP) | from: 2026-08-25
# 1. Programme
- **Launched:** **September 2019**; **Ministry of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry and Dairying**
- **Diseases:** **Foot and Mouth Disease (FMD)** and **Brucellosis**
- **Umbrella:** **Livestock Health and Disease Control Programme (LHDCP)** — **₹2,010 crore** (2026–27)
| Disease | Strategy |
|---|---|
| **FMD** (serotypes **O, A, Asia-1**) | **100%** eligible livestock **twice a year** |
| **Brucellosis** (*Brucella abortus*) | **One-time** for female calves **4–8 months** |
- **20th Livestock Census 2019:** **535.78 million** livestock; **302.79 million** bovines
- **Bharat Pashudhan Portal:** **12-digit** tag ID; **Mobile Veterinary Units:** toll-free **1962**; **MAITRIs** — rural technicians
- **Pandemic Fund Project (2024):** **US$25 million** from **G20 Pandemic Fund**; partners **ADB, FAO, World Bank**
