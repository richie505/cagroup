## GI tags: India's GI ecosystem | from: 2026-08-05
# 1. Law and institutions
- **Act:** **Geographical Indications of Goods (Registration and Protection) Act, 1999**; Rules **2002**; operational **2003**; registrations from **2004–05**; Rules amended **2025**
- **International:** **Paris Convention**, WTO **TRIPS**
- **GI Registry:** **Chennai** — all-India jurisdiction
- **2025 amendment:** fees cut by **80%**; renewal fee **₹3,000 to ₹500**
- **Validity:** **10 years**, renewable; revocation under **Section 27**
- **Who can apply:** association of producers, organisation or authority established by law

## Forest fires: Himalayan vulnerability | from: 2026-08-18
# 1. Parliamentary panel
- **Committee:** Parliamentary Standing Committee on **Science and Technology, Environment, Forest and Climate Change**
- **Recommendations:** **geostationary satellite** for 24x7 fire monitoring; regional climate-fire study; at least **2** NDRF centres for Himalayan fires; **National Pine-Needle Utilisation Policy**
- **Himalayas:** ~**17%** of landmass but ~**57%** of forest fire incidents
- **Chir pine needles:** "**Pirul**" — resin-rich, inflammable
# 2. Data and monitoring
- **Forest and tree cover:** ~**8.27 lakh sq km** (~**24%**)
- **FSI vulnerability:** **54.40%** occasional fires; **7.49%** moderately frequent; **2.40%** high; **35.71%** largely unaffected
- **Season:** **November to June**; peak **March–May**
- **Satellites used now:** foreign polar-orbiting **AQUA** and **Suomi-NPP**
- **Types:** ground, **surface** (common in India), **crown** (rare in India)
- Setting forests on fire punishable under **BNS 2023**; nodal ministry **MoEFCC**; **National Action Plan on Forest Fire** with Central Monitoring Committee
- **Fire lines:** **kachha** (undergrowth cleared), **pucca** (clear-felled strips)
# 3. Background — NDMA
- **NDMA:** under **Disaster Management Act 2005** (**23 December 2005**); chaired by **Prime Minister**; SDMA by **Chief Minister**
- **NDRF:** National Disaster **Response** Force — under **MHA**
