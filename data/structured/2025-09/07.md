## Global plastic pollution crisis | from: 2025-09-15
# 1. Data
- **World Environment Day 2025 (5 June) theme:** **"Ending Plastic Pollution"**
- **OECD Global Plastic Outlook:** production doubled **2000–2019** to **460 million t**; waste **353 million t**
- **Waste fate:** recycled **9%**, incinerated **19%**, landfilled **50%**, mismanaged **22%**
- **Waste by source:** packaging **40%**, consumer goods **12%**, textiles **11%**
- **2024:** ~**500 million t** produced, **400 million t** waste; **2060** → **1.2 billion t**
- **Ocean:** **11 million t** a year enter oceans (Ocean Conservancy); ~**200 million t** already there
- **GHG:** plastics **3.4%** of emissions; could be **19%** of carbon budget by **2040** (UNEP)
- **Health letter:** **18 million** health professionals, **88 countries**
# 2. Global response
- **UNEA-5 (2022):** all **193** members agreed to negotiate a **legally binding plastics treaty** — ⚠ not a ban on production
- **UNEP aim:** cut plastic waste **80%** in two decades
- **EPR:** Extended Producer Responsibility

## Emissions may cut surface solar radiation over India by up to 10% | from: 2025-09-17
# 1. News
- **Study:** **Ashwin Jadhav** and **Rohini Bhawar**, **Savitribai Phule Pune University** — journal **Energy and Climate Change**
- **Finding:** up to **10%** fall in **surface solar radiation (SSR)** over central, southern, eastern India under intermediate/high emissions — due to aerosols, turbidity, post-monsoon clouds
- **Mean SSR loss:** ~**1.10 W/m² per decade** (intermediate), ~**1.32 W/m² per decade** (high)
- ⚠ **Indo-Gangetic Plains** and **NE** may see marginal **increase** (low emission); **north-west India** most stable
- **India solar capacity:** **123,130.13 MW** (31 Aug 2025); renewables **37%** of capacity but ~**18%** of generation

## Isobutanol–diesel blending pilot by ARAI | from: 2025-09-22
# 1. News
- **Announced by:** **Nitin Gadkari** — **ARAI** (Automotive Research Association of India) studying **isobutanol–diesel** blending; pilot ~**18 months**
- **Why not ethanol:** low flash point, volatility, poor miscibility with diesel
- **Isobutanol:** ⚠ **higher flash point**, better miscibility; ⚠ **lower cetane number** (needs additives); up to **10%** blending safe
- **Feedstock:** sugarcane syrup, **B-heavy molasses**, grains; a **150 klpd** plant → ~**125 klpd** ethanol + **20 klpd** isobutanol

## Offshore freshwater aquifer off US north-east coast | from: 2025-09-10
# 1. News
- **Finding:** vast offshore freshwater aquifer beneath **Atlantic Ocean** from **New Jersey to Maine**
- **Drilling:** ~**1,289 feet** below sea floor; ~**50,000 litres** extracted; cost ~**$25 million**
- **Could supply:** **New York City** for **800 years**
- **Scientist quoted:** **Brandon Dugan**, **Colorado School of Mines**
# 2. Background
- **Offshore aquifers:** extend up to **90 km** offshore; ~**1 million km³** freshwater (~**10%** of terrestrial freshwater) — **2021** study in **Environmental Research Letters**
- **Origin theories:** **Ice Age** recharge; connection to onshore aquifers; held by clay **cap rock**
- ⚠ Not recharged by current rainfall directly
- **UN 2023:** freshwater demand to exceed supply by **40%** by **2030**
