## World Patient Safety Day — 17 September | from: 2025-09-17
# 1. Facts
- **Observed by:** **WHO**, **17 September** every year
- **2025 theme:** **"Safe care for every newborn and every child"**
- **Harm:** **1 in 10** hospitalised patients; **4 in 10** in outpatient care
- **India:** **National Patient Safety Implementation Framework (2018–2025)**; **NABH** accreditation — fewer than **5%** hospitals fully accredited; **Society of Pharmacovigilance, India**
- **WHO Global Patient Safety Action Plan** recommends **Patient Advisory Councils (PACs)** — ⚠ not yet widely implemented in India
