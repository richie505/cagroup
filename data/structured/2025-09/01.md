## Erra Matti Dibbalu on UNESCO Tentative List | from: 2025-09-15, APCA-2025-09
# 1. News
- **Site:** **Erra Matti Dibbalu** ("**Red Sand Hills**") — along **Visakhapatnam–Bheemunipatnam** Beach Road (~**30 km** coastal stretch)
- **Listing:** added to **UNESCO World Heritage Tentative List** — one of **seven** Indian sites added
- **Status:** one of India's **34 National Geo-heritage Sites**
# 2. Geology and archaeology
- **Formed:** during **Last Glacial Maximum (LGM)** ~**18,500 years** ago; process began ~**20,000** years ago
- **LGM coastline:** Bay of Bengal ~**50 km** inland (lower sea level)
- **Height:** **10–30 feet**
- **Red colour:** **ferrous minerals** reacting with moisture
- **Finds:** **microlithic, mesolithic, palaeolithic** tools and pottery — study by **Alok Rath** and **P. Thimma Reddy** (mid-1990s)
- **INTACH expert:** **D. Rajasekhar Reddy**
