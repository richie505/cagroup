## Vice-Presidential Election 2025 — C.P. Radhakrishnan elected | from: 2025-09-10

## Presidential Reference — Governors sitting on Bills (Article 200) | from: 2025-09-11, 2025-09-15, 2025-09-22
# 1. News
- **Bench:** **five-judge Presidential Reference Bench** led by CJI **B.R. Gavai**
- **Issue:** Governors delaying assent indefinitely — e.g. **Tamil Nadu Governor** sat on **10 Bills** for **4 years** (since 2020)
- **Earlier ruling (April 2025, TN Governor case):** **three-month** timeline for Governor and President
- **Centre:** Solicitor General **Tushar Mehta**; judges **Surya Kant**, **P.S. Narasimha**
- **For States:** **P. Wilson** (TN), **Amit Kumar** (Meghalaya AG), **Gopal Sankaranarayanan**, **S. Niranjan Reddy** (Telangana)
# 2. Background — Article 200 options
- **Assent**; **withhold** assent; **return** for reconsideration (not Money Bill); **reserve** for President
- ⚠ Article 200 itself prescribes **no timeline** — says **"as soon as possible"**
# 3. Presidential reference under Article 143(1)
- **Trigger:** **April 2025** two-judge Bench held TN Governor **R.N. Ravi**'s delay unconstitutional, used **Article 142** to set timelines; **pocket veto** unconstitutional
- **States:** reference relitigates verdict (**stare decisis**) — **Abhishek Manu Singhvi** (TN), **Gopal Subramanium**; Governor acts on aid and advice — **Article 163**
- **Centre:** Governor not a **"postman"**; AG **R. Venkataraman** — timelines = judicial amendment; States cannot use **Article 32** against Governor
- **GoI Act 1935:** Governor had absolute veto
- **AP** (Centre's ally) also defended States' right to approach SC
- ⚠ Advisory opinion under Article 143 is **not binding**
# 4. More provisions and commissions
- **Reference made:** **May 2025** on **Articles 200 and 201**; April 2025 case — **State of Tamil Nadu vs Governor of Tamil Nadu**
- **Article 201:** President's assent to reserved Bills; **Article 163(2):** Governor's discretion final, not questioned in court
- **Mandatory reservation:** e.g. Bill reducing **High Court** powers
- **Cases cited:** **Nabam Rebia (2006** per source**)**, **K.M. Singh (2020)**
| Commission | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| **Sarkaria (1987)** | Discretion only in rare cases; President to dispose reserved Bills in **6 months** |
| **Punchhi (2010)** | Governor to decide on Bills in **6 months** |

## Immigration and Foreigners Order, 2025 | from: 2025-09-04, 2025-09-12
# 1. News
- **Notified:** **1 September 2025** by **MHA** — replaces **Foreigners (Tribunal) Order, 1964**
- **Parent law:** **Immigration and Foreigners Act, 2025** (passed **April 2025**)
- **Foreigners Tribunals (FTs):** powers of **Judicial Magistrate of First Class**; can issue **arrest warrants**, send to **detention/holding centres**
- **FT size:** capped at **3 members**; ex-parte orders reviewable if appealed within **30 days**
- **FTs exist only in Assam:** **100** tribunals; order applies across India
- **Detention:** for the **first time** a clear legal basis
- **Nodal Officer** in each State; **Border forces** and **Coast Guard** capture biometrics of illegal entrants
- **Employment barred (without Centre approval):** power, water supply, petroleum, defence, space, nuclear, human rights work
# 2. Immigration and Foreigners (Exemption) Order, 2025
- **Notified:** **1 September 2025** by MHA — exempts from passport/visa requirement for entry, stay, exit
- **Exempted:** citizens of **Nepal** and **Bhutan**; **Tibetan** refugees; **six minorities** (Hindus, Sikhs, Buddhists, Jains, Parsis, Christians) from **Afghanistan, Pakistan, Bangladesh**; **Sri Lankan Tamil** refugees who sheltered in India **on or before 9 January 2015** and registered
- ⚠ Exemption is **not citizenship** — they remain "illegal migrants" under Citizenship Act
- **Sri Lankan Tamils** were excluded from **CAA 2019**; civil war ended **2009**
# 3. Background
- **IMDT Act 1983** — struck down by SC in **2005** → **11 IMDTs** became FTs
- **Assam NRC 2019:** excluded **19 lakh** people

|---|---|
| **Article 1** | India a **"Union of States"** |
| **Article 2** | Admit/establish **new States** (e.g. **Sikkim 1975**) |

## SC guidelines on DNA evidence — Kattavellai v. State of Tamil Nadu | from: 2025-09-15
# 1. Guidelines
- **Case:** **Kattavellai @ Devakar v. State of Tamil Nadu**
- **Collection:** documented packaging (FIR no., IO, hospital), signed by doctor, IO and independent witnesses
- **Transport:** to **FSL** within **48 hours**; delays recorded
- **Trial:** samples not opened/resealed without **trial court** permission
- **Chain of Custody Register:** maintained till outcome; part of trial record
# 2. Earlier cases
| Case | Holding |
|---|---|
| **Anil v. State of Maharashtra (2014)** | DNA reliable only with quality control |
| **Manoj v. State of MP (2022)** | Rejected — sample from open area |
| **Rahul v. State of Delhi (2022)** | Rejected — sample in **Malkhana** for 2 months |
- **DNA = opinion evidence:** **Section 45, Indian Evidence Act** = **Section 39, Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam 2023** — needs corroboration

## Kolhan's Manki-Munda system — Ho adivasi protest | from: 2025-09-15

# 2. The system
| Post | Role |
|---|---|
| **Munda** | Head of **one village** — disputes; ⚠ no revenue role pre-British |
| **Manki** | Head of **8–15 villages** — appellate authority |
