## Air Quality Life Index (AQLI) 2025 | from: 2025-09-01
# 1. News
- **Prepared by:** **Energy Policy Institute at the University of Chicago (EPIC)** — 2025 update based on **2023** data
- **India:** **all Indians** breathe air above **WHO** PM2.5 limit of **5 µg/m³**
- **Indian standard:** **40 µg/m³** — ~**46%** of population lives where even this is breached
- **Indo-Gangetic Plain:** most polluted; **544.4 million** people exposed
- **Delhi:** largest life-expectancy gain **+8.2 years** if WHO limit met; cleanest regions of India **+9.4 months**
# 2. Global
- **Bangladesh:** most polluted country — PM2.5 **12 times** WHO limit; gain **5.5 years** nationally, **7.1 years** in **Gazipur**
- **China:** **40.8%** cleaner than **2014** despite **2.8%** rise in 2023
- **Bolivia:** entered top-10 most polluted for first time since **2010**
- **North America:** spike in 2023 due to **wildfires**
- **Cameroon, DR Congo:** air pollution a greater threat than HIV/AIDS, malaria
- **Indian programmes:** **NCAP**, **GRAP** (Delhi NCR)

## Production Gap Report 2025 | from: 2025-09-23
# 1. News
- **Released:** **22 September 2025** by **Stockholm Environment Institute (SEI)**, **Climate Analytics**, **IISD** (UN-supported)
- **Finding:** planned 2030 fossil fuel output exceeds **1.5°C**-consistent level by over **120%**, **2°C** level by **77%**
| Fuel | 2030 excess over 1.5°C path |
|---|---|
| **Coal** | **500%** (330% over 2°C) — most misaligned |
| **Oil** | **31%** |
| **Gas** | **92%** |
- **Expanding:** **China, US, Saudi Arabia, Brazil, Nigeria**; Nigeria doubled 2030 oil target; Brazil **+47%**
- **Needed:** coal near phase-out by **2040**; oil & gas **-75%** by **2050** vs 2020; 2050 path **4.5×** above 1.5°C level
- **Aligning:** **Colombia, Germany, Brazil**
- **ICJ:** failure to curb fossil expansion may be an internationally wrongful act
