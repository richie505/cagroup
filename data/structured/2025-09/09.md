## Ramon Magsaysay Award — Educate Girls, first Indian organisation | from: 2025-09-01
# 1. News
- **Recipient:** **Foundation to Educate Girls Globally (Educate Girls)** — ⚠ **first Indian organisation** to win (source labels the award **2024**)
- **Founded:** **2007** by **Safeena Husain**
- **Works in:** **Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh, Uttar Pradesh**; volunteers called **"Team Balika"**
- **Impact:** **1.9 million+** girls back to school
# 2. Background — the award
- **Established:** **1957** by **Rockefeller Brothers Fund** (USA) with **Philippine government**; first presented **1958**
- **Named after:** **Ramon Magsaysay**, **7th President of the Philippines** (1953–1957); born **31 August 1907**, **Iba**; died in air crash **17 March 1957**
- **Ceremony:** **31 August** in **Manila** — ⚠ not New Delhi
- **Called:** **"Nobel Prize of Asia"**

## Carlo Acutis — first millennial saint | from: 2025-09-08
# 1. News
- **Canonised by:** **Pope Leo XIV** at **St. Peter's Square, Vatican** — ⚠ Catholic Church's **first millennial saint**
- **Nickname:** **"God's Influencer"** — built multilingual website cataloguing **Eucharistic miracles**
- **Born:** **3 May 1991**, **London**; raised in **Milan**; died of **acute leukaemia** at **15** (diagnosed **October 2006**); buried in **Assisi**
- **Also canonised:** **Pier Giorgio Frassati** — Italian, died **1925** aged **24** of **polio**
