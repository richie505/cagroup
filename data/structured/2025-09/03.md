|---|---|

- **India:** largest groundwater user — ~**25%** of global extraction

## Kerala — first fully digitally literate State (Digi Kerala) | from: 2025-09-01
# 1. News
- **Date:** **21 August 2025** — **Kerala** declared India's **first fully digitally literate State**
- **Programme:** **Digi Kerala**, modelled on **Digi Pullampara**
- **Trained:** **21.87 lakh** certified (of **21.88 lakh** identified); **1.51 crore** people in **83.45 lakh** households surveyed
- **Volunteers:** **2.57 lakh**; master trainers trained at **KILA** (Kerala Institute of Local Administration)
- **Evaluator:** **Economics & Statistics Department** (third party)
- **Inclusivity:** all ages — **15,221** trainees above **90 years**; over **13 lakh women**, **8 lakh men**, **1,644 transgender** persons
- **Focus:** **smartphone**-based literacy (not computer) — ⚠ National Digital Literacy Mission limited to **60 years** age
# 2. Pullampara model
- **Panchayat:** **Pullampara**, **Thiruvananthapuram district** — started **2021**
- **Survey:** **3,917** digitally illiterate; **3,300** trained; **15 tasks in 3 modules**, pass = **6 of 15**; success **96.18%**
- **Volunteers:** **NSS**, **Kudumbashree**, SC/ST promoters, library council
- **Milestone:** **September 2022** — first fully digitally literate **panchayat** in Kerala
# 3. Digi Kerala 2.0
- **Announced by:** CM **Pinarayi Vijayan** — cybersecurity literacy, media literacy, service access
- **KFON:** free internet to **14,000 BPL** families; **74,203** commercial connections
- **K-SMART:** digitisation of all LSG services

## Universal Health Care and insurance-driven model (PMJAY) | from: 2025-09-04
# 1. Facts
- **Bhore Committee (1946):** defined UHC — care to all irrespective of ability to pay
- **PMJAY (2018, Ayushman Bharat):** **₹5 lakh** per household per year, in-patient care
- **2023–24:** PMJAY covered **58.8 crore** people, budget ~**₹12,000 crore**; SHIPs ~**₹16,000 crore**; total ~**₹28,000 crore**
- **Private hospitals:** get **two-thirds** of PMJAY spending
- **Utilisation:** ~**80%** nominal coverage but only **35%** insured patients used insurance (2022–23)
- **Pending dues:** over **₹12,000 crore**; **609 hospitals** opted out; **NHA** flagged **3,200 hospitals** for fraud
- **Public health spending:** **1.3% of GDP (2022, World Bank)** vs world average **6.1%**
- **UHC models:** **Canada**, **Thailand**
- **National Health Policy 2017:** public health spending target **2.5% of GDP**

## Amendments to New Drugs and Clinical Trials Rules, 2019 | from: 2025-09-04, 2025-09-06
# 1. News
- **Draft published:** Gazette, **28 August 2025** by Union **Health Ministry**

## HelpAge India study — intergenerational dynamics and ageing | from: 2025-09-30
# 1. Facts
- **Elderly (60+):** **10%** now → ~**20%** by **2050**; life expectancy ~**50 (1970s)** → **70+**
- **Study:** **"Understanding Intergenerational Dynamics & Perceptions on Ageing"** — **5,700** respondents, **10 cities**
- **Findings:** youth see elders as wise (**51%**) but lonely (**56%**); loneliness concern — youth **69%**, elders **68%**; **78%** youth think elders lack digital interest vs **71%** elders cite youth impatience; **88%** youth expect to live with family in old age
- **Schemes cited:** **NPS Vatsalya**, **AB PM-JAY**

## Assisted dying debate | from: 2025-09-29
# 1. Facts
- **Trigger:** deaths of actress **Ruth Posner (96)** and husband **Michael** at a Swiss clinic
- **Types:** **physician-assisted suicide (PAS)**; **voluntary active euthanasia (VAE)**
- **UK:** **Terminally Ill Adults (End of Life) Bill, 2024** — only terminally ill adults; excludes mental illness/disability; **High Court** approval
- **Switzerland:** allows non-residents ("death tourism")
- **India:** SC **2018** — right to die with dignity (**Article 21**), **passive euthanasia** allowed; active euthanasia illegal
