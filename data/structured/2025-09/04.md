## 56th GST Council — two-tier GST of 5% and 18% (GST 2.0) | from: 2025-09-04, 2025-09-06, 2025-09-17, 2025-09-23
# 1. News
- **Meeting:** **56th GST Council**, **3 September 2025**, chaired by **Nirmala Sitharaman** — **10.5-hour** meeting
- **New slabs:** **5%** and **18%** (earlier 0, 5, 12, 18, 28%) — effective **22 September 2025**
- **Sin/demerit goods:** **40%** (cigarettes, paan masala) — highest slab
- **Compensation cess:** continues on tobacco until loans repaid
| Item | New GST |
|---|---|
| **All TVs** | **18%** |
| **Individual health insurance** | **Nil** |
| **Life-saving cancer drugs** | **Nil** |
- **Inverted duty structure** to be corrected
- **Revenue impact:** ~**₹48,000 crore**; Revenue Secretary **Arvind Shrivastava**
- **Decision:** **unanimous**; Bihar Dy CM **Samrat Choudhary**, UP FM **Suresh Khanna**
# 2. Economic implications
- **Slabs dropped:** **12%** and **28%**; retained **0, 5, 18%** + **40%** demerit rate (some special rates below 5%)
- **546 goods** changed — over **80%** cuts, ~**20%** increases
- ⚠ **28% → 40%** for some goods reflects merger of **compensation cess**, not a real hike
- ⚠ **Exempt** goods get **no Input Tax Credit**
- **Q1 nominal GDP:** **8.8%** vs **10.1%** projected; direct taxes **-4.3%**
- **Revenue foregone** (with income tax reforms): ~**₹1 lakh crore**
- **Called:** **"GST Bachat Utsav"** by PM **Modi**
- **Classification fixes:** all Indian breads — **roti, paratha, khakhra** — **exempt**; similar goods in same slab (e.g. salted vs caramelised popcorn)

## Quality Control Orders (QCOs) | from: 2025-09-22
# 1. Framework
- **Law:** **BIS Act, 2016** — **Section 16** lets govt make Standard Mark compulsory
- **Standards:** ~**23,000** BIS standards, most **voluntary**; QCO makes them mandatory
- **Schemes:** **10 Conformity Assessment Schemes** (7 products; 1 each management systems, services, processes); **Scheme-I** most common
- **Process:** draft hosted on **WTO TBT** website; usually **6 months** for compliance
# 2. Impact and criticism
- **Hermetic compressors:** <**2 million (2021-22)** → **8 million (2023-24)**; **room ACs** ~**1 million → 12 million**
- **Birla Aircon, Meerut:** turnover **₹7 crore → ₹42 crore**
- **Critic:** NITI Aayog VC **Suman Berry** called QCOs a "**malign intervention**"
- **Reforms:** **"Manak Manthan"**; certification in **30 days** for **750+** products; ~**80%** of **50,753** certificates to MSMEs

## 'Involution' in China's EV sector | from: 2025-09-16
# 1. Concept
- **Involution (nèijuǎn):** excessive competition → price wars below cost → losses
- **Coined by:** anthropologist **Clifford Geertz (1969)**, **"Agricultural Involution"** (Java wet-rice)
# 2. China EV facts
- **120–130** EV makers
- **Tariffs:** **US 100%** (from 2024); **EU** countervailing — **BYD 17%**, **Geely 18.8%**, **SAIC ~35%**; **Turkiye 40%**; Mexico considered **50%**
- **Response:** "war on price wars"; draft **Pricing Law** overhaul (**24 July 2025**); orderly exit of capacity; MIIT
- **BYD plants:** **Hungary**, **Turkiye**; Chinese EVs = **75%** of EV sales growth in developing countries (2024)
