## Union Budget 2026-27 — key numbers | from: TOPIC-Union-Budget
# 1. Presentation
- **Presented by:** **Nirmala Sitharaman** — Union Minister for **Finance and Corporate Affairs**
- **Her count:** **9th Budget**; India's **6th Digital Budget**
- **Venue first:** **First Budget prepared at Kartavya Bhavan**
- **Constitutional basis:** **Article 112** — **Annual Financial Statement**
  - Economic Survey = **diagnosis**; Budget = **remedy**
# 2. Three Kartavyas (duties)
| Kartavya | Aim |
|---|---|
| **First** | **Accelerate economic growth** — raise **productivity** and **competitiveness**, build **resilience** against global uncertainty |
| **Second** | **Fulfil people's aspirations**, enhance **capabilities** — make them partners in prosperity |
| **Third** | **Sabka Saath, Sabka Vikas** — every family, community, region and sector gets access to resources and opportunities |

## Union Budget 2026-27 — tax proposals | from: TOPIC-Union-Budget
# 1. Direct tax and compliance
- **New Income Tax Act:** comes into force from **April 2026**; simplified rules and forms to be notified, redesigned for common citizens
- **Motor Accident Claims Tribunal:** interest awarded to a natural person **exempt**; **TDS** on it removed
- **Return revision deadline:** extended from **31 December** to **31 March** (nominal fee)
- **Staggered timelines** for filing returns
- **NRI property deals:** resident buyer uses **PAN-based challan** instead of **TAN**
- **Foreign Asset Disclosure Scheme:** one-time, **6-month**, for small taxpayers
- **Updated returns:** allowed even after reassessment begins — additional **10%** tax
- **Prosecution immunity:** retrospective from **1 October 2024** for undisclosed non-immovable foreign assets below **₹20 lakh**
- **Form 15G/15H:** single-window filing through **depositories**
- **Lower/nil TDS certificate:** rules-based automated process for small taxpayers
- **Decriminalised:** non-submission of books/documents; TDS where payment is in kind
# 2. TCS, STT and buyback changes
| Item | Old | New |
|---|---|---|
| **TCS — foreign tour packages** | **2%–20%** | **2%** |
| **TCS — LRS for education and medical** | **5%** | **2%** |
| **TCS — tendu leaves** | **5%** | **2%** |
| **TCS — liquor, scrap, minerals sellers** | — | **2%** |
| **STT — futures** | **0.02%** | **0.05%** |
| **STT — options premium and exercise** | — | **0.15%** |
- **Buybacks:** taxed as **capital gains** for all shareholders — effective tax **22%** (corporate promoters), **30%** (non-corporate promoters)
- **MAT credit:** old credit set-off only for companies moving to the **new regime**; capped at **one-fourth**
# 3. IT services and transfer pricing
- **"Information Technology Services":** single category merging software development, ITeS, KPO and contract R&D
- **Safe harbour margin (IT services):** **15.5%**; threshold raised from **₹300 crore** to **₹2,000 crore**; approvals automated, valid up to **5 years**
- **Unilateral APAs (IT services):** target to conclude within **2 years**, extendable by **6 months**; modified return facility extended to **associated enterprises**
# 4. Data centres, bonded zones and non-residents
- **Data centres:** tax holiday up to **2047** for foreign companies providing **cloud services** to global customers through Indian data centres
  - Related-entity data centre services from India — **15%** safe harbour on costs
- **Component warehousing (bonded warehouses):** safe harbour margin **2%** of invoice value (tax about **0.7%**)
- **Toll manufacturing in bonded zones:** **5-year** income-tax exemption to non-residents supplying capital goods/equipment/tooling
- **Non-resident experts:** global income exempt for a **5-year** stay under notified schemes
- **MAT exemption:** for non-residents paying tax on a **presumptive basis**
- **ICDS:** joint committee of **Ministry of Corporate Affairs** and **CBDT** to merge **ICDS into Ind AS**; separate ICDS accounting removed from **2027-28**
- **Safe harbour rules:** definition of **"accountant"** simplified
# 5. Cooperatives
- **Cooperatives:** deduction extended to societies supplying **cattle feed** and **cotton seeds**; **3-year** dividend exemption for notified **National Cooperative Federations** (investments up to **31 January 2026**)

# 2. Changes over time
- **Time:** **5 PM** until **1999**; **Yashwant Sinha** moved it to **11 AM**
- **Date:** last working day of February until **2016**; **1 February** from **2017** (**Arun Jaitley**)
- **Railway Budget:** separate from **1924**; merged in **2017** (**Arun Jaitley**)
- **Printing:** **Rashtrapati Bhavan** until **1950** → after **1950 leak**, **Minto Road** → **North Block** basement (press from **1980**)
- **Language:** English only until **1955**; then **Hindi and English**
- **Bahi-Khata:** **2019** — **Nirmala Sitharaman** replaced the briefcase
- **Halwa ceremony:** **North Block** ground floor, marks start of printing about **10 days** before; staff under **24-hour Intelligence Bureau** watch
- ⚠ Source spells the press site both **Mint Road** and **Minto Road**

## 16th Finance Commission report | from: TOPIC-16th-Finance-Commission
# 1. Basics
- **Body:** constitutional, **Article 280**; appointed by the **President** every **5 years**
- **Chairman:** **Dr. Arvind Panagariya** (constituted **2024**)
- **Report tabled:** **1 February 2026** with the Union Budget, by **Nirmala Sitharaman**; key recommendations **accepted**
- **Period:** **2026-27 to 2030-31** (**5 years**)
- **Vertical devolution:** States' share in central taxes; **horizontal:** distribution among States
# 2. Vertical devolution
- **States' share:** **41%** of the divisible pool — **retained** as under the **15th FC**
- **Divisible pool:** gross tax revenue minus **cesses**, **surcharges**, cost of collection
- **Shrinking pool:** **89.1%** of gross tax revenue in **2014-15** → about **74–80%** in **2020-24** — due to cesses and surcharges
- ⚠ Share **not increased** — Commission cited the Union's tight fiscal position
# 3. Horizontal devolution criteria
| Criterion | 16th FC weight |
|---|---|
| **Income distance** | **42.5%** (reduced) |
| **Population** | **17.5%** (increased) |
| **Area** | **10%** (reduced) |
| **Forest and ecology** | **10%** (retained) |
- A **new parameter** was introduced
- Gainers: **Karnataka**, **Andhra Pradesh**, **Kerala**, **Tamil Nadu**, **Telangana** — addresses southern States' population-control concern
