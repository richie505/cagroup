# 4. Background
- **Instituted:** **1954**; announced on **Republic Day** by **Ministry of Home Affairs** notification
- **Suspended twice:** **1978–1979** and **1993–1997**
- **Limit:** not more than **120** a year (excluding posthumous and foreigners)

- **No cash prize**; order: **Padma Vibhushan** > **Padma Bhushan** > **Padma Shri**
- **Fields:** Arts, Social Work, Public Affairs, Science and Engineering, Trade and Industry, Medicine, Literature and Education, Civil Services, Sports, Others

# 3. Background — Netaji Subhas Chandra Bose
- **Born:** **23 January 1897**; **died:** **18 August 1945**, air crash near **Taihoku** (Taiwan)
- **125th birth anniversary:** **23 January 2022** — statue near **India Gate** (sculptor **Arun Yogiraj**)
- **Slogans:** "**Give me blood, and I will give you freedom**"; "**Chalo Dilli**", "**Jai Hind**"
- **Organisations:** **Forward Bloc** (founded **3 May 1939**); **Indian National Army** (1943–45)
- **Newspapers:** **Swaraj** (founder), **Forward** (editor); **book:** **The Indian Struggle**
- **Congress President:** **Haripura** (**1938**) and **Tripuri** (**1939**) sessions; **1930** Mayor of **Calcutta**
- **Azad Hind Government:** **21 October 1943**, **Singapore** — recognised by **9 countries**; Bose was PM, Foreign and Defence Minister
- **30 December 1943:** Tricolour hoisted at **Port Blair**, Andaman and Nicobar Islands
- **6 July 1944:** first to call **Gandhi** "**Father of the Nation**" (radio, Singapore)
- **2018:** **Ross Island** renamed **Netaji Subhas Chandra Bose Island**; **2021:** birthday declared **Parakram Diwas**
- ⚠ **Bharat Ratna 1992** (posthumous) **withdrawn in 1997** — the **only** such case
- **Azad Hind Fauj:** originally formed **29 October 1915** by **Raja Mahendra Pratap Singh**, **Rash Behari Bose**, **Niranjan Singh Gill**; INA handed to Bose by **Rash Behari Bose** (**4 July 1943**)
