=== TOPIC special:06 | Reports & Indices

## World Press Freedom Index 2026 | from: TOPIC-World-Press-Freedom-Index
# 1. Key facts
- **Published by:** **Reporters Without Borders (RSF)** — **180** countries
- **India:** **157th** (down from **151st** in 2025) — **"Difficult"** category
- **Top 5:** **Norway (1st)**, **Netherlands**, **Estonia**, **Denmark**, **Sweden**
- **Bottom 5:** **Eritrea**, **North Korea**, **China**, **Iran**, **Saudi Arabia**
- ⚠ India **fell 6 places** — 151 → 157
# 2. Indicators (5)
- **Political context**, **Legal framework**, **Economic context**, **Socio-cultural context**, **Safety of journalists**
# 3. Global trends
- Press freedom at its **lowest level in decades**
- More than **50%** of countries in **"Difficult"** or **"Very Serious"**; about **100** countries declined
- **India's reasons:** violence against journalists, arrests and legal pressure, media ownership concentration, political influence, online harassment
# 4. Background
- **RSF:** est. **1985**; HQ **Paris, France**
- **Article 19(1)(a):** freedom of speech and expression — press freedom **implicitly** protected
