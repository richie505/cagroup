=== TOPIC special:03 | Environment

## India's 100th Ramsar Site — Surha Tal | from: TOPIC-Ramsar-Sites
# 1. What and where
- **Site:** **Jayaprakash Narayan Bird Sanctuary (Surha Tal)** — **Ballia** district, **Uttar Pradesh**
- **Milestone:** India's **100th Ramsar Site** (**June 2026**)
- **Type:** **freshwater** wetland — habitat for migratory and resident birds
- **Roles:** ecological balance, **groundwater recharge**, **fisheries**, local livelihoods
# 2. Exam angle
- ⚠ **99th** was **Shekha Jheel Bird Sanctuary**, **Aligarh (UP)** — **22 April 2026**
- ⚠ **97th–98th**: **Patna Bird Sanctuary (Etah, UP)** and **Chhari-Dhand (Kutch, Gujarat)** — **World Wetlands Day 2026**
- ⚠ With these, **Uttar Pradesh** has **13** sites — second after **Tamil Nadu (20)**

## India and the Ramsar Convention — key facts | from: TOPIC-Ramsar-Sites
# 1. Convention
- **Signed:** **1971** at **Ramsar, Iran** — wetlands of **international importance**
- **India joined:** **1 February 1982**
# 2. India's numbers
- **Total sites:** **100** (June 2026); area about **14 lakh hectares**
- **Rank:** **1st in Asia**, **3rd in the world**
- **Growth:** **26** sites (**2012**) → **98** (January 2026) → **100** (June 2026); **56** added after **2020**
- ⚠ One source gives **26 sites in 2014** (growth over **276%** to 98); the other says **2012**
# 3. Records
| Record | Site |
|---|---|
| **First sites (1981)** | **Chilika Lake (Odisha)**, **Keoladeo National Park (Rajasthan)** |
| **Largest** | **Sundarbans**, **West Bengal** |
| **Smallest** | **Renuka Wetland**, **Himachal Pradesh** |
| **Most sites** | **Tamil Nadu – 20** |
| **Second** | **Uttar Pradesh – 13** (after Surha Tal) |
| **Only site in Andhra Pradesh** | **Kolleru Lake (2002)** |
- ⚠ Older list (98 sites) shows **UP – 11**; it became **13** with **Shekha Jheel** and **Surha Tal**
