## Amaravati to be named capital in AP Reorganisation Act | from: APCA-2025-05
# 1. Cabinet decision
- **Decision:** AP Cabinet approved a proposal to replace the words **"new capital of residuary Andhra Pradesh"** with **"Amaravati"** in the **AP Reorganisation Act, 2014**
- **Purpose:** legal recognition and **statutory legitimacy** for **Amaravati** as capital
- ⚠ The Act is a **central law** — only **Parliament** can amend it; Cabinet adopted a resolution and will lobby MPs
# 2. Related approvals
| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| **APCRDA** | Cabinet ratified decisions of the **47th meeting** of APCRDA; land allotments to institutions |
| **Four projects** | **₹1,732.306 crore** — contracts to **L1** (lowest) bidders |
| **Amaravati Capital City Development Project** | package of **₹560.57 crore**; funded jointly by **World Bank** and **Asian Development Bank (ADB)**; **Design, Build and Operate (DBO)** contracts |
# 3. Background
- **AP Reorganisation Act, 2014** bifurcated erstwhile AP and created **Telangana**; AP became the **residuary state**

- **Named after Dr Satish Dhawan in 2002**; **two** operational launch pads; proximity to **equator** = fuel-efficient launches
