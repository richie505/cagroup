## West Godavari Collector nominated for AP State Red Cross Award | from: APCA-2025-05
# 1. News
- **Who:** **Chadalawada Naga Rani**, Collector, **West Godavari**
- **Award:** **AP State Red Cross Award** for **2023–24 and 2024–25** (two consecutive years)
- **For:** flood relief during **Budameru** river floods
- **Announced by:** **A.K. Parida**, General Secretary and CEO, IRCS AP branch
- **Occasion:** **World Red Cross Day** and **World Thalassemia Day** (**8 May**)
# 2. Background — Indian Red Cross Society
- **Established:** **1920** under **Indian Red Cross Society Act** (Act **XV of 1920**); amended **1992**, rules **1994**
- **President:** **President of India**; **Chairman:** **Union Health Minister**
- **Managing Body:** **18** members — **6** + Chairman nominated by President, **12** elected by branches
- **Network:** **36** State/UT branches; over **1,200** district branches
- **Movement:** **ICRC**, National Societies, **IFRC**

## Major Malla Rama Gopal Naidu — first from Telugu states to get Kirti Chakra | from: APCA-2025-05
# 1. News
- **Who:** **Major Malla Rama Gopal Naidu**, born **16 June 1995**, **Nagaripenta**, **Santhabommali** mandal, **Srikakulam**
- **Feat:** killed **five terrorists** on the **LoC** in **Kupwara**, J&K — zero Indian casualties
- ⚠ **First** from **AP and Telangana** to receive the **Kirti Chakra**
- **Career:** Lieutenant **2016**, Captain **2018**, Major **2022**
# 2. Background — Kirti Chakra
- ⚠ **Second-highest peacetime gallantry award** (after **Ashoka Chakra**)
- **Instituted:** **1952**; civilians and military; posthumous allowed; presented by the **President**
