=== TOPIC 2025-05:03 | Schemes & Social Sector

## Jan Aushadhi stores at all GGHs in AP | from: APCA-2025-05
# 1. News
- **Decision:** AP Health Dept to set up **Jan Aushadhi** stores in all **Government General Hospitals (GGHs)**
- **Review:** **16 GGHs** — **23** private medical stores; margins **50%–600%**; **Nandyal GGH** had none; stocked only **30–40%** essential generics
- **PM-BJAP:** profit margin capped at **15%**; software by **Department of Pharmaceuticals**
- **Branded drugs:** only with prior approval when no generic exists
- **Oversight:** **District Collectors** (who head local Red Cross branches)
- **Next:** feasibility for **District** and **Area Hospitals**
# 2. Emergency preparedness directive
- **Signed by:** **G. Veerapandian**, Commissioner of Health & FW and Mission Director, **NHM**
- **Why:** India–Pakistan tensions
- **Measures:** stocks of medicines, oxygen, blood, trauma kits; **all leaves cancelled**; district nodal officers; 24x7 control rooms; **CBRN** (Chemical, Biological, Radiological, Nuclear) preparedness
