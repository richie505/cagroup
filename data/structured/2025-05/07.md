## Sea slug Phidiana unilineata rediscovered off Visakhapatnam after 160 years | from: APCA-2025-05
# 1. News
- **Species:** **Phidiana unilineata** — a **nudibranch** (soft-bodied marine **gastropod mollusc**)
- **When:** **City Nature Challenge 2025** (April) — global **citizen science** initiative
- **Found by:** **Sri Chakra Pranav**, founder, **East Coast Conservation Team (ECCT)**
- **First recorded:** **1864**, from **Visakhapatnam**, by British zoologists **Joshua Alder** and **Albany Hancock**
- ⚠ No sighting since **1864** — possible **endemic** to Visakhapatnam coast (rocky shores, hydroids)
- **Alder and Hancock** recorded over **20** sea slug species from Visakhapatnam in the 19th century
- **Indicator:** sea slugs indicate healthy marine ecosystems (depend on corals, hydroids)

## Karnataka hands over kumki elephants to AP | from: APCA-2025-05
# 1. News
- **What:** Karnataka gave **four kumki elephants** to AP; **two more** to follow
- **From:** **Dubare Elephant Camp**, **Kodagu** (35 elephants)
- **To:** special forest camp in **Chittoor** district — conflict areas **Kuppam** and **Palakundalam**
- **MoU:** after talks between Karnataka Forest Minister **Eshwar B. Khandre** and AP Deputy CM **Pawan Kalyan** (Aug–Sept 2023); AP to report on elephants' welfare
- **Training:** **25** AP mahouts trained in Karnataka two months earlier; handlers are **mahouts** and **kawadis**
# 2. Background
- **Kumki:** trained captive elephants used to drive away/capture wild elephants
- **Karnataka** has over **100** trained elephants; since **2016** gave over **65** to **Uttarakhand, Jharkhand, UP, MP, Bihar, Maharashtra**
- ⚠ First Karnataka inter-state transfer after a **two-year** gap; **Maharashtra** was the previous recipient

## Artificial reefs off Jalari Yendada, Visakhapatnam | from: APCA-2025-05
# 1. News
- **Launched:** **20 May 2025** by Fisheries Minister **Kinjarapu Atchannaidu**; with **seaweed harvest** programme
- **Phase I:** **22** artificial reef units in North Andhra waters; concrete shapes (triangles, flowers, pipes)
- **Funding:** **60%** Centre, **40%** State under **PMMSY**
- **Distance:** within **2.5 km** of shore — fuel cost cut up to **80%**
- **Target species:** kingfish, tuna, red snapper, prawns, crabs, squids, octopuses
- **Project leader:** Dr **Joe Kizhakudan**, Principal Scientist, **CMFRI**
- **Also:** **634 transponders** for mechanised boats
# 2. Background — PMMSY
- **Pradhan Mantri Matsya Sampada Yojana:** launched **2020**; Blue Economy; **SDG-14** (Life Below Water)
