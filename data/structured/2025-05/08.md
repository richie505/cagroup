## CEEW heat risk study — Andhra Pradesh tops | from: APCA-2025-05
# 1. News
- **Study:** **CEEW** (Council on Energy, Environment and Water) — **"How Extreme Heat is Impacting India: Assessing District-level Heat Risk"**
- **Rank 1:** ⚠ **Andhra Pradesh** — most heat-risk-prone state
- **100% districts high/very high risk:** **AP, Kerala, Maharashtra, Goa**
- **AP districts:** **62%** high risk, **38%** very high risk
- **India:** **57%** of districts high/very high risk — home to **76%** of population
# 2. Trends
- **Major heatwaves:** **2013, 2016, 2019, 2022, 2024**
- **Nights warming faster:** **70%** of AP districts have at least **5** extra 'very warm' nights per summer (March–June)
- **Diurnal Temperature Range (DTR)** has **decreased** — linked to higher mortality, malaria/dengue
- **Urban heat islands:** **Visakhapatnam, Vijayawada, Rajahmundry, Tirupati, Guntur**
- **J&K and Ladakh:** **15+** more very hot days and warm nights per summer
