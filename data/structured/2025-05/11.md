## Sri Modakondamma Jatara, Paderu | from: APCA-2025-05
# 1. News
- **Where:** **Modakondamma Temple, Paderu**, **Alluri Sitharama Raju** district, in the **Eastern Ghats**
- **When:** annual tribal festival in **May** — 2025 on **11, 12, 13 May**
- **Deity:** **Grama Devatha**, form of Goddess **Durga**; revered by **Kondhs** and **Koyas**
- **Rituals:** processions, tribal dances, community feasts, homams, abhishekam, alankaram
