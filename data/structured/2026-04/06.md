## Gaganyaan — safe return mechanism | from: 2026-04-10
# 1. Mission basics
- **India's first human spaceflight** — **3 astronauts**, **LEO ~400 km**, up to **3 days**
- **Launcher:** **human-rated LVM3** (GSLV Mk III) — solid, liquid, cryogenic stages (⚠ **not PSLV**)
- **Orbital Module** = **Crew Module** (pressurised, habitable, thermal protection) + **Service Module** (unpressurised; separates before re-entry)
- **Crew Escape System:** validated by **TV-D1**; uses **solid rocket motors**; works during **launch/ascent** (⚠ not re-entry)
- **Vyommitra:** **female humanoid robot** for uncrewed missions
- India would join **USA, Russia, China** with independent human spaceflight capability
# 2. Return sequence
| Step | Detail |
|---|---|
| **De-orbit burn** | CM separates from SM |
| **Re-entry / aerobraking** | Drag reduces speed from ~**7.8 km/s** |
| **Thermal protection** | Heat above **1500°C** |
| **Parachutes** | Pilot + drogue, then main — deployed ~**12 km** altitude |
| **Splashdown** | **Bay of Bengal**; sea absorbs **7–9 m/s** impact (vs 1–2 m/s on land) |
| **Recovery** | **Indian Navy** — ships, helicopters, divers; GPS, beacons, dye |
- **Tests:** **IADT** (Integrated Air Drop Test), **Pad Abort Test**, Test Vehicle flights
# 3. Aditya-L1 (related)
- India's **first** solar observatory at **L1** (~**1.5 million km**); ISRO's **second** observatory-class mission after **AstroSat (2015)**; ~**125 days** to L1 (vs ~300 days for MOM)

## Kalpakkam PFBR attains criticality — stage II of nuclear programme | from: 2026-04-27, CA360-2026-04, OL-2026-04
# 1. News
- **Prototype Fast Breeder Reactor (PFBR)**, **Kalpakkam (Tamil Nadu)** attained **criticality** — PM **Narendra Modi** called it historic
- **500 MW**, **sodium-cooled**; built by **BHAVINI** (Bharatiya Nabhikiya Vidyut Nigam Ltd, under **DAE**); **200+** Indian industries
- India enters **Stage II**; ⚠ only **Russia** has similar large-scale FBR success
# 2. Concepts
- **Criticality:** self-sustaining chain reaction — **k = 1** (subcritical k<1, supercritical k>1)
- **FBR:** produces more fissile fuel than it consumes; **fast neutrons**, **no moderator**; **MOX** fuel (Pu + U); **U-238 blanket** → **Pu-239**; **liquid sodium** coolant (low dissolved oxygen for corrosion resistance)
- **Critical mass** depends on shape, density, purity; applies to reactors and weapons
- Chain reaction idea — **Leo Szilard (1933)**
# 3. Three-stage programme (Homi J. Bhabha)
| Stage | Reactor | Fuel |
|---|---|---|
| **I** | **PHWR** | Natural uranium → Pu-239 by-product |
| **II** | **FBR** | Pu-based MOX |
| **III** | Thorium reactors | **Th-232 → U-233** |
- Plants: Tarapur, Rajasthan, Kalpakkam, Narora, Kakrapar, Kaiga, Kudankulam; Kalpakkam has **MAPS**, **IGCAR**

## SHANTI Act 2025 and 100 GW nuclear target by 2047 | from: 2026-04-06, CA360-2026-04, OL-2026-04
# 1. Targets
- **Nuclear capacity:** ~**8,180 MW** → **100 GW by 2047** (over **twelvefold**); investment > **$200 billion**
- **Installed electricity capacity (June 2025):** ~**476 GW**, ~**50%** non-fossil
- **Per capita electricity consumption (2024):** **1,418 kWh**
- Aligned with **Viksit Bharat 2047** and **net zero 2070**
# 2. SHANTI Act, 2025 — key provisions
- **Full form:** **Sustainable Harnessing and Advancement of Nuclear Energy for Transforming India**
- **Replaces** **Atomic Energy Act, 1962** and **Civil Liability for Nuclear Damage Act, 2010**
- **Private companies, JVs, foreign entities** can build, own, operate, decommission plants — ends **NPCIL** monopoly
- **Operator solely liable**; ⚠ **supplier liability removed**; operator liability graded by capacity; govt liability capped
- **Statutory status** to **Atomic Energy Regulatory Board (AERB)**
# 3. SMRs and strategy
- **Small Modular Reactors:** up to **300 MW**; factory-built; **passive safety**
- **₹20,000 crore** for **five indigenous SMR** models by **2033**
- **Strategy:** indigenise foreign designs; indigenous SMRs; modularise **220 MW PHWR**
- ⚠ Another source: nuclear capacity **8.8 GW → 100 GW by 2047**; nuclear share ~**3%**; **NPCIL 1987**, **DAE 1954**, **AERB 1983** (all Mumbai)

## Quantum entanglement in heavier atoms | from: 2026-04-01
# 1. Key facts
- **Quantum entanglement:** shared quantum state — measuring one particle's property (momentum, spin) instantly fixes its partner's, regardless of distance
- Earlier shown mainly in **photons** and **electrons**; recently demonstrated in heavier atoms like **Helium**
- ⚠ **Nonlocality** is a **quantum** feature — **classical physics** is based on **locality**

## Space governance and commercialisation of outer space | from: 2026-04-02
# 1. Legal framework
| Instrument | Key point |
|---|---|
| **Outer Space Treaty, 1967** | Foundation of space law; **states responsible** for national space activities incl. **private** entities; space = **global commons** |
| **Liability Convention, 1972** | Compensation for damage by space objects — **absolute liability** on Earth/aircraft; **fault-based** in outer space |

# 2. Exam angle
- **Kessler Syndrome:** chain reaction of debris collisions making orbits unusable
- Only objects **>10 cm** in **LEO** consistently tracked; **25-year de-orbit rule** — weak compliance monitoring

## Keytruda (Pembrolizumab) and counterfeit cancer drugs | from: 2026-04-13
# 1. News
- **Counterfeit Keytruda** market reported in India — high cost (> **₹3 lakh/month**), weak procurement oversight
- Govt removed **basic customs duty**; company **Patient Access Programme** (buy **5** doses → **30** free)
- India cancer burden projected **24.5 lakh** cases by **2045**
# 2. Science
- Developed by **Merck & Co. (MSD)**; **US FDA** approval **2014**
- **Monoclonal antibody**, **immune checkpoint inhibitor** — blocks **PD-1 receptor on T-cells**
- **PD-1–PD-L1** binding switches off T-cells; blocking reactivates immunity — ⚠ does **not** directly kill cancer cells
- **Treats:** lung, cervical, **renal cell carcinoma**, **triple-negative breast cancer**
- **Other immunotherapies:** **CAR-T cell therapy**, **mRNA cancer vaccines**
