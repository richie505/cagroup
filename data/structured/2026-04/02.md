## Inter-State redistribution of Lok Sabha seats on 2011 Census | from: 2026-04-15, 2026-04-16, 2026-04-20, CA360-2026-04, OL-2026-04
# 1. Proposal
- Union considering **Constitutional Amendment** + fresh **delimitation** on **2011 Census**; Lok Sabha to ~**850** seats; enables **33%** women's reservation
- Now: inter-State allocation on **1971 Census**; constituency boundaries on **2001 Census**
- **Hindi belt** (e.g. **UP**) gains; southern States (e.g. **Kerala**) marginal; opposition from **TN, Kerala, Karnataka, Telangana, Punjab**
# 2. Freeze history
| Amendment | Effect |
|---|---|
| **42nd (1976)** | Froze seats on **1971 Census** — to reward population control |
| **84th (2001)** | Freeze extended till **first Census after 2026** |
| **87th (2003)** | Constituency delimitation on **2001 Census**; seat totals still frozen |
- **Art 82** (Lok Sabha), **Art 170** (Assemblies); Delimitation Commission orders **cannot be challenged in courts**
- Principle: "**one person, one vote, one value**"
# 3. Parliament static GK
- **Art 79:** Parliament = **President + Lok Sabha + Rajya Sabha**
- **Art 80:** RS max **250** (**238** elected + **12** nominated); RS permanent — **one-third** retire every **two years**; seats by **population** (not equal)
- RS members elected only by **elected MLAs** (⚠ not MLCs) — **STV** proportional representation
- **Art 170:** Assembly max **500**, min **60** (exceptions: Goa, Sikkim, Mizoram)
- **Art 333** Anglo-Indian nomination **abolished** by **104th Amendment (2019)**
# 4. Constitution (131st Amendment) Bill and Delimitation Bill
- **Introduced:** **Constitution (131st Amendment) Bill** — Lok Sabha max **850** (**815 States + 35 UTs**); **Delimitation Bill** on **2011 Census**; one-third women's reservation
- Amends **Art 81**, **Art 82** (removes post-2026 freeze) and **Art 334A** (early women's reservation)
- **"Schedule" mechanism:** law to list State-wise seats — Lok Sabha up ~**50%** while **keeping each State's percentage share unchanged**
- Earlier constitutional max: **550** members
- **Art 75(1A):** Council of Ministers ≤ **15%** of Lok Sabha → up to ~**122** ministers at 850
- **Art 108** joint sitting — larger Lok Sabha dominance; ⚠ **Rajya Sabha** strength (Art 80) does **not** change automatically
- Gainers: **UP, Bihar, Rajasthan, MP**; relative losers: **Kerala, Tamil Nadu**
# 5. Delimitation Commission facts
- Constituted **four times:** **1952, 1963, 1973, 2002**; last exercise completed **2008** (2001 Census)
- **Statutory** body under **Delimitation Act**; appointed by **President**; chair — **retired SC judge**; members — **CEC** + **State Election Commissioners**
- Orders **not** needing Parliament approval; ⚠ **Article 329** bars courts; decides **SC/ST** reserved constituencies
- **Art 331** (2 Anglo-Indian LS nominees) — discontinued by **104th Amendment (2019)**
# 6. 131st Amendment Bill defeated
- ⚠ **Constitution (131st Amendment) Bill, 2026 was defeated** in **April 2026** — revived debate on delinking women's reservation from delimitation
- Bill sought max Lok Sabha **550 → 850** (Art 81); changes in **Arts 82, 170, 334A** for delimitation on **2011 Census**; needed **special majority** + ratification by **half of States**
- Package of three Bills: **131st Amendment Bill**, **Delimitation Bill, 2026** (new Commission replacing **Delimitation Act, 2002** framework; would fix SC, ST and women's seats), **Union Territories Laws (Amendment) Bill, 2026** (Delhi, J&K, Puducherry)
- **Women's representation:** Lok Sabha ~**14–15%**; State Assemblies ~**9%**; **Rwanda** >**60%**, **Sweden** ~**46%**
- **History:** first Women's Reservation Bill **1996**; passed **Rajya Sabha** in **2010** but lapsed; enacted **2023**; ⚠ **no OBC sub-quota**
- Next Census ~**2027** → implementation likely ~**2033–34**
- **Delimitation Commission** — temporary body, dissolves after work; orders laid before Parliament for **information only**; **Art 329(a)**
- **Art 243T:** SC/ST seats in every municipality in proportion to population; **Art 169:** Council abolition by **ordinary law** after Assembly special-majority resolution
# 7. Lok Sabha vote on 131st Amendment Bill
- **528** voted — **298 in favour**, **230 against**; ⚠ failed to get required special majority of **326** (Art 368)
- **106th Amendment Act** brought **into force from April 16, 2026** (procedural step); Act passed **September 2023**; valid **15 years**
- Census every 10 years under **Census Act, 1948**; Women's Reservation Bill first introduced **1996**
- Lok Sabha: est. **1952**; max **552** (as per source); current **543**; **Art 83** — 5-year tenure
- ⚠ CA360 phrasing: **42nd Amendment (1976)** froze seats **until 2000**; **84th** extended freeze **till 2026**

## Lok Sabha expansion to ~816 seats for women's reservation | from: 2026-04-01, 2026-04-06
# 1. Proposal
- **Aim:** implement **women's reservation** without waiting for **delimitation** after next **Census**
- **Strength:** **543 → ~816** members; ~**273** seats (**one-third**) reserved for **women**
- **From:** **2029 Lok Sabha elections**; state-wise share of seats kept same in initial phase (**1971 Census** basis)
| Parameter | Current | Proposed |
|---|---|---|
| **Total seats** | **543** | **~816** |
| **Women representation** | **~14%** | **~33%** |
| **Basis of allocation** | **1971 Census** | Same (initial phase) |
| **Delimitation** | Mandatory for change | Temporarily bypassed |
# 2. Constitutional angle
- **Article 81:** composition of Lok Sabha — needs **amendment** to raise seats
- **Article 82:** **readjustment / delimitation** after each Census
- **106th Constitutional Amendment Act, 2023** (Women's Reservation / **Nari Shakti Vandan**) — **33%** reservation in **Lok Sabha** and **State Assemblies**; linked to delimitation
- **Amendment needs special majority:** **two-thirds** of members **present and voting** + **majority of total membership**
- ⚠ Seat freeze based on **1971 Census** continues **until after 2026** (first Census after 2026)
- ⚠ Expansion does **not** automatically trigger delimitation
# 3. Women's Reservation Act — features and roadmap
- **Articles inserted:** **330A** (Lok Sabha), **332A** (State Assemblies), **334A** (commencement & duration)
- **Duration:** **15 years**; **sub-reservation** for **SC/ST women**; **rotation** of reserved seats after each delimitation
- Linked to delimitation after **first Census after 2026** — implementation unlikely before **2032–33**
- **Delimitation:** **Articles 82** and **170**; **Delimitation Commission** appointed by **President**; orders have **force of law** — ⚠ **not subject to judicial review**
- Seat allocation freeze till after **2026** — **84th Amendment**
- **Proposed mechanism:** ~**273** extra women's seats allotted to parties by **vote share** in last general election (limited **proportional representation**) from pre-declared lists — hybrid with **FPTP**; temporary for one or two cycles

# 2. CEC — key facts
- **ECI:** constitutional body under **Article 324**; established **25 January 1950**; single-member till **1989**
- **Appointment:** by **President** on recommendation of selection committee — **PM**, **LoP in Lok Sabha**, a **Union Cabinet Minister** (**CEC and Other ECs Act, 2023**)
- **Tenure:** **6 years** or **65 years** of age, whichever earlier
- **Removal:** **Article 324(5)** — like SC judge; **proved misbehaviour or incapacity**; **special majority** in **both Houses**
- ECI conducts elections to Parliament, **State Legislatures**, **President**, **Vice-President**

| Case | Held |
|---|---|
| **Mohinder Singh Gill (1978)** | Art 324 gives **plenary powers** where law is silent |

## Jan Vishwas (Amendment of Provisions) Bill 2026 passed | from: 2026-04-04, 2026-04-09
# 1. News
- **Passed by Parliament** — decriminalises **minor offences**; **trust-based governance**, **ease of doing business**
- **Coverage:** ~**80 Central Acts**; **1,000+ provisions** rationalised
# 2. Key provisions

- **Jan Vishwas Act, 2023:** amended **42 Central laws**
- **Adjudicating Officers** impose penalties; structured appeal; serious offences (public safety, environment, fraud) keep criminal liability
- Linked to **World Bank B-READY** (Business Ready) framework; concern — **regulatory capture**, fines as cost of business
