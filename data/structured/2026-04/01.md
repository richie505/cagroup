## AP Reorganisation (Amendment) Bill 2026 — Amaravati declared capital | from: 2026-04-02, 2026-04-06, 2026-04-07, CARE-2026-04, CA360-2026-04, OL-2026-04
# 1. News
- **Lok Sabha passed** the **Andhra Pradesh Reorganisation (Amendment) Bill, 2026** — formally declares **Amaravati** as **capital of AP**
- **Change:** "there shall be a new capital" → "**Amaravati shall be the new capital**"
- **Amaravati defined** as the area notified under the **AP Capital Region Development Authority (APCRDA) Act, 2014**
- **Retrospective effect:** from **2 June 2024**
- **AP Legislative Assembly resolution** requesting the amendment: **28 March 2026** — reflects **cooperative federalism**
# 2. Background
- **AP Reorganisation Act, 2014** — enacted under **Article 3**; created **Telangana**
- **Section 5:** **Hyderabad** = common capital for **not more than 10 years** from **2 June 2014** (i.e., till **2024**)
- ⚠ The **2014 Act did not name** Amaravati or any city as capital — only said a new capital would be established
# 3. Parliament passage and history
- **Parliament passed** the Bill — Amaravati = **sole and permanent capital**; broad support except **YSR Congress Party** (had pushed **three-capital model**)
- **2019:** then govt proposed **three capitals** (executive, legislative, judicial functions split)
- **2022:** **AP High Court** ruled the State **lacked legislative competence** to shift the capital
- Amendment is to **Section 5** of the 2014 Act
# 4. Amaravati capital project
- **Location:** banks of **Krishna River**, between **Vijayawada** and **Guntur**
- **Planned area:** ~**217 sq. km**; developed via **Land Pooling Scheme** (farmers get developed plots + compensation)
- **Implementing body:** **APCRDA**; master plan with **Surbana Jurong** (Singapore)
# 5. Presidential assent and constitutional angle
- **President Droupadi Murmu** granted **assent** → **AP Reorganisation (Amendment) Act, 2026** — inserts "**at Amaravati**" in Section 5
- **Three-capital model (post-2019):** **Visakhapatnam** (executive), **Amaravati** (legislative), **Kurnool** (judicial) — via **AP Decentralisation and Inclusive Development of All Regions Act, 2020**; ⚠ **repealed in 2021**, judicial capital never operationalised
- **2022 HC:** State cannot arbitrarily change capital — **legitimate expectation** of **Land Pooling** farmers; **rule of law**; judicial review under **Articles 32 & 226**
- ⚠ The **Constitution does not specify** how a State capital is determined — statutory law fills the gap
- **APCRDA Act, 2014:** special planning body — **Town Planning Schemes**, **Betterment Charges**; overriding powers over **Gram Panchayats** in capital region
# 6. Related static GK
- **Article 169:** create/abolish Legislative Council — Assembly resolution by **special majority**
- **Legislative Council:** permanent house; **one-third** members retire **every two years**
- **Article 174:** Governor dissolves Assembly on advice of **Council of Ministers headed by CM**
- **AP Reorganisation Act 2014:** Governor of common capital (**Hyderabad**) had **special responsibility** for **law and order**, internal security; ⚠ institutions of national importance stay with the **Union**
# 7. Gazette notification
- Passed by **Parliament on April 1 and 2, 2026**; **Presidential assent under Article 111**; in force after **Gazette notification** by **Ministry of Law**; effect from **June 2, 2024**
- **Section 5(2)** of 2014 Act amended; future changes to capital area need **Parliamentary approval**
- AP: formed **1956**, reorganised **2014**; Governor **S. Abdul Nazeer**

## Justice Lisa Gill appointed Chief Justice of AP High Court | from: 2026-04-24, 2026-04-28, CARE-2026-04, CA360-2026-04, OL-2026-04
# 1. News
- **President Droupadi Murmu** appointed **Justice Lisa Gill** as **Chief Justice, AP High Court** — assumes office **April 25, 2026**
- Succeeds **Justice Dhiraj Singh Thakur** (retired **April 24**)
- Earlier transferred from **Punjab and Haryana HC** to AP HC; was a **puisne judge** (regular HC judge other than CJ)
# 2. Constitutional provisions
| Article | Provision |
|---|---|
| **214** | High Court for each State |
| **217** | Appointment by **President** after consulting **CJI** and **Governor** |
| **222** | **Transfer** of HC judges — consultation with CJI mandatory; ⚠ judge's consent not needed |
| **217(1)(b) + 124(4)** | Removal — proved misbehaviour or incapacity |
| **235** | HC control over subordinate courts |
- **Eligibility:** citizen; **10 years** judicial office or **10 years** HC advocate
- **Retirement:** HC **62**, SC **65**; oath before **Governor**; CJ by convention from **outside** the State
- **Collegium:** evolved via **Second** and **Third Judges Cases** (not in Constitution)
- **Removal:** motion signed by **100 LS** or **50 RS** members; inquiry under **Judges Inquiry Act, 1968** (SC judge, HC CJ, jurist); **special majority** in both Houses; ⚠ Constitution does not use "impeachment" for judges
# 3. Takes charge
- **Sworn in** by Governor **S. Abdul Nazeer** at **Lok Bhavan** (Amaravati); oath form in **Third Schedule**
- **Art 215:** HC is a **Court of Record** (contempt power); **Art 226:** writs for Fundamental Rights **and other legal rights** — ⚠ wider than **Art 32**
- HC judge resigns by writing to **President**; **Punjab & Haryana HC** serves Punjab, Haryana, Chandigarh
- ⚠ **First woman Chief Justice** of AP High Court after the **2019** reorganisation (AP HC re-established **January 1, 2019** at **Amaravati**); appointment under **Art 217(1)** on Collegium recommendation

## AP urea use stable despite 36% rise in cropped area — e-Panta | from: 2026-04-21, CARE-2026-04
# 1. News
- **Rabi 2025–26:** AP cropped area **+36%**, urea availability only **+3%**; per-hectare urea **513 kg → 338 kg** (~**34%** cut)
- **Driver:** expansion of **e-Panta** digital crop mapping — farmers **17.56 lakh → 35.51 lakh**; area **46.07 lakh → 73.55 lakh acres** (~**60%**)
- **Enforcement:** **18,500+** samples (vs ~15,000); stocks worth **₹58 crore+** detained, **₹6 crore+** seized; licences suspended **14 → 84**
# 2. Fertilizer facts
- **Urea:** **46% nitrogen**; only **30–40%** absorbed by crops
- **Ideal N:P:K = 4:2:1**
- **DAP** = N + P; ⚠ **MOP (Muriate of Potash)** = **potassic** (not phosphatic); **SSP** = phosphatic
- **Chlorophyll** — N and **magnesium** (⚠ not phosphorus); phosphorus → roots, flowering
- **Rhizobium** — legumes; ⚠ **zinc sulphate** is a chemical micronutrient fertilizer, not biofertilizer
- Excess nitrates → **Blue Baby Syndrome**, eutrophication; remedy — **neem-coated urea**, **Soil Health Card**
