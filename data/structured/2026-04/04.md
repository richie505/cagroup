## Rupee as a barometer of credibility | from: 2026-04-14
# 1. Context (2026)
- Rupee under pressure — **BoP** stress; CAD financing hard due to **negative net FDI inflows** and volatile **FPI**
# 2. Theories
- **Dornbusch Overshooting Model:** exchange rate overreacts in short run (sticky prices)
- **Mundell-Fleming / Impossible Trinity:** cannot have **fixed exchange rate + free capital mobility + independent monetary policy** together; India — **managed float**
# 3. Exam angle
- **2013 Taper Tantrum** (US Fed) — rupee fell; RBI tightened short-term rates, **oil-dollar swap**; govt raised **gold import duty**
- ⚠ RBI **buying** foreign currency → **expands** domestic money supply (sterilisation may follow)
- ⚠ Higher **FDI inflows** → rupee **appreciates** (not depreciates)
- Depreciation → imported inflation, higher external debt burden
- **Fowler Committee (1898):** **Gold Exchange Standard** — rupee pegged to sterling at **1s 4d** (**15 rupees = £1**) — for **Home Charges**

## Piped Natural Gas (PNG) push and gas-based economy | from: 2026-04-04
# 1. News

- Gas allocation prioritised for **households** and essential services under **Essential Commodities Act**
# 2. Supply chain
- **LNG** imported → **regasified** at coastal terminals → **trunk pipelines** → **City Gate Stations (CGS)** → steel/polyethylene city network → metered households
- Planning integrated with **PM Gati Shakti**; part of **City Gas Distribution (CGD)**
# 3. Fuel comparison
| Fuel | Composition / form | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **LPG** | **Propane + butane**, liquid in cylinders; ⚠ **heavier than air** | Household cooking |
| **CNG** | **Methane** compressed at high pressure | Transport fuel |
| **PNG** | **Methane** via pipelines; ⚠ **lighter than air**, disperses fast | Homes, industry |
| **LNG** | Natural gas cooled to ~**–160/–162°C** | Shipping/storage form; feeds PNG, CNG |
