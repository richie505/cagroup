## River Basin Management Scheme extended to 2030–31 | from: 2026-04-20, CA360-2026-04, OL-2026-04
# 1. News
- **RBM Scheme** continued for **2026–27 to 2030–31** — outlay **₹2,183 crore** (previous cycle **₹1,276 crore**); **Ministry of Jal Shakti**

# 3. Institutions
| Body | Role |
|---|---|

| **National Water Development Agency (NWDA)** | **Interlinking of Rivers** — e.g. **Ken-Betwa Link** |
