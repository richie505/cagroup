## Fisheries sector — record budget and PMMSY | from: 2026-04-07
# 1. News
- **Union Budget 2026–27:** record **₹2,761.80 crore** for **fisheries**; benefits **3 crore+** fishers
# 2. Data
- **India:** **second-largest** fish producer — ~**8%** of world production
- **Fish production:** **95.79 lakh tonnes (2013–14)** → **197.75 lakh tonnes (2024–25)**
- **Share in Agricultural GVA:** **7.43%**; **seafood exports:** **₹62,408 crore** — **frozen shrimp** dominant
- **Inland waters:** **31.5 lakh ha**; **coastline:** **11,099 km**; **EEZ:** **24 lakh sq. km**; livelihoods ~**30 million**
# 3. Schemes
| Scheme | Key facts |
|---|---|
| **Blue Revolution** | Began in **1980s** — scientific aquaculture |
| **PMMSY** | Launched **2020**; **₹20,050 crore**; "**Blue Revolution 2.0**"; infrastructure + value chain |
| **National Fisheries Digital Platform (NFDP)** | Digital identity of fishers; targeted delivery |
| **FIDF** | **Fisheries and Aquaculture Infrastructure Development Fund** — harbours, cold storage, landing centres |
| **KCC for fishers** | **Short-term credit / working capital** |
- ⚠ PMMSY = long-term infrastructure; **KCC** = short-term credit; PMMSY does **not** rely on fleet expansion
- **Techniques:** **Biofloc**, **Recirculatory Aquaculture Systems (RAS)**

## Balakrishnan Commission on SC status for converted Dalits — third extension | from: 2026-04-14, CA360-2026-04, OL-2026-04
# 1. News
- **Third extension** to Commission of Inquiry headed by ex-CJI **K.G. Balakrishnan** — till **June 2026**
- **Constituted:** **October 2022** — whether SC status be extended to Dalits converted to **Christianity** and **Islam**
# 2. Constitutional framework
- **Article 341:** **President** specifies SCs for each State/UT after consulting **Governor**; ⚠ list modified only by **Parliament**
- **Constitution (Scheduled Castes) Order, 1950:** originally **Hindus** only → **Sikhs (1956)** → **Buddhists (1990)**
- SC reservation in legislatures — **Arts 330, 332**; periodically extended (latest **up to 2030**); ⚠ no reservation in **private sector** jobs
# 3. More details
- Extension of **two more months** — report deadline **October 10, 2024 → October 10, 2025 → April 10, 2026 → June 10, 2026**; notified by **Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment**
- Set up **October 6, 2022**; members — **Justice K.G. Balakrishnan**, **Prof. Sagarambal Krishnan** (as in source), ex-IAS **Rajita Singh**
- K.G. Balakrishnan — **37th CJI (2007–2010)**, ⚠ **first Dalit CJI**

## UGC Equity Regulations 2026 stayed by Supreme Court | from: 2026-04-08
# 1. News
- **SC interim stay** on **UGC (Promotion of Equity in Higher Education Institutions) Regulations, 2026**
- Context: caste discrimination, student suicides (e.g. **Abeda Salim Tadvi case**); concerns over misuse, no general-category representation
# 2. Key provisions
- **Equal Opportunity Centre (EOC)** — nodal body in every HEI
- **Equity Committees** chaired by **head of institution** — SC, ST, OBC, women, PwD representation
- **24/7 grievance system** — helplines, portals, **Equity Officers**, **Equity Squads**
- **Bi-annual public reports**; **annual compliance report** to UGC
- Caste discrimination defined as against **SC, ST, OBC**; ⚠ **no explicit penalty for false complaints**
# 3. Constitutional basis
- **Art 15(1):** no discrimination **by the State** (citizens only; ⚠ "descent" not a ground)
- **Art 15(2):** applies to State **and private** (shops, hotels, public places)
- **Art 15(3):** special provisions for **women and children** (also executive measures)
- **Art 15(4):** SEBCs, SCs, STs; **Art 15(5):** reservation in educational institutions incl. **private**
- **103rd Amendment (2019):** **10% EWS** — **Arts 15(6), 16(6)**
- **77th Amendment (1995):** **Art 16(4A)** — reservation in promotion for SC/ST
- **Indra Sawhney (1992):** creamy layer for **OBCs only**; **M. Nagaraj (2006):** quantifiable data conditions for promotion quota
