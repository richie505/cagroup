# 4. India's nuclear background
- **Doctrine:** drafted **1999**, adopted **2003** — **Credible Minimum Deterrence**, **No First Use**, massive retaliation, civilian control, no use against non-nuclear states
- **Nuclear Command Authority:** **Political Council** — chaired by **PM**; **Executive Council** — chaired by **NSA**
- **Pokhran-I:** **18 May 1974** — **Smiling Buddha** (Peaceful Nuclear Explosion)
- **Pokhran-II:** **May 1998** — **Operation Shakti** (5 tests); **National Technology Day — 11 May**
- **2008:** India-US civil nuclear deal + **NSG waiver**
- **Not signed:** **NPT**, **CTBT**; opposes **TPNW (2017)**
- **Member of:** **MTCR**, **Wassenaar Arrangement**, **Australia Group**; seeks **NSG**
- **PYQ:** reactors with **imported uranium** under **IAEA safeguards**
