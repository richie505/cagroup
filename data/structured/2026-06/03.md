## Participatory water budgeting in rural India | from: 2026-06-01
# 1. Key facts
- **Highlighted by:** **Ministry of Jal Shakti**
- **Water budgeting:** assessment of **water availability vs demand** in a village / watershed / block / district — shows **surplus or deficit**
- **Agriculture:** **80–90%** of rural water use
- **India share:** **17.5%** of global population, **11.6%** of world livestock
# 2. Schemes
| Scheme | Facts |
|---|---|
| **Atal Bhujal Yojana** | Launched **2019**; community-led groundwater management; **229 blocks** in **7 States**; **180 of 229** blocks improved (2023-24, 2024-25); by **March 2026** — **81,700** structures, **8,203** water budgets |
| **National Water Mission** | Water budgeting = foundation of **Integrated Water Resources Management** |
| **Nari Shakti se Jal Shakti** | Under NWM — **women-led** water conservation (SHGs, WUAs, VWSCs) |
| **Mukhya Mantri Jal Swavlamban Abhiyan** | **Rajasthan** |
| **Jalyukt Shivar Abhiyan** | **Maharashtra**, **2014**; apps by **Maharashtra Remote Sensing Application Centre** |
| **Hiware Bazar** | Community-led watershed model |
# 3. Varuni Web Application
- **What:** digital tool for **block-level water budgeting**
- **Developed under:** **Indo-German** project **Water Security and Climate Adaptation in Rural India**
- **With:** **Ministry of Jal Shakti** + **Ministry of Rural Development**; technical support **NITI Aayog**
# 4. PYQ facts
- **Water Credit:** initiative of **Water.org** (⚠ not WHO / World Bank) — microfinance for water & sanitation
- **IBBI:** est. **1 October 2016** under **Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016**

## Decentralised solar — PM Surya Ghar and PM-KUSUM | from: 2026-06-09
# 1. Status
- **Solar share:** ~**30%** of India's installed capacity; **50+ GW** added in two years; in **2025** India added more solar than any country **except China**
- **Decentralised capacity:** ~**13 GW** vs target ~**40 GW** by end of current FY
- **Standalone solar pumps:** **10.9 lakh** installed vs target **14 lakh**
- **Top rooftop States:** **Gujarat, Maharashtra, UP, Kerala, Rajasthan** — ~**70%** of installations
- **Issue:** free power (e.g. **Punjab** — **300 units** free + free farm power) reduces solar uptake
# 2. Schemes
- **PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana:** rooftop solar for **1 crore households**; free power up to **300 units/month**; **3 lakh+** skilled manpower; MNRE
- **PM-KUSUM:** **Pradhan Mantri Kisan Urja Suraksha Evam Utthan Mahabhiyan** — solar pumps, small plants on unused land


## PMSMA completes 10 years | from: 2026-06-17, CA360-2026-06, OL-2026-06
# 1. About
- **Pradhan Mantri Surakshit Matritva Abhiyaan:** launched **9 June 2016** by **MoHFW** (⚠ not WCD), under **RMNCH+A**
- **10-year theme:** **"10 Years of PMSMA – A Decade of Care"**
- **Free antenatal care** on the **9th of every month** at govt facilities
- **Target group:** **2nd trimester (13–27 weeks)** and **3rd trimester (28 weeks–delivery)**
- **Beneficiaries:** **7.50 crore+** pregnant women
- **Screening:** **25 high-risk factors**; **green sticker** = no risk, **red sticker** = high risk (on **Mother and Child Protection Card**)
- **Private doctors:** volunteer **one day a month** (12 days a year); **IPledgeFor9 Achievers Awards**
# 2. e-PMSMA (2022)
- Name-based line-listing; up to **4** extra sessions/month; follow-up till **45th day** after delivery
- **ASHA:** **₹100** per follow-up visit (up to 3) + **₹500** after healthy outcome; beneficiary **₹100** per visit (max 3)

| **1990** | **556** |
| **2014–16** | **130** |
| **2022–24** | **87** (−43 points) |

- **Maternal death:** during pregnancy or within **42 days** of termination
# 4. Related schemes

## PCPNDT Act and portable ultrasound for cancer diagnosis | from: 2026-06-26
# 1. Facts
- **Enacted:** **1994** as **Pre-Natal Diagnostic Techniques (Regulation and Prevention of Misuse) Act**; **2003** amendment added pre-conception — renamed **PCPNDT (Prohibition of Sex Selection) Act**
- **Rules:** registration before buying machines; machine must stay at registered location; no sex disclosure
- **Debate:** **high-frequency linear probes** (breast, thyroid) cannot image foetal sex — reform for community cancer screening with AI
- **2025:** illegal sex-determination network busted in **Karnataka**
- **Rural population:** ~**70%**
- **Sex ratio (Census):** females per **1,000 males**
