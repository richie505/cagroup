## Green hydrogen roadmap and National Green Hydrogen Mission | from: 2026-06-02
# 1. NGHM
- **Nodal ministry:** **MNRE**; launched **2023**
- **Components:** **SIGHT** (Strategic Interventions for Green Hydrogen Transition), **SHIP** (Strategic Hydrogen Innovation Partnership) for R&D
- **2030 targets:** **5 MMT**/yr green H2; **₹1 lakh crore** import savings; **6 lakh jobs**; **50 MMT CO2** avoided/yr; **₹8 lakh crore** investment
# 2. Types of hydrogen
| Type | Method | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Green** | Electrolysis | **Renewable energy** |
| **Grey** | **Steam Methane Reforming** | Natural gas |
| **Blue** | SMR + **carbon capture** | Natural gas |
| **Brown** | **Gasification** | **Coal** |
# 3. Key numbers
- **Steel:** Ministry of Steel allotted **₹455 crore** for green H2 pilots till **FY 2029–30** (replaces coking coal in **DRI** process)
- **Fertiliser:** India **2nd-largest consumer**, **3rd-largest producer** of fertilisers; **SECI** 10-year contracts — **7,24,000 tonnes** green ammonia/yr to **13 plants** — saves ~**$2.5 billion** over a decade
- **Refineries:** SECI-backed projects ~**30,000 tonnes/yr**
- **Green Hydrogen Hub ports:** **Deendayal (Kandla)**, **V.O. Chidambaranar (Tuticorin)**, **Paradip**
- **Transport pilots:** **37** hydrogen buses/trucks, **10 routes**, **9** refuelling stations
- **AP link:** **NTPC** green hydrogen hub at **Pudimadaka, Andhra Pradesh**
- **Cost (2026):** green ₹**397–560/kg** vs grey ₹**150–200/kg**; water **9–11 litres** per kg H2
- **Delivered cost:** midstream + end-use = **70–85%** of final cost; SME window for **10 kW–2 MW** electrolysers suggested

## RBI special FCNR(B) deposit swap facility | from: 2026-06-11

- **Banks may mobilise:** fresh **3–5 year FCNR(B)** deposits till **September 2026** (eligible till **30 September 2026**)
- **Swap window:** open till **16 October 2026** at **concessional rate** — RBI absorbs **hedging cost**; swap at **FBIL Reference Rate**, **at par**, once a **week**, multiples of **$1 million**

| Account | Currency | Purpose |
