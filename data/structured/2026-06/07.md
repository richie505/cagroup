## Summer air pollution in Indian cities | from: 2026-06-04
# 1. News
- **CAQM** reimposed **GRAP Stage-I** in **Delhi** in **May 2026** — summer pollution episode
# 2. Summer vs winter
| Feature | Summer | Winter |
|---|---|---|
| **Main pollutants** | **PM10**, dust, **ground-level ozone** | **PM2.5**, smog |
| **Peak time** | **Midday / afternoon** | Early morning / night (**inversion**) |
| **Driver** | Heat, sunlight, dust storms | Cold, fog, stagnant air, stubble burning |
# 3. Key concepts
- **Ground-level ozone:** ⚠ **secondary pollutant** — **NOx + VOCs** react in **sunlight**
- **PM10:** diameter **10 micrometres or less**
- **Loo:** hot long-distance dust winds (North India); **Andhi:** short local dust storm with thunderstorms
- **AQEWS (Delhi):** **3-day AQI forecasts**; extended to **Jaipur**, **Mumbai**
- **Mumbai AQDSS:** **BMC** with **CEEW** — action on **1,000+** construction sites since **October 2025**
- **PYQ:** cloud seeding uses **silver iodide** and **potassium iodide**

## Biochar — farm waste to black gold | from: 2026-06-23
# 1. Facts
- **Production:** **pyrolysis** — oxygen-depleted heating at **300–700°C**; by-products **syngas** and **bio-oil**
- ⚠ Decomposes **very slowly** (stores carbon for centuries)
- **Benefits:** water-holding +**10–25%**; yield +**10–30%**; **1 t biochar** sequesters **2.5–3 t CO2e**
- **Carbon credits:** **VM0042** methodology — **2–2.8 t CO2e** credits per tonne; **KISAN kiln** by **IIT Kharagpur**
- **India:** crop waste **500 MT+**/yr; Punjab–Haryana burn **20 MT+** paddy straw; MSW **62 MT** (50%+ biodegradable)
- **Potential:** syngas **20–30 MT** (**8–13 TWh**); bio-oil **24–40 MT** (substitutes **12–19 MT** diesel/kerosene)
- **Concrete:** **2–5%** biochar — heat resistance +**20%**, captures ~**115 kg CO2/m³**
- **Trials:** **Akola** (maize stalk, black soil); **Kerala** (coconut leaf stalk); abroad — **Kenya** (rice husk), **Thailand**, **Brazil (Embrapa)**
- **PYQ (2020):** biochar in vertical farming — aids N-fixing microbes, retains water (all correct)

## Europe's 2026 heatwave — Omega Block | from: 2026-06-25
# 1. Facts
- **Cause:** **Omega Block** — **high pressure between two lows**, shaped like **Ω**; stalls the **jet stream** (west-to-east)
- **Heat dome:** sinking, compressed air under the high; ⚠ **suppresses** clouds and rain
- **France:** **18** heat deaths, **40** drownings; alerts in France, UK, Spain, Italy
- **Europe:** warming at **more than twice** the global rate — **fastest-warming continent**
- **Concepts:** **climate whiplash** (rapid swing between extremes); **albedo** loss from Arctic ice; **tropical night**; **urban heat island**
- **El Niño:** developing — raises probability (⚠ not certainty) of weak Indian monsoon
