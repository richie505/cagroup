## SC on SIR — ECI can examine but not decide citizenship | from: 2026-06-01
# 1. Verdict
- **Case:** **Association for Democratic Reforms v. Election Commission of India**
- **Upheld:** ECI order for **Special Intensive Revision (SIR)** of electoral rolls — conducted before **November 2025 Bihar Assembly elections**
- **Held:** SIR **broadly proportionate**; ECI is best placed to decide method of revision
- **Citizenship:** ECI may examine citizenship **prima facie and contextually** (for electoral roll only)
- ⚠ ECI **cannot finally declare** a person a non-citizen — deletion from roll ≠ loss of citizenship
- **Referral:** doubtful cases go to **competent authority under Citizenship Act, 1955** — decide preferably **before the next election** in that constituency
- **Presumption:** name on roll gives only an **evidentiary presumption** of citizenship, not absolute
# 2. Safeguards ordered
- **Aadhaar** accepted as **proof of identity**
- **Deleted voters' names** published with **reasons**; opportunity of **hearing**; **judicial officers** assist hearings
# 3. Background
- **Article 324:** superintendence, direction and control of elections — ECI
- **RPA, 1950:** preparation/revision of electoral rolls; **Section 16** — non-citizen cannot be registered as voter
- **Assam D-voter:** **1997** revision marked "doubtful voters", referred to **Foreigners Tribunals**

## Right to be forgotten vs open justice | from: 2026-06-04, CA360-2026-06, OL-2026-06
# 1. News
- **Trigger:** **Delhi High Court** order of **29 May 2026** on limited online discoverability of judicial records
- **Right to be forgotten:** removal / de-indexing of outdated personal info — called **right to erasure** in European law (**GDPR**)
- **Linked to:** informational privacy under **Article 21** — ⚠ **not absolute**
- **DPDP Act, 2023:** recognises right to erasure; application to court records unclear
- **Delhi HC (Justice Sachin Datta):** recognised RTBF as part of privacy under **Article 21**; search engines may **delist** judgments linked to a name — mainly for **acquitted** persons, private civil disputes, non-parties
# 2. Key cases
| Case | Holding |
|---|---|
| **Rajagopal v. State of TN (1994)** | **Right to be let alone**; court records open to comment |
| **K.S. Puttaswamy v. UoI (2017)** | Privacy a fundamental right; informational privacy |
| **Dharamraj Bhanushankar Dave v. State of Gujarat (2017)** | Gujarat HC denied removal of acquittal details |
| **Orissa HC (2020)** | Online sexual abuse — called for debate on RTBF |
| **Delhi HC (2021)** | Allowed limited removal from search results |
| **Supreme Court (2022)** | Registry to remove a couple's details (marital dispute) from search engines |
| **Kerala HC (2023)** | RTBF not automatic for **ongoing** proceedings |
| **Indian Kanoon matter (2024)** | Warned obfuscation harms public record |
- **PYQ:** Right to Privacy — **Article 21** and Part III freedoms

## FCRA rules tightened and FCRA Amendment Bill, 2026 | from: 2026-06-25, OL-2026-06
# 1. MHA notifications
- **First:** amends **Foreign Contribution (Regulation) Rules, 2011** — registration tied to purposes from a Schedule of **105 permissible purposes**; **States/UTs** of operation specified; existing NGOs get **one year** to choose
- **Proselytisation:** explicitly **excluded** from permitted religious activities
- **Key functionary:** widened (directors, partners, trustees, **Karta** of HUF, etc.)
- **Disclosures:** social-media accounts, **ultimate donors**, donor-advised funds
- **Second:** revises compounding penalties under **Section 41(1)**
| Violation | Penalty (higher of) |
|---|---|
| **Admin spend above 20% limit** | **₹1 lakh** or **5%** of excess |
| **Speculative / stock investment** | **₹1 lakh** or **30%** |
| **Diversion of funds** | **₹1 lakh** or **30%** |
# 2. FCRA Amendment Bill, 2026
- **Designated authority** (appointed by Centre) to manage foreign-funded **assets** when registration is cancelled/surrendered/expires — replaces **Section 15** framework
- **Places of worship:** management may be entrusted to another person, preserving religious character
# 3. Background
- **FCRA 1976 → FCRA 2010**; amended **2016, 2018, 2020**
- Funds via designated **SBI account, New Delhi**; no transfer to other NGOs
- **Barred:** election candidates, parties, legislators, **judges**, govt servants, **journalists**, media companies
- **Validity:** **5 years**; renew **6 months** before expiry; cancellation if inactive **2 years**; re-registration barred **3 years**
- **Article 25:** profess, practise, propagate; **Rev. Stainislaus v. State of MP (1977)** — no fundamental right to **convert** another person
