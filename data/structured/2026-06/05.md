## India–Oman CEPA comes into force | from: 2026-06-03, CA360-2026-06, OL-2026-06
# 1. Key facts
- **In force:** **1 June 2026**
- **Duty-free access:** **99.38%** of India's exports by value; covers **98.08%** of Oman's tariff lines (earlier only **15.33%** zero duty under MFN)
- **Services:** Oman commitments in **127 sub-sectors**
- **India's offer:** tariff concessions on **77.79%** of its tariff lines
- **Intra-corporate transferees ceiling:** raised **20% → 50%**
- **Pharma:** medicines approved by **USFDA, EMA, UK MHRA, TGA** get Omani authorisation within **90 days**
- **Recognises:** **NPOP** organic certification, **Export Inspection Council** certificates, halal certification
- **Excluded (sensitive):** dairy, cereals, fruits, vegetables, edible oils, oilseeds, rubber, leather, spices; **TRQ** and **Minimum Import Price** tools
# 2. Background
- **Indians in Oman:** ~**7 lakh**; merchant families for **200–300 years**
- ⚠ Oman = India's **second-largest** trading partner in the **Gulf** (not largest)
- **Bilateral trade:** **USD 11.18 billion** (**FY 2025–26**)
- **Ports:** **Sohar**, **Duqm**, **Salalah** — gateway to **GCC** and **East Africa**
- **Oman:** capital **Muscat**, Sultan **Haitham bin Tarik**; **GCC** est. **1981**, HQ **Riyadh** — Bahrain, Kuwait, Oman, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, UAE
