| Site | State | Key facts |
|---|---|---|

- **India:** **Mahatma Gandhi Satyagraha Smarak** (East Champaran), **Sabarmati Ashram**, **Mani Bhavan**, **Harijan Sevak Sangh Complex**, **Sevagram Ashram**, **Aga Khan Palace**, **Gandhi Smriti**
# 2. Sarnath
- **Ancient Buddhist Site of Sarnath** inscribed at **48th WHC session**, **Busan, South Korea**, **July 2026**
- India's **45th** WHS; **UP's 4th** (others: Taj Mahal, Agra Fort, Fatehpur Sikri)
- Components: **Chaukhandi Stupa** + archaeological remains; criteria **(iii)** and **(vi)**
- Buddha's **first sermon** (Dharmachakra); ancient name **Rishipattan**; on Tentative List since **3 July 1998** (**28 years**)
# 3. Background
- **World Heritage Convention 1972**; **196** States Parties (2024); **10 criteria** (i–vi cultural, vii–x natural), merged in **2004**
- **ICOMOS** evaluates cultural, **IUCN** natural; **21-member** World Heritage Committee
- ⚠ Tentative listing does **not** confer WHS status; India: **ASI** and **INCCU** propose
- Danger list examples: **Bamiyan Valley**, **Zabid** (Yemen), **Bam** (Iran)
