## Nuclear power in space — RTGs, fission and governance | from: 2026-10-01
# 1. Systems
| System | How it works | Examples / use |
|---|---|---|
| **RTG** (Radioisotope Thermoelectric Generator) | Heat from natural decay of **plutonium-238** → electricity; **no moving parts** | **Voyager 1 & 2**, **Curiosity**, **Perseverance** |
| **RHU** (Radioisotope Heater Unit) | Heat only — ⚠ **no electricity** | Keeps instruments from freezing |
| **Fission reactor** | Controlled chain reaction → much higher power | Lunar/Martian habitats |
| **Nuclear Thermal Propulsion (NTP)** | Reactor heats **liquid hydrogen** propellant | ~**twice** efficiency of chemical rockets |
| **Nuclear Electric Propulsion (NEP)** | Electricity accelerates ionised propellant | Low thrust, very high efficiency |
- **Radioisotope systems** used since **1961**; **Voyager** launched **1977** (5-year mission), now >**20 billion km** away
# 2. Plans
- **India:** **ISRO** invited proposals (**2021**) for a **100-watt RTG**; **NASA** invited ISRO to its **Moon Base** programme
- **NASA Dragonfly** to **Titan** (Saturn) — launch **July 2028**, radioisotope generator
- **NASA (Aug 2025):** lunar fission system **≥100 kW** by Q1 FY**2030**
- **White House memo (Apr 2026):** **20-kW** reactors in orbit by **2028**; on Moon by **2030**
- **China & Russia:** reactor on Moon by **mid-2030s** for **International Lunar Research Station**
# 3. Legal framework
| Instrument | Key point |
|---|---|
| **Outer Space Treaty 1967** | Bans nuclear weapons/WMD in orbit — ⚠ does **not** ban peaceful nuclear power |
| **Liability Convention 1972** | Launching State **absolutely liable** for damage on Earth / to aircraft in flight |
| **UN Principles on NPS 1992** | Safety, radiation protection, re-entry notification — ⚠ only **non-propulsive** use |
| **COPUOS–IAEA Safety Framework 2009** | Guidance — **not binding** |
- **Cosmos 954:** Soviet satellite re-entered over **northern Canada** (**1978**); settled **1981** for **C$3 million**
- **Pu-238 shortage:** US restarted production in **2015** after ~3 decades
