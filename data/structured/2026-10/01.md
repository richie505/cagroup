## AP greenfield ports — doubling OTMP cargo capacity | from: 2026-10-01
# 1. Target
- **Plan:** **Four greenfield** **Other Than Major Ports (OTMPs)** operational by **end of 2027**
- **Capacity:** OTMP cargo handling from **100 MTPA** to **200 MTPA** (double)
- **Visakhapatnam:** AP's **only major port** — throughput around **100 MTPA** (equal to all existing AP OTMPs)
# 2. AP's position
- **OTMPs** handle nearly **50%** of India's total port cargo; **AP ports** account for **16%** of OTMP cargo
- ⚠ AP ranks **second** after **Gujarat** in OTMP cargo handling (**not first**)
# 3. The four ports
| Port | District / location | Details |
|---|---|---|
| **Ramayapatnam** | **Prakasam** | State port; advanced construction; **AP Maritime Board** issued **RFP** for operator — to be finalised by **end of October 2026** |
| **Mulapeta** | **Srikakulam** | State port |
| **Machilipatnam** | — | State port; built by **Megha Engineering & Infrastructures Ltd (MEIL)** |
| **Kakinada Gateway Port** | **Kakinada SEZ** | **Multi-cargo** facility; developer **Aurobindo Group**; **PPP model** |
# 4. Exam angle
- **Hinterland served:** **Telangana**, **Chhattisgarh**, **Jharkhand**, **Karnataka**, **Odisha**, **Maharashtra**
- **Aim:** AP as **maritime gateway of India's east coast**; bridge container gap on **Haldia–Visakhapatnam–Chennai** corridor
