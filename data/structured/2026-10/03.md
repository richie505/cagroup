## TB Mukt Bharat — JOSH Teams launch and progress | from: 2026-10-08, 2026-10-15
# 1. Key facts
- **JOSH:** **Joint Squad for Health** — launched nationally **8 October 2026**, Bharat Mandapam, by Health Minister **J.P. Nadda**
- **Ministries:** **Health**, **Youth Affairs & Sports**, **Defence**
- **Phase 1:** **55 districts** in **11 States**; team = Class-I officers + **MY Bharat** volunteers + **NCC** cadets
- AI chatbot "**Khushi Avatar**"; **2025:** **67,933** panchayats in **697** districts declared TB Mukt
# 2. Background
- *Mycobacterium tuberculosis*; **BCG** vaccine; **World TB Day 24 March** (Koch, **1882**)
- **NTEP** (formerly RNTCP); India target **2025** vs SDG **2030**
- **MY Bharat** launched **31 October 2023**; **NCC** est. **1948**, motto "**Unity and Discipline**"
# 3. Progress
- **New TB case rate 2015–2024:** India **−21%** vs global **−12%**
- **Treatment coverage:** India **92%** vs global **78%**
- **28.1 crore** tests; **20 lakh+** got TB Preventive Treatment; **5.7 lakh** new **Ni-kshay Mitras**; **46,118** GPs certified TB-free; handheld X-ray screening up **34-fold**
- **BPaLM** regimen for drug-resistant TB — **Bedaquiline, Pretomanid, Linezolid, Moxifloxacin**
- **PM TB Mukt Bharat Abhiyaan** launched **9 September 2022**
- ⚠ 8 Oct source: **67,933** panchayats TB Mukt in 2025; 15 Oct source: **46,118** GPs certified
