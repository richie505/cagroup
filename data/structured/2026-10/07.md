## Short-Lived Climate Pollutants (SLCPs) | from: 2026-10-08
# 1. Key facts
- SLCPs cause up to **45%** of present warming; IPCC — rapid cuts can avoid up to **0.6°C** by **2050**
- **Also called:** **super pollutants**, **Short-Lived Climate Forcers**
- **Major warming SLCPs:** **black carbon**, **methane**, **tropospheric ozone**, **HFCs**
- ⚠ **Black carbon** is **not a greenhouse gas** — a particle that absorbs radiation; **sulphate aerosols** cool
- ⚠ **Tropospheric ozone** is **not directly emitted** — formed from methane, NOx, VOCs in sunlight
- **Methane:** ~**80 times** CO2 warming over **20 years**; second-largest contributor after CO2
- **Stratospheric ozone** ~10–50 km; tropospheric surface–10 km
- Ozone causes loss of ~**110 million tonnes** staple crops/year; air pollution ~**7 million** premature deaths/year globally
# 2. India
- Air pollution: **1.67 million** deaths in **2019** (**17.8%**), loss **US$36.8 billion** (**1.36%** of GDP)
- Residential fuels = ~**60%** of South Asia's black carbon; **Indo-Gangetic Plain** hotspot
- **Methane (2016, Third BUR):** Agriculture **73.96%**, Waste **14.46%**, Energy **10.62%**, Industry **0.96%**
- ⚠ India has **not joined** the **Global Methane Pledge** — calls farm methane "**survival emissions**"
# 3. International framework
| Instrument | Fact |
|---|---|
| **Montreal Protocol** | **1987** — ozone-depleting substances |
| **Kigali Amendment** | **2016**; India ratified **Sept 2021**; freeze HFCs by **2028**, cut **85%** by **2047**; can avoid **0.5°C** by 2100 |
| **Global Methane Pledge** | **COP26 Glasgow 2021**; cut **30%** from 2020 levels by **2030**; **159** countries + EC |
| **Climate and Clean Air Coalition** | **UNEP**-led; India joined **2019** |
# 4. India's measures
- **Air Act 1981**; **NAAQS 2009** (**12** pollutants); **National AQI 2015**; **GRAP 2017** (Delhi-NCR)
- **NCAP:** **130** cities, **24** States/UTs; **2025–26:** **65** cities within PM2.5 limit **40 µg/m³**; Delhi **102 µg/m³**; only **14** met PM10 (**60 µg/m³**)
- **PMUY:** >**10.5 crore** LPG connections; **GOBARdhan**; SRI, DSR, alternate wetting & drying
