# 5. Scheme name

## Bharat Maritime Insurance Pool (BMIP) | from: 2026-10-02
# 1. Key facts
- **What:** India's **first** collective domestic platform for insuring large maritime risks
- **Approved:** **18 April 2026**; **launched:** **12 May 2026** (operationalised May 2026)
- **Capacity:** **₹13,906.50 crore** (~**USD 1.5 billion**); **sovereign guarantee:** **₹12,980 crore** (~**USD 1.4 billion**)
- **Duration:** **10 years**, extendable to max **15 years**
- **Administrator:** **General Insurance Corporation of India (GIC Re)**
- **Claims:** up to **USD 100 million** from reserves/reinsurance; sovereign guarantee only after reserves exhausted
- **Covers:** **Hull & Machinery**, **Cargo War**, **Protection & Indemnity (P&I)**, **War-perils**
- ⚠ Eligibility **not only** Indian-flagged ships — also vessels owned/managed by Indian entities and cargo to/from India
# 2. Achievements (by 7 Sept 2026)
- **3,000** Cargo War, **92** Hull War-risk, **3** P&I policies
- War-risk premiums down **35–40%** from West Asia conflict peak
- **First policy (12 May 2026):** **New India Assurance** → **Hoger Offshore and Marine Pvt Ltd** (H&M War-risk)
- **First P&I policy (30 July 2026):** to **Shipping Corporation of India** by **New India Assurance**
- Others: **Vedanta Sterlite Copper** (cargo war), **Balrampur Chini Mills**
# 3. Background
- **International Group of P&I Clubs:** **13** clubs insure ~**90%** of world's large ships
- **Maritime trade:** ~**95%** by value, **70%** by volume
- **Ports:** **12 major**, **217 non-major**; cargo ~**1,668 MMT** in **2025–26**
- **Coastline:** ~**11,098 km**; **EEZ** **2.4 million sq km**; inland waterways **>14,500 km**
- **Fleet (mid-2026):** **1,609** ships, **14.33 million GT** (~**36%** growth since 2015)
- **P&I premium outflow:** ~**USD 45–60 million**/year
