## UPSC at 100 — centenary of the Public Service Commission | from: 2026-10-01, 2026-10-03
# 1. Why in news
- **UPSC** completed **100 years** on **1 October 2026**; India's first **Public Service Commission** constituted on **1 October 1926**
- **HQ:** **Dholpur House, New Delhi**
# 2. Evolution
| Year | Milestone |
|---|---|
| **1855** | **ICS** exam introduced in **London** (replacing EIC nomination system) |
| **1863/1864** | **Satyendranath Tagore** cleared ICS (1863), joined 1864 — **first Indian ICS** member |
| **1919** | Only **78 of 1,255** ICS officers Indian; **GoI Act 1919** provided for a PSC |
| **1922** | ICS exam held in India first time at **Allahabad**; moved to **Delhi** in **1928** |
| **1924** | **Lee Commission** (chair **Arthur Hamilton Lee**) recommended statutory PSC |
| **1926** | PSC set up under **PSC (Functions) Rules 1926**; first Chairman **Sir Ross Barker**; **4 Members**; at **Metcalfe House, Delhi** |
| **1935/1937** | **GoI Act 1935** — Federal & Provincial PSCs; renamed **Federal PSC** in **1937** |
| **1947** | **H.K. Kripalani** — first **Indian Chairman** |
| **22 Aug 1949** | PSC provisions moved in Constituent Assembly by **Dr B.R. Ambedkar** |
| **26 Jan 1950** | Became **UPSC**; **R.N. Banerjee** first Chairman under Constitution |
# 3. Constitutional framework — Part XIV, Articles 315–323
| Article | Provision |
|---|---|
| **315** | PSCs for Union and States; **Joint** PSC for two or more States |
| **316** | Appointment and tenure — UPSC members appointed by **President** |
| **317** | Removal/suspension by President; misbehaviour needs **Supreme Court inquiry** |
| **318** | President regulates conditions of service |
| **319** | Post-office bar — UPSC Chairman **ineligible** for further govt employment |
| **320** | Functions — exams, recruitment, promotions, disciplinary |
| **321** | Parliament/State Legislature can **extend functions** |
| **322** | Expenses **charged on Consolidated Fund of India** |
| **323** | Annual report to **President** (⚠ not CJI) — laid before Parliament |
# 4. Reforms timeline
| Year | Reform |
|---|---|
| **1938** | Combined competitive exams began |
| **1966** | **Indian Forest Service** — **third All India Service** |
| **1969** | **Eighth Schedule** languages for Essay/GS |
| **1979** | **Three-stage CSE** (Prelims–Mains–Interview) |
| **1989** | **OMR** introduced |
| **1992** | **Rose Millian Bathew Kharbuli** — first woman UPSC Chairperson |
| **1993** | Separate **Essay** paper |
| **1994** | **Scribes** for visually impaired |
| **2000** | Online Recruitment Application |
| **2011** | **CSAT** introduced |
| **2013** | Mains: **4 GS** papers incl. **Ethics, Integrity & Aptitude** + one optional |
| **2015** | CSAT qualifying — **33%** |
| **2016** | **UPSC Museum** opened |
| **2018** | **Public Disclosure Scheme** — revamped as **Pratibha Setu** on **9 June 2025** |
| **2019** | **EWS** from CSE 2019; examination control room |
| **2020** | Separate **transgender** category in ORA |
| **2021** | **Women** admitted to **NDA & NA** exam first time |
| **2022** | 4 Group A railway services unified as **Indian Railway Management Service** |
# 5. Inclusion and technology
- **Women:** **3.6%** (3 of 83) in **1952** → **31.2%** (299 of 958) in **CSE 2025**; **11 of top 25** ranks
- **Kiran Bedi:** first woman **IPS** (**1972**)
- **SC:** 9 (1955) → **158** (2025); **ST:** 2 (1956–57) → **73** (2025); **PwBD:** 3 (2002) → **42** (2025)
- **AI facial authentication:** piloted at **NDA/CDS Sept 2025**; at scale in **CS Prelims 24 May 2026** — ~**5.50 lakh** candidates, **6–8 seconds** each
- **569** applicants rejected for false claims (15 years of data)
- **Universal Registration Number:** **May 2025**; **randomised interview boards** from **CMS 2025**; **30-channel helpdesk** from **Feb 2026**; **QPRep portal** for answer-key representations; training via **NIELIT**
- **DGP empanelment:** since **2012–13**, per **Prakash Singh v. Union of India**
- **2023–24:** **24,581** officers' promotion records; **532** inducted into AIS; disciplinary cases **446**
- **Applications:** **42,727** (1950–51) → peak **45,10,044** (**2022–23**); exam centres **8** (1950) → **86** (2026)
# 6. Lee Commission
- **Appointed 1923**, report **1924**; chairman **Lord Lee of Fareham**; recommended statutory PSC

## Central Armed Police Forces (General Administration) Act 2026 in force | from: 2026-10-10
# 1. Key facts
- In force **8 October 2026** (MHA gazette)
- Common framework for appointment/service of **Group-A General Duty** officers in CAPFs
- **Timeline:** introduced in **Rajya Sabha 27 March 2026**; RS passed **1 April**; LS **2 April**; assent **9 April 2026**
- Serving officers challenged it in **Supreme Court**
# 2. Background
| Force | Est. | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **BSF** | **1965** | Pakistan & Bangladesh borders |
| **CRPF** | **1939** (Crown Representative's Police) | Internal security |
| **CISF** | **1969** | Infrastructure, airports |
| **ITBP** | **1962** | China border |
| **SSB** | **1963** | Nepal & Bhutan borders |
- **Assam Rifles:** administrative MHA, operational **Army**; **Article 33** — Parliament may restrict FRs of forces
